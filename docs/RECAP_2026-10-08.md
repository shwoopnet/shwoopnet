# Recap: the strategy and research day, 2026-10-08

Written to be read later, cold. The formal records (pre-registrations, results, the variant tally) live in `kalshi-scalper/README.md`; this page is the story and the state of play. Times are US Central.

## Where things stand in one paragraph

The live bot (L1, buy the 88c to 97c favourite 6 minutes before the close, hold to settlement) runs 24/7 on the owner's real Kalshi account at about $511, sized at 1% of cash per position (5 contracts, about $4.63), stop at 5% of the balance at the last review with the trailing stop on. Its edge is thin (about +0.7c a contract, z 1.45 against a bar of 2.1) and is not proven. Every opening-window (first 5 to 10 minutes) strategy tried so far has failed after cost. Four new tick-level tests (T1 to T4) are pre-registered and their data fetch is running; their results are the next thing to read.

## Things that went wrong, and what they taught

- **A committed symlink broke GitHub Pages builds** for three merges. Fixed, and a test now guards tracked symlinks and what Pages publishes.
- **A time exit searched for its target after the sale point** (C2, C3, OP1 to OP3). First results looked better than registered predictions; that was the audit trigger. Fixed, numbers withdrawn and corrected. Lesson: a result better than its prediction is a reason to audit.
- **First skim design banked half of every win** and far more than net profit. Rebuilt to skim only new net highs.
- **A stale 3-contract guess on the stake row** showed a made-up size before a new session's first look. It now says "sized at the first look".
- **Money on other shards cannot be traded.** A deposit can land on a shard the bot does not use. The owner moved it with one near-certain manual trade. Sizing reads the total balance.

## The strategy search, in plain terms

Variant tally: **2,582** before today's four, none passing the bar (n of at least 300, at least 5 days, day clustered z of at least 2.1, both halves positive, still positive with fees x1.2).

| Idea | Result |
|---|---|
| L1 (the live rule) | Thin edge, failed its own bar, kept running at small size |
| Opening-window taker rules (M1, C1 to C3, OP1 to OP3, S series, four slot search) | All falsified, about -2c to -3c a trade after cost |
| Filters on L1 (Q1 to Q3, OP4, OP5) | All falsified; the tight-book filter removes only 82 of 3,432 entries |
| Resting (maker) entries (P2, H3, H5) | Falsified; filled on the losers |
| Live fills study | 35 fills with 0 losses, 22 misses with 4 losses (one sided p = 0.019); a lead from one look, not a result. Do not chase misses with a higher limit, retry or resting order. Re-read around 2026-10-23 |
| Sizing replay at the deployed rule | Mean +$82.6 over 10 weeks from $505 (an upper bound), worst 1% -$42, deepest drawdown about $73 (14% of the account); 3 contracts flat gave +$43.8 and $44 |

Why opening-window trades are hard: opening spreads are 1.0c in every hour, and a taker round trip pays about 3c in fees plus the spread. Cost, not the absence of signal, is the binding constraint.

## The four tests waiting on the tape (pre-registered, not yet run)

All use the public trade tape for the first 300 seconds after the open of every settled BTC and gold 15 minute market in the last 30 days (about 4,964 markets). Predictions were written first, and all four are expected to fail.

| Test | Rule | Predicted mean |
|---|---|---|
| T1 early taker flow | At least 50 taker contracts and 60% one sided over the first 5 minutes: buy that side, hold to settlement | +1.0c (the only one with a positive prediction) |
| T2 the same, closed before L1 | Same entry, sold at the bid 7 minutes before the close | -0.5c |
| T3 early drift scalp (the owner's recorded trade) | First minute's price drift of 3c or more: buy the leaning side at 55c to 70c one second later, sell at +5c or at 240 s | -1.5c |
| T4 cheap side scalp | Buy a 4c to 10c side from second 61 to 180, sell at +3c or at 240 s | -1.0c |

T2 to T4 exist because the owner wants opening-window trades that open and close inside the window, so they add to L1 instead of replacing it (flat 7 or more minutes before L1's window at 6:40 to 5:30 left).

**Owner's manual scalps, as anecdotes (n = 2, no verdict):** BTC, bought Down at about 62c one minute in, sold a minute later at 68.5% for $1.09 on $1.00; BTC cheap side, Yes at 7.7c, cash out value up 16%. One win each is inside the noise of a single trade (plus or minus 5c).

## Other options discussed

True high frequency market making is out: the bot runs once a minute from a cloud function, quoting both sides was falsified (adverse selection about 4c a fill against about 1c of spread), and 1c of spread on 5 contracts is $0.05. What is left needs order books, which the recorder started collecting on 2026-10-08: resting entries (O1 to O8, L4), locked or crossed books (L14), quote staleness and depth imbalance (L5, L12), BTC against gold consistency (L6). Plan: about 2026-10-12 to 13, download the book snapshots (Tools, Download book snapshots), check how fine they are (the locked-book idea needs under 10 seconds apart), pre-register one maker study and the locked-book count, run each once.

## How to resume

1. **Read the tape results.** The fetch writes to `kalshi-scalper/data/book.sqlite` (not in git) and ends by printing T1 to T4 into `/tmp/tape_result.txt`. If the container was reclaimed, rebuild: `cd kalshi-scalper/src`, `python3 -m scalper.backfill range 68 0` (about 1 hour), then `python3 -m scalper.tapeflow fetch 30` (1 to 3 hours, resumable), then `python3 -m scalper.tapeflow run` (one run; do not re-run with other parameters).
2. **Record the verdicts** in `kalshi-scalper/README.md` under each pre-registration, with the signal count first (fewer than 300 means FALSIFIED by default and says nothing about the idea), and update the tally from 2,582 to 2,586.
3. **Around 2026-10-23:** the forward read of the live fills study and the L1 forward checks (F0 to F9).
4. **Sizing:** do not raise above 1% per position until the forward read; 1.5% would give roughly 1.5 times the average and 1.5 times the bad cases.
5. **Open decisions:** whether to delete the unused single-order functions (`kalshiLiveTrade`, `kalshiLiveArm`); whether to add a manual scalps scoreboard (pairs each manual buy with its sell by market) to the Account card.
6. **Weekly:** the docs refresh routine runs Fridays at 5:07 PM CT and pushes a branch only.
