# Owed priors for S1, S2 and S7 (Nov 12, 2026). Arithmetic from published effect sizes recalled from memory; NOT verified against the papers, NOT measured here. No Databento data used.

Method: PF 1.5 needs mean/sd of 0.162 per event (normal approximation; PF about 1 + 1.25 d for small d, so d 0.04 is PF about 1.05 to 1.07). Costs are the registered ASSUMED ones.

**S1, overnight on SPY QQQ IWM DIA.** Published effect (Cliff, Cooper and Gulen 2008; Lou, Polk and Skouras 2019, recalled): nearly all of the equity premium accrues overnight, on the order of 3 to 5 bp a day gross for the index; overnight sd about 50 to 70 bp. That is d about 0.05 to 0.08 before cost. Round trip assumed cost 2 x 0.5 + 0.2 = 1.2 bp takes d to about 0.03 to 0.06, PF about 1.04 to 1.08. Chance of PF 1.5 in each of three windows: well under 1 percent; arithmetic says it cannot clear whatever the data shows unless the real overnight mean is more than 4 times the recalled one. Recommendation: it still runs ($0.03) as the pipeline's first end-to-end check and as a measurement of the overnight premium and the assumed costs; its verdict is expected "below the bar at n = x". It could be retired unread otherwise.

**S2, overnight clientele, long leg (judged).** Published (Lou, Polk, Skouras 2019, recalled): past overnight-minus-intraday returns persist in the cross section; reported long-short spreads are large but gross, on large turnover and with many small stocks. Long decile leg held close to open: sd of a 50-name basket overnight roughly 40 to 60 bp; needs net mean of about 6 to 10 bp a night, so about 9 to 13 bp gross after 3.2 bp of assumed round trip cost. The long leg alone earns a small part of the spread in the literature as I recall it, and ex-dividend gaps understate it (conservative). Chance of clearing 1.5 in all three windows: low, my guess 3 to 8 percent, dominated by whether the 2018 to 2021 persistence survives into 2024 to 2026 (published sample ends before). It cannot be ruled out by arithmetic, so it should run. The long-short line is reported, not judged.

**S7, first 30 minutes predicts last 30, SPY.** Published (Gao, Han, Li and Zhou 2018, recalled): first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour with R squared about 1.6 percent (correlation about 0.126). For a sign rule, expected gross is about corr x sd x 0.8 = 0.10 sd of the last-half-hour return; last-30-minute SPY sd about 15 to 25 bp, so gross about 1.5 to 2.5 bp, d about 0.10 before costs. Costs 1.2 bp take d to about 0.04 to 0.05 (PF about 1.05 to 1.07). The effect is also reported to be strongest on volatile and macro days, which a sign rule on every day dilutes. Chance of PF 1.5 in each of three windows: well under 1 percent. Arithmetic says it cannot clear after costs whatever the data shows, unless the real effect is about 3 times the published one. Recommendation to the Head: retire S7 unread; S6 needs the same 1 minute pull ($6.83) and is a screen that cannot pass, so the pull itself is worth less than it looked. Decision is the Head's.

Summary: S1 expected below the bar (runs, $0.03), S2 long leg low but not arithmetically excluded (runs), S7 arithmetically excluded at the published effect (recommend retire unread).
