# Combo book recorder (Nov 12, 2026, the owner approved)

Research data only. Nothing here trades, signs or holds a key. It records Kalshi's combo (multivariate, exchange_index 1) markets so a paper test of buying either side of a combo (the cheap side or the house side) can be priced with real depth instead of the top of book alone.

**What it records.** Every 30 minutes one poll lists the open combos (`mve_filter=only`, at most 5 pages of 1,000, so at most 5,000 of the many tens of thousands), keeps every combo with a posted bid or ask at any price on the yes or the no side, or any traded volume (widened on Nov 12, 2026 from a yes price of 0.30 or more; an empty side, a bid of 0 or an ask of 1.0000, is not a price), and writes: their quotes (at most 400 rows, with the leg count and, for the first 100 rows, the leg tickers), the order book of the 40 with the tightest yes spread (best 5 YES and 5 NO bids with sizes), and the public trades of the 15 most traded. The choice is deterministic: tightest yes spread first, then the most volume, then the ticker; a combo with only one side posted has no spread and ranks after every two-sided one, so it is in the quotes (while the 400 row cap allows) but rarely gets a book read. Once a day, in the 00 UTC hour, it also reads settled combos that traded (3 pages) so outcomes sit next to the depth. A coverage row per run says how many combos were listed, whether the list was complete, how many requests and refusals there were, and whether it stopped early. The listing is a sample, not a census: the exchange's order is not ours to choose, so read the coverage file before counting anything.

**Leg tickers (added Nov 12, 2026, the Head of R&D: so nesting and price-consistency checks are possible).** `quotes.csv` has a last column `leg_tickers`: the combo's legs as `market_ticker:side` joined by `|`, read from the same listing answer that gives the leg count (`mve_selected_legs`), so no request is added. It is capped at 600 characters; a longer one is cut at a whole leg and ends with `|...`, and `legs` keeps the true count. To keep a day under 1 MB it is filled for the first 100 quote rows only (the tightest spreads, which are also the ones that get books); rows 101 to 400 leave it empty. A missing legs list is empty, never zero. Older files have 18 columns and stay parseable (the new column is appended last).

**Combo trades and legs (Nov 13, 2026, the owner's one exception to the freeze, for Idea Desk C's CROWDLEG and NOTAKER cards).** The 15 per-combo trade reads are gone. Each poll now reads the whole PUBLIC trade tape (`/markets/trades?min_ts=&max_ts=&limit=1000`, no ticker, cursor followed) from the previous poll's minute to now (the workflow hands it over as `--since`; the script clamps the window to 25 minutes to 2 hours back, and uses 40 minutes when it is unknown), at most 30 pages, and keeps only combo rows (ticker `KXMVE...` of a safe shape), each `trade_id` once, in `trades.csv` with the same columns as before (ticker, created_time, yes price, count, taker side, trade id). The tape is read before the books, so a refused read costs books, not tape. For every combo that printed, `legs.csv` (`t_recv_ms, ticker, event_ticker, source, legs, leg_tickers`) gives its legs: from the listing already in hand when it was listed (`source` list, no request), otherwise from `/markets?tickers=` in batches of 40, at most 5 requests a poll (`source` lookup); a combo still unknown is counted as missing in the coverage row, never guessed. Every quote row now carries `leg_tickers` (it was the first 100). The coverage row adds `tape_min_ts, tape_max_ts, tape_pages, tape_complete, tape_rows, combo_trades, combos_traded, legs_from_list, legs_looked_up, legs_missing`; `tape_complete` is 1 only when the cursor ran out, so a truncated tape is never read as a quiet one. Two read notes (Main's review): the windows of successive polls overlap on purpose, so a reader must de-duplicate trades by `trade_id` across polls; and the tape comes newest first with a page cap, so in a busy hour the oldest combo trades of the window are lost and that poll says `tape_complete` 0: check the first days of coverage rows before counting. What each card reads: CROWDLEG counts YES-taker combo trades per leg in the last 60 minutes (trades joined to legs.csv) and prices NO on listed combos containing the leg (quote rows with leg tickers); NOTAKER takes each NO-taker combo trade joined to its legs and prices the same way. The budget stays 90 (5 list pages + 30 tape pages + 5 look-ups + 40 books + 3 settled pages = 83); the 30 page tape cap is an ESTIMATE until the CROWDLEG_TAPE_TRIAL page count for a past half hour lands, and is raised (with the budget, never past 10 requests a minute) only as far as that count needs. Size and runner minutes: the request budget and so the run length are unchanged (at most 9 minutes of polling); the extra data is the combo trade rows, the legs rows and leg tickers on 300 more quote rows, an ESTIMATE of up to about 1 MB a day more gzipped, to be re-measured from the first files.

**Where it goes.** Gzipped CSV files on its own `combo-data` branch (under `combo/DATE/HHMM/`), pushed with the built-in token. Never main, never the other data branches, never published with the site (`_config.yml` excludes `.github`, `scripts` and `docs`).

**Safety.** Public GET endpoints under `/markets` only. No key, no secret, no auth header, no POST, no order path, no Firestore. A plain User-Agent (`shwoopnet-research-recorder`). Redirects are refused. A 429 waits 5 seconds, doubling up to 60; three refusals in a row end the run early and it says so. A hard budget of 90 requests a run.

**Stated plainly.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Cadence | one poll every 30 minutes (48 runs a day) |
| Spacing | at least 6 seconds between requests, so requests per minute never exceed 10 |
| Requests per run | at most 90 (budget); the caps are 5 list pages, 30 tape pages, 5 legs look-ups, 40 books, plus 3 settled pages once a day (until Nov 13 it was 15 trade reads in place of the tape and look-ups); a trial run of the widened filter used 60, three of them refused and waited out |
| Requests per day | at most 4,320; about 2,900 at the trial's size, about 2 a minute on average |
| MB per day (gzipped) | about 0.9 MB with the leg tickers (an estimate: 0.7 measured in the trial plus about 0.2 for 100 rows of leg tickers, sized on synthetic leg strings because the real ones were not readable when this was written; re-measure from the first files); the trial figure was about 0.7 MB gzipped (about 15 KB a run, 13 KB of it the 400 capped quote rows, so the trial sits at the quote cap); under 1 MB at the caps; about 72 MB over the whole run (80 days). The old filter measured about 7 KB a run and 0.35 MB a day |
| Runner minutes | about 6 a run in the trial (6 min 3 s), 9 at the budget: about 310 to 430 runner minutes a day, 9,400 to 13,000 a month, free on a public repo |
| Ends | 2027-01-31 23:59 UTC by itself (workflow and script); sooner by deleting `.github/workflows/record-combo.yml` |

**Honest limits.** Most combos have an empty book (no bids on either side); in the widened trial 10 of the 40 tightest spreads had any depth, and 3,966 of the 5,000 listed combos had a posted price on some side (the 400 quote rows and 40 books are therefore a small slice of the selection). That is a finding about the market, not a fault. Prices are stored as the exchange returns them, nothing is computed. Gates: `test/comboBookRecorderGates.test.js` (CB1 to CB17).

## What it is expected to capture, and the stop rule (added Nov 12, 2026, the EA's note)

An earlier research pass (COMBO1) found no resting ask on any sampled combo, so this recorder may capture little. What it is expected to capture: which combos show any posted bid or ask over time, how often a two-sided quote exists, the spread when it does, the depth of the top 5 levels for the 40 tightest, and the trades that print. It exists to price a paper test of buying NO on high-priced combos with real depth.

**Stop rule, fixed now:** read the data on or after 14 days of recording (not before). If fewer than 20 distinct combos ever showed a two-sided quote with a bid on the side the paper test would buy, or fewer than 100 combo trades printed in total, stop the recorder (delete `record-combo.yml`) and report that combos cannot be priced. The read counts only quote and trade existence; it scores no strategy.
