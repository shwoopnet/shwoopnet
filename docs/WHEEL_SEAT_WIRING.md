# Wheel seat wiring (status: INERT, Nov 13, 2026)

Piece E of the rotation lane (`docs/ROTATION_LANE.md` sections 5, 5a, 6, 8, 13). This is the live wiring for wheel seats, built the way the second slot's phase A was: the rails exist and are tested, and no seat exists.

**What is in the code.** `functions/kalshiWheelLib.js`, a pure module (no network, database, clock or order code). It holds `WHEEL_SEATS`, a frozen EMPTY list, and `WHEEL_RULE_IDS`, a frozen empty list of the rule ids a seat may name; `WHEEL_FULL_SEATS_ENABLED` is false. It exports a validator for one seat, a whole-list check, the seat's caps, its net floor latch, a shard check and the live-against-shadow check of section 5.2. **Nothing imports it** (a gate fails if anything under `functions/`, `index.html` or `firestore.rules` names it), so no deployed function behaves differently: the off-equivalence fixtures are byte identical and `SLOT_STRATEGIES` gained nothing.

**What a seat is.** A registered second-slot strategy taken from the wheel. `seatToSlotStrategy` maps it to a slot entry with a fixed scale-in (one step, no scaling), and the slot's own validator then judges it, so every slot bound applies unchanged. On top of that the seat validator refuses:
- a rule id not on the fixed code-side list;
- more than 5 probation seats, a repeated id, or any one bad entry (the whole list is refused, so a typo cannot drop one guard and keep the rest);
- a probation seat that is not exactly 3 contracts, whose order could cost more than $3 at its top price, that can buy at 88c or above, or whose net floor is looser than minus $18;
- any full (10 contract) seat while `WHEEL_FULL_SEATS_ENABLED` is false; a full seat is at most 10 contracts and $10 an order, and is refused at 88c or above because the 150 signal, 10 loss record cannot be seen here;
- a loosened or missing exit or kill, an unknown field, a market the slot does not allow, a future `reviewedAt` (the slot's rules), and a missing owner popup date.

**Caps.** `wheelSeatCaps` is the slot's caps from the sizing base with the seat's own per order ceiling ($3 probation, $10 full) on top, the smaller always winning. A seat's orders are ordinary slot orders, so they count in the shared 12 percent stop, the 5.5 percent window cap and the daily and lifetime caps exactly like any slot strategy (reuse, no copy). **Kill.** `wheelSeatKilled` is the slot's own kill check (the net floor of minus $18 after 5 fills, the streak, and the latch at `kalshiSlotControl/killed`): sticky, and a latched seat stays off until a reviewed edit moves `reviewedAt`. **Shard.** `wheelShardCheck` refuses an order when its market's shard is short or unknown (skip code `shard_short`, as the slot logs). **Live against shadow.** `wheelLiveVsShadow` returns `wait`, `stay` or `leave seat` for the first 20 live orders (under 60 percent filled, or more than 1c worse than the shadow's price, leaves the seat). It is not wired to act.

**Not built, on purpose.** No engine change that can send an order for a seat; no mapping of seats into `SLOT_STRATEGIES`, the hold list, `SLOT_SERIES_ALLOWED` or `SLOT_TICKER_RE`; no replay controller for the section 6 performance exits (until it exists the net floor, the slot caps and the shared stop are the protection, section 5a.4a); nothing that reads or writes Firestore for the wheel.

**What an admission needs** (a separate reviewed pull request, one seat at a time): the owner's own popup with the exact spec (market, side, band, look, limit, size, exit, kill); the Chief of Staff's security review; the shard check for that market (the owner moves money himself); the caps checked against the real account; the rule's id added to `WHEEL_RULE_IDS`; the seat added to `WHEEL_SEATS` and mapped into the slot registry in the same edit, with any hold-to-settle exit added to the reviewed hold list; and the deploy order of the second slot (exit watch first, then the minute function). A full seat also needs the premise test read and a reviewed change of `WHEEL_FULL_SEATS_ENABLED`. Nothing stored, requested or typed on the page can add, enlarge or re-arm a seat.

Tests: `test/wheelSeatGates.test.js` (W1 to W10).
