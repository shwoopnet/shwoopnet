The server will place REAL orders by itself, around the clock until you pause it or a stop ends it (this also lifts a pause), one order at a time (up to $2.00 a contract) except gold and Dogecoin, whose size scales with the account in code (one contract per $43 of balance, up to 40, raised one step every 3 days at most; stop at 12% of the balance at the last review). Pauses the bot, then SELLS every open position in the account (the bot's and any you opened by hand) at whatever the book pays right now. Real money, takes the loss at the bid. Do it?
Rules and limits
Buys the side priced 88c to 97c about 6 minutes before a 15 minute market closes, then holds to settlement. Markets trading now: gold, Dogecoin. OICORE, the second strategy, trades Bitcoin only: it buys the cheaper side (5c to 50c) 3 to 2 minutes before the close when open interest jumped by 86,300 or more, 10 contracts, held to settlement. Paused in code: XRP, Bitcoin, Ethereum, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, natural gas. Gold and Dogecoin trade around the clock (the 8 PM to midnight pause was lifted Oct 30, 2026).
Size. Gold and Dogecoin grow with the account (one contract per $43 of balance, plus extra contracts from profit, at most a quarter of the base size). At most $2.00 a contract, and at most 5.5% of the balance in one 15 minute window.
Stop. If its trades over the last 24 hours are down 12% of the balance (otherwise $10), or any order goes wrong, it stops itself and stays off until you start it. Manual trades do not count.
Exit at 25c (fixed, always on). If a position's price falls to 25c, the bot sells it.
Reinvest pool rebuilds after every win (fixed, always on). Half of every win refills the pool, and the extra contracts it buys are capped at a quarter of the base size.
