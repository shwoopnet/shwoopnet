The server will place REAL orders by itself, around the clock until you pause it or a stop ends it (this also lifts a pause), one contract at a time (up to $2.00 each) except gold, whose size scales with the account in code (one contract per $43 of balance, up to 40, raised one step every 3 days at most; stop at 12% of the balance at the last review). Pauses the bot, then SELLS every open position in the account (the bot's and any you opened by hand) at whatever the book pays right now. Real money, takes the loss at the bid. Do it?
Rules and limits
Buys the side priced 88c to 97c about 6 minutes before a 15 minute market closes (XRP and natural gas are entered about 10 minutes out instead, as a test), then holds to settlement. Bitcoin and Ethereum buy only on a smooth climb. Markets, in order: gold, XRP, Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Ethereum, then three tests (AUD/USD, USD/CAD, natural gas). At most one crypto order per 15 minute window.
Size. Only gold grows with the account (one contract per $43 of balance, plus a contract from profit). XRP and Dogecoin are always 3 contracts, natural gas 2, everything else 1. At most $2.00 a contract, and at most 5.5% of the balance in one 15 minute window.
Stop. If its trades over the last 24 hours are down 12% of the balance (otherwise $10), or any order goes wrong, it stops itself and stays off until you start it. Manual trades do not count.
Exit at 25c (fixed, always on). If a position's price falls to 25c, the bot sells it.
Reinvest pool rebuilds after every win (fixed, always on). Half of every win refills the pool.
