Changelog
What's new in Pocket Advisor.
v2.4Shared Market Data & Polish
  • Index/watchlist quotes, market headlines, and the earnings calendar are now computed once by the backend and shared by every signed-in account, instead of each browser tab drawing on the same rate-limited data source independently
  • Fixed a scary false alarm: the Backend Status card no longer shows a red "no heartbeat" warning before you've even connected Alpaca — it now correctly waits until there's something to actually hear from
  • Alerts & trade history now shares the exact same filters and entry list as the Activity Log card — it used to quietly drop some legacy entries the card still showed
  • What to Watch (equities and crypto) shows an honest "no setups right now" message instead of a blank gap when the screener has nothing to report
v2.3Pocket Advisor: Invite-Gated Multi-User Accounts
  • Renamed from Shwoop to Pocket Advisor, with a new mark to match
  • Real multi-user accounts: invited accounts sign up with a one-time code, trade on their own fully isolated paper account, and can't see or touch the owner's data — the owner account can still go live
  • The backend now runs its screener, sizing, and safety checks for every connected account on its own schedule, not just one
  • Activity Log cleanup: filterable by type, hides noisy routine scans by default, and reads as a real table instead of a wall of text
  • Fixed several real bugs: crypto positions hidden behind a false "Market closed" P&L freeze, a Notepad drag that hit an invisible wall, and the header wrongly reporting "Sync failed" during crypto-only hours
  • Labs: "Test Alpaca as a data feed" and "Export raw historical data" are now their own sections instead of nested inside the equities backtest
v2.2Crypto Trading & Trade-Management Hardening
  • A second, fully independent strategy now trades crypto 24/7 — 12 pairs (BTC, ETH, SOL, LTC, LINK, AVAX, DOGE, UNI, XRP, DOT, BCH, AAVE), its own screener, its own auto-trade toggle, and its own risk budget that never competes with equities for room
  • Total risk ceiling raised from 5.5% to 7% — each strategy gets its own independent 7% budget, not a shared one, so one maxing out has zero effect on the other's ability to trade
  • Trailing stop refined for both strategies: once a trade has genuinely moved into profit, its stop can no longer trail back below breakeven — closes a real gap where an already-winning trade could still round-trip into a loss
  • Flatten All now actually pauses both auto-traders immediately, as its own description always promised — previously closed positions but left auto-trading running, letting a new one open right back up
  • Fixed a real duplicate-order risk: order submissions are now idempotent (a lost response no longer resubmits a second, real order), and a slow cycle can no longer overlap with itself
  • Overall P&L now reflects the account's true all-time total from Alpaca directly, not just a sum of closed journal trades — correctly includes unrealized P&L on whatever's still open, and survives switching accounts
  • Labs Demo now runs a live crypto position alongside the equities day, on its own clock that keeps going even when the equities day resets
v2.1Navigation Redesign & Safety Overhaul
  • Navigation rebuilt as a single, persistent sidebar — no more separate popups to open and close, and every destination (including Notepad and History) now lives in one place
  • Position sizing tightened: 1% risk per trade, a 20% cap on any single position, and a 5.5% ceiling on total risk across every open position at once — not user-adjustable, since changing it manually would skew the numbers the strategy was actually tested against
  • Auto-trading now retries a failed order 3 times before pausing itself and alerting you, rather than silently failing — pause state persists through a restart and only clears when you resume it manually
  • Live data status moved into the header as an always-visible, color-coded indicator instead of a buried Settings row
  • Removed several settings that were either redundant, always-on with no real reason to toggle off, or better placed elsewhere — Intraday screener and Market alerts are simply always on now; Auto-send moved to the Status page next to the rest of account health
  • Ongoing: extensive backtesting against a real, year-plus historical dataset (with realistic trading costs modeled, not just a frictionless best case) is actively comparing the current live strategy against newer candidates — see the honesty box on the Labs page for exactly where that stands
v2.0Intraday-First & Backend Server
  • Intraday is now the primary strategy — Trade Overview, the Market Brief, and backtesting are all built around short-term, real-time setups instead of once-a-day picks
  • Position sizing is now risk-based, computed fresh from your real account balance every time — scales automatically as the account grows instead of a fixed share count
  • Full automated safety layer: positions close automatically if their original thesis breaks, get refreshed or closed at market close, and stay in sync with what Alpaca actually filled
  • New backend server: the screener, sizing, and safety checks now run on a real 24/7 schedule, independent of a browser tab being open, with SMS alerts on crashes
  • New Status page: real account equity, today's P&L, open positions with live P&L, and a persistent log of everything the automated systems have done
  • Backtest results now show expectancy and profit factor, not just win rate — and a real entry-price bug that was inflating returns has been found and fixed
  • Guest mode and the onboarding tour removed — this is a single-owner tool now, not a public demo
v1.0 – v1.7Early History
  • Started as a simulated demo: Market Brief, Trade Overview, a live ticker tape, and fake pricing
  • Added a real trade journal (exit tracking, automatic P&L, calendar view), then real accounts with cross-device sync
  • Replaced simulated picks with a real technical-analysis screener; renamed Shwoopnet → Shwoop; added the Labs page and its backtest tool
  • Connected real Alpaca paper trading — one-click order placement, an account panel, and optional auto-send

Pocket Advisor watches, decides, and trades on your behalf so you don't have to stare at charts. It's not a licensed advisor. It runs two strategies: 16 stocks on 15-minute bars, checked every minute during market hours, and 12 crypto pairs checked every 5 minutes around the clock. Entry, stop, and target are all calculated live from real price data, not written by hand. Trades go to whichever Alpaca account you connect — invited accounts are paper-only; the admin account can also connect a live Alpaca account, with a persistent banner and red accents whenever live means real money. Invite-only, still under active testing — not a track record, not financial advice.

Prices are live quotes from Alpaca, falling back to Finnhub if needed. Chart history is real data from Twelve Data — if it can't load, you'll see an honest error, never fake data.
