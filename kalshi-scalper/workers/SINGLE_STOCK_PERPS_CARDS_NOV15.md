The unit of a scalp is one entry and its exit; fees are the market's published taker fee (to be read from the market object or Kalshi's fee article before any read; I do not state a number here).
## Card (a) FUNDING CARRY
- **Mechanism:** a perpetual pays or receives a funding rate each interval; hold the side that RECEIVES funding at entry, exit on a sign flip of the funding rate or after N hours, whichever first (N fixed now: 8 hours, the exit is also the end of the third funding interval, so N is not tuned).
- **Entry:** at the first 1 minute candle after a funding time where the last published funding rate is above its deadband in absolute value; side = the side the rate pays (long if the rate is negative, short if positive, the rule is the sign, nothing else). Size 1 contract notional; price = ask (long) or bid (short) of that candle plus the 1c or 2c haircut.
- **Result:** price move to exit plus funding received minus fees, per interval held.
- **Uniqueness (7e and nearest cards):** nearest = FUNDP1 (V2: funding premium on the hourly list, 3 to 7 samples a day) and FUND1 (the old funding read); a funding-carry rule is the same mechanism as FUNDP1. It differs in entry data (the funding history file and 1 minute candles, stocks) and the exit (sign flip), but by 7g that is a version, not new. **I rate it NOT NEW unless the VA rules otherwise: send to the VA before any read.** Not on the 7e declined list (the declined EXDIV1 is a dividend capture, a different mechanism). Expected n: a few hundred funding intervals over 10 days across 67 names, each name tens: most names will read "no evidence".
## Card (b) OVERNIGHT AND OPENING GAP
- **Mechanism:** a stock perp trades through the night while the cash market is closed; at the cash open the perp's price and Kalshi's own `reference_price` (the underlying) converge. Fade or follow the gap between the perp's mark just before the cash open (09:25 ET) and `reference_price` at 09:31 ET.
- **Entry rule:** at 09:31 ET (the first `perps30` open sample at or after it), if (reference_price - perp mid) / reference_price exceeds 2 x the perp's half spread in absolute value, buy the perp (reference above) or sell it (reference below); exit at 09:45 ET or when the gap has closed to half, whichever first. 1 contract notional.
- **Stock price source (the Head's flag):** NONE is permitted from any vendor. The only source is Kalshi's own `reference_price` in the perps list and `perps30` files; **I have not verified that `reference_price` carries the cash price at the open for stocks** (it may be the stale prior close until the cash market opens). That check is step 0 of any read: count the open samples where `reference_price` changed between 09:25 and 09:35 ET; under 50% changed = the card is NOT_RUNNABLE on Kalshi's data alone.
- **Uniqueness:** nearest = CASHGAP2, CASHOPEN1 and CASHOPEN2 (V2: the cash open on 2 perps, gap and open; read dates 2026-12-01) and OVN1 and OVN2 (overnight premium); the mechanism (gap at the open) is the same as CASHGAP2 and the card is an extension to 67 names. **By 7g, a version on more markets: NOT NEW unless the VA rules otherwise.** Expected n: about 20 weekday opens x up to 67 names; V2 reports that two perps cannot give 100 trades before mid-2027 at a 15% trigger rate: the card inherits that (n per name stays under 100).
## Card (c) 1 MINUTE SCALP: SHORT-TERM TREND AND MEAN REVERSION (the owner's scalp shape: quick entry, target or time exit)
- **Mechanism:** after an abnormal 5 minute move in a stock perp's mid, either the move continues (trend) or reverts; two registered arms, one try each name.
- **Signal:** the perp's 1 minute mid return over the last 5 candles is at least 3 standard deviations of that name's own 5 minute returns over the previous 60 trading minutes (a z, not a fixed percent), and the spread at the signal is under 0.3% of price. **Arm T (trend):** enter in the direction of the move at the next candle's ask or bid; **arm R (reversion):** the opposite side.
- **Exit (fixed, the same for both arms):** target = 1 x the 5 minute standard deviation in the trade's favour (taker exit at the candle's bid or ask), stop = 1 x the standard deviation against, time exit 10 minutes, whichever first; one trade per name per signal, no re-entry for 15 minutes. Fees on both legs plus the 1c or 2c haircut on both prices.
- **Regular-hours only:** signals 09:35 to 15:45 ET weekdays (the cash market's open window), excluding the first and last 5 minutes.
- **Uniqueness:** nearest = PERPBTC1M (V2: Bitcoin perps, 1 minute candles) and the intraday jump family (INPLAYJUMP, SWING1 in sports): all falsified or different markets; 7e declined "taker order-flow signals" (a different signal, tape flow). A volatility-scaled move in a stock perp with a fixed target and time exit is NOT on the declined list and no registered card does it on stocks: **I rate it NEW as a first read** (the VA decides). The trend-or-reversion pair is a try each (2 per name). Expected n: 20 trading days x 6.5 hours x 67 names gives thousands of 5 minute windows: this is the one card that can reach 100 trades per name.
## Card (d) PERP AGAINST A MATCHING KALSHI STOCK BINARY
- **Mechanism:** if a Kalshi stock binary or range (for example a market on a stock's close above a strike) is priced against the same stock's perp, trade the perp when the binary's implied probability disagrees with the perp-implied one.
- **Does such a market exist?** The repo shows Kalshi's index ladders (KXINXU, KXNASDAQ100U, the 15 minute KXINX15M and KXNDQ15M) and EVM4 (V2: KXINX, KXNASDAQ100 with KXUS500PERP and KXUSTECH100PERP; read once, the last third not read). **I have found no single-stock binary in the repo or the census lists.** Step 0 (Forensics' census): list Kalshi's open stock series (earnings, price level or close markets) by ticker and match them to the perp list by name; if none, the card is NOT_RUNNABLE and stops, with no data pulled.
- **Uniqueness:** nearest = EVM1 and EVM4 (binary against perp, on Bitcoin and the indices; EVM4 is closed at the cut Nov 5) and CFRTI and CFRTIK (the 15 minute binary against perp candles): the mechanism is the same relative-value read on other names: **a version unless a stock binary exists with a different information source; NOT NEW**.
## Card (e) EARNINGS-DAY MOVES
- **Mechanism:** a stock perp reprices at an earnings release (the perp trades through the after-hours release, unlike the cash market): follow or fade the first 5 minutes' move after the release.
- **Dates:** earnings dates are NOT in any permitted source I hold (company calendars come from vendors whose terms bar automated reads); the only date source is Kalshi's own earnings markets, where Kalshi lists them (a Kalshi series on earnings mentions or results). Step 0: Forensics lists Kalshi earnings series and their event times; no event list, no card.
- **Rule, if a list exists:** at the release time t0, the perp's mid change from t0 - 1 minute to t0 + 5 minutes; arm F follows that move (enter at t0 + 5, exit at t0 + 30 minutes), arm X fades it; fixed 25 minutes, no tuning.
- **Uniqueness:** nearest = EARNPERP1, EARNPERP2 and EARNPERP3 (V2: earnings and perps; EARNPERP1 FALSIFIED on PF under 1.5 in either half or NOT_ENOUGH_DATA under 30 trades or 20 dates, with the season giving about 25 more events in April to May): **the same mechanism, NOT NEW**. Expected n: a handful of events in 10 days; "no evidence".
