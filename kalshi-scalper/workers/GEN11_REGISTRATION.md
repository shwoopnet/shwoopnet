## Grammar (GEN10's, unchanged; every strategy is exactly one of these)

- **Signal bars:** the 10 s bar ending L = 840, 830, ..., 420 s before the close (43). A strategy has a range [L_hi, L_lo] with both ends from {840, 780, ..., 420} (36 ranges); bars are tried from L_hi down to L_lo every 10 s, YES before NO, and the FIRST qualifying row enters. **At most one entry per window.**
- **Side:** YES, NO or BOTH. The side's price by the last trade at or before the signal bar end (YES = the trade's YES price, NO = 100 minus it) must be in **[15c, 85c]**, and that trade at most 60 s old.
- **Conditions: 1 to 3,** `feature <= q` or `> q`, q a decile (10th to 90th) of that feature over all of Bitcoin's DISCOVERY rows (every bar, both sides; feature values only, no outcome), no feature twice, from **20 features**, all computed only from bars ending at or before the signal bar: GEN9's 16, signed toward the side (s = +1 YES, -1 NO): `f10, f30, f60` (s x (YES-taker minus NO-taker contracts) over the last 10, 30, 60 s), `i10, i30, i60` (the same over total contracts), `c10, c30, c60` (trade counts), `burst` (c10 / max(1, c60 / 6)), `dp10, dp30, dp60` (s x change of the last trade price over 10, 30, 60 s, cents; dp60 missing at 840 s), `big30` (largest print in the last 30 s), `bigdir30` (s x its taker side), `px` (the side's price); plus GEN10's 4: `fw` (s x (YES-taker minus NO-taker contracts) from the window's first bar through the signal bar), `iw` (fw over all contracts since the open), `dpw` (s x (the side-signed last price at the signal bar minus the first traded YES price of the window), cents), `tbig` (seconds from the end of the latest bar at or before the signal bar holding a BIG print, at least 50 contracts and at least 5 times the window's mean print size so far, to the signal bar end; missing if none yet). A missing input fails its condition. **No open interest, no candle volume, no feature from after the signal bar.**
- **Signed condition required:** at least one condition on a side-signed feature (`f*`, `i*`, `dp*`, `bigdir30`, `fw`, `iw`, `dpw`), so the side is chosen by the tape and never by the price band alone. A draw without one is rejected and counted in N.
- **Fill (APPROXIMATE, stated plainly):** at the NEXT 10 s bar (ending L - 10 s; a trade at most 60 s old is required there), the side's last trade price **+ half the median spread + 1c**, the spread being the median of the closing spreads of the 1 minute candles ending at E-120, E-60 and E, E the end of the minute holding the fill bar (no candle: no entry; a fill under 1c or over 99c: no entry). **This fill is derived from trade prices, not from a book: no depth, no queue, no latency beyond the 10 s bar.** GEN9 measured Bitcoin's half spread at about 0.50c, so the modelled entry is about 1.5c above the last trade.
- **Hold to settlement.** Net per contract in cents: (100 - fill - fee) if the side settles yes, else (-fill - fee). No exit, no stop, no sale, no settlement fee.
- **Fees:** 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent per order, ONE order, **gates at C = 10**; C = 1 and fees x1.2 reported.

## Generation, dedupe and counting

Seeded draws (`random.Random(11002)`) until **5,000 distinct** or 300,000 draws. Distinct: at least one signed condition, at least 30 discovery trades, and no kept strategy whose discovery entry set (window and side pairs) has Jaccard above 0.5 with it. **N added = every draw (rejected or not) + 3 information looks** (the unconditioned 840 to 420 s entry, YES, NO and BOTH, held) **+ one unconditioned same-side-and-range look per discovery survivor** (read in validation). Running count before GEN11 about **700,173** (the lead's correction after NEWH13; z bar 5.26); the Bonferroni z bar for the new total is reported.

## Screens (fixed now, GEN10's)

- **Minimum data:** fewer than 300 discovery windows or 15 discovery days is `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` (its N is 0). Expected to be met (558 on 23).
- **DISCOVERY:** PF at C = 10 at least **1.5**, at least **100 trades on at least 15 days**.
- **Freeze:** the survivors, the best 10 at size by discovery PF and the control list are written; `workers/GEN11_frozen.json` (survivors and best 10) with its sha256, and the sha256 of the control and distinct files, in `workers/GEN11_FROZEN.md`, committed and pushed before any validation row is built.
- **VALIDATION (read once):** PF at C = 10 at least **1.3** on at least **50 trades**, PF at fees x1.2 above 1.0.
- **Side-shuffle control, per survivor:** in validation the survivor's entries keep their window and signal bar and each takes the OPPOSITE side's row with probability one half (200 shuffles, seed 1010; a trade whose opposite row does not exist is kept). The survivor must have validation PF **above the 95th percentile** of the 200 shuffled PFs; otherwise its edge is the band and the time, not the side the tape chose, and it is `FALSIFIED` whatever its PF.
- **Family control (kill rule):** discovery FAILURES at the screen's size go through the same validation screen. If the survivors' validation pass rate is not at least 5 points above the control's, the family is `FALSIFIED` as a whole and individual passes are labelled "family control not met".
- **RULES bar (information):** PF 1.5 in each of three windows after fees, positive at x1.2, a named counterparty, deflated z at the bar. **Cannot be met here (two windows).** A pass of both screens and both controls earns only an R6 forward registration.
- **Survivors of validation** are not put in the candles-based forward ledger (their features need the tape). A candidate JSON in `workers/candidates/` is written only if an evaluator can honestly run on R6 data (`source: "r6"`: the same 10 s bars rebuilt from R6's `trades`, the entry at R6's recorded YES ask or NO ask at the first poll after the signal bar, walked through its levels for 10 contracts, held); otherwise the result file describes what it would need.

## Counterparty (named before drawing)

A taker who follows Bitcoin's tape mid-window (buys the side the flow is buying, or the side the price is moving toward) is filled by **the resting quoters** (market makers posting both sides from a spot feed); for the follower to be paid, the quoters must be slow to move their quotes on their own fills, so the side still sits below its true chance for a few seconds after informed flow arrives. A taker who fades the tape (buys the side the flow is selling) is paid by **impatient takers who crossed the spread on the other side** (MECH1: small prints lean to the cheap side, lottery buying), if they pushed the price past its fair chance. Honest alternative, which I expect: at 7 to 14 minutes left Bitcoin's quote is a calibrated function of spot and time, the quoters reprice from Coinbase and Binance within seconds and faster than on gold (Bitcoin's spread is the tighter of the two), the tape mid-window is mostly their own repricing being hit, and a position held to settlement at 15c to 85c pays about 1.5c of spread and padding plus about 1.5c of fee for a coin whose chance is its price. MECH1 already found on Bitcoin's own tape that no taker type carries even the late open interest effect.
