## Card 1: TEMPLAG1 (observed-temperature lag, hourly or daily city temperature brackets)
- **Mechanism (named):** a bracket on a daily or hourly high is decided by the observed temperature the exchange itself relays. When the relayed running maximum has already crossed a bracket's edge, that bracket's outcome is mechanically fixed (a high cannot fall), yet resting prices can lag the relay by seconds to minutes. Counterparty: slow quoters and retail holding the now-impossible side. The edge is the market's own lag to the exchange's own number, not a forecast.
- **Uniqueness:** closest declined rows are the weather-forecast-against-market row (WX1, WX3, WXENS1, WXLOW1, FAMWX, RAINCAL) and the lock/parity row. Different because no forecast and no model is used: only Kalshi's relayed observed value against a bracket edge it has already crossed; not a lock (the bracket still trades below 1.00 and may settle by rounding or by the official source, which is exactly the risk). HOURLYTEMP was a cost measurement with no open card, so no overlap. Mechanism family: information-lag on exchange data (new; no live or shadow strategy uses it). Correlation with live strategies: expected near zero (different markets, different clock); to be shown before any shadow slot.
- **Entry / exit / management:** signal = the relayed running max (or the latest reading, for an hourly) is at or past the bracket edge by a margin m, and the YES ask for that bracket is at or below 1 minus a cost margin; buy 1 contract at the ask seen (IOC), hold to settlement. Variants for the toolkit (each a separate registered try if used): a resting limit one tick under the ask, a time exit if the relayed value reverses, a profit target at the bid reaching 0.97.
- **Numeric prediction (written before any data):** among signals with m of at least 1 degree and ask at most 0.93, at least 97% settle YES; cash profit factor at least 1.5 after the fee in each of three windows. Tries: 4 (m in {1, 2}, ask cap in {0.93, 0.96}); N counted into deflation with the rest of the register.
- **Kill criteria:** gate 0 fails (no relayed observed field, or update interval over 5 minutes); or fewer than 100 signals by the read date (NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, source closed after two such); or any signal settles against the crossed edge more than 3% of the time (the rounding or source risk is the whole question); or cash PF under 1.0 in any window.
- **Data:** a recorder-backed read joins Kalshi's relayed value with the bracket quote at poll time (a Kalshi-only recorder, public endpoints). I do not know the field name; Desk B or the Testing Desk names it at gate 0.
- **Held-out:** constant HELDOUT_FIRST = 2026-12-05T00:00:00Z (closes on or after it); development is the earlier recorded days, 14 days minimum, printed with "no evidence at n = x" below 100. ## Card 2: SCORELAG1 (relayed-score lag in live games, stale quote buying)
- **Mechanism (named):** when the exchange's own relayed live score changes, a moneyline or total market's resting quote is stale for a short time; buying the side the score change favours at the stale quote is paid for by the quoter who had not updated. The edge is quote staleness after an event, not a price move followed after the fact.
- **Uniqueness:** closest rows are INPLAYJUMP (my own: it FOLLOWS a price jump of 0.10 with entry one step later) and the taker order-flow row (declined; TAPEFLOW1, RESIL1). Different: SCORELAG1's trigger is the relayed score field, not any price move, and its fill is IMMEDIATE (before the price has moved); INPLAYJUMP's trigger is price. They can fire on the same event, so the correlation with INPLAYJUMP must be shown below 0.3 before any slot, or it is a declined duplicate under 7d check 3 (record the number). Not a lock or parity scan. Not favourite-and-hold only if the entry is limited to events that moved the fair price by at least 0.10 and not to buying 88c to 97c favourites (the card forbids that band). Mechanism family: information-lag on exchange data, shared with Card 1 but on a different market class (sports).
- **Entry / exit / management:** on a relayed score change that flips or extends a lead, buy 1 contract of the favoured side at the ask seen if the ask is within 0.03 of the last quote before the event and the spread is at most 0.04; exit by a profit target (bid at entry plus 0.05), a stop (bid at entry minus 0.08) and a time exit at 60 seconds, or hold to settlement as a separate registered try.
- **Numeric prediction:** the mean mid move in the 30 seconds after a qualifying score change is at least 3 cents toward the scoring side, and a taker fill at the stale ask nets at least +1.5c a contract after the 7% fee on the 60 second exit, pooled and in each of two halves. Tries: 3 (exit rule x2, spread cap x1.5).
- **Kill criteria:** gate 0 fails (no relayed score field, or relay later than the quote by more than 5 seconds on average, which would mean the quote is not stale); fewer than 100 qualifying events by the read; net under 0c after the fee; or correlation with INPLAYJUMP at or above 0.3 (recorded as a declined duplicate).

- **Held-out:** constant HELDOUT_FIRST = 2026-12-05T00:00:00Z (events on or after it), read once; earlier days are development. Registry line to add: SCORELAG1 | Desk A | NBA, NHL, NFL game markets (Kalshi-relayed score) | held-out from 2026-12-05T00:00:00Z, read once | OPEN once registered.
