## 4. Counterparty and prior
Retail traders pricing a foreign league by team name and reputation, not by that day's starter. **Honest alternative (expected):** Kalshi's market makers copy sportsbook lines that already know the starter, so the model's "starter edge" is the Elo's noise; the cheap tail (ask 3c to 30c) carries the pooled -5.5c to -6.2c longshot loss, and a rating from one season of results has no information the quote lacks. A lift of 6c per contract over that baseline is needed to matter.

## 5. Reference model (fixed now; fitted on game results only, never on a price)
League games come from the league sites only (NPB: calendar and box pages; KBO: the JSON above), all regular-season and postseason games of 2026 before the cut, ties and cancelled games dropped (counted). A Kalshi event is matched to a league game by date and team pair (names via a fixed dictionary of the 12 NPB and 10 KBO clubs); unmatched events are dropped and counted.
- Team Elo per league, start 1500 each at the 2026 opening (no prior-season carry), scale 400, K from {4, 8, 12, 16, 24}, updated after each game in date order. Home advantage `h` in Elo points.
- Starter quality for a start = sum over his earlier 2026 starts in that league of (team won - the team's Elo win probability before that game), divided by (number of starts + 8) (shrinks to 0 for a new starter; "starter" is the first pitcher of the team in that game). `sq_home - sq_away` enters as `b x 400 x diff`.
- P(home) = logistic of (R_home - R_away + h + b x 400 x (sq_home - sq_away)) / 400 on the base-10 scale. **K, h, b are fitted once by log-loss on game results of the first two thirds (by date) of the pre-cut games, the two leagues pooled for b and h and separately for K, then frozen.** The last third of the pre-cut games is the out-of-sample information read.
- **Limitation, stated now:** the announced starter is proxied by the game's actual starter (NPB announces starters a day ahead, KBO likewise; scratches are rare). A game whose actual starter differs from the announced one carries a small lookahead; it cannot be measured here and is not repaired.
- Qualified event: both clubs have at least 20 prior games in the season at the look.

## 6. Universe, look, trade (fixed now)
Qualified matched events before the cut. Candles: ONE 1 minute candlestick call per event on the first market (the other side's quote is 1 - yes bid), window start minus 6 hours to start (start from the event ticker), at least 1.3 s between requests, backoff on 429, md5-of-event-ticker order, time box 2 hours (prefix scored, stated). Fetch only the qualified events (the candidates plus, from the same candle, the all-cheap-side control). **Look:** the last 1 minute candle ending at or before start minus 120 seconds with a valid two-sided quote. **Cheap side:** the side with ask 3c to 30c (YES ask, or NO ask = 1 - yes bid, rounded to 0.0001); if both qualify (cannot, both would be at most 30c with a combined 100c) the lower. **Treated:** model probability of that side at least 3x ask. C = 10 contracts, fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up, held to settlement, fees x1.2 stress. Control: every cheap-side candidate. Halves D1 and D2 split at the midpoint of the pre-cut start dates; cluster = game date; z bar about 5.3 (information only).

## 7. Numeric prediction (written before any result)
Candidate count about 100 to 180 cheap-side events; treated 20 to 60; treated win rate at or below mean ask (about 12% to 20% vs asks near 15c to 25c; the model flags the Elo's noise), mean net about -3c to -6c, PF 0.4 to 0.9, treated minus control about 0 to +2c, not significant. KBO and NPB individually under 40 treated. Out-of-sample model log-loss no better than 0.69 plus or minus 0.01 (baseball is near coin-flip; the starter term adds under 0.003).

## 8. Kill rules
NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if under 150 treated trades in what was fetched (then the forward plan below). FALSIFIED if PF is under 1.5 after fee or the treated mean net is not above 0 in BOTH halves (applied only when treated is 150 or more; below that the point estimates are printed as information and carry no verdict). NOT_YET_FALSIFIED only if mean > 0 in both halves, PF >= 1.5, mean >= 2c and treated beats control; a pass would only mean a forward read on the post-cut seasons, never a trade. NOT_RUNNABLE for a league if its starters or results cannot be parsed with a plain public request (a block is never worked around). If either league is dropped, it is dropped for both arms alike and said.

## 9. Forward plan
Treated accrues at the observed share of events. 150 treated at about 3% to 6% of about 1,485 events a season is 2 to 4 seasons, so a forward probe only makes sense if the observed treated share is at least 8%; otherwise it is not worth building. The post-cut events (NPB postseason, then the 2027 seasons opening late March 2027) are the held-out set and are never touched until a forward read is registered; earliest readable with this accrual is the end of the 2027 regular season (about October 2027) at the observed share.
