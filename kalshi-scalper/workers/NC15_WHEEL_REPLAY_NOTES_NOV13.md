## What the sheet fixes (the code is exactly this)
D = close - 600 s exact (exchange time); signal poll = last poll with exchange time in [D-10, D]; offset = median over the 120 most recent same-run rows (any ticker, by receive time, at or before the poll, readable Date header only) of header + 0.5 - t_recv/1000; imb5 over recorded levels, in the cell at |imb5| >= 0.50 (1e-9), positive buys YES; implied ask in 0.10 to 0.90 inclusive; 10 contracts; limit = ask + 0.01; ladder = the levels the order meets sorted by ask; offers 10 = sizes at or under limit + 1e-9 sum to 10; walked price = size-weighted average of the first 10; second poll = the next poll of the ticker, at most 6 s after the signal poll, else not fill-checked; fillable = both offer 10; fill = higher walked price; fee_up_order = ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1-p) x 100 - 1e-9)/100 (0.50 -> 0.18, 0.15 -> 0.09, 0.90 -> 0.07); cash = 10 x (win - p) - fee; result = coverage `result`; missing = pending (listed for the kalshi-pull job), 7 days after close without yes or no = void (fee only); counts only decision instants at or after the sheet's commit time.
## Choices the sheet leaves open (for Auditor B and the second build to confirm or rule)
1. A ticker recorded in two runs: the signal poll is the latest by exchange time among all runs' candidates in the window (ties by run path); the second poll is the next poll of that ticker IN THE SAME RUN (never a duplicate from another run).
2. Close time: the `close_time` column of the ticker's first readable row (exchange time); no close time = no signal (counted).
3. Offset window: the offset of a poll is the median over the 120 most recent rows with a readable Date header, INCLUDING the poll itself when it has one, at or before the poll, per run folder, by receive time. A row whose own header is unreadable still IS a poll and takes the median of the earlier readable rows (the sheet's wording, and what nc15_wheel_counts.py does; Auditor B's open item A: the note now says what the code does).
4. A ladder level counts only with a readable price and a size above 0. A missing side (no top level price or no bid) is "missing side of the book", no signal.
5. The signal poll's imb5 uses all recorded levels present; the top level prices for the band test are yes_bid_px_1 and no_bid_px_1.
6. Coverage result: the first yes or no found across the runs' coverage rows for the ticker (a later run with a result overrides an empty one). A final value that is neither yes nor no (for example void) is booked void AT ONCE (the sheet, section 0; Auditor B's open item C; v1 waited 7 days); an empty result is missing (pending, then void 7 days after the close).
7. The 7 day void rule is applied at the replay's 'now' argument (the block read time); the replay itself reads no clock for a decision.
8. The admitted series are the seven of the sheet (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and AUD/USD withdrawn); silver and USD/CAD can be withdrawn on 2026-11-19 by the re-count (a parameter, not code).
9. Not built here (not in section 2): the block bar (60 fillable signals, PF 1.3, halves 1.0, etc.), which is the lane's; this scorer outputs per-place records and per-series counts, cash and PF, and the list of pending tickers.
