## The one mechanism that is not in those cards, and why it is not a card yet
A market that stays OPEN after the official number is public (the close time is after the release time) would leave stale quotes with a named loser, the quoter who has not updated. That is a different mechanism from a bracket model against a persistence reference, and it needs only times: each series' close time against its release time. I have not read any Kalshi rulebook and no close times for these series. If the Head wants it, a times-only check of close_time against the public release schedule, from a lists run that already landed, would say whether any of these series closes AFTER its release; I will not start it without the order.
