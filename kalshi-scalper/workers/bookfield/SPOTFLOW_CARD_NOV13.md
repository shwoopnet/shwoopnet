## CARD: SPOTFLOW1 (spot trade size and flow on the underlying's venues, price held flat)
`SPOTFLOW1 | family: spot order flow of the UNDERLYING (not Kalshi's tape); entry mid price (30c to 65c), hold to settlement | risk class: 1 contract | stage Idea | prior about 3%`
- **Field and an honest limit:** the SPOT rows carry price, size and time but NO taker side. Signed flow therefore has to be INFERRED by the tick rule (a trade at a higher price than the previous trade on the same venue is a buy, lower is a sell, equal carries the previous sign, the first trade of a venue is skipped). That is noisier than a real taker flag, and it is the first thing that can sink this card.
- **Mechanism and counterparty:** Kalshi's market makers quote off the spot PRICE; they do not look at the volume behind it. When pooled spot flow is strongly one-sided while the spot price has NOT yet moved, the pressure is absorbed and tends to resolve in the flow's direction over the next minutes. The party who loses is the Kalshi maker who left a mid price quote while one-sided spot flow stood behind it.
- **Why this is not the declined flow family, and why it is not the spot-lead price test:** the declined FLOW1 (and S-FLOW1) read the taker flow on KALSHI's own tape for the same market; this reads the trade tape of three different exchanges, a different source, a different participant population and a different counterparty (the maker watching spot, not a Kalshi taker). It is NOT the spot-lead price tests (W-SPOTLEAD1, CFRTI): those buy when the spot PRICE has moved ahead of the Kalshi quote; this fires only when the spot price is FLAT (return under 0.05%) and the flow is not. The Head's rule says flow is declined, so the Verification Analyst must decide whether "flow of a different venue" is a different family; I think it is a borderline case and say so.
- **Rule (one threshold set, fixed, no sweep):** Bitcoin and Ethereum 15 minute markets (the two series with a long enough run). From 600 s to 360 s before the close, over the three venues together: at least 30 trades; spot return (last pooled price over first, same venue basis) under 0.05% in size; flow share = (tick rule buy size minus sell size) / total size, and |flow share| at least 0.30. If so, at the R6 book poll ending exactly 360 s before the close, buy YES when the flow share is positive and NO when it is negative, at that side's ask if the ask is 30c to 65c and the spread is 3c or less. One look, nothing carried, 1 contract, held to settlement. Fee 7% x p x (1 - p) per contract on a 10 contract order rounded up to the cent, slip 1c, cash basis. Scored on the R6 books, NOT the list-copy quotes the stale-quote audit voided.
- **Numeric prediction:** the entry wins at least 4 points more often than its price on average (a 50c entry wins 54% or more); cash PF 1.3 or better in each half.
