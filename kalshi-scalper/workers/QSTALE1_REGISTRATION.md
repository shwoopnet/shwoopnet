## Counterparty and mechanism (and the contrary hypothesis)
**Counterparty:** the quote providers on the cheaper side's book who stopped updating while flow arrived. When the YES mid sits still for three minutes although contracts traded, and the spread is wider than usual, the makers have stepped back and the takers who are trading are informed or at least moving; the cheaper side's ask is then the price of a maker who has not yet repriced the underdog. The buyer of the cheap side wins when the late flow was right. **If this is true** the data shows: win rate above the average fill by 3c or more (e.g. 31% on a 28c average fill), the stale-and-wide subset beating the stale-only subset (Try B) and the wide-but-not-stale subset, and a payoff ratio near (1 - fill) / fill. **Contrary hypothesis:** a stale quote with a wide spread is a quiet, thin minute that carries NO information; the cheaper side is then priced correctly and costs a wider-than-usual spread plus the fee, so the win rate equals the fill minus about the half spread and the mean per contract is negative by the spread plus the fee (about -2.5c to -4c). The data separates them: win rate minus average fill (information), and the same trade on stale windows without the spread condition. Honest limits: (1) 1 minute candle closes only, so "stale" is a minute-close property, not a book age; (2) a tertile spread rule on a 1c-tick market is a coarse rule (Bitcoin's spread is almost always 1c, so it fires on Bitcoin rarely, 9 windows); (3) even a pass would name no counterparty beyond this hypothesis, so it could only be a capped probe, never L1's lane.

## Universe (fixed now, liquidity and cost on metadata only)
- Series: **KXBTC15M** (OICORE's market; the same cheaper side, so no hedge), **KXHYPE15M, KXNEAR15M, KXZEC15M, KXBNB15M, KXXRP15M** (the L1-free 15 minute crypto series; median quoted spread 0.007 to 0.010 at the 180 s candle per `OICOREX1_INVENTORY.md`, so the cost rule passes for all). Pooled as one group.
- NOT traded (hard non-overlap): **Ethereum, Dogecoin, gold** (L1 buys the favourite there; a cheap-side trade in the same window could hedge L1). XRP is paused in live L1; if L1 resumed it, a cheap-side XRP trade would be the opposite side of an L1 favourite: reported separately in the per-series table so it can be dropped without a rerun.
- A window is ELIGIBLE (before any trigger or outcome) when: the result is settled; two sided quotes (0 < bid < ask < 1) exist at the candles ending 360, 300, 240 and 180 s before the close; the series has at least 48 earlier markets closing in the previous 24 hours (for the staleness reference) and at least 300 two sided candles in its previous 24 hours (for the spread reference); a side with ask 0.05 to 0.50 exists at the look; and the fill candle (ending 120 s before the close) has a two sided quote. A missing candle is "not eligible", never "stale".

## The trigger (Try A, the ONE primary; everything relative to the series' own trailing 24 hours)
In the signal window (the three one minute candles ending 300, 240 and 180 s before the close, baseline the candle ending 360 s):
1. **Stale:** path = sum over the 3 minutes of |change of the YES mid (bid+ask)/2|. Stale when path is **at or below the 33.3rd percentile** (`np.percentile`, linear) of the path of the series' **earlier markets closing in the previous 24 hours** (the same statistic at the same clock position; every one of those windows ended before this look).
2. **Trades printed:** at least one of the 3 minutes has candle volume above 0.
3. **Wide:** the spread (ask minus bid of the candle ending 180 s before the close) is **strictly above the 66.67th percentile** (`np.percentile`, linear) of the spreads of **all this series' two sided candles with end time in the 24 hours before the look candle (look candle excluded)**.
Fire when 1, 2 and 3 all hold. No threshold was tuned: the tertiles are the brief's "top tertile" and its mirror, fixed here. **Try B (the one alternative, separately counted):** conditions 1 and 2 only (staleness with trades, no spread condition).

## The trade
At the candle ending **180 s before the close** (the look): the cheaper side = YES if its ask is 0.05 to 0.50, else NO if its ask (1 - YES bid) is 0.05 to 0.50, else no trade (same YES-first order as OIREL and XOI1). **Entry = that side's ask on the next recorded candle (ending 120 s before the close)**, the OICORE/XOI1 convention; no earlier price is used, so no look-ahead (the fill candle decides only whether a fill exists). C = 10 contracts, taker at the ask, held to settlement (no sell fee). **Fee: 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order** (`gen6.fee`). One order per series per window; up to six orders in one window (correlated; handled by day clustering and reported).

## Windows, thirds, holdout
Eligible dates (a date with at least one eligible window, regardless of trigger or outcome) are sorted and split into **three equal thirds by number of dates**: 90 dates, **W1 2026-08-06 to 2026-09-04, W2 2026-09-05 to 2026-10-04, W3 2026-10-05 to 2026-11-03** (printed by `counts`). **W3 is the frozen holdout, untouched until the rule is frozen (this file), and is scored only if W1 and W2 both pass.** No second look; no variant is run.

## KILL RULES (fixed now, no tuning)
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA**: fewer than 150 trades pooled in the windows scored.
- **FALSIFIED**: PF (C = 10, after fees) under 1.5 in W1 or W2, or (if read) in W3; or PF at fees x1.2 at or under 1.0 in any scored window.
- **NOT_YET_FALSIFIED**: PF at least 1.5 in each of W1, W2 and W3 and x1.2 PF above 1.0. History alone can earn no more. **A pooled day-clustered z below the deflated bar 5.27 (N about 728,726) is reported as NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, never a pass.** No named counterparty beyond the hypothesis above, so no promotion path exists in any case.
- Headline is Try A. Try B has no verdict of its own beyond these same rules and is shown for comparison. Per-series tables, the wide-but-not-stale contrast and the agreement-with-direction splits are information, not tries.
