## Mechanism and counterparty
Far strikes of a minutes- and role-driven count (points, rebounds, assists, threes; yards, receptions) are tails; retail buys the YES of a famous player at a round strike. The market maker prices from a similar game log, so the honest prior is that the ask equals the model within the spread, and where the model says 3x the ask the model is more likely missing information (injury report, minutes limit, matchup) than the market is wrong. This is a test of that prior.

## Rule (ONE, no tuning after TEST is opened)
**Model.** For a player-game, prior games = that player's earlier box score lines (a game counts if he played: NBA/WNBA minutes above 0; NFL appears in any statistical category), at least 3 required, else the market is skipped. m10 = mean of the last up to 10 prior games, n10 their number, m_lg = the league-stat mean over FIT player-games. **mu = (n10 x m10 + k x m_lg) / (n10 + k).** Count X ~ Negative Binomial with mean mu and variance mu + mu^2 / r (a Poisson as r grows). P(YES) = P(X > floor_strike). **k in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16} and r in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64} are chosen once per (league, stat) by the highest log likelihood of the FIT player-games predicted from their own priors**; nothing else is tuned.
**Look.** The last 1 minute candle ending at or before ESPN game start minus 300 s with a valid quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1, ask minus bid at most 0.10). Asks: YES ask = ask; NO ask = 1 minus bid. The CHEAP side is the side whose ask is in [0.03, 0.30]; the other side is never evaluated or printed.
**Sample of markets per game.** Candle fetching is bounded: per game, markets with a model probability are ordered by sha1(ticker) and the first 40 are fetched (deterministic, outcome-blind). Treated and control use the same fetched set.
**Treated trade.** Buy the cheap side at its ask, C = 10, hard limit 0.30, held to settlement, when **model probability of that side >= 3 x ask**. At most **2 per game** (highest model/ask ratio, ties by sha1). **Control**: every cheap side in the fetched set (same look, no model condition), and the same capped 2 per game by sha1, both reported.
**Fee** 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent, divided by C; stress x1.2. Taker at the ask. Markets whose result is neither yes nor no are dropped and counted.
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract (cents) after fee, treated, pooled over leagues, with 95% interval and z clustered by GAME (event teams and date); PF = gross wins over gross losses after fee. Report win rate against mean price; treated minus control; per league and per stat as information only. Information: model calibration on TEST (bucket the model probability of ALL fetched markets, both sides evaluated only when the side is cheap), and the share of Kalshi results that agree with the ESPN stat line (data quality).

## Blind spots, stated now
Injuries, minutes limits, lineup news and opponent are not in the model, so a treated trade that wins big may be a market that knew; the model predicts a player-game from his own log only. NFL appearance in any category understates games with no stat (upward bias in NFL means); NFL is reported on its own as information. The 1 minute candle only exists when the market traded; a market with no valid quote before the start is dropped (selection against thin ladders, stated). The look is 5 minutes before the start and the market may close early when a strike is reached after the start (irrelevant to a pre-game entry).
