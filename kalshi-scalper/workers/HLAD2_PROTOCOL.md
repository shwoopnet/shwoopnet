# HLAD2 (registered Nov 10, 2026, 4:40 PM CT, before any hourly-ladder market of these series is listed, sampled or scored): one pooled cheap-tail rule on the hourly "above K" ladders with big data. Main approved the run (Nov 10).
Research only, public keyless Kalshi data, no order. Cheap side only (ask 4c to 30c). Not favourite-and-hold. ONE rule, no rule scan.
Series (fixed now, all "above K" threshold ladders by rule text, probed Nov 10): CRYPTO = KXXRPD, KXSOLD, KXHYPED, KXDOGED (strike type "custom" in the API, rule text "above"); METALOIL = KXSILVERH, KXWTIH; INDEX = KXINXU, KXDJI. Not used: KXETHD, KXGOLDH (first pages held only recent dates), FX hourly and KXNASDAQ100I (no settled markets).
Sample, by structure and a hash only: for each series list all settled markets (/historical/markets and /markets status settled); keep the 300 with the smallest md5(ticker). 2,400 markets.
Data: 1-minute candles from 45 minutes to 0 before each market's close. Decision quote = the last candle ending at least 30 minutes before the close, both sides quoted, spread <= 0.05. Fee ceil(0.07*C*p*(1-p)*100)/C/100, C = 10, cash per contract.
THE RULE: buy ONE contract of whichever side (YES at its ask, or NO at 1 - the YES bid) has a price in [0.04, 0.30] at the decision quote; hold to settlement. (Each market has at most one cheap side; the other side is the favourite side and is never bought.)
Cells = group (CRYPTO, METALOIL, INDEX) x side (YES, NO) x band (0.04-0.12, 0.12-0.30) = 12; ledger N = 5,187 + 1 + 12 = about 5,200 (z bar about 4.28). Event-clustered z (cluster = the hourly close). Development = the first half of each group's markets by close time; the SECOND HALF IS HELD OUT and read once, only for a cell that is shortlisted: n >= 100 markets in >= 30 events, cash mean >= +2c per contract, z at or above the bar. Report cash PF first for every dev cell with n >= 20.
Counterparty and reason someone is wrong (two opposite stories, so a result in either direction is informative): (a) the market quotes tail strikes from a volatility estimate that is too thin for jumps in the last 30 minutes, so tails are underpriced and buying them pays; (b) retail overpays for lottery tails (longshot bias), so buying them loses and the favourite side wins, which is the side we are not allowed to buy. The rule tests (a); a loss in every cell is evidence for (b) and closes the family.
Prediction before running: fails (prior 3 to 4%): HISTLAD1 (30,000 rules on the Bitcoin hourly ladder) and the earlier calibration scans found no cheap-side cell above the fee.
Execution: one throttled, resumable background script (`src/scalper/hlad2.py`); the page of results is written after all 2,400 markets are fetched or the run is stopped by rate limits (a partial sample is not scored).
