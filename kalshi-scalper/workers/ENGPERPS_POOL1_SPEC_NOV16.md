# ENGPERPS_POOL1: a pooled engine pass over ALL tradeable stock perps (Desk A, 2026-11-16). SPEC ONLY, nothing run. DEVELOPMENT label: only about 7 trading days exist (Nov 5 to before Nov 17); no n of 100 is reachable, so every figure reads "development, no evidence at n < 100" and survivors go to SHADOW_LIST_V2 for forward scoring, never to live.

**Data.** Forensics' census and extract of every stock perp, Nov 5 to before Nov 17: 1 minute candles (bid, ask, last, volume, oi) and the funding prints (ticker, ts, rate). A name is in the pool only if it has candles on at least 5 of the trading days and its funding prints. Loader with a wall and an allow line, Auditor B's CLEAN, Testing Desk second build; nothing is read before them. Opening-gap and session clocks are ET with DST (as engine1_perps). The account size is NOT in this file or any file: results are printed as a percent of the account per week at leverage 1x, 2x, 5x and the page's maximum (the Head multiplies in a message).

**One rule = one signal x one exit, the same for every name (no per-name tuning), trades pooled over all names, one position per name at a time (cooldown = hold).**
- **Signals.** (1) Opening gap g = (mid at the 09:31 ET candle) / (mid at the previous trading day's 16:00 ET candle) - 1; classes |g| at least 30, 60 or 100 bp; entry at the 09:31 ET candle; FOLLOW (long on a gap up, short on a gap down) or FADE (the opposite): 3 x 2 = 6 signals. (2) Funding LEVEL: at each 16:00 ET print rank the names by the rate (all names with a print that day); FADE the crowd = short the top third (longs pay most) and long the bottom third, or FOLLOW it = the reverse; entry at the next 09:31 ET candle: 2 signals. (3) Funding CHANGE (this print minus the previous print): the same two modes on the top and bottom third of the change: 2 signals. (4) Funding CARRY: at the 15:30 ET candle take the side that RECEIVES the next print (short if the last rate is above 0, long if below; the sign convention is CONFIRMED by Forensics from Kalshi's own docs before the run), only for the names whose |last rate| is in the top third (carry A) or top half (carry B) of that day; hold through the print, exit 30 minutes after it, funding booked from the prints; no exit grid: 2 rules.
- **Exits for signals 1 to 3 (12).** Volatility-scaled: sigma = the standard deviation of the previous 60 one-minute log mid returns (at least 45 present), unit u = sigma x sqrt(30); target = 1 or 2 u and stop = 1 or 2 u from the entry fill, tested on candle closes (long at the bid, short at the ask), plus a time stop of 15, 30 or 60 minutes after entry (the exit is the first of target, stop, time): 2 x 2 x 3 = 12.
- **m (counted in full).** 10 signals x 12 exits + 2 carry = **122 new rules**, plus the carried perps tally of 102,184 tries on other perps rules (ENGINE1 perps passes and PERPGEN), so the BH denominator is **102,306**, printed first.

**Costs.** Taker 12 bp a side (the gate) and maker 5 bp a side (flag, resting fills assumed), both ASSUMED (not confirmed); haircut ladder of 0.02% and 0.05% a side relative; funding booked from the extract's print timestamps (entry end < print < exit end, long pays a positive rate; unbooked trades out of the gates and counted); a name that is halted or has a one-sided entry candle is skipped.

**Gates (all must hold).** At least 3 signals a day POOLED over names (trades / trading days); PF at least 1.3 at taker + 0.02% and at least 1.0 at taker + 0.05%, and both of those in EACH half of the trades (halves by entry time); no single day above 30% of the net; positive net at taker + 0.05%. Reported beside it: n, trades a day, net percent of the notional per trade, average hold and gross move, the maker-fee PF (flag), and percent of the account per week at 1x, 2x, 5x and the maximum leverage (liquidation distance for each from the page's margin rules, which Forensics supplies). Below 100 trades every figure carries "development, no evidence at n < 100".
