# ENGPERPS_POOL1: a pooled engine pass over ALL tradeable stock perps (Desk A, 2026-11-16, version 3: v2 plus the trend and mean-reversion signals the Head added). SPEC ONLY, nothing run. DEVELOPMENT label: only about 7 trading days exist (Nov 5 to before Nov 17), no n of 100 is reachable, so every figure reads "development, no evidence at n < 100" and survivors are shadow candidates for forward scoring (SHADOW_LIST_V2), never live. The account size is NOT in this file or any file: results are printed as a percent of the account per week at leverage 1x, 2x, 5x and the page's maximum, with the liquidation distance of each from the margin rules Forensics supplies.

**Data.** Forensics' census and extract of every stock perp, Nov 5 to before Nov 17: 1 minute candles (end_ts, bid, ask, last, volume, oi; bid and ask are the closes) and funding prints (ticker, ts, rate). A name is in the pool only if it has candles on at least 5 trading days and at least 2 funding prints. Loader with a wall and an allow line, Auditor B's CLEAN, Testing Desk second build; nothing is read before them.

**Conventions (as engine1_perps d4a575e4 / 5171d7f5 and later; differences are listed at the end).** ET clocks with the DST offset read at the ET day's midday. mid = (bid + ask)/2; a candle is usable if 0 < bid < ask. Entry fill: long at ask x (1 + h), short at bid x (1 - h); leave: long at bid x (1 - h), short at ask x (1 + h); h is a relative haircut per side. Gross return: long = exit/fill - 1, short = (fill - exit)/fill. Net = gross - 2 x fee (taker 0.0012 a side is the gate; maker 0.0005 is a flag, resting fills assumed, rate unknown), minus funding paid, plus funding received. Haircuts: h = 0.0002 (primary, "taker + 0.02%") and h = 0.0005 ("taker + 0.05%"); stress 0.001 and 0.002 never gate. Funding is booked from each name's OWN print timestamps in the extract: a position pays or receives a print iff its entry candle end < print ts < its exit candle end (an exit AT the print pays nothing); long pays a positive rate and short receives it (the sign convention is to be CONFIRMED by Forensics from Kalshi's own docs before the run; if it differs the rule below is flipped before any result is read); a position overlapping a stretch with no print (two neighbouring prints more than 26 h apart, before the first, or after the last plus 26 h) is "funding not booked", left out of the gates and counted per name. NO fixed funding clock anywhere: print times differ by name (gold's are at 14:00 UTC, indices' at 20:00 UTC), so ranking, carry entry and carry exit all use each name's own print timestamps.

**One rule = one signal x one exit, the same for every name (no per-name tuning), trades pooled, one position per name at a time (a name takes a new entry only after its previous exit end).**

*Signals (18 with the exit grid, 2 carry).*
1. **Opening gap** (6): g = mid at the candle ending 09:31 ET divided by mid at the candle ending 16:00 ET of the name's previous trading day (the latest earlier ET date that has a 16:00 ET candle for that name) minus 1; classes |g| >= 30, 60 or 100 bp; entry at the candle ending 09:31 ET of that day; FOLLOW (long on a gap up, short on a gap down) or FADE (the opposite); a name with either candle missing or unusable that day is skipped.
2. **Funding level** (2): for each ET date D and name, rate_D = the rate of the name's latest print with ts strictly before the 09:31 ET candle end of D and not older than 36 h. On each date rank the names that have a rate_D by rate_D descending (ties by ticker ascending); with N such names (skip the date if N < 3) the top third = the first ceil(N/3) and the bottom third = the last ceil(N/3). FADE the crowd = short the top third and long the bottom third; FOLLOW = the reverse. Entry at the candle ending 09:31 ET of D.
3. **Funding change** (2): chg_D = rate_D minus the rate of the name's previous print (it must exist within 60 h before the latest one); the same ranking and modes on chg_D.
4. **Funding carry** (2, no exit grid): for each name and each print time T in the extract (T is a timestamp only; the rate used is the latest print strictly before the entry): entry at the candle ending exactly T - 30 minutes, side = the side that RECEIVES the print (short if the last rate is above 0, long if below; no trade at exactly 0), only if |last rate| is in the top third (carry A) or the top half (carry B) of that day's names by magnitude (the day = the ET date of the entry; ties by ticker; skip the day if fewer than 3 names); exit at the candle ending exactly T + 30 minutes; funding booked from the prints; a missing or unusable candle at either end is "no mark" (counted, no trade).

5. **Trend and mean reversion** (8): for each name at each candle end e from 10:31 ET to 15:30 ET (both inclusive) on a trading day: R = ln(mid_e / mid_{e - 60 L minutes}) with L = 30 or 60 (the lookback candle must exist, be usable and fall on the same ET date at or after the 09:31 ET candle; else no signal); u as in the exits below (sigma x sqrt(30), at least 45 returns); the signal fires when |R| exceeds c x u, c = 1 or 2; TREND = go with the sign of R (long if R > 0, short if R < 0), REVERSION = go against it; entry at that candle (the FIRST qualifying candle after the name's cooldown ends; no new entry for the name while a position is open). 2 lookbacks x 2 thresholds x 2 modes = 8 signals.

*Exits for signals 1 to 3 and 5 (12).* sigma = the population standard deviation of the 60 one-minute log returns ln(mid_t / mid_{t-60 s}) over the candles ending e - 3540 s to e (e = the entry candle end), using only consecutive minutes where both candles are usable; at least 45 returns, else the entry is skipped (counted). u = sigma x sqrt(30). From the entry fill: target distance k_t x u and stop distance k_s x u, k_t and k_s in {1, 2}; tested on candle CLOSES in order from e + 60 s: long target if bid >= fill x (1 + k_t u), long stop if bid <= fill x (1 - k_s u); short target if ask <= fill x (1 - k_t u), short stop if ask >= fill x (1 + k_s u); a candle that is not usable is skipped for the test (not a mark); the stop is tested before the target on the same candle; the exit is the first hit, else the candle ending exactly e + 60 T minutes (T in {15, 30, 60}), which must be usable (else "no mark", no trade). 2 x 2 x 3 = 12 exits; the exit price is the leave price at that candle. A position never carries past the day's data: if e + 60 T is after the last candle of the name, "no mark".

**Pool, gate and counts.** Trades are pooled over all names, sorted by (entry end, ticker). Trading days = the number of distinct ET dates with a candle ending 09:31 ET for at least one name. **Gate (all must hold):** trades / trading days >= 3 pooled (n counts trades with a mark, funding booked, at the primary haircut); PF >= 1.3 at taker + h 0.0002 AND PF >= 1.0 at taker + h 0.0005, and both of those in EACH half (halves = the first floor(n/2) trades by that order and the rest); no single ET day above 30% of the net; positive net at taker + h 0.0005. Reported beside it: n, trades a day, net percent of the notional per trade, mean hold (minutes) and mean gross move (bp), the maker-fee PF (flag), the per-name trade counts (so one name carrying the pool is visible), counts of skips by reason, funding-unbooked trades per name, and percent of the account per week at 1x, 2x, 5x and the maximum leverage (assuming the notional equals leverage x account and trades are sized equal in notional; weekly = the net percent per trade x trades a day x 5). Below 100 trades every figure carries "development, no evidence at n < 100".

**m, counted in full:** 18 signals x 12 exits + 2 carry = **218 new rules** (gap 6, funding level 2, funding change 2, trend and reversion 8, each x 12 exits, plus carry A and B), plus the carried perps tally of **103,444** tries on other perps rules (ENGINE1 perps passes 1 to 3 and PERPGEN; Auditor B's correction, pass 3 tried 1,260 more), so the BH denominator is **103,662**, printed first; p from the day-clustered one-sided z of the net at taker + 0.02% where the day count allows (with 7 days a p is shown but BH cannot pass; the verdict is the gates above).

**Where this differs from engine1_perps_second (the Testing Desk's primitives):** (a) exits are volatility-scaled target and stop plus a time stop from one rule, not the fixed T3 to TO list; (b) funding is a signal and carry, the booking itself is the same (print timestamps, strict between entry and exit end, 26 h gaps); (c) no SEARCH and VALIDATE split and no boundary clip (the whole window is one development sample; halves are by entry order); (d) the cooldown is the previous trade's exit end exactly (no 1800 s floor); (e) the pool and the 3 a day gate are over names; (f) the same relative haircuts, taker fee, ET clocks and funding booking as engine1_perps 5171d7f5/a66c74aa.
