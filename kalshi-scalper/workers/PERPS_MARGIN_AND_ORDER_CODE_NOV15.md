## Leverage in plain terms (from the strategy guide, general arithmetic)
Notional is the full value held, margin is the money posted, leverage = notional / margin. A stop at distance d needs a position of risk / d, so leverage falls out of the stop. The rule in our own guide: cap leverage at 3x, and refuse an order whose stop is farther than the liquidation price. At 2x to 3x a 1% adverse move uses 2% to 3% of margin; fees on both legs are a large share of a scalp's risk (a 0.24% round trip is about 0.5% of margin at 2x). A scalp card must therefore be read at the REAL fee and spread, which is why the cards print 1c and 2c haircuts.
## What order code the bot would need (none exists; all new, on the same safety pattern as L1)
1. **A separate account and key.** A trade-only API key for the margin account (no withdrawal permission), stored as its own Firebase secret, never the event-contract key. A separate risk budget: the 12% account stop and the 5.5% window cap are for event contracts and cannot be reused.
2. **A margin order client.** Signed POST to the margin order endpoint (I do not know its path or body; the market candle and funding paths above are the only margin paths in the repo). Fixed client order ids, immediate-or-cancel close orders, a position read before every close (a sell on a flat position would open the opposite side), no retry of a lost answer (end the session), as `runExitWatch` does.
3. **Hard limits in code, not settings** (the `L1_SERIES` rule): a named list of perp tickers, a leverage ceiling (2x proposed, 3x at most), a fixed dollar cap per position and in total, a daily loss stop, a liquidation-buffer check (refuse if the stop is beyond the liquidation price), and a size lock like `maxContractsFor` that reads constants only.
4. **Funding awareness:** a position held across a funding time pays or receives it; the scorer and any live logic must book funding; a carry rule must read the funding rate before entry.
5. **Its own exit watch and kill switch** that can only reduce exposure; a per-ticker close claim like `claimClose` so two closers never double-sell; a heartbeat and an alarm like the exit-watch alarm.
6. **Hours:** the perps quote around the clock but stock perps' reference follows the cash session; a scalp rule restricts itself to regular hours (card (c)); a weekend and halt rule (the perps list shows `is_open` and `halt_sources`) so the bot never trades a halted market.
7. **Paper first:** the cards run as research and, if any passes, as a logged shadow before any money; going live needs the owner's own OK, a separate popup, and the favourite-and-hold style bans do not apply here (they are about event contracts) but 7e, the charter and the declined list do.
