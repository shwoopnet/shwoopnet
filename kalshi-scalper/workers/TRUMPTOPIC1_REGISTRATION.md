# TRUMPTOPIC1 registration (Strategy Research V2, written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any price or outcome of the events below was joined; research only)

Brief 4 of `workers/DIRECTOR_BRIEFS_2026-11-04.md`. OWNER RULES (strict): cheap side only (ask 3c to 30c, hard limit 0.30), no favourite-and-hold, no 15 minute crypto or gold candle triggers. This is a YES at 3c to 30c held to settlement on a speech word market. No favourite-side result is computed.

## Difference from earlier work (one paragraph)
MENT1 (earnings-call word markets) used the WORD's own earlier outcomes and the pooled word rate as the fair price, and was a 2-4 events-per-company history; MENTIONBASE1 did the same for NBA announcer words (dev PF 1.25, last third 1.04, no edge); NEWS1 proposed news coverage volume as information and died at its precondition (76 events with a quote); the COUNT* family (COUNT1, COUNT1B, COUNT1D, COUNT2) uses the speaker's own post COUNT to price numeric count brackets (a pace model on a number). TRUMPTOPIC1 is different in all three: the market is a per-word yes or no inside one announced event (KXTRUMPMENTION and KXTRUMPMENTIONB), the information is the CONTENT of Trump's own posts in the prior 24 hours (does the stem appear at all), and the model is a per-stem table of the outcome given that count bucket, not a base rate over all history and not a pace model.

## Rule TRUMPTOPIC1 (one rule, fixed now)
- Scope: markets of series KXTRUMPMENTION and KXTRUMPMENTIONB whose word is not "Event does not qualify", in events with an `occurrence_datetime` (event occ = the earliest of its markets). Word alternatives are split on "/" and lowercased; the stem pattern is each alternative as a whole word (phrases match across spaces) with an optional "s", "es" or "'s" ending, case-insensitive.
- Look L = occurrence - 10 minutes. Feed count k = number of CNN archive posts with `created_at` in (L - 24h, L] whose text contains the stem (a post counts once). Signal needs k >= 2.
- Quote: the last 1 minute candle ending at or before L with a yes bid and a yes ask, no older than 60 minutes. Fill = that candle's ask close (no waiting, so no later price is used). A market with no valid quote is skipped and counted.
- Model: P(yes | stem, bucket of k), buckets k = 0, 1, 2-3, 4+. Fitted on the FIT events only (first two thirds of history by date; never refitted, never on held-out or forward data): p_global(b) = (yes_b + 5 x overall) / (n_b + 5); p_stem(b) = (yes_{stem,b} + 5 x p_global(b)) / (n_{stem,b} + 5). Word key = sorted alternatives joined by " / ".
- Treated trade: signal (k >= 2), fill ask in [0.03, 0.30] (hard limit 0.30), p_model >= 3 x fill ask. Buy YES, 1 contract, held to settlement (the market's `result`, no early exit). At most 2 per event (highest p_model / ask, ties by ticker).
- Fee: 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent with C = 10, divided by 10 for one contract (p = ask). Net per contract = payoff - ask - fee. Stress at fees x1.2 reported for information.
- Control = every YES with a valid quote and fill ask in [0.03, 0.30] in the same events at the same look (equal weight, one contract each; the same events are those with at least one feed-based trigger candidate; candles are fetched for every market of those events only).
- History scoring (this step) is run ONCE on the held-out third (events from 2026-08-06 to 2026-10-02). The fit third is shown for information only (the model saw its outcomes).
- Forward read: events with occurrence from 2026-11-05T00:00Z, scored once at **150 treated trades or on 2027-06-30, whichever is first** (fwdread guard, one read, a marker is written first). The forward model is the fit above, unchanged.

## Take-up from the census (information only)
The held-out third gave 100 feed-only candidate markets in 28 of 48 events over about 8 weeks, so about 12 candidates per week before the price band and the 3x model test. If about half survive and each event is capped at 2, expect **15 to 25 treated a month** (28 events a month x 0.6 events with a candidate x about 1.3 kept). At 20 a month, 150 trades take about 7 to 8 months, so about 2027-06-30; that is why the due date is stated. The forward read is therefore expected NOT_ENOUGH_DATA until about June 2027.

## Snapshot (forward, information only; a git-ignored CSV the coordinator keeps)
`python -m scalper.trumptopic1 snapshot` (run every day, any time of day): for every KXTRUMPMENTION and KXTRUMPMENTIONB event with occurrence on or after 2026-11-05T00:00Z whose look (occurrence - 10 minutes) has passed and that is not yet in the file, it writes one row per market: event, ticker, series, word, occurrence and look times, post count k in the prior 24h, bucket, p_model, last valid yes bid, ask, quote age, in-band flag, signal flag, selected flag, the archive fetch time and its latest post time. It writes no result and no outcome. Idempotent per ticker. The model for p_model is fitted on the fit events at run time from the registered fit set. File: `data/trumptopic1_snapshots.csv`.
