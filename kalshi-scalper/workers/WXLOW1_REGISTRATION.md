## 1. The series (confirmed on the public series list and market listings, 2026-11-03)

- The live low series are the `KXLOWT*` ones (title "Lowest temperature in ..."): New York `KXLOWTNYC`, Los Angeles `KXLOWTLAX`, Miami `KXLOWTMIA`, Chicago `KXLOWTCHI`, Austin `KXLOWTAUS`, Denver `KXLOWTDEN`, Philadelphia `KXLOWTPHIL`, San Francisco `KXLOWTSFO`, Phoenix `KXLOWTPHX`, Seattle `KXLOWTSEA`, Dallas `KXLOWTDAL`, Atlanta `KXLOWTATL`, Houston `KXLOWTHOU`, Las Vegas `KXLOWTLV`. All 14 WXENS1 cities have one. The older tickers `KXLOWNY`, `KXLOWNYC`, `KXLOWLAX`, `KXLOWMIA`, `KXLOWCHI`, `KXLOWAUS`, `KXLOWDEN`, `KXLOWPHIL` are on the series list but return no market (open or settled): not used. Only the `KXLOWT*` form is recorded.
- Structure is identical to the highs: one event per settlement date (`KXLOWTNYC-26NOV04`), 6 brackets (a `less` tail, four `between` brackets two degrees wide, a `greater` tail), the market for date D opens about 10:00 local standard time the day before D (`KXLOWTNYC-26NOV04` open_time 2026-11-03T15:00Z) and closes at 06:00Z the day after D for the Eastern cities (01:00 EST). Two events are open at a time. Settlement: "the minimum temperature recorded at New York City (CLINYC) for Nov 4, 2026 ... according to The Weather Company"; `expiration_value` carries the number.
- Settled history exists (408 settled markets per series in one listing, from 2026-08-27 forward, and `min_close_ts` listings reach the last days), so a retrospective check is possible later; it is NOT used here (see 2).
- Liquidity is lower than the highs on the series seen: median per-bracket `volume_fp` over the open events at the time of looking was 28 (Las Vegas) to 661 (New York), most under 200. So the lows take-up is predicted LOWER than the highs (section 6).
- Inputs: Open-Meteo ensemble `temperature_2m_min` exists and is served for the same three models in one call with `temperature_2m_max` (response keys `temperature_2m_min_ncep_gefs_seamless` (control) and `temperature_2m_min_memberNN_ncep_gefs_seamless`; models answer as `ncep_gefs_seamless` 31 members, `ecmwf_ifs025_ensemble` 51, `icon_seamless_eps` 40 including controls; seen live 2026-11-03 for New York). NWS gives the night temperature as the `isDaytime: false` periods of the same `forecast` URL (seen live: "Tonight" 18:00 to 06:00 next morning).

## 2. Duplicate check

Everything in WXENS1 section 1 stands (WX1, WX2, WX3, WXNEW1, FLB1, scout, FAMWX all point-forecast or other inputs, all falsified or closed; none used an ensemble). WX1's own universe text lists `KXLOW*` as same shape; no earlier test scored the lows with an ensemble. No result for the lows is looked at here.

**What is different, honestly.** It is the same claim on a second sample. The lows come from the same ensemble systems, the same NWS inputs and the same days, so highs and lows of one city-day are positively correlated (the same air mass, the same model errors). It doubles the number of brackets but NOT the number of independent weather outcomes by two; the pooled claim therefore clusters on the settlement date across all cities and both kinds (section 7). The low has its own error shape (overnight lows depend on cloud and wind that ensembles resolve worse), which is why it gets its own verdict first.

## 3. Rule: identical to WXENS1, nothing changed

Every constant of WXENS1 sections 3 to 5, applied to the low: the look is **10:00 local standard time the day before the settlement date** (first poll at or after it and before 12:00; no poll means no trade); ensemble models gfs, ecmwf IFS and icon, equal weight per model and per member, at least 2 models; per-city bias = plain mean of (official settled minimum minus the pooled ensemble mean) over the trailing 14 settled target dates recorded before the look, applied only with 5 or more days; normal kernel sd **1.5 F**; the whole-degree minimum falls in the bracket (`greater K`: at least K+1; `less K`: at most K-1; `between`: inclusive); a bracket is cheap with a YES ask 0.03 to 0.30 inclusive and is a candidate when `p_model >= 3 x ask`; buy YES at the ask, 1 contract, hard limit 0.30, held to settlement, at most 2 brackets per city-day (largest `p/ask` first, ties lower ask, then ticker); fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up at C = 10, plus a fees x 1.2 line; net = payout minus ask minus fee. Not tested: the NO side and any favourite (banned 2026-10-28). Control = all cheap brackets at the same look. Settlement truth = the settled market's result and `expiration_value`; the IEM daily summary `min_tmpf` is a cross-check only. Information-only looks `L0_07` and `L1_07` are recorded and never scored. Daylight time: all target dates up to 2027-02-28 fall in standard time (the clocks went back on 2026-11-01), so the Open-Meteo local calendar day and the market day are the same day; this is stated because it would not hold after 2027-03-14.

- **City selection (frozen):** the 8 recorded cities with the highest MEDIAN per-bracket `volume_fp` of the LOW brackets at the primary look over the first 7 recorded low target dates that have a primary poll (ties alphabetical by series), chosen on volume alone, frozen at that read by `python -m scalper.wxlow1 cities` (refuses to overwrite). The low city set is chosen separately from the highs set and may differ.
- Unit: a city-day = one low `event_ticker`.

## 4. The three claims, each with its own verdict

1. **WXLOW1-L (lows alone):** the rule above on the 8 low cities.
2. **WXLOW1-P (pooled highs + lows):** the **WXENS1 rule unchanged** (same constants, so the highs trades are exactly the WXENS1 selected set on WXENS1's own frozen 8 cities) together with the WXLOW1-L trades on the 8 low cities. Pooling is legitimate only because the rule is identical; if WXENS1's rule or city set were ever changed, pooling is void and this claim is not run. The pooled claim has its own verdict; the pooled halves are by settlement date.
3. WXENS1's own claim remains separate and untouched (it is charged N +2 in its own entry; this entry's N +2 is claims 1 and 2).

## 5. Reads and the interaction with WXENS1 (stated before data)

- **WXLOW1-L:** one read when the low selected set has **300 trades** or on **2027-02-28 23:59 UTC**, whichever first, `python -m scalper.wxlow1 run DATA`; a second run is refused.
- **WXLOW1-P:** one read when the POOLED selected set (highs on WXENS1's frozen cities + lows on the low cities) has **300 trades** or on 2027-02-28, whichever first, `python -m scalper.wxlow1 pooled DATA`; a second run is refused. Because the pooled arrival rate is about twice the single rate, this read comes about twice as early as WXENS1's own.
- **Disclosed cost of pooling.** The pooled read looks at outcomes of the highs trades before WXENS1's own read at 300 highs. That is a second look at part of WXENS1's sample. It cannot change WXENS1: its rule, cities and bars are frozen and the pooled output prints ONLY the pooled numbers (never a split by kind, never the highs alone), so nothing about the highs alone is shown. It remains a look, and the coordinator is told so. Whatever the pooled verdict, WXENS1 and WXLOW1-L are still read at their own registered points (the recorder is free). Nobody reads results before the registered point; only trade COUNTS (`count`) are allowed earlier.
- Counts before any read: `python -m scalper.wxlow1 count DATA` prints lows, highs and pooled selected counts and the date 150 and 300 would be reached (no outcome is read).

## 6. Numeric prediction (before any outcome)

- **Take-up:** lows **0.4 trades per city-day** (range 0.15 to 0.9; lower than the highs' 0.5 because the low brackets are thinner), so about 3.2 a day over 8 cities (range 1.2 to 7.2). Pooled with WXENS1's 0.5 per city-day: **about 7.2 trades a day (range 2.8 to 15)**.
- **Time to 150 trades from the first low primary look:** lows alone about **47 days (range 21 to 125)**; pooled about **21 days (range 10 to 54)**. 300 trades: lows alone about 94 days (range 42 to 250), pooled about 42 days (range 20 to 108). Registered highs-alone figure for comparison: 38 days (range 19 to 94). The pooled claim is what shortens the wait; lows alone do not.
- Win rate **1 to 4 points below the mean price paid**; mean price about 0.10 to 0.15; **PF about 0.75 (0.5 to 1.2); mean net about -2c (-5c to +1c)**, for lows and for the pool. Prior that either claim passes all bars: under 3%. Because lows and highs are positively correlated the prior that BOTH pass is not independent of either.
- Model Brier against Kalshi mid on all brackets: the model is worse in the middle brackets and no better in the tails.

## 7. Bars and verdicts (identical to WXENS1)

`NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` under 150 trades at the read; `FALSIFIED` if the mean net after fees is not above zero in BOTH date halves (equal trade counts, whole days together) or the profit factor is under 1.5, or the fees x 1.2 mean is not above zero; otherwise `NOT_YET_FALSIFIED`, a forward candidate only, which additionally needs the date-clustered z (`calibration.cluster_mean_z`, cluster = settlement date, all cities and both kinds in one cluster per date for the pooled claim) against the deflated bar of about 5.27 and permits a longer forward test, never a live order. The run prints the minimum detectable mean (2.8 x clustered se) and, if above 2c, a `FALSIFIED` is reported as "not shown at this sample size". The recorder scores nothing.

## 8. Limits

Quotes are asks, not fills; the low brackets are thinner than the highs, so the cheap-side fill assumption is weaker still. Highs and lows of one city-day are correlated, so 300 pooled trades carry less than 300 trades of independent evidence (hence the date cluster). A cold-front low at 23:59 local can differ from the model's overnight low; the bias correction partly removes the average error only. Open-Meteo's free ensemble API has a daily call limit per address; the lows ride in the SAME hourly call as the highs (`daily=temperature_2m_max,temperature_2m_min`), so they add no ensemble request; a refused call is a gap, never filled. Station identity comes from each low event's rules text, recorded once. `KXLOWTSFO` showed an empty `status=open` series listing on one pass while its event markets were active: if that repeats, San Francisco lows are dropped by data (counted), not substituted.
