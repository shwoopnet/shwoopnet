## The structural calendar (facts read from the public market object, the public docs at docs.kalshi.com and the history; read before registering)
- **Funding runs every 8 hours for Crypto (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 US Eastern = 04, 12, 20 UTC in EDT, 05, 13, 21 UTC after the Nov 1 clock change), once a day for Metals (10:00 Eastern: 14:00 UTC, 15:00 from Nov 1), once a day for Equities and Indices (16:00 Eastern: 20:00 UTC, 21:00 from Nov 1), once a day for WTI (14:30 Eastern: 18:30 UTC, 19:30 from Nov 1).** The docs say the schedule is a cron in Eastern time that "differs by asset class" (the schedule endpoint itself needs a clearing-member login); the times above are read from the history, and the gold perp's next funding time reads 15:00 UTC on a Saturday, so metals fund on weekends too.
- Funding rate for a period = time-weighted average premium index over [last funding time, now), computed per clock-aligned bar of one second or more, the premium being how far the impact-price book sits above or below the underlying index (docs, Get Funding Rate Estimate and Get Premium Index). A second with no measurable premium counts as 0 (book halted, underlying closed or an input missing).
- `funding_cap` is 0.02 (2 percent per period) for Crypto, Equities, Metals, Indices and 0.01 in dollars for WTI; `funding_deadband` is 0.0001 for Crypto and WTI and 0.00002 for the rest; `funding_invalid_premium_handling` is `dampen` (Crypto, Metals, Indices, Energy) or `exclude` (Equities, 58 markets): an invalid or closed-underlying premium is excluded for stocks and dampened for the others.
- Market object `schedule` gives `is_open`, `next_close_ts`, `next_open_ts` (equities, indices, metals except gold and silver, WTI close at 22:00 UTC on Fri 2026-11-06; Crypto and gold and silver carry no schedule, i.e. 24 hours; the silver `product_metadata` says a maintenance window raises the tick from 1 to 5 centicents and cancels resting orders). `halt_sources` was empty for all 88. Holiday closures come from `/margin/markets/schedules`. Price banding: a bid may be at most the higher of 2 times the best ask or 1,000 ticks above it (tick 0.0001), with the reference price as the fallback only when the other side is empty, the reference is under 30 seconds old and the underlying is open.
- NOT public (stated unknown): the exact settlement time of the mark price used for funding, the rule that builds the settlement mark from the book, the fee schedule (needs a login, hence the 12 bps a side placeholder), and the maintenance window times.
- **Sample counts near events, development dates only (53 snapshots, 2026-10-21 to 10-31, about 4.8 a day).** Snapshots within 30 minutes of a funding or session event: Crypto funding 2 (1 before, 1 after), Metals 3, Indices 1, WTI 4. Per event class that is far under 60 per half.

## PSET1 (sample-level spread and price around funding time; count gate)
**Mechanism and counterparty.** Makers widen or pull quotes into a funding or settlement mark and rebuild after; a taker who trades in the 30 minutes after funding pays the maker's re-quote. Counterparty: the maker repricing after the settlement mark. Honest alternative: it is noise.
**Rule.** Using `origin/perp-data` crypto rows (universe below), a snapshot is "after funding" if it falls 0 to 30 minutes after an 04, 12 or 20 UTC funding time (05, 13, 21 UTC from Nov 1). Claim: median relative spread of the after-funding snapshots is at least 1.25 times the median of all other snapshots, in BOTH halves, with at least 60 after-funding snapshots in each half.
**Numeric prediction.** The claim fails or cannot be counted; ratio about 1.0 (range 0.8 to 1.3).
**Kill criteria.** NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if either half has under 60 after-funding snapshots (the expected state: 1 crypto snapshot after an event in development). At about 0.6 qualifying snapshots a day (4.8 snapshots a day times 3 half-hour windows of 24 hours) 120 are reached about 200 days after 2026-10-21, about **2027-05-10**; the planned 30 second recorder (`perps30/`, not on main or the data branch yet, 0 files) covers a funding window only if it is extended to 03:40-04:20 UTC, 11:40-12:20 UTC and 19:40-20:20 UTC (an owner decision, not made here). FALSIFIED if counted and the claim fails; LEAD if it holds, held-out third then read once.

## PSET2 (funding-window seasonality of the mark price, crypto, with recorded funding)
**Mechanism and counterparty.** The three 8 hour settlement windows are the Asian, European and US sessions; if flow is not symmetric a window has a drift. Counterparty: the other side of the session's net flow (for example US retail buying into the 16:00 to 00:00 ET window). Honest alternative: a coin flip with a 8 hour move of about 1 percent and a cost of about 25 bp.
**Data and cut (stated before any return).** `funding_rates/historical`, one saved JSON; each row is a funding time with `mark_price` and `funding_rate`. **Cut: funding_time before 2026-09-15T00:00:00Z is development; 2026-09-15 to 2026-11-06 is the held-out last third (the pooled recorded dates run 2026-06-03 to 2026-11-06, 156 days, two thirds is 104 days) and is dropped by the scorer before any statistic is computed.** D1 = before 2026-07-24, D2 = 2026-07-24 to 2026-09-14. Disclosure: I downloaded the whole file and printed only counts, first and last dates and funding hours per ticker, and (while testing the endpoints) the first three Bitcoin rates and marks of 2026-11-04 to 11-06 (held out) and the AAPL and gold current estimates; no return or rate statistic was computed on them.
**Universe, chosen on COST.** Crypto perps with development-sample median relative spread at or under 0.10% on the 53 development snapshots (computed from the recorded bid and ask, not returns) and at least 60 development funding periods: Bitcoin 0.0037%, Ethereum 0.0044%, Solana 0.0256%, XRP 0.0388%, KSHIB 0.0641%, HYPE 0.0678%, NEAR 0.0810%, Chainlink 0.0820%, Dogecoin 0.0868%, Litecoin 0.0885%, Sui 0.0918% (11 tickers). Left out on cost: AAVE, ZEC, WLD, UNI, VVV, BCH (over 0.10%); left out on history: BNB and ADA (first funding 2026-08-28 to 08-31, under 60 development periods). Metals, indices, oil and stocks have 56 or fewer days of history so their development halves are under 30 days: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA by construction, not scored.
**Rule (no tuning).** For each ticker and each pair of consecutive funding times exactly 8 hours apart: a long from the earlier mark to the later mark returns g = m1/m0 - 1 minus the funding rate r1 settled at the later time (assumption: a positive rate means longs pay shorts and the rate at time t covers the period ending at t; if wrong, the price-only gross is also printed in PSET3 and PSET2's cost sign flips are visible) minus cost; the short is the mirror. Six cells: window ending 04, 12, 20 UTC times long or short. Fill at the recorded mark (stated optimistic) and cost = 2 x (0.0012 + half the ticker's development median spread). Day clustering by the UTC date of the window end, all tickers pooled per day, z over day means.
**Numeric prediction.** Every cell's mean net is between -30 bp and -20 bp (the cost) with day z between -8 and +2; no cell reaches the bar.
**Kill criteria.** NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if a cell has under 20 days. FALSIFIED if every cell has z under 5.27 (the expected result). LEAD only if a cell has day z at least 5.27 AND mean net above 0 in both D1 and D2 AND at least 100 days; the held-out third is then read once and must give mean net above 0.

## PSET3 (fade a crowded funding rate, with the recorded funding collected)
**Mechanism and counterparty.** When the funding rate is at an extreme high for that ticker, longs are crowded and pay shorts to stay; the counterparty is the levered long who is forced out or who pays the premium. The short earns the funding (a recorded number, not a proxy) and a mean-reverting price. Mirror for an extreme low rate. Honest alternative: persistent funding means a trend, a short is run over, and the carry (about 1 to 3 bp a period) is far under the 25 bp cost.
**Rule (no tuning).** At a funding time t with at least 30 earlier rates of the same ticker (expanding, development rows only): if r > 0 and r is at or above the ticker's expanding 90th percentile, SHORT at that period's mark; if r < 0 and at or below the expanding 10th percentile, LONG. Hold exactly 3 periods (24 hours; all three gaps exactly 8 hours or the signal is skipped), one position per ticker at a time. Net = side x (m3/m0 - 1) + side-signed sum of the next three recorded rates (short receives positive rates) - cost as PSET2. Same universe, cut and halves as PSET2; same day clustering.
**Numeric prediction.** About 250 to 350 trades on about 90 days, mean net -22 bp (range -45 to +5), win rate about 47%, price-only gross about 0 bp (range -20 to +20); no LEAD (prior about 3%).
**Kill criteria.** NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if under 100 trades or under 20 days. FALSIFIED if the day z is under 5.27 with enough data (the expected result). LEAD only if z at least 5.27, both halves above 0, at least 100 trades; then the held-out third is read once.
