This file adds no try, so N stays about 728,702 (z bar 5.27).

**The shape being asked for (OICORE shape):** a cheap side or underdog (about 5c to 50c), held to settlement, low win rate (about 28%), large payoff per win, trigger from public market-structure data (open interest, volume, spread, order flow, book depth, price velocity, cross-series moves).

**Ban column, read carefully.** The only WRITTEN owner instruction in the README is of 2026-10-28: "no further research on the buy-the-favourite-and-hold idea" (L1 and its variants). `V2_STATUS.md` (2026-10-30 2:40 AM) relays a "standing owner rule ... not the cheap-side-late trade", and `workers/DIRECTOR_2026-10-30.md` offered "stop testing through the cheap-side-late trade" as option 1; I found no text that records the owner answering it. Short coin-flip round trips are called closed by the data (GEN1, GEN8, GEN9), and the brief for this task lists them as banned. Codes used: **FAV** = favourite-and-hold family (written ban, Oct 28); **CSL** = cheap-side-late family (relayed rule, Oct 30; 2 to 6 minutes before the close); **RT** = short round trip, never held; **OK** = held cheap side or underdog NOT in the late window, or not on a 15 minute crypto/metal book; **ADJ** = adjacent (model-versus-market or lock, not a structure trigger on a cheap side). Whether OICORE-shape strategies on other markets are allowed is the owner's call; the codes only say which family each past try sat in.

## (b) What was learned, in 10 lines

1. Price alone never pays on the cheap side: C1 to C3, T4, T5, BOUNCE1, TAIL1, UNDER1, the calibration scan. The market is calibrated at every price (win rate equals ask within about 1c), and the cheap side is the worst place to pay spread plus fee (BOUNCE1 -6.21c, TAIL1 -2.77c, all series pooled -2.19c, z -11.69).
2. The only survivor is Bitcoin's open interest jump at 180 s before the close (n 222 PF 2.28, then n 333 PF 1.85), and it is still short of the z bar (4.42 to 4.53 vs about 5.2); forward 22 trades, PF 2.55.
3. It does not transfer: gold (GEN6, GEN7), Ethereum (SLOTEX-ETH), and eight more markets (OICOREX1, 8 of 8 FALSIFIED). It is a property of one series, and its counterparty is unnamed (MECH1 refuted the favourite-taker story).
4. Volume, spread, order flow and tape features on the favourite or mid-window side (IDEAS20, TAPE20, GEN9, GEN10, GEN11, FP1 to FP3, PERPFLOW1) are cost-limited: base loses about 6.5c a round trip, about 3c held; best survivors were chance (family control 0.6% to 0.8%).
5. Round trips die on cost, not on direction: following and fading lose the same (SC1 -4.16c to -4.52c, FP3 control -4.14c to -4.22c).
6. A fixed threshold drifts: the 86,300 cut sat at the discovery 90th percentile but was below the median within weeks (47.7% of windows pass); a new count-based trigger needs a relative or trailing definition from the start (OIREL, OIDRIFT).
7. Fill realism decides: 34% to 42% of core entries fill below 5c, the edge needs a fill within about a minute, and candles show no size at the ask (R6 can answer, OIFILL).
8. Cross-series leads and lags among Kalshi books are priced inside one minute (NEWH12, BK1 to BK7, XLEAD1); the control often equals the hypothesis.
9. Arithmetic locks, ladders and Dutch books are closed at every public resolution (ARB1 to ARB5, LADLOCK, LOCK2, NEWH13, SPORTCONS1, RACELOCK).
10. Multiple comparison is the real enemy: N about 728,702, deflated bar z about 5.27. A new cheap-side idea with an economic story and a named counterparty has a better prior than a generator draw; 7 of GEN6's 13 Bitcoin survivors were one bet (the core), so counting them as several leads overstates the evidence.

## (e) The OICORE rule in one paragraph (README, REF_GEN6_OICORE)

On Bitcoin's 15 minute market (`KXBTC15M`), at the one minute candle ending 180 seconds before the close, compute doi = open interest at that candle minus open interest at the candle ending 360 seconds before the close (the end-of-candle snapshot, so the change never exceeds that minute's volume, FALS4). If doi is greater than 86,299.56 contracts (GEN6's discovery 90th percentile; `>` strict), buy the side (YES checked first) whose ask is between 5c and 50c, at the NEXT candle's (-120 s) closing ask, one entry per market, and hold to settlement (win pays 100c, loss costs the price paid, entry fee only, 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up per order). History: discovery (Aug 6 to Sep 20) n 222 PF 2.28, validation (Sep 21 to Oct 27) n 333 PF 1.85 (z 4.53); win rate about 28% (FALS4: 555 trades, longest losing run 19, net +$492 at C = 10); 34% to 42% of entries fill below 5c; a fill one minute later drops discovery PF to 0.94. Verdict NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, counterparty unnamed (MECH1 refuted "late favourite takers"). Variants carrying the same bet: OIDRIFT (trailing 30 day p90 threshold), OIREL (26.76% of the oi level), GEN6_B01 to B13, GEN7_B01 and B02. The forward ledger at 2026-11-03 04:08 UTC: 22 trades, 7 wins, 15 losses, PF 2.55 at 10 contracts, +13.02c per contract. The nightly-report note says second-slot OICORE orders exist live; the live code is not on this branch, so this is the registered research rule. **A new candidate states how it differs on:** market (not Bitcoin 15 minute), signal (not an open interest count over 3 minutes), time (not 180 s before the close), band (not 5c to 50c at one look), side rule, and whether it can hold a position at the same time as OICORE or L1 (then it is paper only); and it must carry a named counterparty, a fixed threshold definition that does not drift, and its N added to the running count (z bar about 5.27).
