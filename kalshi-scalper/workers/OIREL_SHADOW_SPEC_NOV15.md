# OIREL1 shadow spec for Main (Idea Desk A, Nov 15, 2026; the Head's order of 15:18Z). A spec, no code. LOG ONLY: no order, no money.
Status: DRAFT until Auditor B's CLEAN. Basis: the OIREL haircut recompute (two builds equal at 1e-9, rule 16b met; figures are in the queue, none here) and the research rule `src/scalper/oirel.py`. It is a **7g version of OICORE** (shares 59% to 80% of its trades; not new, not independent), so it takes no new-strategy slot, and it tests whether the RELATIVE jump condition beats OICORE's fixed 86,300-contract line.
## Entry rule (exact)
- **Series:** KXBTC15M only. One decision per window (the first in-band look wins, as every shadow rule).
- **Look:** the bot's existing minute look inside 180 to 120 s before the close (OICORE's own span), using the candles ending EXACTLY 180 s and 360 s before the close.
- **Open interest condition (relative):** level = the open interest of the 1 minute candle ending 360 s before the close; doi = the open interest of the candle ending 180 s before the close minus level; fire when doi / level is STRICTLY greater than 0.26764675317954373 (the matched discovery quantile of FALS5 check 2, fixed; never recomputed). **Provenance (the Head's point 2):** this is the threshold AS REGISTERED, not refit on Aug 6 to Oct 2: `oirel.py` RTHR documents it as the discovery quantile matched to the core's count of windows above 86,299.56 (941 of 4,357 discovery windows, discovery = Aug 6 to Sep 20), registered in FALS5 check 2 on 2026-10-30 (1 try) and frozen in the forward row REF_GEN6_OIREL (freeze 2026-10-30T15:00Z). Caveat in plain words: the number was fitted on Aug 6 to Sep 20, which is inside the haircut recompute's window, so the recompute's discovery row is NOT out of sample for the threshold; only the forward rows are. A missing or unreadable candle, or an open interest of 0 or missing at either, is "no data": no row, counted, never a no-trade and never interpolated.
- **Side and price:** the cheaper side with its ask 0.05 to 0.50 inclusive, YES tried first (YES if its ask is in the band, else NO at 1 minus the YES bid); the quote must be two-sided (0 < bid < ask < 1). The row's price is the ask at the look. (The research fill is the ask of the candle ending 120 s before the close; the shadow row logs the quote at the look and the later fill-side ask when the look is the 120 s one: see "fields".)
- **The 8c floor (the Head's point 1):** OICORE keeps its live floor of 8c (`SLOT_SKIP_BELOW`). OIREL1 does NOT: it logs the whole 5c to 50c band, with the price in every row, so the scorer reads the 8c split both ways (all rows 5c to 50c, and only rows at 8c or more, which is OICORE's own entry range) and compares each with OICORE on the same windows. The pass rule is judged on the 8c-or-more reading (the live-comparable one); the full band is information.
- **Size:** 1 contract. Hold to settlement. No exit, no stop.
## Can the bot's shadow logger carry it? YES, with one reviewed addition.
`functions/kalshiShadowLib.js` already has the optional entry field `oiRiseMin` (an ABSOLUTE rise, at least 1,000 contracts) and a candle read (`args.candles`, `oiRiseFromCandles`: the same two candles, 180 s and 360 s before the close, field `open_interest_fp`), so it already reads open interest candles and needs no new endpoint. What is missing: a RELATIVE field. Needed: one new optional entry key (for example `oiRelMin`, a number between 0.05 and 1) validated beside `oiRiseMin`, and one function returning doi / level from the same two candles (null when either is missing or 0). No slot-engine path is needed and OICORE is untouched. It needs a deploy (`deploy-sizing.sh`) and the gates for the registry shape. The logger reads no key and sends no order. Cost: one candle read per window it already makes for OICORE only if both rules share the read (the rule must not add a second read of the same ticker and minute; confirm in the review).
## Fields to log (numbers and labels only)
rule `OIREL1`, ticker, side, price (ask at the look), contracts 1, depth at the touch (as the other rows), the quote (bid, ask), seconds left at the look, `oiLevel` (the level), `oiDoi` (the rise), `oiRel` (the ratio), and the same window's OICORE reading (`oiRise` absolute, whether OICORE's line 86,300 was crossed) so the pair can be compared on identical windows. NO result at logging time; the settlement fills in `won` and `pnl` as for the other shadow rows.
## Scorer and rules (lifecycle 7g, written before any forward row)
- **Unit:** each window where OIREL1 fires. **Cash:** per-order rounded fee, 10 contracts at the scorer (fee_up_order), the candle-haircut labels not used (the book touch is logged); label "upper bound, not fill-checked" until a fill check exists.
- **Pass (7g, a VERSION replaces its parent only if all hold):** at least 60 forward signals; cash PF beats OICORE's cash PF over the same period by at least 0.10; net per contract-week not lower than OICORE's; its second half (floor(n/2) by close time) not worse than its first; and no day over a rule-set concentration line if the lifecycle has one at the time.
- **Kill:** net under -1 cent per contract after 50 signals, or fewer than 40 signals in 30 days (the standard shadow kill). If OICORE itself is closed for cause, OIREL1 closes with it (a version follows its parent).
- **Forward read date:** 14 days after the first logged row, or 60 signals, whichever is LATER (history had about 6.6 firings a day, so 60 signals is about 9 days; the 14 day minimum governs). One read, by the scorer, no peeking before.
## What this spec does not decide
Whether to deploy it (Main, with the Head and the owner's merge); whether the shadow logger's candle read can be shared with OICORE's; and that a pass changes OICORE's threshold only by a reviewed edit, never automatically.
