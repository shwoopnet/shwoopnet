# OIREL1 shadow spec for Main (Idea Desk A, Nov 15, 2026; the Head's order of 15:18Z). A spec, no code. LOG ONLY: no order, no money.
Status: DRAFT until Auditor B's CLEAN. Basis: the OIREL haircut recompute (two builds equal at 1e-9, rule 16b met; figures are in the queue, none here) and the research rule `src/scalper/oirel.py`. It is a **7g version of OICORE** (shares 59% to 80% of its trades; not new, not independent), so it takes no new-strategy slot, and it tests whether the RELATIVE jump condition beats OICORE's fixed 86,300-contract line.
## Entry rule (exact)
- **Series:** KXBTC15M only. One decision per window (the first in-band look wins, as every shadow rule).
- **Look and fill (the research rule, option (a), Auditor B's fix):** side from the quote of the candle ending 180 s before the close (the SIGNAL candle); price = the ask of the bought side on the candle ending 120 s before the close (the FILL candle). So the row is written at the bot's look about 120 s before the close, once both candles exist; it is NOT written at an earlier look in the 180 to 120 s span. This is exactly what both recompute builds measured, so the shadow reading and the recompute use the same price. It differs from OICORE's own span (180 to 120 s, quote at the look): the pair comparison on the same window therefore compares different fill moments, and the row logs both the signal-candle quote and the fill-candle quote.
- **Open interest condition (relative):** level = the open interest of the 1 minute candle ending 360 s before the close; doi = the open interest of the candle ending 180 s before the close minus level; fire when doi / level is STRICTLY greater than 0.26764675317954373 (the matched discovery quantile of FALS5 check 2, fixed; never recomputed). **Provenance (the Head's point 2):** this is the threshold AS REGISTERED, not refit on Aug 6 to Oct 2: `oirel.py` RTHR documents it as the discovery quantile matched to the core's count of windows above 86,299.56 (941 of 4,357 discovery windows, discovery = Aug 6 to Sep 20), registered in FALS5 check 2 on 2026-10-30 (1 try) and frozen in the forward row REF_GEN6_OIREL (freeze 2026-10-30T15:00Z). Caveat in plain words: the number was fitted on Aug 6 to Sep 20, which is inside the haircut recompute's window, so the recompute's discovery row is NOT out of sample for the threshold; only the forward rows are. A missing or unreadable candle, or an open interest of 0 or missing at either, is "no data": no row, counted, never a no-trade and never interpolated.
- **Side and price:** the cheaper side with its ask 0.05 to 0.50 inclusive, YES tried first (YES if its ask is in the band, else NO at 1 minus the YES bid); the quote must be two-sided (0 < bid < ask < 1) on BOTH the signal candle and the fill candle (a failure on either is no row, counted, as in the recompute). The row's price is the fill candle's ask of the bought side (the side is decided on the signal candle).
- **The 8c floor (the Head's point 1):** OICORE keeps its live floor of 8c (`SLOT_SKIP_BELOW`). OIREL1 does NOT: it logs the whole 5c to 50c band, with the price in every row, so the scorer reads the 8c split both ways (all rows 5c to 50c, and only rows at 8c or more, which is OICORE's own entry range) and compares each with OICORE on the same windows. The pass rule is judged on the 8c-or-more reading (the live-comparable one); the full band is information. NOTE: the haircut recompute was run and passed on the WHOLE 5c to 50c band; nobody has printed its 8c-or-more split, so the recompute's bar is NOT claimed for the 8c subset. A measurement of that split from the same landed file under the same two builds (no new try) can be asked for before the forward read.
- **Size:** 1 contract. Hold to settlement. No exit, no stop.
## Can the bot's shadow logger carry it? YES, with one reviewed addition.
`functions/kalshiShadowLib.js` already has the optional entry field `oiRiseMin` (an ABSOLUTE rise, at least 1,000 contracts) and a candle read (`args.candles`, `oiRiseFromCandles`: the same two candles, 180 s and 360 s before the close, field `open_interest_fp`), so it already reads open interest candles and needs no new endpoint. What is missing: a RELATIVE field. Needed: one new optional entry key (for example `oiRelMin`, a number between 0.05 and 1) validated beside `oiRiseMin`, and one function returning doi / level from the same two candles (null when either is missing or 0). No slot-engine path is needed and OICORE is untouched. It needs a deploy (`deploy-sizing.sh`) and the gates for the registry shape. The logger reads no key and sends no order. Cost (the Head's fix, per Auditor B's read of PR 945): OIREL1 adds up to TWO public candle reads per BTC window under the shared shadow read budget (at most 3 public reads a minute for every shadow rule together); OICORE is a SLOT rule, so its candle reads are not in the shadow cache and cannot be shared. NO result at logging time; the settlement fills in `won` and `pnl` as for the other shadow rows.
## Scorer and rules (lifecycle 7g, written before any forward row)
- **Unit:** each window where OIREL1 fires. **Cash:** per-order rounded fee, 10 contracts at the scorer (fee_up_order), the candle-haircut labels not used (the book touch is logged); label "upper bound, not fill-checked" until a fill check exists.
- **Pass (7g, a VERSION replaces its parent only if all hold):** at least 60 forward signals; cash PF beats OICORE's cash PF over the same period by at least 0.10; net per contract-week not lower than OICORE's; its second half (floor(n/2) by close time) not worse than its first. (No day-concentration clause: the lifecycle names no testable line, so none is claimed.)
- **Kill:** net under -1 cent per contract after 50 signals, or fewer than 40 signals in 30 days (the standard shadow kill). If OICORE itself is closed for cause, OIREL1 closes with it (a version follows its parent).
- **Forward read date:** 14 days after the first logged row, or 60 signals, whichever is LATER (history had about 6.6 firings a day, so 60 signals is about 9 days; the 14 day minimum governs). One read, by the scorer, no peeking before.
## What this spec does not decide
Whether to deploy it (Main, with the Head and the owner's merge); whether the two extra candle reads fit the shared shadow budget (they cannot be shared with OICORE, a slot rule); and that a pass changes OICORE's threshold only by a reviewed edit, never automatically.
