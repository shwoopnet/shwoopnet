# Probation-seat spec sheets, Idea Desk A (Nov 12, 2026, for the Head of R&D; 3 contracts, $18 drawdown kill). Research view only; probation fills are never research evidence. No held-out outcome was opened for this.

## A. CFRTI-K (Bitcoin 15 minute): the honest line first
Development read (once, closes 2026-09-12 to 2026-11-04, 5,127 windows): **n 610, cash PF 1.09, mean +1.12c a contract, win 17.4%, z 0.71 against the bar 5.46; thirds PF 0.95 / 1.20 / 1.10.** Book replay (53 development rows with an R6 book): 38 filled at a 6 s delay, PF 1.23, "no evidence at n = 38". Forensics' note: at 1 contract the fee rounds UP (up to 0.9c above the unrounded fee), which is about the size of the +1.12c mean, so after the real fee the expected edge is about zero. **No evidence of an edge; it is the best Bitcoin candidate I have, and it is not near the bar.** The held-out read (closes from 2026-11-09, one sitting, 2026-11-20) has not happened.

1. **Spec.** Series `KXBTC15M`. Look = close minus 120 s (the decision uses the last whole-minute perp candle ending at that instant). Cheap side: YES if its ask is 0.05 to 0.30 (inclusive, with a 1e-9 tolerance), else NO if 1 minus the YES bid is 0.05 to 0.30; quote must be active, YES ask above bid, spread 0.03 or less. Buy it when (model chance of that side minus its ask) is 0.05 or more. Size 3 (a 0.30 ask is $0.90 an order, under the $3 cap). Limit: the replay used the ask seen, immediate-or-cancel walking up to 5 book levels at or below it; a limit of ask + 1c is a different rule (higher fill rate, 1c worse price: say which you want). Exit: hold to settlement, no stop.
2. **Inputs at the decision (any missing = no order).** (a) the market's quote (yes bid, yes ask, status) and `floor_strike` from the market object (the strike is the BRTI 60 second average at the window open); (b) the Bitcoin perpetual `KXBTCPERP` 1 minute candles from the public candle endpoint (`/margin/markets/KXBTCPERP/candlesticks?period_interval=1&start_ts&end_ts`, the endpoint `scripts/record_perps_candles.py` uses; the development pull used the same candles through `kalshi-pull`): the perp price P(close-120 s) and P at each of the 10 earlier whole minutes (index = mid of bid and ask close of the candle), and P(close-900 s) (the window open). Formula, frozen: index = floor_strike x P(close-120)/P(close-900); log changes between the 11 neighbouring minute marks (at least 6, else no trade); sd = sample standard deviation (n-1) x sqrt(4/3); model chance of YES = Phi(ln(index/floor_strike)/sd); NO uses 1 minus it. Constants: edge 0.05, band 0.05 to 0.30, spread 0.03, look 120 s, none tuned.
3. **Signals a day.** 610 trades over 54 days = about 11 a day (11.9% of windows). Fillable: 38 of 53 (72%) at a 6 s delay, 37 of 53 (70%) at 2 s, so about 8 fills a day. At a 17% win rate a run of 20 or more losses is normal: each loss costs about 3 x (ask about 0.15 to 0.30) + fee, roughly $0.5 to $1, so the $18 kill is about 20 to 35 losing fills, i.e. 2 to 4 days of trades; expect it to be hit by luck.
4. **Evidence (plain).** n 610, PF 1.09, z 0.71, Sep 12 to Nov 4; replay n 38, PF 1.23; fee rounding eats the mean; held-out pending on Nov 20 (n at least 50 rows from Nov 9).
5. **Hard to build by Sunday.** A new input for the slot engine: a public GET of the perp candles each decision (about 1 request a minute, no key) and the model formula; the engine so far reads only the quote, candles of the same market and open interest. Perp candles carry no open field and I have not measured their freshness at the decision instant (Bitcoin 15 minute candle freshness was measured, the perp's was NOT). No simpler faithful version: replacing the perp by anything else is a new rule.
6. **Overlap.** OICORE also buys the cheaper side of the same Bitcoin 15 minute market, in the 180 to 120 s before the close; CFRTI-K decides at exactly 120 s on the cheap side (ask 5 to 30c), so when both fire they are on the SAME side: the direction lock is not hit (same-side is allowed), but the exposure doubles on that window and the slot's per-market open cap and shared stops apply. Expected overlap rate: not measured.

## B. DEPTH1: not one rule until its read; and Ethereum is NOT in its universe
1. **There is no single live rule yet.** DEPTH1 is 10 book features x 2 markets = 20 tries; the live rule would be the one try that clears the registered bar at the Nov 14 read (cash PF 1.5 in each of three blocks and pooled, both halves above 1.0, net above 0 at fee x1.2, z 5.27, shuffled-book control, 300 trades). My prediction in the registration was **FALSIFIED (best feature PF about 1.15)**; I put the chance that any try passes at under 5%. Until it does, a side rule, a feature and a threshold cannot be written, and writing one now would be choosing after the fact.
2. **Universe correction.** DEPTH1's registered markets are gold (`KXGOLD15M`) and Bitcoin (`KXBTC15M`) only. Ethereum was recorded but is not read. A probation seat on Ethereum has no registration behind it.
3. **If a try passes, the spec would be:** look 480 s (8 minutes) before the close; decision poll = last book poll at or before that instant (within 10 s); feature sign > 0 buys YES, < 0 buys NO, 0 or undefined no trade; fill = best ask of the implied side at the first poll at or after the look + 1 s (within 20 s), ask 0.10 to 0.90, at least 1 contract displayed; 1 contract in the research, 3 in probation; hold to settlement. Inputs: the 5-level book (`yes_bid_px_1..5`, sizes, `no_bid_px_1..5`, sizes) from the public orderbook read; for f4, f5, f6 also the book 30 to 60 s earlier, for f7 the public trade tape of the last 60 s. The live bot reads books once a minute at most, so only the single-poll features (f1, f2, f3, f8, f9, f10) are buildable by Sunday; f4, f5, f6, f7 need a poll loop from 9 minutes out (and f6 and f7 sit in the closed taker-flow family).
4. **Signals a day** for a continuous feature: it fires on nearly every window with a decision poll: about 190 a day across gold and Bitcoin, mostly outside the ask band 0.10 to 0.90 rarely; fill share not known (R6 replay is the fill model itself).
5. **Overlap, important.** On gold, DEPTH1's look (8 minutes out, EITHER side, ask 0.10 to 0.90) comes before gold L1's (about 6.5 minutes, favourite 0.88 to 0.97): a DEPTH1 gold position on the opposite side would hit the direction lock and BLOCK gold L1's own entry (a hedge), the live strategy being the one refused. I would not run gold under DEPTH1 as a probation seat. On Bitcoin its look (8 minutes) is earlier than OICORE's (3 to 2 minutes): a NO-versus-OICORE hedge can arise; the lock would refuse OICORE.
6. **Recommendation:** list DEPTH1 as conditional (as the Head did); do not build anything for it before the Nov 14 read.

## C. CFRTI-K parity vectors and the perp mark (added Nov 12, for Main's pure function)
- **File:** `DESK_A_CFRTI_K_PARITY_VECTORS.json` (20 vectors: 16 real development windows, 4 constructed by removing perp marks from a real window and labelled so), generated by `desk_a_verify/cfrti_k_vectors.py` from the landed LISTS, CANDLES and PERPBTC1M runs; every decision equals the frozen scorer's `decide()` reason. Development windows only (closes 2026-09-12 to before 2026-11-05); inputs and decisions, no outcome, no result, no pnl. Cases: fired YES (3), fired NO (3), no edge (2), not cheap (3), spread over 3c (1), no decision candle (1), edge 0.0494 (just under) and 0.0500 (just at), side price exactly 0.30, exactly 6 log changes left (fires), exactly 5 left (no order), opening mark missing, decision-minute mark missing. Counts over the whole development sample: not cheap 3,668, no edge 848, trade YES 315, trade NO 295 (610), spread 1, no decision candle 1. Cases the real sample never produces (under 6 changes, no opening minute) exist only as the constructed ones.
- **The perp mark at a minute:** public `GET .../margin/markets/KXBTCPERP/candlesticks?period_interval=1&start_ts=&end_ts=` (the base `https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin` that `scripts/record_perps_candles.py` uses; the development pull used the same candle shape through `kalshi-pull`). A candle is identified by `end_period_ts` (the END of its minute). Mark = (bid close + ask close) / 2 x 10,000 where `bid` and `ask` are the candle's `bid.close` and `ask.close` objects (perp quotes are 1/10,000 of a bitcoin; the unit cancels in every ratio the rule uses); a minute missing either side, or with a side at 0, is a missing mark. `reference_price` is only a fallback in the scorer and was not used in the development (candles carried bid and ask). Marks used: the candle ending at close-120 s (mark 0), the 10 candles ending at close-180 s, -240 s, ... -720 s (marks 1 to 10), and the candle ending at close-900 s (the opening mark).
- **Perp freshness at the decision instant:** a landed file holds final candles, so how old the newest perp minute is when the bot asks at close-120 s (the publication lag of the newest candle) CANNOT be measured from it; it needs a live probe (read the newest candle a few seconds after a whole minute and see whether the candle ending at that minute is there yet; one public request per try, a dozen tries). What the landed files do show: of 5,128 development windows, the decision-minute mark was present in 5,128 (100%), all 11 volatility marks in 5,128 (100%), the opening mark in 5,121 (7 windows without).
