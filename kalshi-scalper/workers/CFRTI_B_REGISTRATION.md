## The rule (exactly this and nothing wider)
- Market `KXBTC15M` only. Decision time t0 = close minus 120 s.
- **Signal and price both from the R6 order book:** the cheap side's best ask at the last R6 poll at or before t0 (exchange time: offset = median of Date header + 0.5 s minus `t_recv_ms` over the run; the poll must be at least 0.25 s and at most 4 s before t0). YES ask = 1 minus the best NO bid; YES bid = best YES bid. Spread (ask minus bid) 3c or less. Cheap side: YES if its ask is 0.05 to 0.30, else NO if 1 minus the YES bid is 0.05 to 0.30. **No quote-recorder price anywhere.**
- The spot model, the edge threshold and every other constant are as registered for CFRTI1-BTC (`cfrti1.py` development path): index = the last trade with receive time at or before t, no more than 10 s old; volatility from the 60 earlier 10 s steps, at least 30 changes, sample standard deviation times the square root of 8; model chance of YES = Phi(ln(index / strike) / that figure); buy when the model chance of the cheap side minus its ask is 0.05 or more; strike from the quote recorder's market record (a fixed property of the market, not a price). One contract, held to settlement, fee 0.07 x p x (1 - p) unrounded, cash.
- **Two lines, 2 tries, both added to the ledger:** **B3** (the index from the three venues, as registered) and **BK** (the index from Kraken trades only). **B3 cannot go live under the owner's venue ruling** (no live probe may read Bitstamp or Coinbase): it exists to answer one question, whether spot information exists at all, which bears on whether Coinbase's terms are worth reading. **BK is the only line that could run.**
- **Judged on the fill:** the first R6 poll at least 6 seconds after t0 (exchange time) at which the same side's ask is at or below the ask seen and at least 1 contract is displayed at it; paid at that ask; a vanished ask is no trade (counted). The 2 second line (first poll at least 2 s after t0) is printed beside it.
