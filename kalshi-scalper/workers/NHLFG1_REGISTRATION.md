## 3. The rule (fixed now)
**Mechanism (stated in advance).** Retail underprices tail players' first goal and multi goal chances when the ladder quotes sum to less than the exact total (a first goal ladder partitions 1 minus the no-goal probability, about 0.99 here since an empty net game has a first goal; so the sum of YES asks against 1 is a clean over or under round measure). Counterparty: market makers and retail who price tail rungs from a headline rate. No outside model.
**Universe and order.** Games in **strict md5 order of the game id** (the event ticker after the series name, e.g. 26JUN14CARVGK), one process, no sharding. A game is used for a series only when ALL its volume positive rungs of that series have candles and at least 8 (first goal) or 3 (2+) have a valid quote; a market is tried up to 8 times with growing backoff before the game is dropped (logged). All 25 NHLG1 events are NOT excluded here (different series, no candle of these rungs was ever read) but reported as overlapping above.
**Look.** Last 1 minute candle ending at or before ESPN game start minus 120 seconds with a valid two sided quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1, ask - bid <= 0.10), window start-3600 to start (`period_interval 1`). Cheap side = YES if ask in [0.03, 0.30]; else NO if 1 - bid in [0.03, 0.30]; the other side is never evaluated or printed.
**Rule per series.** Buy the cheap side at its ask on every such rung, C = 10 contracts, hard limit 0.30, held to settlement, **max 2 per game by sha1 of the ticker** (lowest first; no price used). Fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order, divided by C (x1.2 stress). Results other than yes or no are dropped and counted.
**Ladder-sum tercile.** Ladder sum per game = sum of YES asks at the same look over its rungs. **Tercile cuts come from the first third of the fetched games in fetch (md5) order only**, then are applied to all games. The report gives the pooled rule, the lowest tercile rule (most under-round) and the pooled rule by tercile. Mean sum of asks vs mean realised YES count per game is information (first goal: against 1).
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract after fee, SE and z clustered by game and by ET event date (the date part of the game id); lower 95% bound = mean - 1.96 x game clustered SE; PF = gross wins over gross losses after fee; win rate vs mean price; halves = fetched games split at the median start time; fees x1.2. Information only: uncapped all cheap and ask buckets 3-8c, 8-15c, 15-30c, and in vs not in NHLALL1's games.
