# PERPGEN protocol: rule generators on Kalshi perpetual markets (Strategy Research V2; registered 2026-11-06, before any rule was scored)

Research only. No live code, no `functions/`, no `index.html`, no merge, deploy or promote verb. Owner's order (relayed by the lead, 2026-11-06): generators at the GEN1/GEN2 scale (many runs of 5,000 or more distinct rules, several hundred thousand in total, N counted exactly) on Kalshi PERPETUAL markets, from keyless history only. This file is written and pushed BEFORE any rule is scored; the stage-one run that wrote it (data layer, no scoring) computed counts, spreads and calendars only, never a return of any rule. Data layer: `src/scalper/perpdata.py`, report `workers/PERPDATA_REPORT.md`, frozen cuts and universe `workers/PERPGEN_CUTS.json` (sha256 `d5cded742d321d66b1ecacb41b9eaac8e3794b016bbc0556e5909d81f71a2082`).

## Counterparty (stated before the search, as every rule here must)
Perp traders and makers on a young, thin venue: leveraged retail that chases moves and pays funding, makers whose quotes lag a fast move or skew with inventory, and funding payers who hold through settlement. A rule is only interesting if it can name which of these is on the other side. A pattern with no named counterparty stays a LEAD, never a CANDIDATE.

## Ledger check (why this is not a reworded tested idea)
Earlier perp work (README: FUNDP1, CARRY1, BASIS1, PBOOK1 to 3, PSET1 to 3, LL1, XS1, RO1, CPREM1, CLEAD1, CXS1, CSPR1, OVN, RV, PERPEQ) tested single registered rules on the hourly recorder (about 4 to 7 samples a day, 2026-10-21 onward), never a rule SEARCH and never the exchange's own 1 minute candles, which go back to 2026-06-03 (crypto), 2026-09-10 (gold, silver), 2026-09-23 and 2026-10-06 (other metals), 2026-10-06 (US 500) and 2026-10-14 (Tech 100, WTI). PSET2 (fixed 8 hour windows ending at funding times, FALSIFIED) and the PSET3 funding-extreme fade (z 1.21) used hourly data. PERPGEN is the first generator on this data; features overlap earlier single rules in spirit (momentum, funding, lead-lag, time of day) and are building blocks here, the gates decide. L1 and its variants (favourite at 88c to 97c on the 15 minute binaries) are out of scope.

## Data (what exists, what does not)
Source: `GET /trade-api/v2/margin/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks` (period 1 or 60; at most 5,000 candles per call, read in 3 day windows) and `GET /margin/funding_rates/historical`, keyless. Fields per candle: bid and ask open, high, low, close; trade price open, high, low, close, mean, previous; volume and notional volume; open interest and notional open interest. **There are NO bid or ask SIZES in candles**, so no book-size feature exists; volume and open interest stand in for flow. A usable close quote needs bid close above 0 and ask close at or above bid close; an ask field above 2 times the bid is the int64 no-ask sentinel (9.2e14) and is blanked (`perpdata.py`, schema in its docstring). No forward fill anywhere; a missing minute is missing. Snapshot `as_of` 2026-11-06T00:00:00Z (candle end times before it). The 1 hour candles are a consistency check of the 1 minute closes (0 mismatches of about 60,000 compared, see the report). Funding: 7,392 rows, crypto every 8 hours (04, 12, 20 UTC, 05, 13, 21 after the clock change), metals, US 500 and Tech 100 daily, WTI daily; `funding_rate` as served (decimal per interval); the sign convention (positive means longs pay shorts) is an ASSUMPTION, not verified without a login, so results are reported both ways (below).

## Universe (chosen on cost only; frozen in `PERPGEN_CUTS.json`)
**Cost rule:** median 1 minute spread (bps of mid) over the ticker's DISCOVERY bars at most **6.0 bps**, and at least 7 calendar days in its discovery third. Never any return. Result, 16 perps in two groups:
- **CRYPTO (10):** BTC 0.63, ETH 1.28, HYPE 1.92, SOL 2.72, SUI 3.40, XRP 3.50, DOGE 3.78, LTC 4.11, LINK 5.16, ZEC 5.17 bps. Excluded by cost: BCH 6.57, NEAR 7.15, kSHIB 8.17, ADA 9.88, BNB 9.72, VVV 10.63, WLD 13.63, AAVE 15.06, UNI 16.03 (and the inactive kPEPE).
- **NONCRYPTO (6):** Gold 0.47, US 500 0.95, Tech 100 1.09, Silver 1.51, Copper 4.05, WTI 4.03 bps. Excluded: Platinum 6.26, Palladium 7.65, Aluminum 8.10, US 2000 (inactive).
- Equities: excluded entirely (history since 2026-11-05; their forward ledger is PERPEQ's, in `workers/PERPEQ_COMMON.md`).
Rules are pooled and scored per GROUP (a trade is a (ticker, time) pair; trades of a group are pooled; days are distinct UTC entry dates). The two groups are scored separately and every (rule, group) pair counts once toward N.

## The date split, fixed now (UTC calendar days, per ticker)
For each ticker, calendar days from its first usable bar to 2026-11-05 are cut into thirds: **DISCOVERY = first third, VALIDATION = second third, HOLDOUT = last third** (a bar belongs to the day it starts in; exact dates per ticker in the report and `PERPGEN_CUTS.json`; for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP: discovery 2026-06-03 to 07-24, validation 07-25 to 09-14, holdout from 2026-09-15; gold and silver: 09-10 / 09-29 / 10-18; US 500: 10-06 / 10-16 / 10-26; Tech 100 and WTI: 10-14 / 10-21 / 10-29). The loader (`perpdata.py`) physically cannot return holdout dates: dev files hold discovery and validation only, the holdout rows are in a separate directory opened only by `load_holdout(ticker, frozen_id)`, which needs a hand-written guard file `data/perpgen/holdout_guard.json` naming the frozen id and tickers and refuses a second read of the same (id, ticker, kind) (a read-once record is written into the guard file). `load_stage(…, "validation", frozen_id=…)` refuses until `workers/PERPGEN_FROZEN_<id>.json` (the committed discovery survivor list) exists. `perpdata.py selftest` checks all of it. Cross-asset features at a timestamp use a leader's data only if that timestamp lies in the leader's own discovery or validation region (or, at a holdout read, in a holdout the guard unlocked for the same id); otherwise the feature is missing and the atom is false. This makes some leader and target pairs unevaluable (crypto leaders in the non-crypto targets' validation dates) and that is accepted.

## Features (fixed list; each computed at decision time t from candles whose end time is at or before t, mid = (bid close + ask close) / 2 on usable bars; a window needs at least 90 percent of its minutes present, else missing)
- **Returns** (log mid return, bps): r5, r15, r60, r240, r1440 (minutes).
- **Range and volatility:** vol60, vol240 (standard deviation of 1 minute mid log returns), volratio (vol60 over vol1440), rng60, rng240 ((max mid minus min mid) over mid).
- **Spread and cost:** sp (spread bps at t), spr (sp over the trailing 1,440 minute median spread).
- **Flow (no book sizes exist):** vlr15, vlr60 (log of notional volume in the window over its trailing 1,440 minute average per window), doi60, doi240 (log change in open interest), tvm (last trade price over mid minus 1, bps; last non-missing trade in the previous 5 minutes).
- **Funding** (last published rate at or after its `funding_time`, never earlier; bps): fr_last, fr_chg (last minus previous), fr_avg3 (mean of last 3).
- **Distance from rolling extremes:** dhi240, dlo240, dhi1440, dlo1440 (mid over rolling max or min of mid, minus 1, bps), pir1440 (position in the 1,440 minute range, 0 to 1).
- **Cross-asset leads** (leader return over the window ending at t, bps; a ticker does not lead itself): xbtc15, xbtc60, xbtc240, xeth60, xeth240, xgold60, xus500_60, and relative strength rel_btc60, rel_btc240 (own return minus BTC's).
- **Time** (categorical, equality atoms): hod4 (UTC hour block 0 to 5), dow (0 to 6), wkend (Saturday or Sunday UTC), mtf (minutes to the next funding time in buckets under 30, 30 to 120, 120 to 240, 240 and over).
33 numeric features, 4 categorical.

## The rule grammar
- **Decision times:** every UTC quarter hour (minutes 00, 15, 30, 45), for each ticker with a usable quote and all needed windows.
- **Atom:** numeric feature, operator (at least or at most), quantile level in {0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90}; the cut value is that quantile of the TICKER'S OWN DISCOVERY decision-time values (computed once from discovery rows, stored with its sha256, never re-estimated on validation or holdout). Categorical atom: feature equals a value. 330 numeric atoms plus 19 categorical.
- **Entry condition:** conjunction of 1, 2 or 3 atoms on distinct features (sampling probabilities 0.2, 0.4, 0.4).
- **Direction:** long or short (a rule is the pair; the reversed direction is its control, not a second rule).
- **Hold horizon** (fixed list): 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours.
- **One position per ticker:** a signal while that ticker's position is open is ignored (counted).
- **Fill and exit (latency of one minute, no look-ahead):** the decision uses bars ending at or before t; the entry fills at the CLOSE quote of the bar ending at t + 60 s (long at the ask, short at the bid); the exit at the opposite side's close quote of the bar ending at t + 60 s + horizon (first usable bar within the next 5 minutes, else the trade is void and counted). Trades whose entry or exit is outside the stage's region are dropped (counted). A trade whose fill quote has spread above 10 times the ticker's discovery median or above 100 bps is skipped as a defect (counted); this only removes trades.
- **Funding on held trades:** each funding settlement with `funding_time` in (entry time, exit time] pays or receives `rate` of notional: long pays when the rate is positive (ASSUMED sign). Reported three ways: price only, assumed sign, flipped sign. The main figure is the assumed sign.
- **Fee:** 12 bps of notional per side (0.24 percent per round trip; a placeholder from the repo's perps guide, NOT confirmed against Kalshi's fee page); also reported at 5 bps and 24 bps a side. The recorded bid and ask crossing is paid in addition (it is in the prices). Break-even fee per side is reported for every survivor.
- **Metrics:** per-trade net = side times (exit over entry minus 1) plus funding minus 2 times the fee. PF = sum of winning nets over absolute sum of losing nets. Day-clustered z = mean of per-date mean nets over (standard deviation of per-date means over the square root of the number of dates), date = UTC entry date.

## How a run is made, and how N is counted exactly
A RUN is one seed, one group, at least 5,000 DISTINCT new rules. Rules are drawn by `random.Random(seed)` (seed = 1000 + run number), canonicalised (atoms sorted by feature name, then `|dir|horizon`), and **the canonical string is appended to the run's ledger `workers/perpgen_ledger/RUN<nn>_<GROUP>.ids.gz` BEFORE the batch (500 rules) is scored, so a crash cannot lose a count**. A canonical string already in any earlier ledger is not scored again and not recounted. **N = the number of lines across all ledger files** (distinct (rule, group) pairs scored at any stage, discovery included, not only the ones that pass), so the ledger is the exact count, and the README entry of each run states the running total. The prior running N (about 730,489 per the lead's tally at registration) is added for the deflation. Runs may be many (the order is several hundred thousand rules in total); a run that scores fewer than 5,000 new distinct rules says so.

## Survivor bars (numeric, fixed before any run)
**Stage 1, discovery (per rule per group, discovery regions only, 12 bps a side).** All of: at least 200 trades; at least 20 distinct entry dates; PF at least 1.3; mean net above 0 at 24 bps a side; PF above 1.0 in BOTH halves (split at the median trade date); no single date supplies more than 25 percent of the net profit. Survivors are clustered (entry-set Jaccard at least 0.9 within group, direction and horizon: keep the best PF); the survivor list is frozen as `workers/PERPGEN_FROZEN_<id>.json` with its sha256 and committed and pushed BEFORE validation is read (`load_stage` enforces it). Rules that pass discovery are counted in N regardless.

**Stage 2, validation (frozen survivors only, read once, 12 bps).** All of: at least 100 trades; at least 10 distinct dates; PF at least 1.3 AND PF above 1.0 in both halves with mean net above 0 in both (split at the median trade date); PF at least 1.0 at 24 bps; the reversed-direction control on the same entries has PF under 1.0 and a lower mean net than the rule; the same direction and horizon with NO condition (the unconditional core) has a lower mean net than the rule, so the conditions add something. Survivors are then clustered at Jaccard 0.5 and the number of independent clusters is the K that is reported (51 GEN4 passes were two bets).

**Stage 3, audit (every validation survivor).** Pass all of A1 to A10 below, or it is dropped with the reason written.

**Stage 4, holdout (audit survivors only, read once).** Frozen as `workers/PERPGEN_HOLDOUT_FROZEN_<id>.json` with sha256, committed and pushed, then the guard file is written by hand and `load_holdout` reads each ticker once. A rule whose expected holdout trade count (validation trades per date times holdout dates, computed BEFORE the read) is under 60 is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA and its holdout is not read. Bars: at least 60 trades on at least 8 dates; PF at least 1.3 at 12 bps and at least 1.0 at 24 bps; both halves PF above 1.0 and mean above 0; control PF under 1.0. Under any of these: FALSIFIED. The holdout is never read for tuning, a failed read is final, and a rule is never re-registered to be re-read.

**Stage 5, deflation and labels.** `z_N = inverse normal CDF of 1 minus 0.05 / N_total` one-sided Bonferroni, N_total = the prior running N plus this protocol's ledger total at the read (about 5.27 at 730,489, 5.33 at 1,000,000, 5.36 at 1,200,000). A rule gets a label: **FALSIFIED** (fails any stage), **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** (a bar's sample size is not met and the part is unread), **LEAD** (passes stages 1 to 4 but its day-clustered z over ALL dates pooled, at 12 bps, is under z_N), **CANDIDATE** (passes stages 1 to 4, pooled z at least z_N, audit clean, a named counterparty, and registered with the forward ledger FWDLEDGER). A CANDIDATE permits only a forward paper test and a report to the owner; it never permits anything live. Also reported for information: z_K = deflation over the K clusters only.

## Audit checklist (the GEN4 lesson: every survivor is guilty until proved clean)
GEN4 produced 51 apparent survivors that were two bets sharing one core, and the earlier lookahead bugs in this repo (the confirmation-bar entry priced at the pre-confirmation level, the intrabar-high trailing stop) all flattered. So, per survivor:
- **A1 independent recompute:** a second, plain-loop implementation (no vectorised code shared with the generator) reproduces the trade count and total net to 1e-9, and 200 random decision times reproduce every feature value exactly.
- **A2 truncation test:** delete every bar after the decision time and recompute the entry decision and the fill price for 200 sampled trades: they must be identical (nothing in the decision reads the future).
- **A3 field audit:** the decision reads only close fields of bars ending at or before t (plus open, high, low of those same bars); no field of a later bar; the ask sentinel (9.2e14) never reaches a feature (a count of rows where a feature input was blanked).
- **A4 funding timing:** a feature using funding is re-run with the rate delayed by one funding interval; a PF that falls to under 1.0 on the delayed version is fine, but a PF that RISES when the rate is advanced by one interval (using the future) flags that the timing assumption matters and the rule is dropped unless it holds with the delay.
- **A5 latency:** the same trades with the fill 5 minutes later keep PF at least 1.0.
- **A6 date-shuffle placebo:** the feature series is permuted by whole UTC days (200 shuffles); the rule's PF must exceed the 95th percentile of the shuffled PFs, else the profit is the unconditional drift.
- **A7 beta check:** the buy-and-hold return of the group over the stage is reported; a long (short) rule whose net does not exceed the unconditional same-direction core by at least 5 bps a trade is dropped.
- **A8 concentration:** the top 3 dates supply at most 40 percent of net profit; no single ticker supplies more than 50 percent; PF at least 1.1 after removing each ticker in turn.
- **A9 defects:** removing every trade with a spread over 10 times median at the fill, or a missing neighbour minute before the fill, keeps PF at least 1.3.
- **A10 cost realism:** break-even fee per side at least 15 bps (the 12 bps placeholder must not be the thing the edge depends on); PF at the 24 bps stress at least 1.0; no size is assumed beyond one contract and the candle carries no touch size, so the capacity is UNKNOWN and said so.

## Predictions, written before any run
Discovery survivors under 1 percent of rules scored (at 12 bps a side plus the crossing, the cost of a round trip is 24 bps plus the spread against hold horizons of minutes to a day, so most rules lose); of the survivors, about 10 to 25 percent pass validation (GEN4's 45 percent was one shared core); zero or one cluster survives the audit; **zero CANDIDATES**. If a run contradicts this, suspect the data layer or the fill rule before believing it.

## What this registration does not do
No rule has been scored. No result of any generator is reported in this file. The first stage's only outputs are the data layer, `PERPDATA_REPORT.md` (counts, gaps, sentinels, spreads, funding calendar), the cuts and the universe.
