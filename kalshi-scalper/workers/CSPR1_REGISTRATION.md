# CSPR1 registration: crypto perp spread by hour, a cost map (Strategy Research V2 worker, 2026-11-06, written before any outcome was computed)
Discovery only; this is a cost map, not a trading rule. Code `workers/cryptoperp/cryptoperp.py`, committed before it is run.
## Duplicate check
CASHSPREAD1 (stock, index, metal perps around the US cash open and close) and COST1. Nobody mapped the crypto perp spread by hour of day. Not a duplicate.
**Mechanism and counterparty.** Kalshi crypto perp makers quote wider when the major venues are quiet (Asia night) or around US data; a taker who trades only in the cheapest hours pays less. Counterparty: the maker whose width changes by hour.
**Test.** Relative spread (ask minus bid over mid) of the universe tickers (valid quotes only) in six 4 hour UTC bins (00-04, 04-08, 08-12, 12-16, 16-20, 20-24; US Central is UTC minus 5 hours to Oct 31). Claim: the cheapest bin has median spread at most 0.75 times the overall median in BOTH halves AND the same bin is cheapest in both halves, with each bin holding at least 60 ticker-samples per half.
**Numeric prediction.** Cheapest bin about 0.85 times the overall median; claim fails about 65%.
**Kill criteria.** NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if any bin has under 60 ticker-samples in a half. FALSIFIED if counted enough and the claim fails. LEAD (a usable cost map, not an edge) if it holds in both halves; the held-out third is then read once and must show the same cheapest bin. Universe, cost fees and cut as below (fees unused here).
## Data, cut, universe, cost (stated before any return, premium or outcome was computed)
Kalshi side: `origin/perp-data` crypto-class rows only, de-duplicated by (ts, ticker), samples 3 to 7 a day (not hourly). **Development 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-31 (11 dates). D1 = to 10-26, D2 = 10-27 to 10-31. HELD OUT and never opened: 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-06 (last third by date; the scorer opens no file after the cut).** Disclosure: while learning the schema I printed the header and the crypto rows of the 2026-11-06 file (bid, ask, price, reference, open interest, one snapshot of 20 coins); no return, premium or spread statistic was computed from it. Sentinel screen from FUNDP1: a quote is valid only if status active, bid and ask above 0 and ask at most twice the bid (int64 no-ask sentinel).
Other venues (public, keyless; sandbox hosts checked: Coinbase, Bitstamp, Hyperliquid reachable; Binance returns 451, unused): Coinbase 1 minute spot candles (Bitstamp as fallback) and Hyperliquid perp 5 minute candles, each at the sample time (last complete candle, so up to 5 minutes stale for Hyperliquid; stated limitation).
Universe chosen on COST only: crypto perps active in at least 80% of development samples that have a spot product, ranked by median recorded relative spread, the 10 cheapest. Kalshi price units differ from spot by a power of ten per ticker; the power is set from the median ratio to spot (a unit, not a return); a ticker whose ratio is not within 50% of a power of ten is dropped and reported.
Cost: a long buys at the recorded ask and a sell is at the recorded bid, at entry and exit (the spread is paid in full) plus a fee per side of **0.05% (verdict)** and **0.12% (stress, placeholder, the real Kalshi perp fee is not in the data)**. Funding is NOT in the data (no funding field) and is ignored (holds are under 12 hours); stated.
Day clustering: z is the mean of per-day means over its standard error across days. N: +1 for this test (one rule, direction and threshold fixed). Running count before this worker about 730,471, deflated z bar about 5.27.
Premium reference level: a perp can sit at a standing basis to spot (USD versus USDC, funding), so the signal is the premium minus the EXPANDING median of that ticker's own earlier premiums (needs 8 earlier samples; no later data used).
## Common verdict rules
NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if development has fewer than 100 trades or 20 trading days; mean, halves, z are then information only and no FALSIFIED is declared. FALSIFIED if at least 100 trades and 20 days and the verdict-fee mean or either half or the stress mean is at or under 0. LEAD only if at least 100 trades, 20 days, verdict mean above 0 in both halves, stress mean above 0 and day z at least 5.27; the held-out third is then read once. **Date it becomes enough:** about 20 dates are needed before a fresh cut; at the recorder rate (about 4.6 samples a day) that is about 2026-11-20 with a new cut, and 100 trades needs the same order.
