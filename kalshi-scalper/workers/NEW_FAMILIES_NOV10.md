All: cheap side only (ask 3c to 30c), universe fixed before entry, registered before any outcome is read.

1. AAA gas price ladders (KXAAAGAS*, weekly/monthly/annual, Economics) against a persistence model on EIA retail gasoline prices. (1) A strike ladder on a slow, autocorrelated price. (2) Free and keyless: EIA weekly retail prices (EIA is reachable from here, as in FUT1) plus Kalshi /historical; about 1,000 candle requests, 1 to 2 runner hours. (3) Retail bets on direction and round numbers; makers quote wings loosely on a series that moves a few cents a week. (4) The model gives a probability for each strike; only YES strikes at 3c to 30c where the model probability exceeds price plus fee count (the opposite side would be the favourite side, which is not allowed). If the model never exceeds the price, it fails. Honest prior 8%. (5) History readable in 1 day.
2. Mentions markets (KXEARNINGSMENTION*, KXTRUMPSAY*, KXPORTERMENTION and similar, 457 series). (1) "Will X say word Y in this event". (2) Free: Kalshi /historical, a hash-sampled 400 markets, about 1,200 requests, 1 to 2 hours; no transcript source needed for the first pass (calibration by series group only). (3) Retail overpays for dramatic or topical words and underpays for routine ones. (4) If routine words (company name, product, "guidance") sit at 10c to 30c while saying them is near certain, cheap-side YES in those groups pays above fee; I do not know that this is true. Prior 10%. (5) 1 day, then a forward week of earnings events.
3. Cheap wings of monthly and weekly commodity, index and foreign-index ladders (KXNATGASMON, KXSOYBEANW, KXCOFFEEEOY, KXNIFTYL, KXKOSPI, KXRUTPOS). (1) Kalshi-only calibration of wing strikes and ladder monotonicity, no outside data. (2) Free, about 1,500 requests, 2 hours. (3) Makers on thin series quote wings with a flat haircut regardless of the horizon left. (4) Long-dated wings carry a fixed premium; a wing that is too high AND cheap enough to buy is the only way to win and my earlier ladder tests (HISTLAD1, LADCONV1, WX3) found none on BTC and weather, so the prior is 5%. (5) 1 day.
4. Deribit implied digital against Kalshi hourly BTC and ETH ladders (KXBTCD, KXETHD). (1) Keyless public option chains give a call-spread digital probability for "above K at the hour"; compare with the Kalshi price and trade only a tail where Kalshi is lower. (2) Free, forward only (no free option history): a poller every 5 minutes for 2 weeks, about 5 runner minutes a day. (3) Retail and slow makers on Kalshi versus an options market that prices volatility. (4) Gap only if Kalshi misprices vol, and HISTLAD1 and LADCONV1 already priced these ladders from realized vol and failed: prior 5%. (5) 2 weeks.
5. Rotten Tomatoes score ladders (RT* series, Entertainment). (1) "Score above X%" before release, scored from the public review count and average as reviews accrue. (2) Needs a free RT page read; reachability not checked (one request will tell), forward only, runner minutes small. (3) Retail anchors on trailers and hype, not the review-count curve. (4) Early scores are volatile, so a cheap tail can be right more often than 3c to 30c implies; no evidence yet. Prior 5%. (5) 3 weeks (few releases per week).
