## Addition, 21:23Z (committed before the code change): the EST bound
US daylight saving time starts on the second Sunday of March: **2027-03-14** (Sundays in March 2027: the 7th and the 14th), not March 7. So Eastern is EST (UTC-5) through 2027-03-14T06:59Z, and every UTC day up to and including **2027-03-13** is entirely EST. The script's upper bound moves from 2027-03-06 to **2027-03-13**; a day on or after 2027-03-14 REFUSES. Test: day 2027-03-13 (a Saturday, with the same table keyed by weekday 5: no hours, so 0 requests) is accepted; day 2027-03-14 REFUSES; and a day in EDT would have built a ticker an hour off (a real 09:30 ET close at 13:30Z would be named 0830 in EST: a ticker that does not exist for that window), which is why the refusal exists. The lower bound 2026-11-14 is unchanged (EST began 2026-11-01).
