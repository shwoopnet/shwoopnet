Rule (Main, 21:13Z): the candle job is written as files of at most 1,000 requests each (expect two), requests in chronological order of the close, request ids numbered across the whole job (c0001, c0002, ...), each file with its own ticker_allowlist (only the tickers of its requests), max_requests = its own request count, job_id OICAND_CANDLES_<k>.
