# EARNPR1 registration (2026-11-04), written and pushed before any price, candle or outcome was joined to any press release

**Discovery lane, research only. No live code, no order, no promote verb.** Owner rules obeyed: cheap side only (ask 3c to 30c); no favourite-and-hold; no 15 minute crypto or gold trigger. **Arm B is a cheap-side trade, not a favourite hold:** it buys the NO side only when its ask is 3c to 30c, i.e. when the YES bid is at least 70c. The favourite (YES, 70c or more) is never bought; Arm B pays 3c to 30c for the unlikely outcome (the word is NOT said) and wins only if the market's favourite is wrong.

EARN1: numeric company metrics, NOT_RUNNABLE. **Difference in one paragraph:** MENT1 priced a word from how often it was said on earlier calls; it never saw what the company had just said. EARNPR1 uses a different information source that exists before the call: the company's own earnings press release (8-K Item 2.02, Ex-99.1), filed on average 3 to 5 hours before the call, whose text says which topic words the company has already put in writing. It is a text fact, not a quote pattern, joined at the word level, and it trades only the cheap side (3c to 30c), whereas MENT1 traded any price (fair minus ask at least 8c, with many favourites). The base-rate model of MENT1 is not reused; the model here is conditioned on the release text.

## Rules (fixed; not changed after the result)
1. **Text rule.** Release text = Ex-99.1 of the matched 8-K (HTML stripped, lowercase, curly quotes straight, entities decoded, punctuation to a space). The Kalshi word is split on "/" into alternatives; a word is IN the release if any alternative occurs as a whole word or phrase, with an optional plural "s" or "es" (or possessive 's) after it. Stem key for pooling = first alternative, lowercase, one trailing "s" dropped for words longer than 3 letters not ending in "ss". Code `src/scalper/earnpr1.py` (`clean_text`, `in_release`, `stem_of`), tests in `tests/test_earnpr1.py`.
2. **Look.** The last valid two-sided candle (0 < bid < ask < 1) of the market whose end is at least 10 minutes after the filing acceptance and at least 10 minutes before the call start, on MENT1's cached hourly candles (reused, `data/ment1/candles_*.json`; the quote is the close of that hour). No new price fetch. A market with no such candle is not tradable (counted as no quote).
3. **Model (FIT only).** For each stem and each cell (word in release / not): rate = (k + 2 g) / (n + 2) where n, k are the FIT markets of that stem and cell and g is the pooled all-words rate of the cell ((k + 0.5) / (n + 1)); a stem with fewer than 5 FIT markets in the cell uses g. Arm A model = rate(stem, in release) = P(YES); Arm B model = 1 minus rate(stem, not in release) = P(NO).
4. **Arm A:** word in release, YES ask 3c to 30c, model at least 3 times the ask: buy YES at the ask. **Arm B:** word not in release (Ex-99.1 available), YES bid at least 70c so the NO ask (1 minus the YES bid) is 3c to 30c, model at least 3 times the NO ask: buy NO at its ask. At most 2 per event per arm (highest model over ask), 10 contracts each, held to settlement, taker fee 0.07 p (1 - p) per contract, stress fees x1.2.
5. **Scored on TEST only** (the model never sees TEST outcomes). FIT is reported as in-sample information, never judged. Halves D1/D2 of TEST by event date.
6. **Control:** every cheap side in TEST with a valid look quote and no text condition (every YES with ask 3c to 30c, for Arm A; every NO with ask 3c to 30c, for Arm B), plus the within-event untreated cheap sides. The statistic is treated mean net per contract minus control mean net, event-clustered.
7. **Kill rules (per arm, as in the brief):** `NOT_RUNNABLE` if filing-to-call lead median under 20 minutes or under 40% of events have a quote (neither holds, see census); `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` if the arm has under 150 treated trades or under 60 events; `FALSIFIED` if profit factor under 1.5 after fee, or net not above 0 in both halves D1 and D2, or treated mean not above control mean; otherwise `NOT_YET_FALSIFIED` (needs the forward read). The deflated bar is z 5.27 at N about 730,413 for any claim of a find.
8. **Numeric prediction (the brief's, for the whole pre-cut period):** Arm A 120 to 300 trades, win 25% to 45% at 12c to 20c, PF 0.9 to 1.3; Arm B 30 to 100 trades, PF 0.7 to 1.2; P(the quote already priced it) 60%, P(pass) 6%. **My prediction for TEST only (a third of the events and the hourly grid):** Arm A 30 to 120 trades, Arm B 15 to 70, so most likely `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` on count; PF below 1.3 on both; the cheap-side baseline is -5.5c to -6.2c per contract (RULES1, MECH1S), so the treatment must lift the win rate by at least 6c per contract to matter.
9. **Forward plan if NOT_ENOUGH_DATA:** the same rules, model frozen on all pre-cut events, on events from 2026-10-04 to 2026-12-15, read once on 2026-12-16; treated trades per week estimated from the TEST result. N: +4 (Arm A and Arm B at 2 each; running count about 730,413 plus 4).
