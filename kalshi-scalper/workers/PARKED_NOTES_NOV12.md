## Doubles at one set all (parked by the Head of R&D, 19:13Z: "parked, correctly ... No card")

**The fact:** in ITF, challenger and tour doubles the deciding "set" is a first-to-10 match tiebreak, not a full set. A short tiebreak pulls a favourite's chance toward 50%: a pair with a 60% chance of a full set has about 55% in the tiebreak, and a pair with 75% has about 64% (arithmetic from a per-point model; not measured on any data).

**The idea:** if the in-play price at one set all treats the decider as a full set, the underdog pair is about 5 to 11 points too cheap at that moment. The other side would be a maker or bettor using a singles-style model.

**Events:** about 117 doubles matches a day on Nov 10 (ITF men 59, ITF women 47, challenger 7, tour 4); perhaps a third reach one set all.

**Why it is parked:** the state "one set all" cannot be read from Kalshi alone. Kalshi lists set-winner markets for tour-level SINGLES only; no doubles market settles at a set. The price path does not mark set ends.

**What it needs before it can be a card:** a live or after-the-fact set-by-set score source for ITF and challenger doubles whose terms were READ and allow automated reads. None is on the permitted list today, and every score site this desk knows of is barred by the repo gate or has unread terms. If Kalshi ever lists doubles set markets, the state becomes readable from Kalshi alone and this note becomes a MAPBREAK-style card.
