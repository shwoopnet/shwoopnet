# ECONCENSUS: census of the economics release ladders and hourly city temperature (Desk B, Nov 15, 2026; the Head's item 3, scope ruled 14:44Z)

**Purpose.** Count, from Kalshi's own public settled markets, how many settled markets and how many release dates each macro release series has, and how many hourly city temperature markets, BEFORE any test is registered. The Head: "No test is registered until the count supports one." Families to look at later (not tested here): underdog bias, overreaction, late drift by settlement, from the market itself (no forecast against the market: declined; no Synoptic read; daily high and low temperature and rain stay closed).

**Job.** `desk_b_jobs/ECONCENSUS_LISTS.json` (47 requests, at most 137 pages, cap 149, one every 5.0 s, about 13 minutes), built by `build_econcensus_job.py`; the runner's own `check_job` and `dry_run` accept it (checked on this branch). One request `/series?category=Economics` (catches series Forensics' 224 rows missed: that source answer was truncated at the runner's 5 MB cap after NASDAQ100U, so 224 is a floor); then one settled list per series of the 10 big ones (4 pages), 32 smaller ones (2 pages) and the four hourly temperature series (8 pages, 30 days of closes). A series name that does not exist returns an empty list. Stage 2 (any series the /series answer shows that this list lacks) is a second small job built from that answer.

**Held out.** A FIXED date constant: closes at or after 2026-10-17T00:00:00Z are never requested (every /markets request carries `max_close_ts` = 1792195199). The settled lists do carry a result for every market in range, so the landed run will hold results of EVERY card's windows before 2026-10-17 that touch these series; it goes into HELDOUT_PULL_RUNS.md in the landing pull request, and the readers are the census count table only (the count reads tickers, close times, volume and last price, not results). Cards with held-out windows on these series (EVENT1, ECONLAG, ECONFADE) must be checked by Main against the cut; if one holds out dates before 2026-10-17, the run date range is cut again before dispatch.

**What the census prints.** Per series: settled markets, distinct close dates (release dates), median volume, and the price levels at close (distribution of the last price in 10c bands) so the count says whether an underdog or late-drift test could have 100 independent releases. Counts only. No result is read for the table.

**Not covered:** international and state series (list rows exist for counts only), series needing candles, the hourly temperature's later stages.
