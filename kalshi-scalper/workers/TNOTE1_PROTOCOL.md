# TNOTE1 (registered Nov 10, 2026, 3:50 PM CT, before any KXTNOTED candle is fetched and before any ladder-derived yield is computed): daily 10-year Treasury yield ladder vs persistence
Research only, public keyless Kalshi data, no order. Cheap side only (YES ask 3c to 30c). Not favourite-and-hold. Probed first: KXTNOTED has 9,218 settled markets over 664 days (2023-02-14 to 2026-07-01), only in /historical (no live settled markets after 2026-07-01); rule "yield curve par rate for 10-year U.S. treasury note above x % on date D", strikes on a 0.01 grid, close 19:25Z (3:25 PM ET) on D. FRED was unreachable from here (connection failed), so the information source is built from the settled ladders themselves, as in AAAGAS1 (no outside data): each settled day's ladder brackets the yield v_D between the highest YES-settled strike and the lowest NO-settled strike; v_D = their midpoint.
Model fixed now: decision = the candle ending closest to 20 h before the close (the previous day's par yield is known by then; the market already sees the overnight move, which the model cannot). mean = v_{D-1}; sd = max(0.02, sample sd of the previous 20 daily changes v_d - v_{d-1}). P(v_D > K) = 1 - Phi((K - mean)/sd). One variant (persistence), 1 cell. 
Universe fixed before any outcome: days from 2026-01-01 to 2026-07-01 (earlier days supply the sd), strikes with v_{D-1} - 0.02 <= K <= v_{D-1} + 0.10 on the 0.01 grid (13 strikes a day, about 1,600 markets).
Entry: buy YES at the ask when 0.03 <= ask <= 0.30, spread <= 0.05 and model P > ask + fee + 0.02 (fee = ceil(0.07*C*p*(1-p)*100)/C/100, C = 10); hold to settlement, one contract; the NO side is the favourite side and is not tested. Cash PF first, mean cents per contract, event-clustered z (cluster = day); development = first two thirds of scored days; the last third read ONCE only if development shows PF >= 1.5 with n >= 100 trades on >= 30 days and z at or above the bar. N about 5,187 (z bar about 4.27).
Prediction before running: fails (prior 4%): the market sees the live yield and the overnight move, the model sees only yesterday's close; AAAGAS1 (the same design on a slow price) lost with PF 0.47 to 0.49.
