Directive (main, owner, Nov 9): scan single legs on new series; research only; free public Kalshi /historical; no order, no key.
Series, fixed now (existence checked with a 5-market listing only): KXFEDDECISION, KXFED (Fed rate), KXCPIYOY (inflation), KXPAYROLLS (jobs), KXGDP, KXINX (S&P 500 ranges), KXNASDAQ100, KXWTI (oil), KXGAS (gasoline), KXTOPSONG (music). Selection: all settled markets of each series from /historical, then at most 300 per series chosen by md5 of the ticker (never by a price or an outcome); hourly candles over the 30 hours before each market's close (`src/scalper/newser1.py`, DB `data/newser1.sqlite`, same schema as `nflpc1.sqlite`).
Test: SINGLELEG1's rule unchanged (`leg1.py`, decision quote 4 hours before close, spread at most 5c, C = 10 fee, series x side x band cells, first two thirds of each series by close time development, last third held out and read once for shortlisted cells, event-clustered z): shortlist 100 development markets in 30 events, net edge >= 2c, z >= the quantile for 0.05/N. Potential cells 10 series x 2 sides x 10 bands = 200; N = 4,500 (single-leg ledger so far) + 200 = 4,700 (z bar about 4.27). The 24-hour quote is NOT run (SINGLELEG2 showed quote time does not matter).
Prediction (before anything is fetched): zero shortlisted cells (about 93%). Econ and rates markets are priced off consensus and futures; their quotes are likely sharp; the counterparty (retail on round milestones) is thin in these series. NOT_ENOUGH_DATA is likely for several series (settled counts under 100 development markets).
Fetch: one loop, about 3,000 requests at about 4 seconds, restarted as needed.
