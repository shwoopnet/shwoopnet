## Common verdict rules
Per-trade net = gross minus recorded crossing (buy at ask, sell at bid) minus 0.24% round trip per two-leg round trip (0.48% for four legs). PF = sum of wins over sum of losses. Control = the same entries with the direction reversed.
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA:** fewer than 100 development trades or fewer than 30 trading-date clusters at the read date. The date the bar would be met at the observed rate is reported.
- **FALSIFIED:** at least 100 trades on at least 30 clusters, and any of: PF under 1.5 in either half, net per trade not above 0 in both halves, PF under 1.2 at fee x1.2, trigger not better than control, day-clustered z under 5.27.
- **LEAD:** all pass; then the held-out part is read once and PF under 1.5 there is FALSIFIED.
Bars are expected to be out of reach in the development window (event counts below); the honest output is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA plus the information figures, and the recorder request.
