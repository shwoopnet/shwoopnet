# Maker (resting) entries: research estimate and the cheapest first test (Nov 10, 2026, 2:10 PM CT). Cash basis, unrounded fee, no slip. Script `src/scalper/maker_est.py`; proxy only (candles, not the trade tape).
Fees (what is verified): Kalshi docs (fee_rounding page, read Nov 10) say the trade fee is rounded up per FILL to $0.000001, with a rounding fee and a rebate so the NET fee is close to the model fee (aligned to 0.01c for direct members, 1c for others). The taker formula 0.07 x C x p x (1-p) and a maker coefficient of 0.0175 on some series are from third-party sources only (web search); the official fee-schedule PDF returned 429 and was not read. Most sources say most series have NO maker fee. So maker fee = 0 and 0.0175 are used as bounds.

Reading: the maker fee saves 0.1c to 0.5c per contract, but a resting order is adversely selected: the signals it does NOT fill are the winners (taker PF 3.5 to 10 on the missed ones), so per signal the maker path earns LESS than taking (ETH FAV6: taker +1.00c; maker about 0.77 x 0.75c = +0.58c per signal at fee 0.0175). A fill proxy from candles is an upper bound on the fill rate, so the real result is no better. Cheap tails stay below 1.0 either way (0.73 and 0.78 at best).
Cheapest first test, MAKERSHADOW1 (shadow only, no order): every shadow signal row (GOLDALL and the batch 2 rules) also records: the touch at the decision (our bid for YES, the NO bid), whether the public candles or trades show the market traded through it within 3 minutes after the decision and before 2 minutes to the close, the fill time, and the settlement. Read at 150 signals per rule: the maker path is better only if its mean cents PER SIGNAL (an unfilled signal counts 0) at fee 0.0175 beats the taker path's mean per signal and its filled-trades PF is >= 1.3. Prior of a pass 10% (history says no; gold may differ). Count 1 per rule when read.
Other structural fee levers: (a) rounding: with the per-fill rebate, 10 contracts vs 1 should matter little for the net fee; unverified for our account type (aligned to 1c for non-direct members) so check one real fill's net fee before assuming. (b) price: fee = 0.07 p (1-p) is 0.2c at 97c, 0.52c at 92c, 0.63c at 10c, but relative to the winning margin it is 7% of p (6.4% at 92c, 6.8% at 97c), so a higher favourite price does NOT lower the fee burden per cent of margin. (c) two-leg round trips: every early exit pays a second fee (the 25c exit watch, CHEAPEXIT1 showed exits are worse than holding). Not a lever that adds PF 1.3.
