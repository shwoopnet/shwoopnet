Status of all three: **UNPROVEN**. A shadow is a forward paper record. It places no order, holds no key, makes no Kalshi write, and nothing here promotes anything (no promote verb). Owner order (via main, Nov 12): "put the nearest leads on the SHADOW list as forward records with no money".

## Rules common to all three
- **Fee** for every taker leg: 0.07 x p x (1 - p) per contract, cash, unrounded (cents). Maker legs (MMSIM1 only): reported at 0 and at 0.0175 x p x (1 - p), as registered. Size: **1 contract**.
- **Forward only.** Each shadow has a date constant (below). Only decisions timestamped on or after it exist in the ledger. Data used in development, and the held-out windows that the existing read-once markers guard, are never re-scored as shadow evidence (the exact non-overlap line is in each section).
- **Price bands.** CFRTI1-BTC is cheap (0.04 to 0.30 ask). W-SPOTLEAD1 is mid (0.50 to 0.87 ask, inside the 0.30 to 0.88 mid band). MMSIM1 is a two-sided quote and is not a single-price rule: see its section (its registered rule is scored as registered; the band is applied as a reported subset, never as a change to the rule).
- **No live figures.** Ledgers hold market and spot data only (public), never an account balance, order, position or live P&L. Gold L1 and OICORE correlations come from the nightly report outside this repo and are not copied in.
- **Where written.** New data branch `shadow-data` (never main, never published with the site; the same pattern as `quote-data` and `perp-data`), files `shadow/<lead>/<date>.csv.gz`, one row per decision, appended once a day. Writer: a Research V2 script worker (a new `src/scalper/shadowlog.py`, built after the owner approves this file; it only reads the `quote-data` branch and public candles and derives rows deterministically from recorded data). **Public data only, no key, no order, no Kalshi write.** A committed marker line per lead (same `readonce.py` pattern) guards each scoring read.
- **Bars used.** The project bars as they exist: cash PF, day-clustered z, "FALSIFIED needs about 100 trades, otherwise 'no evidence at n = x'". Multiple-testing: these three leads are 3 more tries, so the shadow z bar is the Bonferroni one-sided z of **2.13** (0.05 / 3) on the day-clustered mean, in addition to PF >= 1.3 and a positive mean in each half. This is a bar for "go back to the owner as a probe spec", not a proof, and a pass is a prompt to re-test, never a result (POWER2 note on CFRTI1: a no-edge rule passes a bar like this about 24% of the time at n 50).
- **Reading a shadow early is not allowed.** The scoring script refuses before its date constant and refuses a second scoring read by the committed marker. Counting rows per day for the coverage check is allowed; P&L is not computed in that check.

## 1. MMSIM1 shadow (hourly Bitcoin ladder KXBTCD, two-sided resting quotes)
### (1) Fixed rule (exactly the registered simulation, nothing re-tuned)
- Universe: each KXBTCD hourly event, the 7 strikes nearest the money as the book recorder (scripts/record_book.py) records them. Window T-50 min to T-15 min, T = the event close.
- Entry: on each of YES and NO for each strike, if no resting order or inventory on that side, post ONE contract at that moment's best bid of that side and join the back of the queue (queue ahead = visible size at that price). Never repriced; it stays until filled or T-15 min.
- Fill: YES bid at p fills when cumulative contracts traded at p by takers selling YES (taker side "no" at yes_price p) after the post time reach queue ahead plus 1; a NO bid at q fills when cumulative contracts at yes_price 1 - q with taker side "yes" reach queue ahead plus 1. Fills are timed to the poll that shows them (30 s tape). Cancels ahead are ignored.
- Exit: at T-15 min cancel unfilled orders; a strike with one filled side sells it at the best bid of that side then (taker, fee 0.07 x p x (1-p)); a strike with BOTH sides filled is held to settlement and pays exactly 1.
- Band: the rule is scored as registered (all prices) and that is the primary figure. Reported beside it, descriptive only: the subset of fills whose price is in the mid band 0.30 to 0.88. A cheap-band subset is reported too. Neither can rescue a miss of the primary.
- Primary report is 1 contract UNROUNDED at maker 0.0175 (the registered bound); also reported: maker 0, 1 contract rounded up to the cent, 10 contracts rounded, as in Amendment 1.
### (2) What is recorded
- Per (event, strike, side) quoting decision, including those where nothing filled or the book was unquotable: `event, strike, side, post_ts_recv_ms, post_price, queue_ahead, filled (0/1), fill_ts_recv_ms (the 30 s poll that showed it), flatten_ts_recv_ms, flatten_price, held_to_settlement (0/1), result, fractional_size_fill_share (Amendment 2)`. Also one row per skipped opportunity with a reason (`no_book`, `side_already_held`, `missing_poll`).
- Timestamps: the receive time written on each book/tape row by the recorder (`t_recv`), plus the server Date header the recorder already stores, so clock skew is measurable. Decisions use receive time only; a row whose Date-header skew exceeds 5 s is flagged, not dropped.
- Code: **no new recorder.** `.github/workflows/record-book.yml` and `scripts/record_book.py` already write the book and tape to `quote-data`. New code is only the ledger writer `shadowlog.py` (mode `mmsim1`) which reuses the simulation logic of `src/scalper/mmsim1.py` unchanged, plus a refusal on any change to that logic (hash of the rule constants committed with this file). Built by a V2 script worker after approval. Public data, no key, no order.
### (3) Kill and return
- Date constant: **SHADOW_START_MMSIM1 = 2026-11-24T00:00:00Z.** Events closing before it are not shadow evidence: those from 2026-11-07T15:00Z are the MMSIM1 held-out set (read once on or after 2026-11-24, five of them already spent). The shadow scored set is events closing at or after the constant, so it does not overlap development or the held-out read. (The decision log may start earlier for coverage checks; nothing before the constant is scored.)
- KILL: at n >= 400 shadow events, PF (primary) under 1.0 AND mean per event under 0 = DROPPED, labelled "no evidence at n = x", not FALSIFIED. FALSIFIED needs about 100 trades in the usual sense; for an event-level statistic with sd about 42c per event we require n >= 1,000 events for the word FALSIFIED (PF under 1.0 and day-clustered z below 0). Also killed if the real-vs-sim check fails (below).
- Reality check (diagnostic only, not a kill by itself): the share of simulated fills that Kalshi's tape confirms within one poll, and the fractional-size share, are reported next to every PF.
- RETURN to a probe spec (owner popup, separate; a live version is NOT approved by this file): n >= 1,000 shadow events, PF >= 1.3 at the maker bound, positive mean in each third, day-clustered z >= 2.13, daily correlation with gold under 0.3 once 32 overlap days exist. Otherwise at the date cap **2027-02-28**: NO CALL, back to the research queue.
- Read-once marker for the scoring read: `SHADOW-MARKER MMSIM1: not yet read; first read allowed on or after 2026-12-14 (about 400 events); after the read: READ ON <date>, commit <hash>`. A second interim read at 1,000 events is a separate marker line added before it.
### (4) Earliest date and power
At about 20 to 24 events a day (recorder gaps lose some), 400 shadow events are reached about **2026-12-14**, 1,000 about **2027-01-12**. Honest power: development per-event mean about +3.7c at the bound, sd 41.7c (z per event 0.089 x sqrt(n)); the selection-shrunk estimate (PF about 1.14) is about half. At n = 400 the unshrunk expectation is z about 1.8 and the shrunk one about 0.9, so power against z 2.13 is about 0.4 unshrunk and under 0.15 shrunk. At n = 1,000 it is about 0.9 and 0.4. POWER2 already warns that the old PF 1.3 bar sits above the shrunk estimate; the 400-event read can only kill or say "no call", it cannot confirm.
### (5) Label: UNPROVEN. A simulation on queue-model fills; no real fill has been observed.

## 2. CFRTI1-BTC shadow (settlement-index replication, Bitcoin 15 minute, cheap side)
### (1) Fixed rule (constants of src/scalper/cfrti1.py, no tuning)
- Market: KXBTC15M only. Look: close - 120 s. One decision per window.
- Reference: the average of the Bitcoin prices from Coinbase, Kraken and Bitstamp, each the last tick at or before the look within 10 s, **receive time (`t_recv_ms`) for every venue**; all three venues must contribute in the run folder or the folder is flagged and skipped (logged as `venues_lost`).
- Sigma: standard deviation of 60 ten-second log returns of that index before the look, scaled by sqrt(8). Model p(YES) = Phi(ln(S/K)/sd), K = the quote row's strike.
- Quote must be the last row at or before the look within 3 s, status active, spread at most 0.03.
- Entry: buy the **cheap side** only (ask 0.03 to 0.30 as in the script; the shadow band is cheap, 0.04 to 0.30, rows at 0.03 are logged but not scored) when model p(side) - ask(side) >= **0.05**. Side = the cheap side (YES if its ask is in the band, else NO at 1 - yes_bid). 1 contract. Hold to settlement. Fee 0.07 x p x (1 - p) on the entry only.
### (2) What is recorded
- For EVERY BTC window with a readable quote (acted on or not): `ticker, close_time, look_t_send_ms (quote), spot_t_recv_ms per venue, index S, strike K, sd, model p_yes, cheap side, ask, edge, acted (edge >= 0.05), result`. The windows below 0.05 are the signals not acted on and carry the same fields, so the filter itself can be checked on forward data.
- Timestamps: quotes by `t_send_ms`, spot by `t_recv_ms`; the three exchange time formats are never used for the cut.
- Code: **no new recorder.** `scripts/record_quotes.py` (`record-quotes.yml`) already writes `quotes.csv`, `spot.csv`, `results.csv` to `quote-data`. New code: `shadowlog.py` mode `cfrti1` that reuses cfrti1.py's computation unchanged and writes the ledger above. Built by a V2 script worker. Public data, no key, no order.
### (3) Kill and return
- Date constant: **SHADOW_FIRST_DAY_CFRTI1 = 2026-11-23**, or the day after the single held-out read if that read happens later, set by a committed amendment before any shadow scoring. The held-out (days from 2026-11-05, read once at 50 rows, allowed from 2026-11-20) is a separate test; shadow rows are strictly later days, so they neither overlap development nor the held-out read.
- KILL: if the held-out read FAILS, CFRTI1-BTC is closed (its own pre-registered verdict) and this shadow stops. Otherwise, at n >= 50 shadow edge rows: PF under 1.0 and mean under 0 = DROPPED ("no evidence at n = 50"). FALSIFIED needs n >= 100 shadow rows with PF under 1.0 and mean under 0.
- RETURN: n >= 100 shadow edge rows, PF >= 1.3, mean > 0, day-clustered z >= 2.13, and a positive mean in each half = a probe spec for the owner (separate popup; the rule risks 33% win rate with loss = cheap price, no live use implied). At the date cap **2027-01-31** with neither = NO CALL, back to the research queue.
- Read-once marker for the scoring read: `SHADOW-MARKER CFRTI1-BTC: not yet read; first read allowed on or after 2026-12-10; after the read: READ ON <date>, commit <hash>`.
### (4) Earliest date and power
About 3 edge rows a day, so 50 shadow rows about **2026-12-10** and 100 about **2026-12-26** (the first-day start is about Nov 23). Honest power: the development cell was picked after the series split (z 2.11 does not count); development mean +15.6c, win 33%, per-trade sd about 46c. If the shrunk mean is about +5c: at n = 50 expected z about 0.8 (power against 2.13 about 0.1), at n = 100 expected z about 1.1 (power about 0.15). If the full +15.6c were real: n = 50 z about 2.4 (power about 0.6), n = 100 z about 3.4 (power about 0.95). A pass is therefore a prompt, a fail closes it only after 100 rows.
### (5) Label: UNPROVEN. One cell found after looking at five series.

## 3. W-SPOTLEAD1 shadow (Refinement's lead: spot-vs-strike probability against the book, 15 minute Bitcoin and Ethereum, mid band)
### (1) Fixed rule (Refinement's registration of 2026-11-11, items 1 to 8, MAIN mode as scored)
- Markets: KXBTC15M and KXETH15M. Look L = close - 180 s. One decision per window per asset.
- Strike proxy K = open of the Coinbase 1 minute candle starting at close - 900 s. Spot S = close of the Coinbase 1 minute candle ending exactly at L (the registered deviation note: Kraken ticks only as a cross-check). sigma = sd of the 60 most recent 1 minute Coinbase log returns with candle close at or before L; sd_remaining = sigma x sqrt(3); p_yes = Phi(ln(S/K)/sd_remaining); p_no = 1 - p_yes. Kalshi's own strike is logged when the market object carries it, but the proxy decides the trade.
- Book: asks from the R6 2 second book: YES ask = 1 - best NO bid; NO ask = 1 - best YES bid. Execution row = the last book row (receive time) at or before L + 2 s.
- Entry: buy the side with edge = p_side - ask_side >= **0.10**, only if its ask is in **[0.50, 0.87]** (mid band; the 88c to 97c favourite band is excluded). One trade per window per asset; if both sides qualify, the larger edge. 1 contract, hold to settlement (Kalshi's recorded result), fee 0.07 x p x (1 - p) on entry.
- Not allowed to change: edge 0.10, look 180 s, band, sigma window. The registered sensitivities (edge 0.05 and 0.15, look 120 s, sd x1.5, BTC alone, ETH alone) are reported as descriptive and cannot rescue a miss.
### (2) What is recorded
- Per window per asset, acted on or not: `ticker, close_time, L, S, K_proxy, K_kalshi_if_present, sigma, p_yes, yes_ask, no_ask, book_row_t_recv_ms (and its lag from L + 2 s), edge_yes, edge_no, acted, side, ask, result`. Windows with no book row within 10 s of L + 2 s are logged `no_book` (never silently dropped, since a missing book is how the 2 s versus 10 s gap shows up). A second line is logged for the +10 s variant row.
- Timestamps: the book rows by `t_recv`, Coinbase candles by their candle time (exchange time, minute aligned; the resolution limit is stated, not hidden).
- Code: the R6 recorder (`.github/workflows/record-15m-book.yml`, `scripts/record_15m_book.py`, on origin/claude/r6-15m-book-recorder and r6-btc-eth-extension, writing `book15r6/` on `quote-data`) is the book source. **Before the shadow start the V2 worker must confirm it is still running and merged to main** (a recorder that has stopped means no forward data and the date below slips). New code: `shadowlog.py` mode `spotlead1` reusing Refinement's `refine_tools/spot_split.py` and `src/scalper/spot.py` logic unchanged, plus a public Coinbase 1 minute candle pull (no key). Built by a V2 script worker. Public data only, no key, no order.
### (3) Kill and return
- Date constant: **SHADOW_START_SPOTLEAD1 = 2026-11-13T00:00:00Z**; windows closing on or after it. The registration's data (R6 windows Oct 29 22:15Z to Nov 11 16:00Z, first main score n 58, PF 1.58) is development and is never rescored here; windows between Nov 11 16:00Z and the constant are also excluded.
- KILL: at n >= 80 shadow trades (the registration's own n) PF under 1.0 at +0c AND mean under 0 = DROPPED ("no evidence at n = x"). FALSIFIED needs n >= 100 with PF under 1.0, mean under 0. Also dropped if more than 25% of acted windows are `no_book` at L + 2 s (the rule cannot be executed as written).
- RETURN: n >= 100 shadow trades, PF >= 1.3 at +0c, positive mean in each half, day-clustered z >= 2.13, and PF still above 1.0 at the +10 s variant row. That goes to the owner as a probe spec only (separate popup). Date cap **2027-01-15** with no call = back to the research queue as NO CALL. The correlation with gold L1 is reported once 10 overlap days exist.
- Read-once marker: `SHADOW-MARKER SPOTLEAD1: not yet read; first read allowed on or after 2026-12-05; after the read: READ ON <date>, commit <hash>`.
### (4) Earliest date and power
The first score had 58 trades over about 13 days on both assets, about 4 to 5 a day, so 80 shadow trades about **2026-12-01**, 100 about **2026-12-05**. Power: the development PF 1.58 is on a rule that was scored once before the deviation note was written and sits in the same sample the lead came from, so I treat it as inflated. Per-trade sd is about 0.42 (win near 0.8 at a mid ask); a shrunk mean of +0.04 gives expected z about 0.95 at n = 100 (power against 2.13 about 0.15), the unshrunk +0.10 gives about 2.4 (power about 0.6). With about 920 windows an asset held so far and edge rows only a few per cent of them, the shadow cannot confirm before January; it can drop the lead.
