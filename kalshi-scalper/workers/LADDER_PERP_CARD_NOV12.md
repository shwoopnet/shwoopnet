# CARD (not registered, nothing scored): a perpetual-fed taker rule against the hourly Bitcoin ladder's order book (Idea Desk A, Nov 12, 2026; ordered by the Head of R&D)

Verdict of the card, up front: **the arithmetic says it cannot reach the research bar (z 5.46) in this quarter, and its probability of an edge is about 1%. Stop here unless the Head of R&D wants a cheap falsification at the lifecycle's smaller bar.** Nothing was scored. Facts below are structure and counts only (strike spacing, hours recorded, the perp's volatility, the basis already measured).

## The one specification it would be
Ladder `KXBTCD` ("above K at the hourly close"), the recorder's 7 strikes nearest the money (`book/` on `quote-data`, top 5 levels every 3 s from 55 minutes before the close). **One look: 30 minutes before the close** (not a grid). Model: the chance the index is above K at the close is Phi(ln(L / K) / (sigma x sqrt(30 min))), sigma from the perp's last 30 one-minute changes. **Level L:** the strikes are ABSOLUTE prices, so the perp's level cannot be used (below); instead L is anchored at the first recorded look (55 minutes before the close) as the ladder's OWN implied level (the strike where the YES mid is 0.50, interpolated between the recorded strikes) times the perp's return since that anchor. Cheap side only (YES if its ask is 0.05 to 0.30, else NO if 1 minus the YES bid is), buy when the model's chance of that side minus its ask is 0.05 or more, 1 contract, hold to the hourly close, fee 0.07 x p x (1 - p) unrounded. **Fill line:** the first R6-style book poll at least 6 s later at the ask seen (a vanished ask is no trade), the 2 s line beside it. One try (N +1). Cutoff: closes before 2026-11-07T15:00:00Z are development, at or after are held-out, readable once with the others from Nov 24.

## The level and the basis (the part that decides it)
- Strike spacing is $100 (0.13% of the level; strikes such as 78,099.99, 78,199.99), so the 7 recorded strikes cover about plus or minus $300 around the money.
- The perp's basis to Kraken, measured on 6,317 development minutes: median -0.015%, 5th to 95th percentile about plus or minus 0.03% (the ratio's 9,996 to 10,001.7 over 10,000). At $77,700 that is about plus or minus $23.
- Volatility from the perp's development 1 minute candles (Oct 29 to Nov 7): 10 minute log-return sd 0.17% ($135), 20 minutes 0.24% ($187), 40 minutes 0.36% ($278), 60 minutes 0.43%.
- So a level error of 0.03% is 0.17 sigma at 10 minutes left, 0.12 at 20, 0.08 at 40: **at the money it moves the model's probability by about 7c, 5c and 3c.** A model using the perp's LEVEL would carry that straight into its edge, and the 5c edge threshold sits inside that noise. That is why the level is anchored on the ladder's own implied level (the basis cancels while it is constant). The price of the fix: the rule then tests only whether the perp's MOVE since the anchor predicts the ladder's later repricing, and a basis that drifts inside the 25 minutes between anchor and look still enters as noise.

## Events, expected trades, and what z 5.46 needs
- Recorded hourly closes by Nov 12 06:00Z: 275 (171 development, 104 after the cutoff); usable by the coverage rule (at least 3 strikes, at least 80% good polls, first poll by T-50, last by T-9.5): 154 development and 99 later. About 82% of hours are covered.
- Of 7 strikes, about 4 have a cheap side in 5c to 30c at 30 minutes left; a 5c model edge picks perhaps 10% of those: **about 0.4 trades an hour, about 60 on development, about 130 more by Nov 24, about 190 in all.** The strikes of one hour move together, so the independent unit is the hour, and the date-clustered effective N is nearer the 100 hours that have a trade than 190.
- A cheap-side contract has a per-trade standard deviation of about 0.35. For a date-clustered z of 5.46 the trades needed are about (5.46 x 0.35 / e)^2: **1,460 at e = 5c, 365 at e = 10c, 163 at e = 15c.** At 0.4 trades an hour that is about 150 days for 5c and 38 days for 10c; on Nov 24 only an effect above about 14c a trade could clear z 5.46. No such effect is plausible. **The research bar cannot be reached by Nov 24, nor this quarter, for any plausible effect.**
- What CAN be reached: FALSIFIED at the lifecycle's smaller bar (cash PF under 1.0 with 100 or more trades, about 250 hours of data, early to mid December) and a re-test lead (cash PF 1.3, positive mean, 100 trades). Both are weak evidence.

## Counterparty, and why they would be slow
None named. Who is on the other side of a ladder quote: market makers keyed to the settlement index through spot feeds. The perp is Kalshi's own contract and, measured, moves with Kraken at zero lag (1 minute change correlation 0.946 at lag 0, near zero at plus or minus 1 and 2), so it is not FASTER than the spot the makers watch; it is the same information on another instrument, plus a basis. A maker would be slow only if it ignored spot for minutes, which nothing recorded suggests. **No counterparty can be stated, so any pass would read "no counterparty named".**

## Declined-list and family checks
A taker rule (no resting quote), so not the closed market-making family; not a ladder lock, a monotonicity scan or a favourite-and-hold rule (cheap side, held to a hourly close, the 5c to 30c band). **Closest earlier test: G3H1** (a directional cheap-side entry on the same ladder and recorder, FALSIFIED by CORR1 after a universe look-ahead found); the new part is only the input (the perp's return instead of the ladder's own prices). It overlaps nothing live. The strike selection uses the recorder's own 7 strikes chosen 55 minutes before the close (before the look), so no universe look-ahead.

## Probability and recommendation
About **1%** that the rule shows an edge at the lifecycle bar, below 1% at the research bar. Reasons: the information is spot, which the makers have; the basis noise equals the edge threshold; and the effective sample is about 100 hours. **Recommendation: do not register.** If the Head of R&D wants the cheapest possible closure instead, register it as one try with a script before the data is opened and let it falsify itself in December; the cost is one try and about an hour of scripting.
