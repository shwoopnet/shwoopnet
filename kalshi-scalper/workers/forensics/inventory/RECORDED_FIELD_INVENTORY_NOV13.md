# What we record and which card has read it (Forensics desk, Nov 13, 2026)

Ordered by the Head of R&D (18:49Z): a field inventory from the recorder definitions and the card registry only. **No data value was opened.** Research only; no live figures.

**Method and its limits.** Column lists come from `scripts/record_*.py` on `origin/main` (`c6f54bed`). "Read by" comes from a text search (`fieldscan.sh`, this folder) of the committed `.py` and `.md` files of `main`, the research branch (`epic-ptolemy-0amjd9`), `idea-desk-a/b/c`, `testing-desk`, `history-miner`, `refine-live`, `stocks-desk`, `probe-desk`, `rnd-queue` and this desk's branch, for each field name as a whole word,
plus what this desk already knows of the cards. A field named in a loader is not a card that tests it, and a card that reads a column by position would not show: "none" means NO committed file names it, which is evidence of non-use, not proof. Every branch contains `main`'s files, so a count on a branch includes `main`'s. Recorders whose files are on branches not searched (the research branch's README is large) may carry more mentions in prose than shown.

## 2. The unread fields ranked: could a counterparty pay for them?

Ranked by a stated mechanism (who is on the other side and why they lose), not by any value. A candidate must still clear the four uniqueness checks of `docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md` 7d and the declined list 7e before anything is built; where a field's likely strategy is only a variation of a live or closed one it is marked "version risk" (7g).

1. **Station observation and its 24 hour maximum against the daily-high strikes** (`nwsobs.temp_c, max_c_24h_field`, `obs`/IEM extremes). Who pays: makers and traders who price the forecast late in the day after the observed high is already a fact. Different field from the closed forecast family. Version risk against WXDAYEND (same mechanism, new input).
2. **NY Fed SOFR reference table** (`percent_rate, p1, p25, p75, p99, volume_billions, revision`) against the Kalshi SOFR markets. Who pays: traders ignoring month-end, tax-date and settlement-day repo pressure that the table's percentile spread shows before the 8:00 ET print. Nothing committed names these fields. SOFR1 and ONRRPCAL exist; ONRRPCAL was ruled a version of SOFR1.
3. **Cross-series timing, NC15 against R6** (`t_recv_ms` and the books of the S&P 500, gold, silver, WTI, FX and crypto markets). Who pays: the slower maker on the lagging market of two that share a driver. Desk A named this the one untouched idea; N is small (recorder started Nov 11).
4. **Spot trade size and signed flow** (`quotes.SPOT.size`, `exchange_time` for three venues). Who pays: Kalshi makers who watch spot price but not the volume behind it. Different field from the spot-lead price tests (W-SPOTLEAD1); the stale-quote audit voided the list-copy quotes, so it would be scored on R6 books.
5. **Perp marks and notional open interest** (`liquidation_mark, settlement_mark, notional_oi_usd, funding_deadband, halt_sources, is_open`) for the stock and index perps listed Nov 5. Who pays: retail perp traders who push a mark away from the index; the liquidation mark and notional OI are read by no code. Family partly open (V2 perps tests, mostly NOT_ENOUGH_DATA); cost and venue limits are the Head's.
6. **OKX liquidations** (`sz, bk_px, bk_loss, pos_side`) against the Bitcoin 15 minute market. Who pays: forced sellers or buyers whose flow the Kalshi book prices late. Forward only; no card yet; the effect over 15 minutes may be small.
7. **Book levels 2 to 5** (price and size) of NC15 and R6 outside DEPTH1. Who pays: the taker who hits a thin book whose stack reveals intent. Version risk against DEPTH1 and BOOKIMB1 (top of book imbalance).
8. **Open interest changes outside Bitcoin** (politics, daily, combo, ref quotes). Who pays: market makers facing new positions. Version risk against OICORE (7g) unless the market class differs; politics spreads are wide (the POL-i2 cost line).
9. **A combo against the product of its legs** (`legs, leg_tickers` with the leg markets' quotes). Who pays: retail parlay buyers (the house side). Desk B's S3 is the live version of this; the unread part is the leg-level quotes.
10. **Reference-chart fields** (Steam `rank, name, value, value2` against KXSTEAMTOPSELLER). Who pays: traders slow to read a public chart. A small market; a count of one series.

Not ranked (measurement or already closed): clock and timing columns (`t_send_ms, t_recv_ms, offset_ms, rtt_ms`, read by the audit and the lag cards); `tape_sum_count`, `volume_fp` coverage fields; in-game sizes (the in-game lag family is closed); the Deribit fields (recorder stopped); `signals` (no entry enabled); tape side and large-print size (the order-flow family is closed).
