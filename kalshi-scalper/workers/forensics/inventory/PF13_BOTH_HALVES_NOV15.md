**Method and limits (read first).** Read only: a text scan of the result-type files (`*RESULT*.md`, `*RESULT*.txt`, `*result*.txt`, ledgers, `RND_QUEUE.md`) on the branches `claude/epic-ptolemy-0amjd9` (the research branch, 446 result-type files), `idea-desk-a/b/c`, `testing-desk`, `history-miner`, `refine-live`, `stocks-desk`, `probe-desk`, `forensics-desk` and `rnd-queue`, at their tips on Nov 15 about 14:30Z. I looked for lines that print a profit factor of 1.3 or more and, on the same or the next two lines, printed sub-window figures; then read each candidate line. **Nothing was recomputed.** The scan finds what is printed on one line or in a short run of lines; a result printed only in a table, or with PF spelled another way, can be missed, so **this list is a floor, not a census**. 761 lines with PF 1.3 or more were found on the research branch (107 files); most are single-window or tiny-n figures with no halves printed. Desk A's pass list: none was found in my scan (no file named so on `idea-desk-a`; its result files carry 1 line with PF 1.3 or more, a CFRTI-K block rule marked "NOT MET"); I did not ask Desk A. **Haircut column.** "Raw" = taker at the printed ask (next candle's ask or the cheap rung's ask) with the fee, no 1c or 2c slip; "x1.2" = the fee stress only. Only the lines marked 1c show a haircut.
