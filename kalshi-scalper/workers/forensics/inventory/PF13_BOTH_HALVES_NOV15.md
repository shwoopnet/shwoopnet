I looked for lines that print a profit factor of 1.3 or more and, on the same or the next two lines, printed sub-window figures; then read each candidate line. **Nothing was recomputed.** The scan finds what is printed on one line or in a short run of lines; a result printed only in a table, or with PF spelled another way, can be missed, so **this list is a floor, not a census**. 761 lines with PF 1.3 or more were found on the research branch (107 files); most are single-window or tiny-n figures with no halves printed.
