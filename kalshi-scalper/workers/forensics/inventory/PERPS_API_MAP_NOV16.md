Base https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/margin. Evidence: the docs pages under https://docs.kalshi.com/margin-rest/ (fetched by a web-fetch agent and quoted) and 5 public probes (the Head's budget: 5, all used, one GET each, generic User-Agent, 5 s apart). Probe answers are kept off the branch; only counts are written here.

## Funding cadence: ONE print a day, not three
The help pages say funding is every 8 hours at 12 AM, 8 AM and 4 PM ET, but both pages describe the BTC perp only. For stock and index perps the API returns one print a day (21:00Z in winter), so our files are not missing prints; the three-a-day schedule was never in the data. **Real gap found:** the 2026-11-07 funding prints exist at Kalshi (SPY has one at 2026-11-07T21:00Z) but our funding file for that day is missing: one request without a ticker and start_ts 2026-11-07, end_ts 2026-11-08 would return every market's Nov 7 print (a back-pull for Main). Candles on Nov 7 do not exist (Saturday, closed).

## Recorder proposal for what cannot be back-served (book depth, reference and mark prices, and a safety copy of the trade tape)
Public GET only, no key, no order, a generic User-Agent, redirects refused, a 429 waits and doubles, three in a row end the run, own orphan branch `perp-live-data`, gzipped CSV by hour, built-in token only, stops itself on 2027-02-26, one concurrency group that never cancels a running job, 15 minute triggers each recording to the next 4 hour boundary (the pattern of the other recorders).
(1) MARKETS SNAPSHOT: GET /margin/markets (one request returns all 126 markets), every 60 seconds: 1,440 requests a day; keep per market ts, ticker, status, bid, ask, bid and ask size, price, reference_price, settlement_mark_price, liquidation_mark_price, open_interest, volume_24h, leverage_estimate: about 126 rows x 130 bytes, about 16 KB raw and about 3 KB gzipped a minute, about 4 MB gzipped a day. At 5 minutes: 288 requests and about 0.9 MB a day.
(2) BOOK: GET /margin/markets/orderbooks for the active markets (about 80), depth 5, every 60 seconds (1,440 requests; or every 5 minutes, 288): about 80 x 10 levels, about 4 KB gzipped a minute, about 6 MB a day (1.2 MB at 5 minutes).
(3) TRADE TAPE (safety copy): GET /margin/trades with no ticker, min_ts of the last poll, cursor to the end, every 5 minutes: about 288 requests a day plus pages; hold trade_id for dedupe; small. Skip it if a weekday probe shows the tape is back-servable (one request).
Total at 1 minute: about 2,900 requests a day (about 2 a minute), at 5 minutes about 600 (0.4 a minute), both far under the 10 tokens per request budget. Runner minutes: a continuous job like R6 is about 43,000 billed minutes a month (free on a public repo but above the Charter's 250 guideline: the owner's merge accepts it); the 5 minute version could run as a schedule of short jobs but GitHub drops starts (see the Oct 31 offsets note), so a continuous 4 hour job is the reliable form.
