## 2. Funding sign, from Kalshi's own help pages (fetched Nov 16, 2026)
https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357613-how-funding-works (page updated June 3, 2026): "If the funding rate is positive (perp trading above spot): long holders pay short holders." "If the funding rate is negative (perp trading below spot): short holders pay long holders." "If the rate is zero: no payment is made." The same rule is on https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/14679970-what-are-perpetual-futures ("If the rate is **positive**, long holders pay short holders."). **So positive funding = longs pay shorts (carry for a long, income for a short).**
Interval and times on the page: "Every 8 hours, Kalshi computes a time-weighted average price (TWAP) of 1-minute candlestick premiums over the 480 candles in that funding period", at 12:00 AM ET, 8:00 AM ET and 4:00 PM ET; clamp "+2% / -2% per 8-hour interval"; "If the absolute value of the rate is below 0.01%, it is automatically set to zero" (the deadband). Both pages describe the BTC perp only. **Our data does not match the 8 hour schedule:** the stock and index perps show ONE print a day at 21:00Z (4:00 PM ET in winter; 20:00Z in summer) and gold shows one at 14:00Z; the funding history route we record may return only some prints, or those markets use a different schedule. Not resolved. Leverage on the BTC page: "Up to 6x"; no 15x or 40x sentence on either page.
