# Single-stock and index perps: census and data inventory (Forensics desk, Nov 15, 2026; the Head's order, owner order via the EA)
Counts and metadata only. No return, result or P&L was computed. Built by `perps_census.py` (this folder) from the `perp-data` branch (checked out Nov 15, 21:20Z): `perps/` hourly (to 2026-11-15T21:00Z), `perps30/` (30 s, two files: 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-13, close windows only), `funding/`, `kalshi1m/`, `cash1m/`. One row per ticker: `PERPS_CENSUS_NOV15_TICKERS.tsv` (24 columns). Zero Kalshi requests were made for this document.

## 1. The universe and what we hold on each
97 perp tickers: 94 class Equities (single stocks plus KXSPYPERP and KXQQQPERP) and 3 class Indices (KXUS500PERP, KXUSTECH100PERP, KXUS2000PERP). The underlying is read from the ticker name only (the perps list carries no title column; not checked against Kalshi's own titles).
- **Quoting at all:** 60 of 97 have a two-sided quote (bid > 0, ask > 0, bid < ask) in the data; 37 never do: status `inactive`, bid and ask 0, no funding, no candles (e.g. KXABTPERP, KXACNPERP, KXGSPERP, KXTSMPERP, KXUS2000PERP; full list = rows with two_sided_rows 0 in the TSV). At the Nov 15 21:00Z snapshot: 33 active with a two-sided quote, 27 active with no two-sided quote (a Sunday, cash market closed), 37 inactive.
- **First trade date:** 54 of the 60 first show a two-sided quote at 2026-11-05T18:39Z (the stock perps listing); six earlier: KXUS500PERP and KXUSTECH100PERP from 2026-10-21T16:56Z (the first row of the hourly file), KXAMGNPERP, KXCOSTPERP, KXMCDPERP, KXQCOMPERP from 2026-11-03T23:12Z. "First trade" here means the first two-sided quote I hold, not a first print (no trade tape is held); KXUS500PERP and KXUSTECH100PERP may be older than Oct 21 (the recorder starts there).
- **Spread (basis points of the mid, over every two-sided row since listing: hourly and the two 30 s files; about 390 to 460 rows per ticker):** per-ticker medians across the 60: min 1.0, median 18.0, max 42.6. Examples: KXUS500PERP 1.0, KXUSTECH100PERP 1.2, KXQQQPERP 1.6, KXSPYPERP 3.2, KXNVDAPERP 4.0, KXAAPLPERP 4.8. The p90 is heavy tailed: median of p90s 186.6 bps, and many tickers' p90 is a junk wide quote (KXAAPLPERP and KXQQQPERP p90 are 20,000 bps = a resting 1 cent bid against a real ask): treat the median, not the mean or p90, as the spread.
- **Top-of-book size (median of the smaller of bid size and ask size, contracts as recorded):** across the 60: min 30, median 376, max 29,519 (KXUSTECH100PERP). Examples: KXSPYPERP 199, KXQQQPERP 207, KXNVDAPERP 800, KXUS500PERP 843, KXAAPLPERP 1,410.
- **Volume (the 24 hour figure on each row, median over rows, units as recorded) and open interest:** volume across the 60: min 4,261, median 74,526, max 55,498,100 (KXUSTECH100PERP; KXNVDAPERP 21,187,468, KXAAPLPERP 2,900,729, KXUS500PERP 3,009,763, KXSPYPERP 35,717, KXQQQPERP 45,787). Open interest (contracts) min 763, median 10,287, max 1,738,722 (KXNVDAPERP); notional open interest in dollars min 4,514, median 51,192, max 6,104,292 (KXUS500PERP).

## 2. Funding (`funding/`, one file a day)
Per ticker 8 prints, each at 21:00:00Z, from 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-14; the file for 2026-11-07 is not on the branch, so Nov 7 has no print (9 days, 8 prints). The 37 inactive tickers have none. Per-ticker rate: median across tickers 0.000000 (most prints are 0); the largest median is 0.000078 (KXUSTECH100PERP); range over all tickers -0.000233 (KXUS500PERP) to +0.000992 (KXUSTECH100PERP). Per ticker median, min, max are in the TSV.

## 3. Candles (`kalshi1m/`, Kalshi's public 1 minute candlestick route, recorded daily)
60 tickers have candles: 4,261 to 8,344 rows each (median about 6,110), first minute 2026-11-06T00:00Z, last minute between 2026-11-13T22:01Z and 2026-11-14T21:15Z (the file of Nov 14 holds only part of the tickers). The file for 2026-11-07 is not on the branch. Gaps over 60 minutes per ticker: 2 to 10 (median 3), longest 40.9 to 50.3 hours (the weekend close, plus the missing Nov 7 file). Gaps over 5 minutes per ticker are in the TSV. **How far back the route serves 1 minute candles: NOT tested.** It needs one request to the Kalshi API, and my standing rule is zero Kalshi requests unless Main allocates a share of the request budget; I asked the Head to confirm that allocation (the Head's order says "at the shared pace" but the allocation comes from Main). The two perps recorders' own earliest candle is the stock perps' listing (Nov 5, 18:39Z), so a stock perp has no earlier history to find; only the three index perps and crypto/metals/energy perps could go back further.
