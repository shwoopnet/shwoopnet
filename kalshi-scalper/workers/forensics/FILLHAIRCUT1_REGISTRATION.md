# FILLHAIRCUT1 registration: the simulation-to-real-fill haircut for taker rules (2026-11-12, committed BEFORE anything was computed)

Asked for by the Head of R&D (message, Nov 12, 2026). A measurement, not a search: no rule is scored, no settlement result is read, zero tries. Public recorded data only (recorder R6, `book15r6/` on `quote-data`), zero Kalshi requests, no live account figure. Script: `fillhaircut1.py`.

**Dates and windows (constants).** `KXBTC15M` windows whose `close_time` is from 2026-10-29T00:00:00Z up to and including 2026-11-08T23:59:59Z (the files this desk already holds; nothing closing on or after 2026-11-09T00:00Z, V2's DEPTH1 block). Inside each window only the span from 180 to 120 seconds before the close. A poll is usable when both best bids are present and YES bid < YES ask. The `result` column is not loaded.

**Decision instants.** Every usable poll in the span at which the cheaper side (the lower ask; equal asks dropped) has its ask a0 in [0.05, 0.30]. Buckets by a0: 5c to under 10c, 10c to under 20c, 20c to 30c. The size shown at the ask is the other side's best bid size; the size at or below a price is the sum of the other side's bid levels (top 5) whose implied ask is at or below it. Polls in one window are not independent: every interval is a 95% interval from resampling whole windows (seed 20261112, 2,000 draws), and the count of windows is given beside the count of instants.

**Part 1, quote survival.** For h = 1, 2, 5, 10 seconds: the first usable poll at least h seconds after the instant (no more than h + 4 s after). Polls are about 2 seconds apart, so the 1 second row is in practice the next poll; it is labelled so. Reported per bucket: share with the ask still at or below a0; share at or below a0 + 1c; size shown at or below a0 at the instant and at h (median, and the shares with at least 1 and at least 10 contracts); the move of the ask (mean, and the 50th, 75th, 90th, 95th percentile) over all instants and over those where the ask at a0 is gone.

**Part 2, what takers pay.** Tape trades in the same span in which the taker BOUGHT the cheaper side, the cheaper side and its ask taken from the last usable poll received at least 2.0 s (and no more than 6.0 s) before the trade, that ask in [0.05, 0.30]. Price paid = `yes_price_dollars` for YES, 1 minus it for NO. Realized slippage = price paid minus that ask, per trade and per contract, by bucket and by trade size (under 2 contracts, 2 to under 20, 20 or more). Share of the displayed size that trades: per decision instant, the contracts takers bought on the cheaper side at or below a0 in the 1, 2, 5 and 10 seconds after the poll, divided by the size shown at or below a0 at the poll (share of instants with any such trade; median and 90th percentile of the ratio; share of instants where it reaches 1).

**Part 3, the haircut.** An immediate-or-cancel buy of Q = 1 and Q = 10 contracts, decided at the instant, arriving at the first usable poll at least d seconds later (d = 1, 2, 5, 10), with a limit of a0 and, separately, a0 + 1c: it takes the levels at or below the limit at that poll, best first. Reported per bucket and pooled: share filled in full, share filled at all, mean price paid minus a0 on the filled contracts, and the mean move of the mid from the instant to 30 seconds later for filled against missed orders (a no-outcome measure of which signals a limit loses). The stated haircut is (a) the share of simulated fills to drop and (b) the cents to add to the price of the rest. A cell with under about 100 windows is labelled "no evidence at n = x".

**What it cannot measure, said in advance.** The books are 2 second snapshots, so nothing under about 2 seconds is resolved by the book (the tape in Part 2 is the only sub-second evidence); hidden or flash liquidity between polls is invisible; our own order's effect on the book is not modelled; and the value of a missed fill (whether the missed signals win more) needs settlement results, which this measurement does not read (ENTRYREPLAY1 has the one read of that on the baseline, and it was not stable).
