# CFRTI development signal rows replayed on the R6 books (2026-11-12; method written BEFORE the rows were replayed)

Asked for by the Head of R&D and Desk A (messages, Nov 12). Input: `kalshi-scalper/workers/desk_a_verify/cfrti_dev_signals.csv` on `claude/idea-desk-a` (68 rows: form, t0_utc, ticker, side, ask_seen; every close before 2026-11-05; no outcome column). This desk loads NO outcome and scores nothing: zero tries. Desk A joins the outcomes it already holds. Nothing closing on or after 2026-11-05 is replayed. Script `cfrti_dev_replay.py`, output `results/CFRTI_DEV_REPLAY.csv`.

Method (the FILLHAIRCUT1 convention): for each row, on the R6 book of that ticker, the arrival poll is the first usable poll received at least 2.0 s (and separately at least 6.0 s) after t0, no more than 4 s later than that. The order is an immediate-or-cancel buy of the row's side with the limit at ask_seen (no slip), for 1 and for 10 contracts: it fills at that poll's ask levels at or below the limit, best first. Returned per row and delay: the lag of the poll actually used, the ask at arrival, the size shown at or below the limit, filled or not for 1 and 10 contracts, and the price paid. Also returned, as a check on the input: the R6 ask of the last usable poll at or before t0 beside ask_seen. A row with no R6 book (the recorder began 2026-10-29 22:15Z and has gaps) is returned as `no_book`, never guessed.
