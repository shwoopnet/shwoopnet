# GOLDHAIRCUT1 registration: the fill haircut for the gold 15 minute market at gold's look (2026-11-12, committed BEFORE anything was computed)

Asked for by the Head of R&D (message, Nov 12): a paper record of the gold favourite rule assumes a fill at the touch; this says how much to trust that. A measurement in the FILLHAIRCUT1 style: no rule scored, no settlement result loaded, zero tries. Public R6 books and tape only, zero Kalshi requests, nothing about the live account. Script `goldhaircut1.py` (the FILLHAIRCUT1 script with the constants below).

**Constants.** `KXGOLD15M`. Closes from 2026-10-29T00:00:00Z up to and including 2026-11-11T23:59:59Z (period A to Nov 8, period B Nov 9 to 11, shown beside each other and pooled). The span from 400 to 330 seconds before the close. A decision instant is a usable poll in the span at which ONE side's ask is in [0.88, 0.97]; that side is the favourite and a0 is its ask. Buckets by a0: 88c to under 91c, 91c to under 94c, 94c to 97c. Delays 1, 2, 5, 10 seconds (the first usable poll at least that much later, no more than 4 s after that; polls are about 2 seconds apart, so "1 s" is in practice the next poll). Limits: a0 and a0 + 1c. Order sizes 1 and 15 contracts (15 is the size the rule's balance ladder is held at in code). Intervals 95%, resampling whole windows, seed 20261112.

**Reported.** Part 1: share of instants with the ask still at or below a0, and within +1c, and the move when gone. Part 2: what takers who bought the favourite paid against the ask of the last poll at least 2 s earlier, and the share of the displayed size that trades within 1, 2, 5, 10 s. Part 3: share of immediate-or-cancel buys filled in full for each limit, size and delay; price paid against a0 on the filled; the mid 30 seconds after the decision for filled against missed orders. Also the share of instants where the size shown at the touch covers 15 contracts (the paper record's "would fill" test). A cell with under about 100 windows is labelled "no evidence at n = x".

**Said in advance.** Gold is closed at weekends, so the window count will be small and the buckets may each fall under the 100 line. On a favourite a limit that only fills when the ask stayed or fell keeps the favourites that were weakening and loses the ones that were strengthening; whether that costs money needs outcomes, which this does not load. One cent is large here: the rule wins a few cents and loses most of the stake.
