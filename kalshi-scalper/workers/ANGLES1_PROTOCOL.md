## Data and split
Series traded (17): silver, WTI, copper, natural gas, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NEAR, ZEC, HYPE, BNB, XRP, SOL (15 minute). Gold, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin are NOT loaded. Files alts2, alts3, alts4, e3b, fresh, rep, l1b, book, five (.sqlite), deduped by ticker (first file wins), settled markets with 1 minute candles (bid and ask close, high, low, volume, oi). Per series, markets sorted by close time: first two thirds DEVELOPMENT, last third HELD-OUT. Pooled across the 17 series; per-series splits are descriptive only and not counted.
Candle position: left = close_ts - candle end_ts (seconds before close). Lead "10m" = last candle with left 570 to 640, "6m" = last candle with left 330 to 400 (as SWEEP1). OPENQ1 lead L in {840, 780, 720, 660, 600} = the candle with |left - L| <= 8 (840 is the first candle).
Entry filters (all cards except TAIL1): bid > 0, ask < 1, ask > bid, spread <= 0.03. Buy 1 contract at the ask of the chosen side (NO ask = 1 - YES bid).
Side bands: CHEAP = either side with ask 0.04 to 0.30. MID = the UNDERDOG side (lower mid) with ask above 0.30 and up to 0.88 (the favourite side is never held to settlement; the 0.88 cap means 88c to 97c is never entered).
Exits: HOLD = settles 1 or 0; TIME k = sell at the side's bid (YES bid_c; NO = 1 - YES ask_c) at the candle ending 60 k seconds after entry (|diff| <= 8 s), k in {2,3,4} unless a card names others; if that candle is missing or the bid is 0, the trade falls back to HOLD (stated, applies to every cell alike).
Fee, CASH basis: net = payoff or sale proceeds - entry price - 0.07 p (1-p) per contract UNROUNDED on the entry and on a sale (none on settlement). TAIL1 also reports the per-order ROUNDED fee: ceil(0.07 C p (1-p) in cents) / 100 per order at C = 10 and C = 100, divided by C per contract. PF = gross wins / gross losses of per-trade net.
Statistic: day-clustered z (calibration.cluster_mean_z, cluster = UTC day of close), one sided, positive. Trades are one per market per cell.

## Cards and bins (fixed now)
OPENQ1 (first-quote misprice). Feature d = |mid - 0.5| of the entry candle, bins [0,0.1), [0.1,0.2), [0.2,0.3), [0.3,inf); mid exactly 0.5 skipped. FOLLOW = buy the favourite (mid above 0.5; ask must be 0.30 to 0.88): exits TIME 2,3,4 only (no hold of a favourite). FADE = buy the underdog (ask 0.04 to 0.88): exits HOLD, TIME 2,3,4. Entry leads 840 (the card), 780, 720, 660, 600 (sensitivity). Tests: FOLLOW 4 bins x 3 exits x 5 leads = 60; FADE 4 x 4 x 5 = 80. Total 140.
WINPOS1 (quarter-hour position). Position = close minute of the hour in {0, 15, 30, 45} (UTC). Bands CHEAP and MID (underdog), leads 10m and 6m, HOLD. Tests 4 x 2 x 2 = 16.
TAIL1 (extreme tail, hold). Either side with ask 0.01 to 0.03 (no spread filter, no bid filter; entry price = ask, price bin = round(ask x 100) in {1,2,3}), leads 10m and 6m, HOLD. Fee bases: unrounded per contract, rounded per order at C = 10, at C = 100. Tests 3 prices x 2 leads x 3 fee bases = 18.
PRICECLUSTER1 (round quotes). Levels L in {10, 25, 50, 75} cents; trade = buy a side whose ask is exactly L cents (spread filter as above), leads 10m and 6m. L = 10, 25, 50 HOLD; L = 75 TIME 3 only (no favourite hold). Cluster tests 4 x 2 = 8. Contrast tests (same exit, trades with ask at L-1 or L+1 cents, difference in mean per trade of the cluster minus neighbours, day-clustered z) 4 x 2 = 8. Total 16.
ACTIVITY1 (window volume vs trailing). At the entry candle, V = sum of candle volume of this window up to and including the entry candle; M = median of the same cumulative figure (to the same seconds before close) over the series' previous 96 windows (previous 24 hours; at least 48 present, M > 0); r = V / M. Terciles = the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of r over all DEVELOPMENT markets (pooled) at that lead, frozen in code before outcomes are read. Bands CHEAP and MID (underdog), leads 10m and 6m, exits HOLD and TIME 3. Tests 3 x 2 x 2 x 2 = 24.
SETTLELAG1 (descriptive, no strategy, not counted). From quote-data results.csv.gz (ticker, result, close_time, seen_ms): lag = seen_ms - close_time per series; report the distribution (min, quartiles, 90th, 99th, max) and the share seen within 5 and 30 seconds. No quote-state check, no strategy.

## Counts and bars
Tests added N = 140 + 16 + 18 + 16 + 24 = 214. Shared N = 1,100,000 + 214 = 1,100,214 (z bar inv_cdf(1 - 0.05/N), about 5.33, computed in code); the smaller ledger N = 8,795 (+214 = 9,009, z about 4.39) is also reported.
Development gate (a cell is a dev survivor): cash PF >= 1.5 AND n >= 100 AND z >= the bar. Held-out (last third, read ONCE, only for dev survivors at z >= 4.39): cash PF >= 1.5, n >= 50 and positive mean; a SURVIVOR passes development at the strict bar (5.33), held-out, and pooled dev + held-out z at least the bar. Counts of tests and survivors are reported whatever they are. Verdict words: FALSIFIED, NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, basis named next to every figure (cash, unrounded fee unless stated).
