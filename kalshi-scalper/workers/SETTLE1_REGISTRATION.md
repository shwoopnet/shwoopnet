**What the table says.** No non-crypto family averages: settlement is ONE print at the close against ONE print at the open (Pyth 1 minute candle close, ICE or Google Finance for FX/indices). Bitcoin and Ethereum are the averaging families (60 s BRTI average), and they are excluded here. So the "average over a window" mechanism (a lower-variance last minute) does not exist outside crypto. The only candidate mechanism left in the rulebook is a **reference that is public and real time while Kalshi's quote lags it**: the Pyth feed is public, but this sandbox cannot read Pyth (401), so the test uses OKX perpetuals as the reference. Reference availability by family (OKX perps listed 2026): silver `XAG-USDT-SWAP`, platinum `XPT-USDT-SWAP`, palladium `XPD-USDT-SWAP`, WTI `CL-USDT-SWAP`, natural gas `NG-USDT-SWAP`, indices `SPY`/`QQQ`/`US500`/`US100`, 20 year bonds `TLT`. **None for copper, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD** (OKX has no spot or perp on the pairs; Yahoo/Dukascopy 429, Pyth Benchmarks 401, per LAG15): those families are `NOT_RUNNABLE` here. The index ladders are `IDXFUT1` (registered 2026-11-03; not duplicated). Rates: TLT is a 20 year bond ETF perp, not the 10 year Pyth yield, and the yield family is hourly; not run.

## Counterparty and mechanism
The thinner 15 minute metal and energy books are quoted by makers off a feed. The settlement is a single print, so at one to three minutes before the close the probability is a function of the distance of the current price from the open print and of the remaining volatility (a normal on the remaining minutes). If the Kalshi quote for the winning side is still 5c to 30c while a real-time reference says the price is already at least one remaining-sigma on that side, the seller of that cheap side is paid below fair value. **Honest prior of a pass: 2%** (`LAG15`: the quotes track spot at one minute resolution; `GOLDFV1`/`DAILY2`: Kalshi's quoters price the reference in real time). The expected result is that almost no looks have a cheap implied winner, and those that do are reference noise (a thin perp on a weekend, a basis jump).

## Rule (fixed now)
- **Families (5, each one try per look):** `KXSILVER15M` vs `XAG-USDT-SWAP`; `KXPLATINUM15M` vs `XPT-USDT-SWAP`; `KXPALLADIUM15M` vs `XPD-USDT-SWAP`; `KXWTI15M` vs `CL-USDT-SWAP`; `KXNATGAS15M` vs `NG-USDT-SWAP`. Never gold, Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin, XRP. Markets: every settled market in `data/alts3.sqlite` (silver and WTI from 2026-08-06, natural gas from 2026-08-27, platinum and palladium from 2026-09-17, all to 2026-10-13), window = (close - 900 s, close].
- **Looks (2):** D = close - 180 s and D = close - 120 s. 5 families x 2 looks = **10 tries**.
- **Reference:** `R(t)` = close of the OKX 1 minute bar whose open is t - 60 s (the price at t). Start print `R0 = R(close - 900)`. `x = ln(R(D) / R0)`; ties (x = 0) skipped. `sigma` = RMS of the 1 minute log returns of `R` over the 60 minutes ending at D (zero mean, at least 40 returns); `m` = (close - D) / 60 minutes (3 or 2); `z = x / (sigma x sqrt(m))`. Implied winner: YES if x > 0, NO if x < 0. **Triggered** if `|z| >= 1.0` (model p of the implied side >= 0.84, `Phi(|z|)`). Basis and scale cancel in the ratio; the level of OKX is never compared with Kalshi's.
- **Quote and fill:** the Kalshi candle ending at D + 60 s (the first minute after the signal). Two-sided (`bid_c > 0`, `ask_c < 1`), spread at most 0.10. Price of the implied side: YES `ask_c`, NO `1 - bid_c`. **Treated trade if that price is in [0.05, 0.30]; hard limit 0.30.** One order per market per look, C = 10, taker, held to settlement. Fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order; check at x1.2 and +1c slippage.
- **Measured for every triggered market-look, outcome unread until the count is fixed:** share of triggered looks with a quotable implied side at all, the share priced at or below 0.30 (and at or below 0.50), mean implied-side price, mean model p. This is the "how often is the cheap side still cheap" measurement.
- **Control (descriptive, not a try):** market-looks with `|z| < 0.25` where either side's price is in [0.05, 0.30]: buy the cheaper side, same fill, same hold.
- **Calibration (information, not a try):** win rate of the implied side among triggered looks against mean `Phi(|z|)` and against the mean price paid (all prices), per family.

## Windows and holdout
Dates (UTC) that have at least one triggered market-look with a quotable implied side (decided without any outcome), pooled over families, sorted and cut into equal thirds: W1 discovery, W2 validation, **W3 frozen holdout, never loaded into any print** unless W1 and W2 both pass for the pooled treated trades; then read once. Families are also printed separately (descriptive).

## Numeric prediction (before any outcome)
Triggered share of market-looks about 15 to 25% (a one-sigma move from the open is common). Quotable implied side priced at or below 0.30: **under 2% of triggered looks**, i.e. treated trades about 20 to 120 pooled over W1+W2 across the 10 cells (NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, under 150, about 75% likely). Implied-side win rate about 80 to 88% (calibrated). Where the implied side IS cheap, win rate about 25 to 45% against a model p of 0.84 or more (a reference artefact), **PF 0.3 to 1.2 per window, point 0.7**; control PF 0.6 to 0.9.

## Kill criteria and verdicts
- Pooled treated trades in W1 + W2 under 150: `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` (every informational line still printed). Per family fewer than 8 dates with a trade: that family `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA`.
- `FALSIFIED`: PF under 1.5 after fees in W1 or in W2 (pooled or per family), or not positive at fees x1.2 and +1c, or treated mean minus control mean not positive in both windows, or the implied side's calibration is more than 10 points under `Phi` on all triggered looks (the reference is not a reference).
- All of those passed with a day-clustered z of the net per trade at least 5.27 (N about 728,765 after this batch; 33 days cannot reach it unless the edge is huge): `NOT_YET_FALSIFIED`, which earns a W3 read and a forward-recorder registration only, never a pass.
- No parameter, look, family or band changes after this commit. A later variant (a third look, a different band, copper or FX with a new reference, the hourly ladders) is a new counted try.
