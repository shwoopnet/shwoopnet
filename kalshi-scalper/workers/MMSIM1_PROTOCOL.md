Data (already held): `quote-data` branch `book/<date>/<hhmm>/book.csv.gz` (7 strikes nearest the money of each KXBTCD hourly event, top 5 YES and NO bid levels with sizes every 3 s from 55 minutes before the close) and `trades.csv.gz` (public trade tape every 30 s, trade ids de-duplicated). 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-11 (219 files at registration). No Kalshi request is needed.
Simulation, frozen now:
- Quoting: for each hourly event and each of its 7 recorded strikes, from T-50 min to T-15 min, on each side (YES, NO), if we hold no resting order or inventory on that side, post ONE contract at that moment's best bid of that side and join the BACK of the queue (queue ahead = the visible size at that price). The order is never repriced; it stays until filled or T-15 min.
- Fill rule (queue model): a resting YES bid at price p is filled when the cumulative contracts traded at p by takers selling YES (taker_outcome_side "no" at yes_price p) after the post time reach the queue ahead plus 1; a resting NO bid at q is filled when cumulative contracts traded at yes_price 1 - q with taker side "yes" reach its queue ahead plus 1. The trade tape is polled every 30 s, so fills are timed to the poll that shows them. Queue shrinkage from cancels is ignored (conservative, fewer fills).
- Flatten at T-15 min: unfilled orders are cancelled; a strike with a filled leg on only one side sells it at the best bid of that side at that moment (a taker sale, fee 0.07 x p x (1-p)); a strike with BOTH sides filled is held to settlement and pays exactly 1 (cost b + q already paid).
- Fees (cash, unrounded; the maker fee is UNCERTAIN so it is a bound): maker legs at fee 0 and at 0.0175 x p x (1-p); the taker flatten sale at 0.07 x p x (1-p); the pair-held-to-settlement pays no exit fee. Report both bounds.
Metrics: fills per day, share of strikes with one fill and with two, cash cents per strike-pair quoted, daily cash P&L, PF of the per-event P&L, the daily P&L correlation with gold L1's and OICORE's daily results from the live nightly report over the overlapping days (12 days or fewer, so indicative only), all by thirds.
Splits: first two thirds of events by time = development; the last third is HELD OUT and read once.
Bar: cash PF >= 1.3 on per-event P&L at the 0.0175 maker bound, n >= 300 events, a positive mean in each third, AND daily correlation with gold under 0.3. N tried: 1 (one fixed rule, no parameter scan); ledger about 5,190.
