# NFLPC1 PROTOCOL (registered Nov 8, 2026, before any NFL prop candle, price or result is fetched)

Research only. No trading code, no key, no order. Public Kalshi `/historical` only. Owner request via main (Nov 8): player-prop combos (QB passing yards plus team total or spread, stacks). Counted as a NEW try family; overlap check: PROPS1 (player props vs a game-log count model, single legs, 9 treated), NFLANYTD1, NFLFTD1, OUT1 (single legs), ARB5 (crossed totals), COMBO1 to 3 (15 minute pairs). Never done: same-game pairs of cheap NFL tickets scored as one costed trade with the other leg's price as a conditioning feature.
**Principle (stated up front):** two separately settled contracts have additive expected value, so correlation alone is no edge. The only claim: a leg is mispriced GIVEN the other leg's quote (for example a cheap QB over-yards ticket while the game total quote implies a high-scoring game). That is a conditional price-feature rule, scored like COMBO1.

## Universe and data (fixed now; selected from listings and tickers only, never from a price)
Series: KXNFLPASSYDS (QB over X passing yards, YES), KXNFLTOTAL (game total over X), KXNFLSPREAD (team wins by over X). History depth from `/historical` listing: PASSYDS 229 games, TOTAL and SPREAD 350 games (Aug 2025 to Feb 2026 season). Games with all three: counted before fetching. Leg markets fetched per game: for PASSYDS the 4 highest strikes per QB; for TOTAL the 6 highest strikes; for SPREAD the 6 highest strikes per team (cheap tails by construction). Candles: the last 1 minute candle ending at least 10 minutes before the game's close time field of the market is NOT used (close times are after the game); the decision time is kickoff minus 60 minutes, kickoff taken from the ticker date plus the first TOTAL or SPREAD market's open pattern is unavailable, so the decision uses the hourly candle that ends nearest 3 hours before the market `close_time` minus the typical 3.2 hour game length: concretely, t = close_time minus 4 hours rounded down to the hour (fixed for all legs, so every leg of a game is quoted at the same time).
Cuts: games in date order, first 2/3 development (thirds W1/W2/W3 by date), last 1/3 held out and read once. Pair rows: PASSYDS x TOTAL and PASSYDS x SPREAD within a game; both legs cheap (ask 3c to 30c), both YES (these ladders are over tickets), fee on each leg at 10 contracts, held to settlement, net = sum of the two legs, one trade. Features: both legs' asks, bids, spreads, 1 hour change of the bid where available, strike ranks, the two asks' difference, the pair type (2 values), week of season.
Scan: the harness (`scan.py`) grammar and bar (PF 1.5 in each of W1, W2, W3, floors 40 / 150 / 3 dates, deflated by the one-sided Bonferroni at 0.05 / N); one run of 20,000 rules (seeds 81801, 81802). `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` if fewer than 150 development pair rows or fewer than 60 distinct games in development.
