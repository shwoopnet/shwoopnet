Candles with a missing or crossed bid or ask are dropped; a gap is never bridged. mid = (bid_close + ask_close) / 2.

**Clock.** Regular hours only: 09:35 to 15:45 ET (14:35 to 20:45 UTC while the clocks are on EST, true for all of November after Nov 1), weekdays. Windows (filters on the entry candle's end time in ET): W0 all regular hours; W1 the open 09:35 to 10:00; W2 10:00 to 11:00; W3 power hour 15:00 to 15:45.

**Vol unit.** sigma1 = sample std (n - 1) of the 1 minute log returns of mid over the 60 candles ending 5 minutes before the signal candle (at least 40 valid returns, else no signal); U = sigma1 x sqrt(10) (a 10 minute move, the name's own).

**Entry rules (7), all FOLLOW the move (trend); reversion is Desk A's PERPSCALP1 arm R and is not repeated.**
- E1, E2 volatility expansion: the realised vol of the last 15 candles (std of 1 minute log returns) divided by sigma1 is at least r, r = 2, 3; direction = the sign of the 15 minute mid move; fires on the first candle meeting it.
- E3, E4, E5 opening-range breakout, N = 5, 15, 30 minutes: range = high and low of mid over the N one-minute candles after the cash open (09:30 ET to 09:30 + N); the first candle after the range whose mid closes beyond it (above the high: long; below the low: short); one per name per day per N.
- E6, E7 volume spike, q = 5, 10: the candle's volume is at least q x the mean volume of the previous 60 candles (only candles with volume above zero count in the mean; at least 20) and the candle's mid move is nonzero; direction = the sign of that candle's mid return.
Common: spread at the signal under 0.3% of price; at most one entry per name per 15 minutes; one position per name; no entry in the last 15 minutes of the window's end (time stop must fit before 15:45 ET: an entry after 15:35 ET is skipped and counted).

**Entry and exit prices.** Entry at the NEXT candle (t + 1) after the signal candle t, long at its ask close, short at its bid close (no look-ahead: the signal uses candle t only). Exits (6): (target, stop) in units of U from the entry mid: (1, 1), (1.5, 1), (2, 1), each with time stop T = 10 or 30 minutes; checked on every later candle's bid and ask closes (a long exits at the candle's bid when bid >= target price or bid <= stop price; a short at the ask), time exit at the candle ending at entry + T (long at its bid, short at its ask), forced exit at 15:45 ET at the latest.

**Costs (execution models, 2).** TAKER: 12 bps of notional per leg (the reported base taker tier, an assumption stated and printed, not confirmed against Kalshi's fee page) plus the crossing of the quoted spread (entry at ask or bid, exit at bid or ask, which IS each name's half-spread each side; it is measured per name from the candle quotes and printed with the 60 s recorder's markets.csv spread as a cross-check). STRESS: 2 bps more on each leg. MAKER: 5 bps per leg; entry as a passive limit at the signal side's own touch (long at bid close of candle t, short at ask close), filled only if a later candle within 5 minutes trades through it (Desk A's lane 6 fill model decides; until its file is named the maker arms print only the fill rate and the adverse-selection check, and are NOT scored); exit passive at target, taker at stop and time stop. ADVERSE SELECTION: for the signals where the maker limit filled versus those that did not, print the mean TAKER-model net of the same signal; a maker arm whose filled trades do worse than its unfilled signals is reported as adverse.

**Funding.** Not booked (a stock perp funds once a day at 16:00 ET after the window); the count of trades spanning a funding time is printed.

**Per trade net return** (long): (exit - entry) / entry - 2 x fee_bps - 0; (short): (entry - exit) / entry - fees; PF = sum of positive net returns / absolute sum of negative net returns. Halves floor(n/2) by entry time; unit = the UTC trading day (day-clustered z of the mean net return, as OIREL-X); at least 10 trade days and 100 trades pooled; BH over m_total 6,290,996 on cells with n >= 48; "no evidence at n = x" under 100.

**PASS (paper only, a shadow candidate, never live):** n >= 100, PF >= 1.3 at TAKER with both halves >= 1.0 and PF >= 1.0 at STRESS, unrounded. A signal-rate floor: under 3 trades a day pooled is parked.

**Margin report (information, not a try).** Page max leverage (stocks: 6.45x from an unconfirmed fee-page summary, minimum margin 15.5% of notional; indices, gold, BTC and ETH: the Head or Desk A supplies, else the same figure is assumed and labelled). Price terms (bps) and percent of margin both printed.
