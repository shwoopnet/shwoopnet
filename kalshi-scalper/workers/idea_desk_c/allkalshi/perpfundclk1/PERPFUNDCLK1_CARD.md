# PERPFUNDCLK1: positioning into the daily stock-perp funding print (Idea Desk C, Nov 16, 2026; the Head's order, 23:15Z). CARD FIRST: nothing read, nothing built.

**Hypothesis (a mechanism, not a chart shape).** A stock perpetual settles funding once a day at 21:00Z (16:00 ET). Holders on the PAYING side (longs when the rate is positive, shorts when it is negative) can avoid the payment by closing before the print. If enough of them do, the perp is pushed against them in the last 30 to 60 minutes (a paying long closes by SELLING at the bid), and after the print the pressure is gone and the price reverts. A trader who takes the other side early is paid for providing the liquidity those closers need.

**Counterparty (named).** The funding payer who closes into the print: the seller of the last hour (long payers) or buyer (short payers) pays the spread and the move; a payer who stays pays the funding cash. We are on the other side of the closers and, in the post-print arm, of the same flow in reverse.

**Information available before entry (no look-ahead).** The paying side is read from the PREVIOUS print's funding_rate for that name (kalshi `funding/` history, published at the previous print time; the rate for today's print is unknown until 21:00Z, and kalshi1m has no reference price, so no premium is computed): a positive previous rate means longs paid, so the expected payers today are longs; negative means shorts; a zero rate is no signal and is skipped. This is a persistence proxy for the premium and is labelled so: it is the weakest part of the card, and the forward shadow (perps30 close files and the live recorder's markets.csv hold reference_price, from Nov 16 18:18Z) can replace it with the true premium later.

**Universe.** The 9 stock perps of the owner's liquid subset (NVDA, MU, SPCX, COST, TSLA, PLTR, META, AAPL, GOOGL), whose funding print is daily at 21:00Z (the print time is read per name from the funding file; a name whose prints are not at 21:00Z is left out and counted). Indices, gold, BTC and ETH have other print times and are out.

**Arms (T = the print time of that name and day, candles end on the minute).**
- **A, pre-print drift:** entry at the candle ending T - 60 min (offset 60) or T - 30 min (offset 30), exit at the candle ending T - 5 min. Direction: AGAINST the payer (previous rate positive: short; negative: long). Ends before the print, so no funding is paid or received.
- **B, post-print reversion:** entry at the candle ending T + 1 min, exit at the candle ending T + 31 min, direction WITH the payer (previous rate positive: long). No funding crosses (entry is after the print).
- **C, through the print:** A's entry, exit at T + 31 min; the print is inside the hold, so the funding cash is booked: a short receives a positive rate (a long pays it), net += -side x rate, as in PERPSCALP2.
Thresholds on the previous rate (2): any nonzero rate, and absolute rate at or above the median nonzero previous absolute rate of the development window (a constant fixed from that window's funding files before any return is read; a count of funding rows is read first, no price). **Cells: 3 arms x 2 offsets (A and C; B has one) x 2 thresholds**: A 4, C 4, B 2 = **10 cells**; m_new 10, m_total 6,290,660 + 10 = 6,290,670 today (7,492,484 + 10 from Nov 17).

**Costs.** Taker both legs: entry at the next candle's ask (long) or bid (short), exit at bid or ask; fee 12 bp a leg; price haircut 2 bp a leg (gate) and 5 bp a leg (stress), as PERPSCALP2; funding cash counted in arm C (and printed as spanning-print count for all). Net return long (exit - entry)/entry, short (entry - exit)/entry, minus fees. Round trip about 24 bp plus the haircuts and the crossed spread: the arm needs a gross move of more than about 30 bp to clear; stock perps move far more than that into 16:00 ET on active days, which is the bet, and it will fail on quiet ones.

**Numeric prediction (written before any read).** Pooled over the nine names on days with a nonzero previous rate: arm A gross mean +10 to +20 bp a trade (a short drifts down on a positive previous rate), hit rate 53 to 58 percent; arm B gross mean +5 to +15 bp; after 24 bp of fees plus 4 bp of haircut the expected net is NEGATIVE for A and B alone unless the gross reaches 28 bp, so I expect a null; arm C's funding cash is small (stock rates are near zero). A pass needs gross of about 30 bp or more a trade with consistency across days, which I give a prior of 5 percent.

**Gate and kill (fixed now).** Pass: n >= 100 pooled trades in the cell, at least 10 trade days, PF >= 1.3 at the gate haircut with both halves >= 1.0 and PF >= 1.0 at the stress haircut, day-clustered z over trading days, BH over m_total on cells with n >= 48. **Rate gate: under 3 pooled trades a day on average, or more than 30 days to reach 100 trades, parks the card.** At most 9 names a day and a nonzero previous rate on only some days make this gate the likely binding one: the first print is the count of trading days with a nonzero previous rate per name (funding rows only, no returns) and the pooled trades a day it implies; if it is under 3 a day, the card is parked without reading any candle (kill criterion 1). Kill 2: n >= 100 and PF < 1.0 on A, B and C at the gate: FALSIFIED. Kill 3: the gross mean (before fees) of the best cell below +10 bp: no edge to clear the cost.

**Date-constant cut and holds.** Development = candles and funding prints through 2026-11-15 23:59:59Z (epoch 1794787199); 2026-11-16 is a buffer day; the forward read from 2026-11-17T00:00:00Z (epoch 1794873600), once, on 100 pooled trades or 2026-11-24, whichever is later, on the Head's go, with rows at or after 1797897600 dropped and counted (H16 holds NVDA and AAPL from 2026-12-22). Candle files: perp-data kalshi1m and funding per-day files only (the PERPSCALP2 allow line pattern; a new allow line is needed for this card: files kalshi1m and funding, the same scope). The same days as PERPSCALP1, PERPSCALP2, PERPSLANE4 and POOL1 are used: development, labelled; the funding-print clock is a different signal on those candles.

**Margin view.** The page maximum per name is the Head's list (NVDA 4.5, MU 3.3, SPCX 3.5, COST 4.9, TSLA 4.2, PLTR 3.8, META 4.5, AAPL 4.9, GOOGL 4.9; Forensics' table a67ac7f9 on claude/forensics-desk is the source for the per-name page max): percent of margin at 10, 25 and 50 percent of the page max with liquidation distance 1/L - 0.045 (maintenance assumed 4.5 percent), printed per cell as in PERPSCALP2.

**Uniqueness (7e, 7g and nearest cards).** Closest: FUNDP1 and FUND1 (funding premium as a CARRY or premium read; FUND1 was dropped by the owner on Oct 21) and card (a) FUNDING CARRY (closed on the census: stock funding rates are almost always 0): this card does not hold for carry; it trades the price flow of payers around the print clock, in minutes, and is a different mechanism (positioning into a scheduled settlement time), but it uses the same funding history field and is adjacent to FUNDP1; I rate it NEW as a timing-into-settlement mechanism and ask the Verification Analyst to score it. Not market making; not a short round trip by the owner's list (A and B are 30 to 55 minute holds, C about 90). Not on the declined list as far as I can see (the Head's ledger decides). PERPSCALP1 and PERPSCALP2 are cost-bound taker scalps on 1 minute candles: this card enters at one clock time a day, not on a price signal.

**Honest risk.** About 9 names over about 9 development days is 80 name-days at most; with a nonzero previous rate on a fraction of them, the development sample is likely under 100 trades per cell: the print will say "no evidence at n = x". The mechanism may well need the true premium (reference price) that kalshi1m lacks.
