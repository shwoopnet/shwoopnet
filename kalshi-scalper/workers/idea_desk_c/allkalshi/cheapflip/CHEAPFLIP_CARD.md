**What the VA left untried (this card's scope).** Exits as a MULTIPLE of the entry price (2x, 3x, 5x); entry at ANY time before the last 2 minutes (including earlier than 14 minutes left); bands 1c to 3c, 1c to 5c and 1c to 10c.

Only book.csv and coverage.csv are read. Nothing else.

**Rules (fixed now).** Unit = a market and a side (YES or NO) in one 15 minute window. A YES ask = 1 - the best NO bid price; a NO ask = 1 - the best YES bid price (the book carries bids only); a side's bid = its own best bid price; the size at the touch = that best bid's size. **Entry:** the FIRST poll (by receive time, polls with a usable book) at which the side's ask is inside the band [1c, upper] AND at least 120 seconds remain to the close_time, one entry per market-side-band, entry price a = the ask at that poll (ask size = the opposite best bid's size, recorded and printed, not a filter). **Exit rule for a multiple k in {2, 3, 5}:** at the first LATER poll (before the close) at which the side's bid is at or above k x a, SELL at that poll's BID (not at the target price: HS1 found prices jump through targets); otherwise HOLD to settlement (the market's result from coverage.csv, read last; a void refunds the price and keeps the fee). **Costs:** a haircut of h on each leg against the trade, h = 1c and 2c (entry at a + h, sale at the bid - h, never below 1c); fee per order at 10 contracts by the repo's fee function (engine1_core fee_up_order) on both traded legs, entry fee only when held. Labelled: candle-free tick replay, upper bound, not fill-checked; the bid size at the touch is printed as the share with 5 contracts or more.
**Table, per band (3) and per series, and pooled over series:** n (entries), days, windows; the share whose BID reaches 2x, 3x and 5x entry at any later poll before the close (independent of the haircut); the median time from entry to that first touch (seconds); the share where the bid size at the touch is 5 or more; and, for each k, the net PF of "sell at first touch of k x else hold" at h = 1c and 2c with n and window (first and last date). No pass bar, no BH survivors, no shadow path; a descriptive read reported whatever it shows.
**m counting (full).** 3 bands x 3 multiples = 9 pooled cells (each at two haircuts, which are columns); per-series rows are printed for information and are not tries; m_new 9, m_total 6,290,660 + 9 = 6,290,669 today (7,492,484 + 9 = 7,492,493 from Nov 17).

## Amendment 2 (Nov 17, 2026, before any data row was read): rulings from the blind second build's readings

(1) A poll at or after the close is never an entry or a touch (touches only strictly before the close); the count of such polls is printed (`polls_at_or_after_close`). (2) An entry that SELLS needs no result and counts; an entry that would HOLD with no result is dropped and counted per series (`held_entries_without_result`, counted once per band and k); a window with no result is counted (`windows_without_result`, `no_result_by_series`). Touch shares include entries with no result. (3) Result strings are exactly `yes`, `no`, `void`; anything else, including empty, is no result. Conflicting results for one market keep the first and are counted. (4) A crossed book is usable and not filtered; an ask of exactly 1c enters. (5) The print is JSON lines: header (m_carried, m_new, m_total, label), stats, a manifest line (sha256 of repr of the sorted (relpath, sha256) tuples of every book.csv.gz and coverage.csv.gz in the checkout, and the file count), then one line per (band, k) with a pooled cell and per-series cells carrying n, days, windows, first_day, last_day (UTC close dates), reach_share, median_touch_s (mean of the two middle values for an even count), bid_size_5_share_at_touch, ask_size_5_share_at_entry, pf_h1, pf_h2 and net_per_trade_h1, net_per_trade_h2 (the MEAN per trade in dollars at 10 contracts). PF is none for no trades and inf for gains with no loss.
