## Counts and reading
- Class A (never used as a signal): volume on perps minute bars is the only fast-clock row (crypto 76, index and metal 73, stock 21 a day); funding rate and settlement-mark gap are under 10 a day. The perps cost is flat across families at about 24 to 25 bp for the liquid names (BTC 24.5, gold 24.5, US 500 24.8, US Tech 100 24.9) and 28 to 45 bp for stocks, so a volume signal must beat roughly a quarter of a percent a round trip.
- The 15 minute non-crypto binaries cost 4.2 c a contract if sold early and 2.1 c if held to settlement (spread 1.4 c, fee 1.4 c at the median ask), so a hold-to-settle edge needs 2 c a contract. Their rows are DECLINED or DUPLICATE in the matcher.
- In-game sports cost 5.6 c a contract sold early (spread 2.0 c, fee 1.8 c).
- 15 minute non-crypto spread outside the wall is not parsed: KXINX15M and the held series are counted, not read. ETH has no candle days in the files read (its short history).
- Median spreads are quote spreads in minute bars and may not be tradable depth.
