# MECH1 fast-clock feasibility, first part (Desk C, Nov 17, 2026): signals a day and Desk A's matcher

Ordered by the owner's ruling of Nov 17 (the same signal on a new instrument class is a version): **A. signal fields never used as a signal on any market in the registry** first, then **B. new clock classes** (a clock the registry uses only with no signal), then **C. the rest, marked VERSION_NEW_MARKET, DUPLICATE or DECLINED**. Within a class, by signals a day, highest first. Counts from the clock alone (no data read); held and NOT under H20 or H21 only. Signals a day assume a fixed 10% tail trigger, at most one entry per name per 15 minutes (perps) or per window (15 minute markets), independent observations: an UPPER BOUND, clustered signals give fewer. Round trip cost columns wait for an allow line (bid and ask columns only).

Class A fields (volume, funding rate, settlement mark) are the only signal fields in the held data that no registry row uses on any market. The volume rows are the only A rows above 10 signals a day; funding and settlement-mark rows are under 10 (hourly or daily clocks), so they are fast-clock candidates only if a faster clock appears (the perps tape back-pull is not landed). Cross-venue spot (spot_elsewhere) is already in the registry, and the held spot tape covers only the held 15 minute markets.
Other caveats: the 15 minute non-crypto price-change row (276 a day) is a ceiling (about 30 polls a window, independence) and DECLINED anyway. Only HYPE, BNB and NATGAS among the 15 minute non-crypto series are unheld. The hourly ladder rows wait for a hold check (H20 names a BOOKIMB1 holdout). Sports is low confidence (seasonal game count). Stock perps are 390 minutes a day.

Cost columns (round trip, median): FASTCLOCK_COSTS.json when the cost script has run (needs an allow line).
