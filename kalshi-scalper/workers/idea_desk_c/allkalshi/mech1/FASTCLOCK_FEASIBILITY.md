Caveats: (1) the 15 minute non-crypto price-change row (275 a day) assumes about 30 polls a window and independence, so it is a ceiling; the matcher DECLINES it (D_CHEAP_REGIME_GRAMMAR_15M). (2) Only the non-crypto 15 minute series HYPE, BNB and NATGAS are unheld (H21 and H20 hold the rest); the hourly ladder row waits for a hold check of the recorder (H20 names a BOOKIMB1 holdout). (3) Sports is low confidence (seasonal game count). (4) Under 10 a day: funding print, perps open and close, time-of-day rows: listed, not fast-clock candidates. (5) The matcher's rule 7 note: a perp family on a signal already in the registry for another family is VERSION_NEW_MARKET; Desk A's open question (rule A or B) decides whether any of those counts as new.

Cost columns (round trip, median): see FASTCLOCK_COSTS.json when the cost script has run; it needs an allow line for the three nc15 series' bid and ask columns, kalshi1m bid_close and ask_close, and ingame yes_bid and yes_ask.
