**How it differs (declined list and ledger checked).** SOCCER1 and SOCCERELO1 (released) were a cheap-side (ask 0.03 to 0.30) rule against an outside price (de-vigged DraftKings, a walk-forward Elo from ESPN), held to settlement: 0 of 801 treated, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA. SOCDERIV tests model-versus-price gaps across a game's own legs 30 to 90 minutes before kickoff; ESOCTIE the e-soccer tie. SOCDRIFT: Kalshi prices only, no outside model, entry on the winner market's own price path from 24 h to 2 h before kickoff, any band up to 0.85 (no 0.88 to 0.97 favourite hold, no cheap-side-late: those are the owner's bans), unit the game, both exits stated. Ban check: not market making, not a short round trip (the exit leg is a pre-game sell hours before kickoff, the hold leg is to settlement), not cheap-side-late (every look is at least 2 h before kickoff). The in-match exposure of the hold exit is real and is the cost of H.

**Clock (the data has no kickoff).** occurrence_datetime is one value per game, schedule-shaped (:00, :15, :30, :45), and sits about 3 h after the real kickoff (EPL examples: close at match end 18:34Z, 16:07Z against occurrence 19:30Z, 17:00Z); close_time is the early close at full time and is NEVER used for timing or selection (it is after the event). K = occurrence_datetime - 3 h is an ESTIMATE of kickoff, correct to about 0.5 h. A game is dropped (counted) unless occurrence is one value across its 3 markets and on a quarter hour, and its ticker date is within one day of it. Margin: every exit mark is 2 h before K, so a 0.5 h error never reaches the match.

**Rule grid (600 rules).** Decision D = the last full hour boundary at or before K - 24 h, K - 12 h, K - 6 h (3). Entry = the ask of the latest two-sided hourly candle ending in (D - 1 h, D] for YES, the bid for NO side (a NO buy pays 1 - yes bid). Exits (2): **H** hold to settlement; **M2** sell at the bid of the hourly candle ending at the last hour boundary at or before K - 2 h (none = held to settlement, counted, printed both ways as in NBATOTAL). Bands on the bought side's ask (5): 0.10 to 0.25, 0.25 to 0.40, 0.40 to 0.55, 0.55 to 0.70, 0.70 to 0.85 (top inclusive). Sides (2): YES or NO of the outcome market. Filters (10): none; move_down, move_flat, move_up (the outcome's own mid change over the previous 6 h: at or below -2c, within 2c, at or above +2c; the anchor is the latest two-sided hourly candle ending in (D - 7 h, D - 6 h]); spread2, spread4 (ask minus bid at most 2c, 4c); vol_rise, vol_notrise (candle volume summed over (D - 3 h, D] against (D - 6 h, D - 3 h]); tie, team (outcome is the tie market or a team market). N_RULES = 3 x 2 x 5 x 2 x 10 = 600.

**Unit and gates (as NBALADDER and NBATOTAL).** The unit is the game (z and the BH p from game-level sums), at least 40 games and 10 trade days in a cell; BH ranks cells with at least 48 trades over m_carried + 600 (m_carried 6,290,660 plus the other families' new rules in the same print). Pass bar: n 100 or more, PF 1.3 or better at the 1c primary haircut with both halves 1.0 or better, PF 1.0 or better at 2c, unrounded; fee ceil(round(0.07 x n x p x (1-p) x 100, 9)) / 100 per order; label "candle replay, upper bound, not fill-checked". Halves by (close_time, ticker) order. Dollars a week at 5 contracts divided by 46.1/7 days. Under 100 trades "no evidence at n = x". Rate rule: under 3 signals a day or over 30 days to 100 trades, park or cut.

**Data.** One batched candle request per game: 3 tickers, period_interval 60 (hourly), window [K - 31 h, K - 2 h] (29 candles each, far under the 10,000 window-minute limit and the 5 MB answer limit); 919 dev games, so about 920 requests in two job files of at most 470, cap requests + 12, 5 s pacing, live endpoint only (closes from Sep 14 are after the historical cut of Sep 13). Nothing from closes on or after 2026-10-30T02:53:19Z is requested. Time fields only until the candles are cleared; results are read last.
