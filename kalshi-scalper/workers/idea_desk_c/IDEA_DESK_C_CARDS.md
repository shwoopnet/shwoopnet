## Card C1. PROPUNDER: NO on NBA player props at the strike priced nearest 50c

`PROPUNDER | family: crowd preference for the "over" on named players, mid-priced NO held to settlement (about 3 hours) | risk class: mid price (40c to 60c), 1 contract | stage Idea`

- **Published finding.** Practitioner, NOT peer reviewed and NOT verified from a data file: PFF's 2022 midseason prop review and 4for4's season-long prop review report blind unders hitting 60% to 63% over two seasons on NFL season-long player props; PFF also reports that blind NFL game-prop unders since 2020 were profitable only on passing yards (https://www.pff.com/news/nfl-betting-2022-midseason-player-prop-performance-review, https://www.4for4.com/2023/preseason/key-winning-season-long-player-props). No academic study of prop over bias was found. Peer-reviewed support for the mechanism (bettors prefer overs, the share on the over rises with the line) is Paul and Weinbach's NBA and NFL totals work, where the effect has DECAYED since about 2010 (Kelly and Chen).
- **Why it is the only outside effect above the line.** At 63% the PF at 50c after the fee is 1.56; at 60% it is 1.38. The peer-reviewed totals effects are 1.35 at their peak and 0.81 now. So this card stands on an unverified number for a different product (NFL season-long, not NBA single-game) and is very likely smaller on Kalshi.
- **Kalshi family.** NBA player props (`KXNBAPTS`, `KXNBAREB`, `KXNBAAST`; YES means "X or more", so YES is the over). On Nov 10: points 8 events, 389 markets, about 6.0 million contracts; rebounds about 1.8 million (Desk B's listing). Family row: TOUCHED, PROPS1 not enough data (it read the CHEAP side, 9 treated; control cheap side PF 0.68 on 397). No test has read the mid strike.
- **Rule (two sentences).** At a fixed minute 60 minutes before the listed start, for each player's points ladder take the one strike whose YES mid is nearest 50c, inside 40c to 60c with a spread of 3c or less, and buy NO at the NO ask plus 1c, 1 contract. Hold to settlement; the other side is fans buying a star to reach a round number, and any maker who quotes the ladder symmetrically.
- **Events.** Cheap window (closes from about 2026-09-07): the NBA season is about 22 days old, about 170 games; at most 2 players a team with a mid strike, so about 700 bets, clustered by game (a fast or slow game moves every prop the same way). That is BELOW the about 1,000 to 1,200 the bar needs; last season's props, if Kalshi listed them, cost one candle request a market (about 5,000 requests, five runs) and are the only way to a verdict this month. Forward: about 30 bets a night.
