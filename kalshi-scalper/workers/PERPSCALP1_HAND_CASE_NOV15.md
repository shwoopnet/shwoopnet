# PERPSCALP1 (card (c) of SINGLE_STOCK_PERPS_CARDS_NOV15.md): hand case, written BEFORE the code (Idea Desk A, Nov 15, 2026)
**Rule (fixed now):** per name, on the 1 minute bid and ask close candles of `perp-data/kalshi1m` (candles with a missing bid or ask are dropped, a gap is never bridged). mid_t = (bid_close + ask_close) / 2. sigma1 = the sample standard deviation (n - 1) of the 1 minute log returns of the mid over the 60 candles ending 5 minutes BEFORE the signal candle (at least 40 valid returns, else no signal); sigma5 = sigma1 x sqrt(5). **Signal** at candle t (end time, UTC weekday, 14:35:00Z to 20:45:00Z inclusive, which is 09:35 to 15:45 ET while the clocks are on EST, true for all of November 2026 after Nov 1): the candle ending exactly 300 s earlier exists and |ln(mid_t / mid_(t-300))| >= 3 sigma5, and (ask_t - bid_t) / mid_t <= 0.003; at most one entry per name per 15 minutes (the next entry no earlier than 15 minutes after the last entry candle); no position open. **Arm T (trend):** long if the move is up, short if down; **arm R (reversion):** the opposite side. Entry at candle t: long at ask_t, short at bid_t. **Exit:** target = mid_t x (1 + sigma5) for a long (mid_t x (1 - sigma5) for a short); stop = mid_t x (1 - sigma5) for a long ((1 + sigma5) for a short); looked at on every later candle ending after t and at most t + 600 s: a long exits at that candle's BID when bid >= target or bid <= stop (first candle met), a short exits at that candle's ASK when ask <= target or ask >= stop; otherwise the time exit at the candle ending exactly t + 600 s (or the last candle present at or before it), long at its bid, short at its ask. **Haircut:** both legs cross by h of price against the trade, h = 0, 1 bp, 2 bp (a stand-in for the binaries' 1c and 2c; the Head rules whether it stays). **Fee: 12 bps of notional per leg (reported taker base tier, docs/PERPS_THEORY.md, NOT confirmed against Kalshi's own fee page: an assumption stated here and printed)**, so a round trip costs 24 bps; there is no per-order rounding on a notional fee. Net return per trade = (exit - entry) / entry for a long ((entry - exit) / entry for a short) minus 0.0024. PF = sum of positive net returns / absolute sum of negative ones. Halves floor(n/2) by entry time; day-clustered z of the mean net return by UTC entry day (the OIREL-X formula), trade days printed; "no evidence at n = x" under 100 trades; each name x arm is one try.
**Funding is not booked in this read** (8 prints a day, mostly 0; a position of at most 10 minutes crosses a funding time rarely): the count of trades that span a funding time (21:00Z) is printed.
