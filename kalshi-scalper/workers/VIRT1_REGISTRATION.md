Research only. Nothing places an order. No live code, no functions/, no index.html, no L1, no promote verb. Verdict words: FALSIFIED, NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, NOT_RUNNABLE, DUPLICATE. **Standing rules: cheap side only (buy at ask 3c to 30c, taker, held to settlement); no buy-the-favourite-and-hold result is computed or printed; fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded UP to the cent per order, C = 10, stress line fees x1.2; no 15 minute crypto or gold candle is used.** Honest prior of a pass: about 3% (this is the ESPORTM1 shape, which was FALSIFIED on every number that existed; lead 2 of 5).

## 4. Split (fixed now, by date, own split)
Events pooled and ordered by scheduled start. **Development = start before 2026-10-28T00:00:00Z** (first two thirds by count, 13,133 events, rounded to a date). **Held out = start at or after 2026-10-28T00:00:00Z** (6,567 events): never fetched, never rated into a scored row, no candle, no result stored in this test's cache; it is read ONCE on or after **2026-11-21** (14 days after this registration) by the same `virt1.py run --heldout`, with K, dbar and every rule frozen. Halves of development: **D1 = start before 2026-10-18T12:00:00Z, D2 = from then to the cut** (the median development start). K and dbar are fitted on D1 only (outcomes only, no price), so D2 is out of sample for them.

## 5. Question, counterparty, prior
Is the cheap side of a virtual game priced below what a player rating built only from earlier settled Kalshi results implies? **Counterparty:** retail bettors on 8 to 15 minute virtual games who cannot see the operator's skill gap between controllers, and who bet the famous team name and not the controller. **Honest alternative (expected):** market makers see the same results history (and a faster, richer one: head to head, recent form, the controller's current streak), so the quote beats a Kalshi-only rating; a model at least 3 times the ask flags the rating's error, and the cheap tail then loses to the favourite-longshot bias and the fee (pooled cheap-side baseline about -5.5c to -6.2c). **Prior of a pass: 3%.**

## 6. Reference (fixed now; per series, so a name in one game never meets another game)
Two components, player and team, both starting at 1500, scale 400. **Player** = the gamertag in the last parentheses of the market's `yes_sub_title` ("Chelsea (Zaroth)" is player Zaroth, team Chelsea); Russia Liga Pro has no parentheses: the whole name is the player and there is no team component. **Rating of a side = player rating + team rating** (Liga Pro: player only). Expected score of A: E = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B - R_A) / 400)), with R the sum. After a game each present component is updated by K/2 x (score - E): score 1 for a win, 0 for a loss and **0.5 for a draw (eSoccer only)**. `K` is chosen once per series from {16, 24, 32, 48} by the lowest log-loss of the walk-forward probabilities against settled outcomes (no price) on the D1 events whose two players each have at least 8 earlier games, then frozen. A game updates the ratings when its earliest `close_time` is at or before the look. A pair is "ready" when both players have at least 8 earlier settled games in that series (a team needs none).
**Draw structure (eSoccer, explicit).** Three markets per event, each a YES/NO contract: A wins, TIE, B wins; exactly one resolves YES. From E: P(draw) = dbar x 2 x min(E, 1-E) (zero draw chance for a lopsided pair, dbar at an even pair), P(A wins) = E - P(draw)/2, P(B wins) = 1 - E - P(draw)/2, with dbar = (draws among D1 ready games) / (sum of 2 min(E, 1-E) over the same games) at the chosen K, clipped to [0, 0.95]. Every eSoccer market (including TIE) is a candidate; a NO side's model probability is 1 minus the market's YES probability (so it includes the draw mass: NO on "A wins" pays when it is B or a tie). eBasketball and Liga Pro have two markets and no draw (an event with a TIE market or any other shape is dropped and counted); an event whose first listed result is neither yes nor no is dropped.

## 7. Universe, look, trade (fixed now)
Development events (section 4) whose pair is ready. **Candle fetch (to respect the shared 429 limit):** ready development events in md5-of-event-ticker order inside each series, the three series interleaved round robin, at most 1,000 events per series, at most 100 minutes of fetching, 1.3 seconds or more between requests (more on a 429, stop and report on a repeated block, never route around one). eSoccer: one batch request for the event's three tickers; eBasketball and Liga Pro: one request for the first market only (the other side is its mirror). Window: start minus 3600 s to start minus 60 s, 1 minute candles, `api.elections` fallback as in `api._get`. **Look:** the last candle ending at or before start minus 120 s with a valid two-sided quote (0 < yes bid < yes ask < 1, closing values, **ask minus bid at most 0.10**, a registered guard against stale wide quotes); no such candle: no row, counted. Both sides priced from that quote (YES ask; NO ask = 1 - YES bid). **Cheap side:** the side whose ask is in [0.03, 0.30]; if neither, no row. **Treated trade:** model probability of that side at least 3 x its ask, ask at most 0.30; buy 10 contracts at the ask, held to settlement; **at most one treated trade per event (the highest model/ask ratio)**. **Control:** every cheap-side row of the same fetched events. Cluster: the event; also by US Central day. A quote is not a fill; stated. No other prefix, universe, K grid, ratio, spread guard or look time is tried after the scored read.

## 8. Numeric prediction (written before any result)
Treated win rate at or below the mean ask; treated mean net about -4c to -6c per contract, PF about 0.5 to 0.7 in each half; control about -5c; the Elo Brier no better than the quote mid. If a half beats the bar I check first whether a game was already in play at the look (the ticker time is the scheduled start; a late quote could be live) and whether eSoccer draws are mispriced as a group (TIE-yes rows) before believing anything.

## 9. Kill rules
**FALSIFIED** if the treated PF after fees is under 1.5 in either half (D1 or D2), or the treated mean net is not above 0 in both halves. **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** if under 150 treated trades in what was fetched (with the projection to all ready development events). **NOT_YET_FALSIFIED** only if both halves have PF at least 1.5 and mean above 0; it is a lead only with the event-clustered z at least 5.3 (running count deflation) and treated mean above control mean, and a lead means the frozen held-out read, never a trade. Fees x1.2 PF reported.
