# BINPATH PROTOCOL (registered Nov 7, 2026, before any rule is scored)

Research only. MODEL RESULT, never a live figure: every number this study produces is a Binance spot path priced by a fitted quote model, never a Kalshi price or fill. No trading code, no key, no order, no fetching (local files only: `data/hist_binance/`, `data/fresh.sqlite` development region). Code: `src/scalper/scan_binance.py` (a new adapter on `src/scalper/scan.py`; scan.py is not edited).

## Question
Do rules on the underlying BTC or ETH minute path predict the sign of the 15 minute window outcome beyond what the contract price already implies? Kalshi KXBTC15M / KXETH15M settle YES if the price at the close is above the window's open reference (the strike), so the outcome is path-determined. Binance BTCUSDT / ETHUSDT spot is a PROXY for Kalshi's reference, with a proxy error measured below.

## Data and windows (calendar cuts on the CLOSE time, UTC, windows are the quarter hour closes)
- W1 closes 2019-01-01 to 2020-12-31, W2 2021-01-01 to 2022-12-31, W3 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31. Held out: closes from 2025-01-01 (2025 plus 2026 to Nov 6). Read ONCE through `scan.guard_check` / `guard_record`; the dev loader refuses any close from 2025-01-01. This task does NOT read the held-out; it writes the frozen list and stops.
- Window: open reference K = open of the 1 minute bar that opens at open_ts; decision price P = close of the last bar that ended at or before close_ts - L; settlement price = close of the bar that ends at close_ts. YES wins if settlement > K (a tie, K equal, is NO; negligible). Every feature uses bars that ended at or before the decision.
- Void rows: any of the open bar, the settle bar, or the 16 closes ending at the decision bar is missing (gaps are not filled). Zero-volume bars are kept.
- Disclosure of a held-out touch: the quote model and the proxy check use Binance bars of 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-27, which lie inside the held-out slice. They are used for prices (the model target is Kalshi's ask) and for the outcome AGREEMENT count only; no rule is scored on them, and no rule is chosen with them. The held-out read later still includes those days.

## Proxy error (reported, development Kalshi windows only: KXBTC15M and KXETH15M closing before 2026-10-28, fresh.sqlite)
Agreement rate of the Binance window outcome with Kalshi's settled result (overall, by series, by distance-to-strike band, and among the windows the quote model prices into the band at 6 minutes left); also the agreement of Binance's settle price against Kalshi's stored strike, and the difference between Binance's window open and Kalshi's strike in bp. The Kalshi reference is an average of the last 60 seconds of a multi exchange index, not a Binance minute close; the report says so.

## Quote model (fitted on Kalshi development data, discovery third only)
Form (fixed now): YES mid = Phi(c0_L + c1_L x z), z = d / (sigma15 x sqrt(tau)), d = 1e4 x ln(P / K) in bp, tau = minutes left (6 or 10), sigma15 = standard deviation of the 15 one minute log returns ending at the decision bar, in bp, floored at 1 bp. (c0, c1) are fitted separately for L = 360 and 600 by least squares on the YES mid (bid+ask)/2 in cents over valid quotes (harness `_valid_quote`), BTC and ETH pooled. Half spread h is the median half spread (ask minus bid over 2) in three mid zones (mid under 0.30, 0.30 to 0.70, over 0.70), fitted on the same rows. Side ask = side mid + h, rounded to the cent and clipped to 1c to 99c; a NO side mid is 1 minus the YES mid. Fit set: Kalshi development windows from the first 4 of the 6 UTC dates of the discovery third (harness thirds of the 17 development dates are 6, 6, 5); held-out slice: the other 2 dates of W1 (the form is NOT changed after looking at it); the final parameters are refit on all 6 W1 dates, and its out-of-sample error is reported on W2 and W3 development dates (drift check; no refit). Reported: RMSE and MAE of the mid in cents (overall and in the zones the entries use), and the agreement of the band entry set (model ask 88c to 97c vs Kalshi's actual ask 88c to 97c): precision, recall, and the PF of model priced vs actually priced entries on the same Kalshi windows. Gate: if the mid MAE on the held-out slice is over 8c, scoring is aborted and the study is reported as QUOTE MODEL INVALID. Regime caveat in every result: the model is fitted on 2026-10-11 to 10-16 Kalshi data and the market regime in 2019 to 2024 may differ (Kalshi's 15 minute crypto markets did not exist before Dec 2025; no price exists for the test years).

## Entry rule (no discretion)
At look L in {360 s, 600 s} before the close, buy ONE contract of the side whose modelled ask is 88c to 97c (band arm; at most one side qualifies), or 3c to 30c (cheap arm, a second arm, its own table). Taker at the modelled ask, held to settlement, no exit. Fee: the harness `kalshi_fee_cents`, rounded per order, 1 contract (stress x1.2). Net cents per contract = (win - ask) x 100 - fee.

## Features (every one from bars that ended at or before the decision; side signed s = +1 YES, -1 NO where marked)
`ask` (modelled side ask), `dist` (s x d, bp), `zd` (s x z), `r1 r3 r5 r9` (s x log return over 1, 3, 5, 9 minutes, bp), `vol15 vol60` (stdev of 1 minute log returns, bp), `rng5 rng15` (high minus low over the last 5 and 15 bars as bp of price), `mrise3` (side mid change over 3 minutes under the quote model, cents, the rise3 analogue), `mrng5` (max minus min of the modelled side mid over the six minute marks back to 5 minutes, the rng5 analogue), `vlr15` (log of the last 15 minutes volume over the average 15 minute volume of the last 24 hours), `tbi5` (s x taker buy imbalance over 5 bars, (2 x taker_buy_vol - vol) / vol), `fmin` (minutes since the last 00, 08 or 16 UTC funding time), `fr_last` (bp, last settled Binance perp funding rate; missing in 2019), `hour`; categorical `hod4`, `dow`. A window in a gap yields a missing value and its atoms are false.

## Rule grammar and bar (the harness, unchanged)
1, 2 or 3 atoms on distinct features (0.2, 0.4, 0.4), `ge` or `le` at the 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90 quantile of the feature over W1 rows only (cut table hashed, never re-estimated), categorical equality, direction yes or no (the side bought, band rows only), look 360 or 600. Survivor = PF 1.5 or better in EACH of W1, W2, W3 after fees, at least 40 trades per window, 150 total, 3 distinct dates per window (the harness bar, `scan.BAR`, not raised). A survivor is labelled deflated when its day clustered z over all development cells reaches the one-sided Bonferroni z at 0.05 / N, N the harness `n_total()` read back from disk after the run (the lead's prior 730,489 plus PERPGEN and scan ledgers plus this study's). Only deflated survivors can ever be a CANDIDATE at the held-out read; an undeflated one is at most a LEAD. Stage counts reported per run: rules passing W1 alone, W1 and W2, all three (the screen), then the exact float64 re-score.
Audits on every survivor (development data only): (1) truncation: delete every bar after the decision and recompute the features for 200 random rows, they must be identical; (2) reverse control: the OPPOSITE side bought at its own modelled ask on the same windows must have pooled PF under 1.0; (3) fee stress x1.2 pooled PF at or above 1.0. Only survivors passing all three are frozen with `scan.freeze`. The frozen list is written and committed; the held-out is not read here.

## Runs and N accounting
Four datasets, scored separately: `binpath_BTC_band`, `binpath_ETH_band`, `binpath_BTC_cheap`, `binpath_ETH_cheap`. Plan: 4 runs of 5,000 distinct rules on each band dataset (40,000) and 2 runs of 5,000 on each cheap dataset (20,000), 60,000 in all (at least 5,000 per series per run; more only if registered here first). Each run writes its rule ids to `workers/scan_ledger/` and closes the file BEFORE scoring (harness behaviour); one unconditional baseline line per dataset and look (the band or cheap rule with no atom, 4 x 2 x 2 = 16 ids with direction yes and no) is also ledgered because the baseline is a scored try. Planned total 60,016, so N becomes about 1,290,559 plus whatever other workers append, z bar about 5.3.

## Prediction and kill criteria (stated before scoring)
Expected: ZERO validated survivors in all four datasets (confidence about 90%); zero deflated survivors (about 95%). The unconditional band PF in each window is expected between 0.85 and 1.15, because a quote model fitted on 6 days cannot price 6 years exactly and the 88c to 97c band pays 3c to 12c against a 3c to 12c loss; any rule clearing PF 1.5 in all three windows would more likely be a quote model artefact (a feature correlated with model error) than an edge, and the reverse control plus the held-out read are what separate them. Kill: (a) QUOTE MODEL INVALID (mid MAE over 8c on the held-out slice) aborts scoring; (b) a truncation audit mismatch on any survivor voids that survivor and stops the study for review; (c) the harness selftest failing stops everything; (d) no run may be re-drawn after looking at its result.

## What this can and cannot say
It can say how often rules on the underlying path beat the contract price a model implies, over 6 years and several regimes. It cannot say anything about a Kalshi price, fill, spread, depth, or fee beyond the model's stated error, and nothing here is evidence for a live change. A pass at the held-out would only permit a forward paper test with a named counterparty.
