# Literature scan for published edges (V2, Nov 11, 2026; about 10 searches, terms-checked sources only; several figures from search summaries, unverified)
Result: nothing new and testable. Each published item maps to something already done or someone else's row.
1. Favourite-longshot bias (Burgi, Deng, Whelan, "Makers and Takers", UCD WP2025_19): the same finding as L1 (favourite) and the cheap-side tail; duplicates under the charter. Maker side loses less than taker side pre-fee (about -10% vs -32%, summary only): our MAKER_ENTRIES_RESEARCH / MAKERSHADOW1 already found resting entries are adversely selected (unfilled signals are the winners).
2. Late-window averaging lock on the 60 s settlement average (Kalshi contract terms, an oddsshopper write-up that itself concludes prices are about fair): covered by CFRTI1 (index rebuild, FALSIFIED pooled, BTC lead on watch) and LAG2; a clean test needs the paid BRTI feed.
3. Fee structure (peak near 50c): a cost filter, duplicate of the 88c to 97c rule.
4. Strike ladder monotonicity and cross-strike lock: README 5987 found 0 violations in 238 strikes; LADCONV1 done; THREELEG1 not feasible (grids do not line up).
5. Option-implied binary value vs prediction market (Portnaya, arXiv, Polymarket data): Deribit options are Refinement's row (OPT1).
6. Quote lead-lag (OpenMarket, arXiv): a latency edge (16 to 19 ms), not testable on our data.
7. Kalshi CPI accuracy (Fed FEDS paper): accuracy, not mispricing; about 12 prints a year, untestable.
8. Weather NWS lag (vendor blog, low quality): Refinement's family.
Gaps: no credible source on time-of-day effects, market-making inventory models for event contracts, or silver, WTI, copper, gas and FX contracts. Probe-ready candidates from the literature: 0.
