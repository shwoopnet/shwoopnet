## 3. Rule (fixed now)
- Universe: events of the census with a kickoff time found in PROPS1's NFL `game` table (match by Eastern date and team set; Kalshi codes mapped to ESPN abbreviations; unmatched games dropped and counted).
- Order of fetching: games sorted by md5(event ticker), ascending; for each game ALL its volume rungs are fetched (the ladder sum needs the whole ladder); a game counts only when every rung has been fetched. Time box 2.5 hours of fetching in total (one extension of at most 60 minutes only if throughput is under 8 a minute). 1-2 threads, polite, backoff on 429.
- Look: candles window kickoff minus 6 hours to kickoff, 1 minute; the last candle that ends at or before kickoff minus 120 s and has a valid quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1, spread at most 0.10) is the look.
- Trade (the pooled rule): every player rung whose YES ask at the look is in [0.03, 0.30]; per game at most 2, chosen by sha1(ticker) ascending (no selection on price or player). Buy YES at the ask, 10 contracts, held to settlement, hard limit 0.30. Fee 0.07*C*p*(1-p) rounded up per order; net per contract = win - ask - fee/10. Result read from Kalshi `result` once, at scoring.
- Ladder sum S of a game = sum of YES asks over ALL fetched player rungs with a valid look (NOTD excluded, any price). Expected scorers E = mean number of distinct players with at least one rushing or receiving touchdown per game, from ESPN box scores of the FIRST TWO THIRDS (by kickoff) of the matched census games, fixed before any Kalshi outcome is joined. Under-round game: S < E. Terciles of S: cuts from fetched games in the first two thirds by kickoff only.
- Pooled rule statistics: n, win vs mean price, mean net per contract with game-clustered and date-clustered SE and 95% bounds, profit factor, halves (by game kickoff median among traded games), fee x1.2, win rate by ask bucket (3-10, 10-20, 20-30c), tercile table, mean S/E.
- Filter rule: the same trades restricted to under-round games (S < E). Reported with the same statistics.
