# SOFR1 (registered Nov 10, 2026, 3:15 PM CT, before any KXSOFRD market is listed or any outcome or candle is read): daily SOFR ladder vs persistence on the NY Fed SOFR history
Research only, public keyless data, no order. Cheap side only (YES ask 3c to 30c). Not favourite-and-hold.
Series: KXSOFRD (daily, Kalshi public /series list Nov 10). Information source: the New York Fed published SOFR history (public API, no key; reachability to be checked at run time with one request; if unreachable the information source is built from the settled ladders themselves as in AAAGAS1, no outside data).
Model fixed now: mean = the most recent SOFR published before the decision time (published 08:00 ET the next business day, so the value for the previous business day is known at the decision); sd = max(0.01 percentage points, the sample sd of the previous 60 daily changes). P(SOFR_D > K) = 1 - Phi((K - mean)/sd). One variant only (M0, persistence), so the calendar jumps (month end, mid-month tax dates) are NOT modelled: that is the point, whether makers price them.
Entry: decision candle = the one ending closest to 12 h before the close (earlier on ties); buy YES at the ask when 0.03 <= ask <= 0.30, spread <= 0.05, and model P > ask + fee + 0.02 (fee = ceil(0.07*C*p*(1-p)*100)/C/100, C = 10). Hold to settlement, one contract; the NO side is the favourite side and is not tested. Strikes in the universe: those within -0.05 to +0.15 percentage points of the last published value (fixed before any result).
Scoring: cash PF first, mean cents per contract, event-clustered z (cluster = settlement day); development = first two thirds of scored days by date, the last third read ONCE only if development shows PF >= 1.5 with n >= 100 trades on >= 30 days and z at or above the bar. Cells 1; ledger N about 5,186 (z bar about 4.27).
Details that depend on data not yet seen (strike grid, what date's SOFR a market settles on, the close time) are fixed by an Amendment written BEFORE any candle or result is read, after one /markets list call and the rules text of one market.
Prediction before running: fails (prior 8%): the ladder is priced by makers who see the same published SOFR, and the jump dates are on a public calendar.

## Amendment 1 (Nov 10, 2026, 2:45 PM CT, before any candle was fetched or any outcome was read; the only market data seen is the rules text and result of four markets printed by the rule probe, which are not used)
- A KXSOFRD market with ticker date D settles on the SOFR for D-1 (effective date), closes at 11:59Z or 12:59Z on D (before the 08:00 ET publication). Two kinds per day: "at least x" (floor_strike set, YES if SOFR >= x) and "at most x" (floor_strike None, YES if SOFR <= x), x on a 0.01 grid, both written `T<x>` in the ticker.
- The NY Fed API (markets.newyorkfed.org, public, keyless; answered 200 with 112 daily rates from 2026-06-01) is the information source. Decision time = the candle ending closest to 12 h before the close; the latest published rate then is the last effective date before D-1 (published the morning of D-1): mean = that rate. sd = max(0.01, sample sd of the previous 60 business-day changes) in percentage points.
- SOFR is published to 2 decimals, so P(YES) for "at least x" = 1 - Phi((x - 0.005 - mean)/sd) and for "at most x" = Phi((x + 0.005 - mean)/sd).
- Universe: both kinds, strikes with |x - mean| <= 0.15 percentage points, days from 2026-07-01 (earlier days only supply the sd). Entry, fee, scoring, pass bar and N unchanged (1 cell).
- Tickers with an -E suffix (exact-value markets, e.g. KXSOFRD-26SEP10-E3.65) are not part of the ladder and are excluded; only -T tickers are used (a structural rule, no outcome used).
