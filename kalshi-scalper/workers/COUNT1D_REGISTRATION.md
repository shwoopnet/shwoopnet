# COUNT1D registration (Strategy Research V2, written 2026-11-03 BEFORE any quote, candle or result of a forward KXTRUTHSOCIALD period was read; research only, forward only)

Brief: the daily Truth Social post-count brackets (KXTRUTHSOCIALD), the COUNT1 pace model, with the count taken from CNN's public archive under definition D1. Counterparty claim (unchanged from COUNT1): retail anchors on the last period's final count and under-prices a busy day's upper brackets. OWNER RULE (strict): cheap side only, no favourite result: every entry is a bracket with ask 3c to 30c.

That 19 of 19 is a post-hoc observation: D1 did not win the registered selection (the split put every daily event in the confirmation half). So it is a lead, and this test re-confirms it forward before any money question is asked.
**Periods used: ONLY daily periods whose ET calendar day starts on or after 2026-11-04 00:00 ET.** The 19 matched daily periods, every COUNT1 development and holdout period, and every period before 2026-11-04 are never used for any trading statistic (not for the rule, not for the control, not for the source gate). No forward-period quote or result has been read as of this commit.

## Rule COUNT1D (one rule, fixed now; the COUNT1 structure unchanged)
- Series KXTRUTHSOCIALD. Period = the ET calendar day (start/end parsed from the market rules text; a DST day is 23 or 25 hours). Strike semantics: `between` lo..hi inclusive on the total, `less` total < cap_strike, `greater` total > floor_strike, `greater_or_equal` total >= floor_strike.
- Look = 12:00 ET (start + 12 h, 50% of a normal day). Count so far c = number of archive posts (definition D1: every post, no filter) with `created_at` in [start, look - 10 minutes].
- Pace model, parameters frozen, no tuning: profile[h] = mean archive posts per hour for ET hour h over the 28 full ET days before the period start; E_sofar / E_rem = sum of the profile over the elapsed / remaining hours (partial hours pro rata); rho = clip((c + 10) / (E_sofar + 10), 0.5, 2.0); remaining count R ~ negative binomial with mean mu = E_rem x rho and variance mu + mu^2 / r, r = 10; total = c + R; bracket probability from that distribution. (Sensitivity r = 4 and r = 30 reported, never used to choose.)
- Entry: signal quote = ask close of the last 1 minute candle ending at or before the look (at most 60 minutes old); FILL = ask close of the first candle ending after the look (within 15 minutes). A trade needs fill ask in [0.03, 0.30] (hard limit 0.30). Signal: p_model >= 3 x fill ask. At most 2 brackets per day (highest p_model / ask), 1 contract each, held to settlement (the market's `result`, no early exit). A market with no valid quote is skipped and counted.
- Fees: 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent with C = 10, divided by 10 for one contract (p = fill ask). Net per contract = payoff - ask - fee.
- Control: every bracket of the same days with fill ask in [0.03, 0.30], equal weight, one contract each.
- Code is `src/scalper/count1.py` (model, fee, select, fill_ask, bracket_prob, unchanged since the COUNT1 commit) imported by `src/scalper/count1d_fwd.py`. No parameter of COUNT1 is edited.

## SOURCE gate (runs every day on forward data; stated up front)
For every forward day that has settled: archive total over the ET day (definition D1) is inside the winning bracket, yes or no. Once 20 forward days have settled, the share of days inside must be at least 90%, evaluated on every prefix of settled days in date order from 20 on. If ANY prefix of 20 or more days is under 90%, the test is VOID, latched: no later recovery un-voids it, no forward read is scored as a rule result, and a new registration is needed. Before 20 settled days the gate is PENDING (it only counts). Command: `python -m scalper.count1d_fwd gate` (reads which bracket won, no price, no P&L; it is not the read and may be run daily).

## Take-up (trades per day): UNMEASURED
COUNT1's 0.42 trades per WEEKLY period (11 trades on 26 events) is not transferable: a day has 8 brackets and a different count distribution, and the daily series never produced a scored trade (0 trades on 6 complete events). Plausible range stated before any forward quote: **0.1 to 0.6 trades a day** (a guess, not an estimate). At that range 150 trades need 250 to 1,500 days; by the due date 2027-03-01 (117 days after 2026-11-04) expect 12 to 70 trades. So the expected read is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA. First look at the take-up, on information only (no outcomes): at day 20 of snapshots, `python -m scalper.count1d_fwd takeup` prints the number of days on which the rule's signal fired, from the snapshot CSV, and refuses before 20 snapshot days. It decides nothing; it changes no parameter.

## Daily snapshot (a recorder-style local CSV, information only)
`python -m scalper.count1d_fwd snapshot [--date YYYY-MM-DD]` for the forward day (default: today in ET; refuses a date before 2026-11-04 and refuses before 12:16 ET of that day, so the 1 minute candles cover the fill window exactly as the read does). Writes one row per bracket of that day to `data/count1d_snapshots.csv` (git-ignored; the coordinator keeps it by hand): date, ticker, strike type and strikes, look time, archive count c, mu, p_model, signal ask, fill ask, in-band flag, signal flag, selected flag (top 2), the archive fetch time and its latest post time. It never writes a result and never an outcome. Idempotent per (date, ticker): a second run on the same day replaces nothing and writes nothing new. Public Kalshi API and the CNN archive only, one request at a time with 0.4 s sleeps and the client's 429 back-off. To go on the daily recorder watchlist later, `scripts/` would need one line that runs `python -m scalper.count1d_fwd snapshot` at 12:20 ET and pushes the CSV to the `quote-data` branch; that is a separate change by main, not made here.

## The one read (guard: `scalper.fwdread`)
`python -m scalper.count1d_fwd forward`: due at 150 forward trades OR on 2027-03-01, whichever is first (the trade count is computed from quotes only, no outcome, by the same selection); refuses before then (`--force-info` prints the counts only), refuses a second read, writes a started marker first and prints the README line to append (never appends). The read scores every settled forward day once: trades, win rate, mean net, PF, halves (first half and second half of the forward days by date), control, r = 4 / 30 sensitivity, skipped markets, source gate.

## Kill rules / verdicts (fixed now)
- **VOID**: the source gate fails as above. Whatever the P&L.
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA**: fewer than 150 trades at the read. Report n, mean net, PF, and the months-to-go at the observed take-up (150 / (trades per day x 30.4)).
- **FALSIFIED**: at 150 or more trades, mean net after fees not above 0, or PF under 1.5, or mean net not above 0 in BOTH halves, or the control does as well (rule mean net minus control mean net under 1c).
- **SURVIVES (forward only)**: gate not void and all of the above clear. Nothing here promotes anything. Multiple testing: one rule, N +2 (the coordinator keeps the running total, about 730,235, z bar about 5.27); no verdict can be a pass at n under 150 whatever the PF. A pass would allow only a capped probe of the COUNT1 shape (1 contract, ask 3c to 30c, at most 2 a day, worst day 60c, kill after 20 fills under -$3.00 or 40 fills with PF under 1.0), registered separately.
