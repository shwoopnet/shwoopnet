# INGAMELAG1 PROTOCOL (registered Nov 8, 2026, FORWARD test, before any recorder row exists)

Research only. Data: the in-game recorder of PR 744 (branch `sports-data`, `ingame/YYYY-MM-DD/HHMM/`, columns t_recv_ms, server_date, event, kind, ticker, strike, yes_bid, yes_ask, bid_size, ask_size, last_price, volume; NBA moneyline both teams plus 2 spread and 2 total markets nearest 50c at the first poll, 20 s when a game is hot else 120 s). No Kalshi history is used. No order, no key.
**Mechanism and counterparty:** inside one NBA game the moneyline, spread and total markets are quoted by the same makers; when the moneyline jumps, a side market that has not yet moved is a stale quote. Counterparty: the slow maker of the side market. Overlap check: ESPN1 (ESPN win probability vs the moneyline), SWING1/SC1 (reversion after a jump, single market), SERIESLAG1 (series after the game); cross-market lag INSIDE a game was never tested.
## Event and rule (fixed)
Event: a poll where the home-team moneyline YES mid has moved by at least 8c versus its mid 3 minutes (or the earliest poll within 3 to 4 minutes) earlier, after tip-off. Signal side: the team whose moneyline rose. Model (fitted on the first 40 percent of events in time, never re-fitted): for each side market kind, the regression slope s of its YES mid change over the same window on the moneyline mid change. Entry: at the first poll after the event, buy YES of the side market in the direction of the move (spread: the moving team's spread YES; total: not used, direction undefined, reported as information only) if its mid has moved by less than half of s x (moneyline move) AND its ask is 3c to 30c. One contract per event per kind, taker at the ask, exit at the bid 10 minutes later (or settlement if the game ends first); fee: ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1-p) x 100) cents per 10 contracts per side divided by 10 on entry and on exit, stress x1.2.
## Judging and cuts
Events in time order: first 40 percent fit the slopes, next 30 percent development, last 30 percent held out and read ONCE. Unit = game. `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` under 150 development trades or under 60 games. `FALSIFIED` if mean net not above 0, PF under 1.5 or game-clustered z under 2.5. 1 try (spread kind) plus the total kind as information.
## Prediction
Makers re-quote all markets of a game within seconds: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA or FALSIFIED (about 96 percent). Read when 60 games exist (the recorder gets about 6 to 12 NBA games a day: about two weeks after it goes live). The deliverable is the measured catch-up time of side markets after a moneyline jump.
