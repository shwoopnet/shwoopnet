## 0. Common terms (as Desk A's sheet, section 0, unless stated)
- **Size and limit.** 10 contracts (the wheel's size). Limit = the Tie's YES ask at the signal instant plus 0.01 (the registered ask + 1c). YES ask = 1 minus the best NO bid of the Tie market.
- **Walk, fill price, fillable.** The order meets the Tie's NO bids (ask = 1 minus price), sorted by ask; "offers 10 at or under the limit" = sizes of levels with ask at or under limit + 1e-9 sum to 10 or more; walked price = the size-weighted average ask of the first 10. Fill price = the HIGHER of the walked prices at the decision poll and the second poll. The decision poll and the second poll must each offer 10 at or under the limit; else a miss, counted, not scored.
- **Second poll.** The next fast poll whose `polls_tickers.csv` asked set contains the Tie ticker, at most 4 s after the decision poll (the fast tier polls about every 3.1 s, 97.9% of cycle gaps under 4 s in the first run). Later or none: a miss, counted.
- **The book at a poll** = the last stored row of that ticker (`book_fast.csv`) at or before the poll's `t_recv_ms`, AND the poll's asked set (`polls_tickers.csv`: the latest earlier row of the same tier and chunk) must contain the ticker, AND the poll's `http_status` is 200 with `tickers_answered` equal to `tickers_asked`. Otherwise that poll does not exist for the ticker.
- **Fee and cash.** One function, `fee_up_order(p, 10)` = ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1 - p) x 100 - 1e-9) / 100. Cash = 10 x (win - p) - fee. Hold to settlement; no stop, no target, no time exit.
- **Result (one actor, one trigger).** The Tie market's `result` in the recorder's own settled or closed list reads (`answers/`, every 10 min with a 2 h look-back and, after PR 860, every 6 h with a 48 h look-back). Missing at the block read: the Chief of Staff's list job built at the block read, exactly as Desk A's section 0. 7 days without yes or no: void (price refunded, fee kept), counted.
- **Exchange-time offset.** As Desk A's section 0 (median over the 120 most recent rows at or before the poll of header + 0.5 - t_recv), from this run folder only.
- **Any missing item is "no signal"** (counted by reason), never carried forward.

## 1. The rule
- **Market and event.** KXESOCCERGAME events with exactly three markets (two teams and one `-TIE`). S = the event key's date and four-digit clock read as US Eastern time. **Eligible** only if the Tie market's `open_time` (coverage file) is 10 to 240 minutes before S.
- **Decision instant** D = S - 60 s, exchange time. **Decision poll** = the last fast poll with exchange time at or before D and at most 10 s before it that exists for all three tickers (section 0). None: no signal.
- **Signal instant.** The first of D_k = S - 60k, k = 1..10, at which the stored books of all three markets are two-sided (0 < YES bid < YES ask < 1), each book read at the last poll at or before D_k that exists for it. None: "not two-sided", no signal. (This is the registered step-back, on books instead of candles; the order still goes at D.)
- **Quantities at the signal instant.** f = round(the larger of the two team mids, 6) (NOTE 11); bucket from the frozen edges (0, 0.45, 0.55, 0.65, 0.75, 1.0). Tie ask, Tie bid, spread = ask - bid.
- **Base rate b (walk-forward, known at D).** The Tie share among EARLIER events in the same bucket whose Tie market's close + 300 s is at or before D. The earlier events are: (a) the registered DEVELOPMENT events (closes 2026-09-12 to before 2026-10-23, f from their candles exactly as the frozen scorer computes it), and (b) forward events of THIS place (from its start date, f from the books as above). **The registered held-out window (2026-10-23 to before 2026-11-13) and anything between it and the place's start are NOT used**, so no held-out result is read before its approved read (rule (h)); this stays so after the held-out read too, so the definition never changes. A forward event whose f cannot be computed (no books) is left out of b, counted. A bucket with under 300 earlier events: base-building, no signal.
- **Trade** if b - Tie ask >= 0.10 and spread <= 0.04 (both with the 1e-9 tolerance), buy YES on the Tie at limit = Tie ask + 0.01, 10 contracts, fill-checked at D (section 0). Never at 88c or above in practice (the Tie trades about 15c to 35c); if a Tie ask is ever 0.88 or more the lane's 150 signal rule applies.
- **Log source and stop.** `wheelbook/<date>/<HHMM>/` on the `wheelbook-<year>w<week>` branches, to 2027-01-31 (the recorder's stop).

## 2. What is still missing or assumed (named)
1. **Per-poll asked tickers:** fixed by PR 860 (`polls_tickers.csv`), but no run folder with it had landed when this was written; the place starts at the first such folder.
2. **Closed markets polled to the far expiry:** fixed by PR 860 (a market leaving the open list is closed). Before it, settled markets filled the 90-ticker batches; nothing in this rule reads a settled market's book.
3. **Mixed f sources in b:** development events' f comes from minute candles, forward events' f from books. A difference between the two shifts events across bucket edges; it is the same rule on both, stated, not corrected.
4. **Gaps between recorder jobs:** an event whose D falls in a gap has no decision poll (no signal, counted); its result can still enter b through the 48 h wide read or the block-read list job, but only if its f is known, so a gap removes it from b too.
