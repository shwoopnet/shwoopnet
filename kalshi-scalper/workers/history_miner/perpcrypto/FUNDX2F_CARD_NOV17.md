## Amendment 2 (2026-11-17 ~02:10Z, answering Forensics' two open points before any real read)
- (a) A print that makes no trade (no row at or before it, no row strictly after it, no entry row within 5,400 s, or no exit row within 30 minutes of the hold's end) does NOT hold the ticker's position open: the next print may signal. Only a trade that was built (entry and exit found) holds the position until its exit instant; a print strictly before that exit instant makes no trade, and a print exactly at the exit instant may signal.
- (b) The gate days are UTC dates of the ENTRY row's time: a trade counts toward the gate when its entry row time is from 2026-11-17T00:00Z up to but not including 2026-11-24T00:00Z.

## Amendment 3 (2026-11-17 ~02:20Z, Auditor B 01:26Z; text only, the audited code is unchanged and no script sha changes)
1. Funding boundary: funding is booked on prints STRICTLY between the entry and the exit instants, on both sides. The sentence in the FUNDX2 card's base text ("up to and including the exit row", the Funding cash line) is SUPERSEDED by the Funding boundary clause of its Amendment 2. The FUNDX2 card itself is not edited (its hash is the audited one); this line is the note.
2. Net formula, additive on notional: net = side x (exit - entry) / entry - 2 x fee - 2 x haircut - side x funding, where side is +1 long and -1 short, fee 0.0012 per side (taker; 0.0005 per side is the maker stress), haircut 0.0002 per fill (primary) and 0.0005 per fill (stress), and funding is the sum of the labelled rates on the prints strictly between entry and exit (positive = longs pay). The haircut is NOT a multiplier on the fill prices.
3. Entry and exit rows: the entry row is the first row strictly after the print and must be at most 5,400 s after the signal row (the last row at or before the print); the entry row must be two sided (bid and ask present AND 0 < bid <= ask: a locked row, bid = ask, is two sided; a crossed row or a zero bid is not); entry is at the far touch (buy at the ask, sell at the bid). The exit row is the first row at least H hours after the entry row and must be within 1,800 s of that target, two sided in the same sense (bid and ask present AND 0 < bid <= ask); exit is at the near touch (sell at the bid for a long, buy at the ask for a short). Otherwise the print makes no trade (counted).
4. Gate days (as Amendment 2 (b)): UTC calendar days 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-23, counted by the UTC date of the ENTRY row's time. A print that makes no trade holds no position.
5. Run command order (Auditor B 01:27Z): the per-date funding files are listed on the command line IN DATE ORDER (funding/2026-11-17.csv.gz first, then each later date), because the first print at an instant is kept; the same order is used for the snapshot day files.
