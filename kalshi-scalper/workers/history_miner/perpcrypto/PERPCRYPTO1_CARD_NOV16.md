## Two families, 16 cells (all counted as tries)
FUNDX (needs the labelled funding history): a funding print is an extreme when its rate is at the clamp (|rate| >= the market's funding_cap from the recorder) or in the top or bottom decile of that market's OWN earlier prints (expanding window, at least 60 earlier prints, no print after the decision is used). Entry at the first price after the print (the next minute candle for BTC, the next snapshot for the others); fade (short when longs pay, long when shorts pay) or follow; hold H in {8 h, 24 h}; funding paid or received during the hold is booked at the printed rates. Cells: trigger {decile, clamp} x direction {fade, follow} x hold {8, 24} = 8.
BASIS (needs only the recorder's price and reference_price; forward from the live snapshots, history only on BTC with a spot source): b = (price - reference_price) / reference_price at each snapshot. When |b| >= th, th in {5 bps, 10 bps}: converge (short when the perp is above the reference, long when below) or follow; hold H in {1 h, 4 h}; entry at the snapshot's far touch (ask to buy, bid to sell). Cells: th {5, 10} x direction {converge, follow} x hold {1, 4} = 8. One position per market at a time; no overlap.
Mechanism and counterparty (stated, not shown): funding at an extreme means one side is paying heavily to be positioned, a crowded trade that may unwind (fade) or that may trend (follow); a basis above the reference is paid for by the longs and may close by convergence (fade) or signal a squeeze (follow). Prior low: Kalshi perps are thin and the funding rule is Kalshi's own; the record on earlier perps tests (PERP1 to PERP3) found no edge after costs.

## Costs and margin (printed in every result)
Taker 12 bps and maker 5 bps a side, both ASSUMED until Kalshi's fee page is read (round trip 24 and 10 bps of notional), plus the per fill haircut ladder 0.02 percent and 0.05 percent per fill; funding booked from the data. The bar is the perps convention: PF 1.3 or better at taker with the 0.02 percent haircut with both halves 1.0 or better, AND PF 1.0 or better at taker with the 0.05 percent haircut; a pass only at maker is flagged, not counted. Results are in PF of notional PLUS margin terms at 10, 25 and 50 percent of the maximum leverage: the margin return (net per trade over the margin), the share of trades whose adverse excursion inside the hold reaches the liquidation distance (a liquidation loses the whole margin), and the fees as a share of margin (table in MARGIN_TABLE.md). Leverage does not change the PF of notional; it changes the margin return and the liquidation count.

## Gate, rank and holds
The 3-a-day gate is pooled over the six markets and fixed once on the first 7 distinct trade days (decision counts only), stored and committed before the first outcome print with its sha256 quoted; a cell under 3.0 trades a day is DROPPED as a rate failure, not a bar failure. Survivors are ranked by dollars a week at 5 contracts (a paper figure; contracts and the contract size in dollars to be read from Kalshi's perps article, else stated as notional of 5 units). n >= 100, day z >= 2.0, BH over every cell ever tried, and m printed first (the carried count of the day plus 16).
Holds: the BTC perp is not a 15 minute BTC market and I know of no card holding the perp tickers; Auditor B to confirm (the Head's note). PERPMON1 (parked) holds the stock perps only. Forward window: from the first day the labelled data exists; development windows are fixed by date constants in the loader before any run.
Uniqueness: not lead-lag, not favourite-and-hold, not taker order flow; closest existing PERP1 to PERP3 (daily trend on perps, FALSIFIED) and PERPMON1 (stock perps, a weekend move); 7g: the FUNDX and BASIS cells are different signals; within each family the follow and fade cells are versions of each other and are counted as tries.
