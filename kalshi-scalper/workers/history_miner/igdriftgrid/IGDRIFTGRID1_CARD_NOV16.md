# IGDRIFTGRID1: in-game drift grid on NBA and NHL (History Miner, registered Nov 16, 2026 ~16:35Z, before any forward row; the Head's 16:19Z order: one family spec, a 3-a-day gate, ranked by dollars a week)

Question: inside a game, does a market's own drift over the last 30 minutes continue (follow) or reverse (fade)? NBA and NHL fire every day, so a rule here can clear 3 a day.
Counterparty and prediction: slow repricing after scoring runs (follow) or overreaction to them (fade); prediction: at least one cell has 2c PF >= 1.3 at n >= 100 and a measured rate >= 3 a day. Kill: a cell is dropped when its measured rate over the first 7 forward days is under 3.0 a day, or its first read has 2c PF under 1.0 (kill_fast).
Uniqueness (7d, 7e, 7g): not lead-lag (one market's own path), not favourite-and-hold (entries 20c to 80c either side), no trade tape. Closest existing: IGLATEDRIFT1 (late follow at tip + 120 min, 8c: that exact cell is EXCLUDED here so nothing is tried twice) and PREDRIFT1 (pre-game, no evidence). Different timing and a fade side; flagged as a possible version family of IGLATEDRIFT1 under 7g: the Head decides if the fade cells count as new.

Data and holds: the same as IGLATEDRIFT1 (forward window 2026-11-17T00:00Z to before 2026-12-15T00:00Z, ingame_fwd_load.py e639ad63, IG_FWD_RESULTS job d535923d, Auditor B's signed hold table). Markets: NBA totals, NHL totals, NHL moneyline (first team ticker in string order). No NFL, no spreads (this order says NBA and NHL; NFL spread is held by BOOKS2).
Same games feed IGFADE2, IGLATEDRIFT1 and these cells: one sample of games, n not to be added across specs.

Grid (11 rules, all counted as tries): decision offset D in {45, 75, 120} minutes after tip (tip = occurrence_datetime minus 3 h, schedule test as IGLATEDRIFT1); lookback L = 30 minutes; threshold in {0.05, 0.08}; direction in {follow, fade}. The cell (D 120, 0.08, follow) is IGLATEDRIFT1 and is excluded: 3 x 2 x 2 - 1 = 11.
Rule per cell: quotes q_now = the last two-sided poll in (tip + D min - 300 s, tip + D min] and q_before = the same for tip + (D - 30) min. d = mid(q_now) - mid(q_before). If |d| >= threshold: follow buys the side d moved toward, fade the other; at q_now's ask (YES ask, or 1 - yes_bid), only if 0.20 to 0.80. One trade per game and kind, hold to settlement. Ten contracts, haircuts 1c and 2c, fee per order, as the signed plugin.
Gate and ranking: a cell must show a measured rate (trades per day with games, first 7 forward days) of 3.0 or more, else it is DROPPED that day and printed as a rate failure, not a bar failure. Survivors are ranked by dollars a week at 5 contracts = rate x 7 x (mean net per contract at 2c, from the cash of 10 contracts divided by 10) x 5, a paper figure. The pass bar is the usual one: n >= 100, 2c PF >= 1.3, each half >= 1.0, day z >= 2.0, BH over every cell ever tried.

Hand case (invented, tip T): follow, D 75, 0.05: polls (T + 2700: 0.40/0.42) and (T + 4500: 0.47/0.49): d +0.07, follow buys YES at 0.49; result yes, h 2c: paid 0.51, fee ceil(17.493) = 0.18, cash 10 x 0.49 - 0.18 = +4.72. Fade at the same d buys NO at 1 - 0.47 = 0.53, in range; result yes: paid 0.55, fee ceil(17.325) = 0.18, cash -10 x 0.55 - 0.18 = -5.68. d +0.04: no trade at 0.05. Ask 0.85: no trade. A poll only 400 s before the mark: no quote.
