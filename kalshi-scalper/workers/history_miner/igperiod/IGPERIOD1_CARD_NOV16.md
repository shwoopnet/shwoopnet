# IGPERIOD1: NHL period-boundary moves (History Miner, registered Nov 16, 2026 ~18:50Z, before any forward row; the Head's 18:27Z order)

Question: when an NHL market comes back to life after an intermission, does the first move away from its frozen level continue (follow) or reverse (fade)?
Why this could exist (stated, not shown): during an intermission nobody scores and the book is stale; the first poll after it is the first fresh price after 15 to 18 minutes of no information, so the move may be an overshoot by the few who trade first (fade) or the correction of a stale level (follow). No named counterparty; prior low. Prediction: a cell with 2c PF >= 1.3 at n >= 100 and a measured rate >= 3 games a day. Kill: a cell is dropped when its rate over the first 7 distinct forward tip days (fixed once, stored) is under 3.0 games a day, or its first read has 2c PF under 1.0.

THE DATA HAS NO GAME CLOCK. A period end cannot be read from a field. The boundary is therefore DEFINED FROM THE PRICE PATH: a quiet stretch of one ticker is a run of two-sided polls, each at most 360 s after the one before, whose mids all stay within 0.005 of the first poll's mid, lasting at least 720 s; it ends at the first poll whose mid moves more than 0.005 from the flat level (the "after" poll). The schedule tip (occurrence_datetime minus 3 h, schedule test as the other specs, a proxy with truth unproven) only BOUNDS where to look: the first two quiet stretches that START between tip + 30 min and tip + 140 min are boundary 1 and boundary 2. A real period boundary found this way can be a dead stretch of play (a false boundary); that is stated here and left to the data: the rate and the PF are of this DEFINED event, not of "intermissions".
Cadence caveat: cold games are polled every 5 minutes (PR 960), hot games about every 20 s, so a stretch is easier to see in cold polling and the "after" poll can be up to 5 minutes late.

Rule per cell (boundary b in {1, 2}, threshold th in {0.02, 0.04}, direction in {follow, fade}; 8 cells): d = mid(after poll) - flat level. If |d| >= th: follow buys the side d moved toward, fade the other, at the after poll's ask (YES ask, or 1 - yes_bid), only if 0.20 to 0.80. One trade per game, kind and boundary. Hold to settlement. Ten contracts, haircuts 1c and 2c, fee per order as the signed plugin. Markets: NHL totals (all tickers of the event in string order, the first with a stretch decides) and the NHL moneyline (the first team ticker in string order). No NBA, no NFL (basketball has no periods of this kind in this card), no spreads.
Gate and rank: as IGDRIFTGRID1: the gate is fixed once on the first 7 distinct tip days (decision counts only), stored and committed before the first outcome print with its sha256 quoted; the unit is games; survivors ranked by dollars a week at 5 contracts = games a day x 7 x mean net per contract at 2c x 5, a paper figure; the pass bar is n >= 100, 2c PF >= 1.3, each half >= 1.0, day z >= 2.0, BH over every cell ever tried.
Not a duplicate of IGDRIFTGRID1 (and how it differs): IGDRIFTGRID1 measures the drift over a fixed 30 minutes at three schedule offsets (tip + 45, 75, 120) on NBA and NHL totals and the NHL moneyline, whatever the play; IGPERIOD1 fires only at a data-defined stretch end, at any time offset, NHL only, and the move is measured against a frozen level over at least 12 minutes, not over a fixed window. Closest: IGFADE2 (a fade of a move between two polls no more than 60 s apart, any time): this card keys on the end of a quiet stretch with no 60 s pair requirement, so the two overlap only where a stretch ends in a jump. 7g: the fade cells may be counted as versions of IGFADE2 and of IGDRIFTGRID1's fade cells by the Head's rule; m is printed first as 8 for this spec and 33 with the other in-game specs (IGFADE2 1, IGLATEDRIFT1 1, IGDRIFTGRID1 11, IGDRIFTSPREAD1 12). Same games: n is not added across specs.
Data and holds: as IGDRIFTGRID1 (forward window 2026-11-17T00:00Z to before 2026-12-15T00:00Z, ingame_fwd_load.py e639ad63, IG_FWD_RESULTS d535923d, Auditor B's hold table); ticker wall KXNHLTOTAL and KXNHLGAME.
Hand case (invented, tip T): polls of one NHL total ticker at T + 2400, 2700, 3000, 3300 (mid 0.51 flat, 0.50/0.52) and T + 3360 (0.55/0.57): stretch from T + 2400 lasting 900 s, ending at the T + 3360 poll; d = 0.56 - 0.51 = +0.05. Cell (1, 0.04, follow) buys YES at 0.57; result yes, h 2c: paid 0.59, fee ceil(16.933) = 0.17, cash 10 x 0.41 - 0.17 = +3.93. Cell (1, 0.04, fade) buys NO at 1 - 0.55 = 0.45; result yes, h 2c: paid 0.47, fee ceil(17.437) = 0.18, cash -4.70 - 0.18 = -4.88. Threshold 0.02 also fires; a stretch of only 600 s (T + 2400 to T + 3000): no boundary; a gap of 400 s inside the run: the run breaks; a stretch starting at tip + 20 min: not a boundary (too early).
