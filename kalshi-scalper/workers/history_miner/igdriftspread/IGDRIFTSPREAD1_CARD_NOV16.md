# IGDRIFTSPREAD1: in-game drift grid on NBA and NHL spreads (History Miner, registered Nov 16, 2026 ~17:40Z, before any forward row; the Head's 17:22Z go)

Same question, same rule and same gate as IGDRIFTGRID1 (signed, Auditor B, commit 993dbccb5), on the spread markets: inside a game, does a spread market's own drift over the last 30 minutes continue (follow) or reverse (fade)?
Why a second spec: IGDRIFTGRID1's wall is NBA and NHL totals plus the NHL moneyline; the spreads were out of its wall (NFL spread is held by BOOKS2 and stays out; the NBA moneyline is held by SPORTS-PRE to 2027-01-01 and stays out).
Counterparty and prediction: slow repricing after scoring runs (follow) or overreaction (fade); prediction: a cell with 2c PF >= 1.3 at n >= 100 and a measured rate >= 3 games a day. Kill: a cell is dropped when its rate over the first 7 distinct forward tip days (fixed once, stored) is under 3.0 games a day, or its first read has 2c PF under 1.0.
Unit: games (one trade per game and kind, so trades a day = games a day); the rate, the gate and the ranking are all in games.
Uniqueness: not lead-lag (one market's own path), not favourite-and-hold (entries 20c to 80c, either side). Closest existing: IGDRIFTGRID1 (same cells on totals and the NHL moneyline) and IGLATEDRIFT1; the cell (120, 0.08, follow) is NOT excluded here because IGLATEDRIFT1 has no spread leg, so there are 12 cells. 7g: the spread cells are the same signal on a related market, so they are counted as tries: m is printed first as 12 for this spec, 23 with IGDRIFTGRID1's 11, 24 with IGLATEDRIFT1's cell; BH counts every cell of all in-game specs. Same games as IGFADE2, IGLATEDRIFT1 and IGDRIFTGRID1: one sample of games, n is not added across specs.
Data and holds: as IGDRIFTGRID1 (forward window 2026-11-17T00:00Z to before 2026-12-15T00:00Z, ingame_fwd_load.py e639ad63, IG_FWD_RESULTS d535923d, Auditor B's hold table). Ticker wall: KXNBASPREAD and KXNHLSPREAD only (Auditor B 15:51Z: no card holds them).
Rule per cell: the audited IGDRIFTGRID1 decision applied to a spread event as if it were a totals event (every ticker of the event in string order, the first with both quotes decides; tip = occurrence_datetime minus 3 h, schedule test; quotes at tip + D and 30 min earlier; |d| >= threshold; follow or fade; at the decision poll's ask, 0.20 to 0.80; hold; ten contracts, haircuts 1c and 2c). D in {45, 75, 120} min, threshold in {0.05, 0.08}, direction in {follow, fade}: 12 cells.
Gate and rank: as IGDRIFTGRID1: gate fixed once on the first 7 distinct tip days (decision counts only), stored and committed before the first outcome print with its sha256 quoted; the rank by dollars a week at 5 contracts uses all days and only cells whose stored gate is ok.
Hand case: a spread event with polls (T + 2700: 0.40/0.42) and (T + 4500: 0.47/0.49) at D 75, 0.05, follow buys YES at 0.49 (result yes, h 2c: paid 0.51, fee 0.18, cash 10 x 0.49 - 0.18 = +4.72), exactly as the totals case in IGDRIFTGRID1; NFL, moneyline and total events are not in this wall.
