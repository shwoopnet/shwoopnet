# NFLCOMBO1 PROTOCOL (registered Nov 8, 2026, before any NFL moneyline, rushing or receiving candle or result is fetched)

Research only. No trading code, no key, no order. Public Kalshi `/historical` only. Owner structure (via main, Nov 8): per game, **Combo A** = Team A to win + Team A QB passing yards over a floor line + one Team A rusher's (variant R) or receiver's (variant W) yards over a floor line; **Combo B** = the same for Team B; test each ALONE and the two-sided pair (A and B bought together), one costed trade each, fees on every leg at 10 contracts, held to settlement. This is a favourite-and-hold style structure when the legs are 80c to 97c (the owner's standing ban is for live trading; this is research only and any live use needs the owner to lift it explicitly). Overlap: NFLPC1 (cheap pair legs, QB x total or spread), NFLFAV1 (queued), PROPS1, ARB5; the moneyline plus QB plus rusher or receiver stack has never been scored.
**Principle:** the legs settle separately, so the combo's expected value is the sum of its legs' expected values; correlation changes the shape of the outcome (how often all legs win together), not the mean. The test therefore reports (1) mean net per combo and per leg, (2) PF, (3) tail profile: share of combos where all legs win, share where none win, share where a team wins but a prop leg fails, mean win, mean loss, worst single combo, loss-to-win ratio, and (4) for the pair: guaranteed cost (the two combos' total price), the payoff when A wins, when B wins, when both lose, and the share of games in each state.

## Legs (chosen from tickers and listings only, never from a price)
Team codes come from the moneyline market suffixes of the game. Leg 1: the team's moneyline YES. Leg 2: the QB of that team with the most listed passing-yards markets in the game, his LOWEST listed strike (a floor line). Leg 3: variant R = the team's rusher with the highest listed top strike, his lowest strike; variant W = same for receiving yards. A game or team missing any leg is dropped and counted. Each leg is quoted at the last hourly candle ending at or before (the QB leg's close_time minus 4 hours); a leg without a two-sided quote there drops the combo. Entry at each leg's ask, fee `ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1-p) x 100 - 1e-9)` cents per 10-contract order divided by 10, stress x1.2, held to settlement. All legs YES.

## Tests (fixed, no rule search; one primary read per line, no variants after the result)
Lines: A alone, B alone, A+B pair, for each of variants R and W (the primary line is pooled A and B alone per variant; the pair lines are reported as described). 4 tries counted (R and W, each: pooled single combos; pair). Cuts: games by date, first 2/3 development, last third held out and read ONCE only if a line is NOT_YET_FALSIFIED. Judging: unit = game; `NOT_ENOUGH_DATA` under 100 development combos; `FALSIFIED` if development mean net per combo is not above 0, or PF under 1.5, or game-clustered z under 2.5. Information for every line: the tail profile above.
