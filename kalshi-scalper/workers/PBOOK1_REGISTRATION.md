# PBOOK1: touch-size imbalance predicts the next-sample mid, after crossing the spread
Strategy Research V2 worker, registered 2026-11-06 before any return, gap closure, spread-ratio event or imbalance trade was computed (only the spread census used to pick the universe was run). Research only, discovery only, nothing live.

**Data.** origin/perp-data recorder (53 usable development snapshots, 3 to 7 a day, irregular, NOT hourly, so "the next sample" is 0.5 to 12 hours later: the first later sample in that window; none in the window means no trade). Columns bid, ask, bid_size, ask_size, reference_price from the recorder. Screens: status active, 0 < bid <= ask <= 2 x bid (drops the int64 no-ask sentinel, as in fundcarry.py), sizes finite, non-negative, not both zero. No outside data.
**Universe, chosen on cost only.** Tickers with at least 40 valid development snapshots whose MEDIAN recorded spread is at most 5 bp of mid: KXBTCPERP, KXETHPERP, KXGOLDPERP, KXUS500PERP, KXUSTECH100PERP, KXSILVERPERP, KXSOLPERP, KXWTIFUTURE, KXXRPPERP, KXCOPPERPERP (census run before registration: median spreads 0.37 to 4.11 bp; every other perp is 5.7 bp or wider; single stocks are inactive in development).
**Cost.** Every trade crosses the spread at the recorded bid and ask at entry and again at exit (buy the ask, sell the bid), plus a fee of 12 bp a side (verdict, the unverified placeholder), 5 bp a side as the stress read. Funding is NOT recorded and is ignored (stated, direction unknown).
**Dates.** Recorded dates are 2026-10-21 to 2026-11-06 (17). The LAST THIRD (6 dates, 2026-11-01 onward) is HELD OUT and never loaded; the cut is 2026-10-31. Development 11 dates, D1 = 10-21 to 10-26, D2 = 10-27 to 10-31. Scorer workers/pbook/pbook.py (loader reuses workers/fundcarry/fundcarry.py, which opens only dates up to the cut). Times in US Central in the result (UTC minus 5 hours to Oct 31).
**Statistics.** Day clustered z (mean of day means over their standard error). N tried +3 (one rule per test; PBOOK1 to PBOOK3), running about 730,466, deflated z bar 5.27.
**Verdicts.** NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if fewer than 60 events or fewer than 10 event days (the date the history will be enough: at about 4 samples a day and these trigger rates, a fresh development third of at least 20 days and about 10 untouched dates is about 2026-11-20 with a re-registered cut, later if the trigger is rare). FALSIFIED if the mean net return after cost is at or below 0 on the full development set, or positive but under the z bar or with either half non-positive. LEAD only if mean net is above 0 in D1 and D2 and day z is at least 5.27, and even then only a candidate for the held-out dates, never a trade.

**Mechanism and counterparty.** A thin-book perp market maker who has built inventory skews: it posts more size on the side it wants to shed. If the bid size is much larger than the ask size, the quote is leaning up and the next sample mid is higher (and the reverse). The trader on the other side of the edge is the maker who is slow to re-centre.
**Rule.** At sample t, imbalance I = (bid_size - ask_size) / (bid_size + ask_size). If I >= 0.5 go long (buy the ask), if I <= -0.5 go short (sell the bid); exit at the next sample at the opposite touch. One trade per ticker per sample.
**Numeric prediction.** Gross move in the heavy side direction about +0.5 bp, net about -26 bp (spread plus 24 bp of fees), win rate under 40 percent. Gross is near zero because touch sizes at these books are quantised and the sample gap is hours.
**Kill.** As in the verdict rules above.
