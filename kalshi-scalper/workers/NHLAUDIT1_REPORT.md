# NHLAUDIT1 report (2026-11-04): audit of the NHLALL1 combined read. Verdict by the pre-committed rule: GO (marginal) to the frozen forward read; not a promotion, N +0
Read-only. Script `src/scalper/nhlaudit1.py` (independent, does not import `nhlall1.py`), numbers `workers/NHLAUDIT1_RESULT.json`. Reads only the append-only cache and `data/nhlg1.sqlite` for results of already-fetched tickers. No Kalshi fetch, no NHLG1 or post-cut event touched, no live code.

## Decision rule (committed before the arithmetic was run)
GO to the frozen forward read only if (a) the independent re-implementation matches, (b) leave-3-out net stays above 0, (c) the median of 200 salt re-draws of the 2-per-game selection is above +3c. NO-GO otherwise. GO means "spend the free forward read", nothing more.

## 1. Code audit of `nhlall1.py`
- **Re-implementation matches exactly.** 179 fully fetched games, 75 usable (at least 8 valid quotes), n 150, 41 wins, win 27.33% at mean price 18.99c, net +7.245c, PF 1.50709, game SE 3.681c, lower 95% bound +0.0299c, date-clustered z 2.196 (49 dates), fees x1.2 +7.03c and lower bound -0.19c. Same to every printed digit as `NHLALL1_RESULT2.json`.
- **Side/win coding: correct.** `win = (result == yes) == (side == yes)`; all 150 trades are YES side (0 NO), so the ALTLAD1 NO-as-win bug cannot occur here. The 120 s cut is `end_ts <= start - 120` on the candle END time, so a candle ending exactly 120 s before is allowed and nothing later is read; the look lag is min 120 s, median 300 s, max 3,480 s, 24.7% of trades priced from a quote more than 10 minutes old (stale carry-forward is possible; not a lookahead, but the ask may not have been takeable at that size).
- **Fee: correct and slightly conservative.** ceil to the cent per 10-contract order. Rounding off changes the mean by +0.05c; zero fee is +8.34c.
- **Time basis:** the look uses ESPN start only; ESPN start earlier than the true puck drop would make the look earlier (conservative), later would leak. Not testable without a second time source (no Kalshi fetch allowed). Lag distribution gives no sign of leakage (the 300 s and 600 s looks give +6.0c and +5.8c, descriptive, below).
- **sha1 selection is outcome-independent** (ticker only; verified, no price used). 2 trades in all 75 games, 0 duplicate tickers, no game appears twice.
- **Usable-ladder filter:** at the rung level, a missing quote is not outcome related (YES rate kept 24.7% vs dropped 25.9%, n 1,110 and 332). At the game level, usable games are somewhat higher scoring (6.72 goals vs 6.11 in 104 unusable, SD 2.5, about 1.5 SE), plausible via liquidity; only 75 of 179 fully fetched games are usable, so the result describes liquid games only.
- **Residual untestable risk (flag):** a rung enters the universe only if its lifetime `volume > 0`, which includes in-game and post-goal trading. A rung that never traded would be a loser excluded for a reason that correlates with the outcome. The listing in `kmarket` holds no zero-volume rungs at all (0 of 1,442 in these games), so the size of the bias cannot be measured without a new fetch. Winners had far higher volume (median 596 vs 136). The direction is upward, magnitude unknown. This is a real, unquantified inflation channel and should be checked in the forward read by using only pre-puck-drop volume or the full listing.
- Other: game dedupe fine; date key `ev[10:17]` correct; halves are a median split of the combined set (not the registered 61/14 blocks), fine. Defect that does NOT inflate: the stop at exactly 150 uses quotes only.

## 2. Arithmetic
- **Winners:** 41 winners at prices 8c to 29c (median 22c, no one huge payout). Top 5 winners are only 14% of gross profit, so no single-winner dependence. Leave-k-out (remove k biggest-payoff winners, n-k left): k=1 +6.68c PF 1.46; k=2 +6.11c PF 1.42; **k=3 +5.54c PF 1.38**; k=5 +4.39c PF 1.30. Strictest version (winners flipped to losses, n fixed): k=3 +5.25c, k=5 +3.91c.
- **Bootstrap** (4,000 resamples): by game 2.5% to 97.5% = +0.22c to +14.8c, P(mean <= 0) 2.1%, P(PF < 1.5) 50%; by date +0.84c to +13.4c, P(mean <= 0) 1.3%.
- **Salt re-draw (200 different salts for the same 75 games, n 150 each time):** net median **+3.27c** (5% to 95%: -1.26c to +8.70c), PF median 1.22 (0.92 to 1.62), lower 95% bound median -3.35c, only 12% of draws have a lower bound above 0, only 11% reach PF 1.5, 89.5% have net above 0. **The registered draw sits at the 91st percentile of its own re-draws**, so the headline +7.2c is a lucky-side draw of an underlying cheap-rung mean of about **+2.2c** (uncapped, n 846: +2.17c, lower -0.67c, z 1.50). Cap-2 by hash is a random subsample, so the honest central estimate is 2 to 3c, not 7c. The +0.03c lower bound does not survive redraws.
- **Calibration inside the 150 pool:** 3-10c: win 14.3% at 8.1c (n 21, +5.6c); 10-20c: 31.3% at 14.2c (n 48, +16.2c); 20-30c: 28.4% at 24.7c (n 81, +2.4c). Uncapped (n 846): 11.6% at 6.8c (+4.4c), 20.3% at 14.2c (+5.2c), 25.5% at 24.8c (-0.7c). The edge sits in the 3-20c rungs; the 20-30c half of the pool is break-even, consistent with the favourite-longshot pattern and with the first block.
- **Zero-winner games:** 40 of 75 (53%) have no winner, 29 have one, 6 have two (a game wins at most 2 trades; outcome within a game is positively dependent through game script, which the game clustering handles).

## 3. Decision
(a) match: yes. (b) leave-3-out net +5.54c (> 0): yes. (c) re-draw median +3.27c (> +3c): yes, by 0.27c. **GO by the letter of the rule**, but narrowly, and with these conditions for the forward read: freeze the rule and the 2.75 / 3.66 cuts; judge it on the UNCAPPED all-cheap line as well as the cap-2 line (expect about +2c, not +7c; size the pass bar on that); fix the volume-universe issue by listing rungs without in-game volume; counter-evidence (NFL anytime TD -5.48c, MLB HR -2.17c) stands. The registered LEAD should be read as "z 2 on a selected hypothesis, lucky draw", not as an edge. No promote verb, z bar 5.27 unchanged.
