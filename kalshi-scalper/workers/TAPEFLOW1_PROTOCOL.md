## Rule, frozen now (nothing is tuned; no parameter is scanned)
- Time grid: every 30 s of tape time (the hourly tape is polled every 30 s, so nothing finer is honest) for each ticker with a book.
- Flow: V_yes(t) = contracts whose taker_outcome_side is "yes" and V_no(t) = taker side "no", summed over trades with created_time in (t - 5 min, t]. A trade's count is count_fp.
- Baseline: for each series and each 5 minute bucket of time to the close, the MEDIAN over all (ticker, grid time) cells of the trailing 5 minute volume of EITHER side (yes and no cells pooled), computed from DEVELOPMENT events only and used unchanged for the held-out. (The median is per series, not per market, because a single market has too few cells; the time-to-close bucket stops the rise of volume near the close from making every late cell a signal.)
- Signal at t: the side s with the larger V_s/(3 x baseline) satisfies V_s >= 3 x baseline, V_s > V_other and V_s >= 50 contracts (a fixed floor, so a zero median cannot make a single print a signal). 3x and 50 are fixed. At most one open order or position per ticker; a new signal waits until the ticker is flat. Signals only from t >= the ticker's first book row + 5 minutes and while t <= T - 4 min (T = the close).
- Entry: fade, so flow "yes" (takers buying YES) means we buy NO, flow "no" means we buy YES. Post ONE contract at that moment's best bid of OUR side and join the back of the queue (queue ahead = the visible size at that price). Never repriced. Cancelled unfilled 5 minutes after the signal or at T - 4 min, whichever first.
- Fill (the mmsim1 queue model): our bid at own-price p is filled when the cumulative contracts traded after the post time at that price by takers on the opposite side reach queue ahead + 1 (own YES bid at p: trades at yes_price p with taker "no"; own NO bid at q: trades at yes_price 1-q with taker "yes"). Queue shrinkage from cancels ignored (conservative).
- Exit, whichever first: (a) target: from the fill, rest a sell at fill price + 0.02, filled by the same queue model (own YES ask at s: trades at yes_price s with taker "yes"; own NO ask at s: trades at yes_price 1-s with taker "no"; queue ahead = the opposite side's visible bid size at the matching level, 0 if the target sits inside the spread, and if it sits deeper than the five visible levels it is treated as NOT fillable by the queue, which is conservative); or the best bid of our side reaches the target at a book row, in which case we sell at that bid as a taker. (b) time: 10 minutes after the fill. (c) forced flatten at T - 3 min, which is earlier than (b) whenever the close is nearer. (b) and (c) are a taker sale at the best bid of our side at the last book row at or before that moment (a missing bid counts as 0). Never held to settlement; a position with no book row to sell into is scored at 0 for the bid (the worst case).
- Fees, cash basis, unrounded, 1 contract: maker legs (entry and a queue-filled target) at coefficient 0 and at 0.0175 x p x (1-p); every taker sale at 0.07 x p x (1-p). The bar uses the 0.0175 bound; both are reported. Cash P&L of a fill = exit price - entry price - fees.
- "Unfilled would have been": for signals whose entry never fills, the same exit rule applied as if joined at the signal time and filled at once at the join price (no queue). It measures what the queue is filtering, not a tradeable result.
