# POLCOMBO1 (V2, Nov 11, 2026, owner addition (m) to the politics program): political parlays and combos
Card: POLCOMBO1 | combo price vs joint probability implied by related single markets (correlated legs: same party, same election night, same bill), hold to settlement | risk class: low win rate, loss = the combo price, shard = Kalshi multi-variable (MVE/combo) markets | stage Idea
Question: do correlated political legs make the combo price assume independence (combo too cheap, so a buy) or too much correlation (too dear, so a sell is not a taker edge)? Data: Kalshi's own multi-leg (MVE) markets under the Exotics series (KXMVECROSSCATEGORY and siblings) and the legs' own single markets; PARLAY1 (Nov 10) already built the census (254 combos with 5+ legs, 243 cross-category, 30 with a combo ask and quoted legs, none clearing the fee; script src/scalper/parlay1.py). Whether Kalshi lists politics-only combos at all: UNKNOWN from my data; Refinement's politics census (origin/claude/refine-live, kalshi-scalper/refine_tools/) is the source and decides whether a card can be tested; none found = CLOSED by census.
Fees stated: taker fee 0.07 p(1-p) per contract on the combo; third-party figures (Citizens, Sportico, SCCG, unaudited) say combos carry about 10 to 26.5% vig, a maker fee of half the taker fee since August and a markup of about 3.1% on uncorrelated combos; unaudited, not verified from Kalshi's own fee schedule. Combos sit on their own shard of Kalshi's exchange (the money has to be on that shard); this is a note, not a recorded fact.
Cheapest test: (1) census: count open politics-only combos (same event or election night) with a combo ask and quoted legs; (2) for each, joint probability from the legs under two bounds: independence (product, overstates a negative-correlation combo, understates a positive one) and perfect positive correlation (the minimum); the combo is a candidate only if its ask is below the independence value by more than the fee AND the legs are plausibly positively correlated (then the true joint is higher still); (3) no outcome read until a rule is registered. Prior: 4% (PARLAY1 found the haircut exceeds the mispricing on 254 combos; politics adds correlation, which can only help the buyer of a combo of positively correlated legs if the market prices independence).
Declined-list check (7e): not favourite-late, not a declined market, not a hold of a favourite; PARLAY1 is the closest closed row and this is the politics-only, correlated-legs angle it did not cover. Uniqueness: signal = related single-market prices (a different field), entry = a combo buy, exit = settlement hold with a different entry rule. Sample: needs Refinement's census; settled politics combos are likely few (NOT_ENOUGH_DATA risk). Capital lock-up: days to the election or bill date; rank in the politics ranking by (days to settle, fills per week) once the census exists.
