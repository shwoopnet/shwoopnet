## Mechanism and counterparty
A daily high is the maximum of the day's temperature, so it can only rise during the day (a daily low can only fall). Once the station has read M degrees F, every bracket whose top is below M is DEAD (its YES pays 0) and, for the high, the open top bracket ("X or above" with X below M) is already WON. The settlement is the NWS climate report value for the same station, which is built from the same ASOS sensor, so it is at least the 1 minute reading rounded (checked below as the violation count). Counterparty: a resting quoter on a dead bracket who has not updated (a YES bid left above 0 on a bracket that cannot win; a YES ask left under $1 on a bracket that has already won), or a casual buyer lifting a dead YES. Structurally riskless on the certain brackets only if the violation count is zero; that is the whole justification for trading the favourite side here (a certain payoff is not buy-the-favourite research: there is no forecast, only an observed fact). Honest prior of a pass: 5%, because WX2 found the book at the extremes 6 hours out and quoters on these markets are bots; the useful output is the measured size of the leftover.

## Rule (fixed, nothing fitted)
Day = close_time minus 24 hours to close_time (local standard midnight, as Kalshi sets it). Looks: every candle end t with t minus day start between 8 and 23 hours. At t, observation cutoff t minus 15 minutes (the fact must have been published; covers delay). M = maximum (high) or m = minimum (low) of all one minute readings from day start to the cutoff, integers as published. No reading in that span: no look.
Brackets from the market record (floor_strike F, cap_strike C, strike_type): "greater F" pays YES iff value at least F+1; "less C" pays iff value at most C-1; "between a b" pays iff a <= value <= b.
- High: DEAD if "less C" and M >= C, or "between a b" and M >= b+1, or "greater F" never. WON if "greater F" and M >= F+1.
- Low: DEAD if "greater F" and m <= F, or "between a b" and m <= a-1, "less C" never. WON if "less C" and m <= C-1.
- **Try A (DEAD, buy NO at the ask = 1 minus yes bid):** the first look at which a DEAD bracket has yes bid at least 0.03 (NO costs at most 0.97). One trade per bracket. Pays 1 if the bracket settled no.
- **Try B (WON, buy YES at the ask):** the first look at which a WON bracket has yes ask at most 0.97. One trade per bracket. Pays 1 if the bracket settled yes.
Cost: taker at the quoted price, fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent with C = 10 per contract bought at price p (NO price for A), and a x1.2 line. Outcome = the market's own result. A trade that loses is a VIOLATION (the observed fact did not decide the bracket). Information lines, not rules: trades with candle volume at least 10; margin variant (dead only if M is at least 1 beyond); count of looks by hour of day.
