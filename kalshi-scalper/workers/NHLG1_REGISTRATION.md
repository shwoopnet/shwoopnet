## 3. The idea, the rule, the look, the probability rule, the fit, the trade
**Idea.** Kalshi lists, per game, a ladder per skater: 1+, 2+, 3+, 4+ goals. The 1+ rung ("anytime scorer") of a depth player and the 2+ and 3+ rungs of everyone are cheap (3c to 30c). A player's goals in a game are close to a Poisson count with a player rate (shots per game x shooting percentage x ice time, here summarised by his own goals per game). If a rate built only from his earlier games says the cheap rung is at least three times as likely as its ask, buy it and hold it.
**Who is on the other side.** Retail buying stars and long shots at round prices; market makers pricing depth players from season totals. Honest prior: the makers use the same logs plus lineup, line and power play assignments the model cannot see (blind spot stated). Prior of a pass: 4%.
**Universe.** Settled `KXNHLGOAL` markets with close time before **2026-10-03T16:00:00Z** with a `floor_strike`, with listing volume above 0 (total volume is listing metadata, not a price or outcome; a market with no trade at all is not tradeable evidence). **The frozen last third (games from 2026-10-03T16:00Z) is never fetched or joined.** Every query is bounded by the cut.
**Reference data.** ESPN keyless `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/hockey/nhl/scoreboard?dates=YYYYMMDD` for 2025-10-01 to 2026-10-02 (one request a day) and `summary?event=ID` for every FINAL game that starts before the cut (the box score: per skater goals, shots, time on ice; goalies excluded). 1 request a second, one thread, honest User-Agent `shwoopnet-research-nhlg1/1.0`, cache in `data/nhlg1.sqlite` (git-ignored) and raw listings in `~/.cache/nhlg1/`.
**Matching.** Kalshi game = event ticker (date ET, away/home 3-letter codes, mapped to ESPN abbreviations by a fixed table in code: TB to TBL, NJ to NJD, SJ to SJS, LA to LAK, UTAH to UTA, anything else equal); ESPN game of the same unordered team pair whose start in US Eastern is within one day of the ticker date, closest wins. Player = `yes_sub_title` name before the colon, normalised (ascii, lower case, punctuation and Jr/Sr/II/III removed). A game that does not match, or a player with no ESPN history name, is counted and dropped. A player listed by Kalshi but absent from that game's box score (scratched) is a 0 goals appearance for the settlement but is NOT used as a history point for later games (he did not play).
**Split.** Pre-cut matched games sorted by start; **FIT = first two thirds, TEST = last third**, TEST halves **T1** (older) and **T2** (newer) by game count. Nothing in TEST is used to fit anything; Kalshi prices and results are never used in FIT (the fit is on ESPN goals only).
**Model (fixed now; the grid is the only freedom, fitted on FIT).** For player p in game g (start t): prior = his goals in earlier games he played (any date from 2025-10-01), at least 5 required else the market is skipped; last up to 20 (g20 = their mean, n20 = count). `rate0 = (sum + k x m_lg x 1) / (n20 + k)` per game, with m_lg the FIT mean goals per skater game (all skaters, also those with no Kalshi market). Opponent defence factor: opponent goals allowed per game over its last up to 20 games before t, shrunk `(sum + 10 x lgG) / (n + 10)`, lgG = FIT mean goals scored per team game; `f = (oppGA / lgG)^gamma`. `rate = rate0 x f`. X ~ Poisson(rate). `P(YES at rung N+) = P(X >= N) = 1 - sum_{j<N} e^-rate rate^j / j!`. **Fit:** k in {5, 10, 20, 40, 80, 160} and gamma in {0, 0.5, 1} by FIT Poisson log likelihood of every FIT skater-game goal count (at least 5 prior games). Chosen values printed. Nothing else is tuned.
**Look.** The last 1 minute candle of the market ending at or before **ESPN game start minus 300 seconds** with a valid two sided quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1 and ask - bid <= 0.10). Cheap side is YES if its ask in [0.03, 0.30]; else NO if 1 - bid in [0.03, 0.30]; else no candidate. The other side is never evaluated or printed.
**Treated trade.** Buy the cheap side at its ask, **C = 10 contracts, hard limit 0.30, held to settlement**, when **model probability of that side >= 3 x ask**. At most **2 per game** (highest ratio, ties by sha1 of the ticker). **Control:** every cheap side of the same sample at the same look, no model condition, also at most 2 per game by sha1 of the ticker. Both reported. The NO side of a rung is evaluated by the model as 1 minus P(YES).
**Fee.** 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent, divided by C; stress x1.2. Taker at the ask. Results other than yes/no are dropped and counted.
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract after fee (cents) over treated trades, SE and z clustered by game; PF = gross wins over gross losses after fee; win rate against mean price paid; treated minus control; T1 and T2 separately; fees x1.2. Information only: model calibration on the cheap TEST sample (Brier of model vs the quote mid), match rate, mean gap p_side minus ask; **overround of each TEST game's ladder** (sum of the 1+ YES asks over players with a valid look, against the sum of model probabilities and against the number of distinct scorers; descriptive, prices and counts only).

## 4. Time box and candle fetch
Candles: the TEST games in md5 order of the event ticker, **8 markets per batched request**, 1 to 2 threads, backoff on 429 (about 40% of calls), cache `data/nhlg1.sqlite`. Time box: **90 minutes of fetching in total**. The sample is the md5-ordered prefix of TEST games whose candles were completed in that time; nothing else is dropped. If the prefix holds fewer than 150 treated trades, ONE extension of 60 minutes is allowed; after that the number is reported as is.

## 5. Numeric prediction (stated before)
Treated trades 100 to 400 on the prefix. Win rate of the treated about equal to or below the mean price paid (the market already prices a player's rate). Treated mean net **-3c to +0.5c** per contract, PF 0.7 to 1.1, T1 and T2 not both positive. Control (all cheap) about -3c (fee plus the favourite-longshot discount). Overround of a goal-scorer ladder: sum of 1+ asks about 1.15 to 1.35 times the expected scorers. Prior of FALSIFIED about 90%.
