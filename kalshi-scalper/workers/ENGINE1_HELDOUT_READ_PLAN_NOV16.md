**Rules.** Exactly the 190 as listed (series, bucket, band, side rule, filter, exit), same costs (h 0.01 and 0.02, 10 contracts, per-order rounded fee, raw ask), same scorer (engine1_core 53317e4b, driver 1281ce0a). Spread filters use the SEARCH tercile cuts of the ORIGINAL archive SEARCH half (rebuilt from the archive extract under Line B1), never recomputed on new data. No rule is added, dropped or altered; m for BH = 190.

**Pass bar (written now).** A rule passes the held-out if n >= 90 (the 3 signals a day gate times 30 days), PF at 1c >= 1.3, PF at 2c >= 1.0 and, after Benjamini-Hochberg at 5% over m = 190 on the one-sided day-clustered p of the 1c cash, it is in the survivor set. Below n of 90 the rule reads "no evidence", not a failure. A rule that clears the bar but not BH is "holds up, not proven". Prediction: most of the 190 fall under PF 1.0 (winner's curse: they were selected on their VALIDATE window); I expect under 10% hold up.

**Pull size.** 12 series (the 190 span BTC, XRP, DOGE, ETH, GOLD, HYPE, SOL, WTI, BNB, NEAR, SILVER, ZEC): lists about 50 requests (settled markets, ticker and close_time, per series in 10 day ranges); candles one request per hour block of at most 44 tickers (12 series x 4 windows = 48 tickers an hour, so 2 requests an hour) x 744 hours = about 1,490; total about 1,540 requests, about 2.1 hours at the 5 s pacing, 4 runs of the 40 minute cap. All closes are after 2026-09-17, so the live endpoints serve them (no historical route). If the pull is too big: the 8 series of the 17 shadow picks (HYPE, ETH, BTC, NEAR, SOL, GOLD, XRP, BNB, 32 tickers an hour, one request an hour, about 790 requests).

**Loader wall.** A new closed-by-default loader (engine1_heldout_load.py) and a job generator, like VOLFAIR1's: refuse unless HELDOUT_PULL_RUNS.md names the loader's sha256 and the landed runs; (1) lists: ticker and close_time ONLY, closes outside the window (and the EVENT830 cuts) dropped and counted unparsed; (2) result only for kept markets; (3) candles by market_ticker first, only kept markets parsed, candles ending after the close dropped; request window per hour block = the earliest close minus 21 minutes to the latest close (features need candles back to 300 s before the entry candle, the earliest entry is 840 s before the close).
