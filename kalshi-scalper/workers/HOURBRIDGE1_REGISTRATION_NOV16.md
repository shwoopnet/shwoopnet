# HOURBRIDGE1 registration (Desk A, Nov 16, 2026). Written before any quote or result value is read; only column headers were seen. First read, lighter gate. Borders the parity family (ladder-implied against the market price): see the uniqueness note.

**Question:** in the last quarter hour before an hourly close, the hourly Bitcoin "above K" ladder (KXBTCD) gives a whole distribution of the price at that close. The 15 minute Bitcoin market that closes at the same instant (KXBTC15M) settles on the same reference but asks about its OWN strike (set at its open, 15 minutes earlier). Is the ladder-implied chance of that strike different from the 15 minute market's quote by more than costs, and is the gap followed by the result?

**Data (recorder files on quote-data, `quotes/<date>/<HHMM>/`, headers seen only):** `ladder.csv` (t_send_ms, t_recv_ms, ticker, yes_bid, yes_ask, bid_size, ask_size, strike, status, close_time), `quotes.csv` (same columns without close_time: the 15 minute and gold markets, last 150 s of each window), `results.csv` (ticker, result, close_time, seen_ms). Bitcoin only (KXBTCD and KXBTC15M); Ethereum (KXETHD, KXETH15M) as a second family only if Bitcoin passes. Dates from 2026-10-28 (the recorder's start).

**Definitions (frozen):**
- Event: an hourly close H (the KXBTCD event's close_time) with a KXBTC15M market closing at H. At the decision instant D = H - 100 s (the last recorded row of each file at or before D, each at most 6 s old; a stale or missing side skips the event, counted).
- Strike of the 15 minute market, K15 = its `strike` column. Ladder rows: the KXBTCD markets of that hour (each its own `strike`), two-sided (0 < bid < ask < 1), mid p(K) = (bid + ask) / 2, decreasing in K.
- Ladder-implied chance P_L = p(K15) by LINEAR interpolation between the two ladder strikes that bracket K15 (no extrapolation: K15 outside the recorded strikes = skip, counted).
- Gap g = P_L - mid15 (mid15 = the 15 minute market's YES mid at D). Signal: |g| >= 0.04. Spread guard: the 15 minute market's YES spread <= 0.04 and the bracketing ladder spreads <= 0.04, else skip, counted.
- Entry: g >= 0.04: buy YES at its ask; g <= -0.04: buy NO at 1 - its YES bid. One entry per event. Hold to settlement (the result in results.csv, read only after the cut check). Per-order rounded fee at 10 contracts, haircut 1c and 2c on the entry (C8 applies: the entry is in the last two minutes, so 2c is the primary).
- A void result keeps the entry fee and refunds the price.
- Stats: n, wins, PF at 1c and 2c, halves, day-clustered z; "no evidence at n under 100"; tries: 1 rule x Bitcoin = 1 try (plus the sign split is NOT a try: a single rule on both signs); BH not needed for one try.

**Prediction (numeric, before any read):** at least 100 events with |g| >= 0.04 in development, and cash PF at 2c of 1.3 or more with each half above 1.0. **Kill:** under 100 usable events, or 2c PF under 1.0 in either half.
