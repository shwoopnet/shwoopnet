Worker: data inventory analyst for the Head of Strategy Research. Owner mandate (Nov 7): one table of every dataset so analysts can run backtests first. Method: `ls`, `du`, read-only sqlite3 (`mode=ro`) table, row and time-column counts, python header reads of the recorder branches through `git show origin/quote-data:<path>` and `origin/perp-data:<path>`, and the README ledger plus `workers/*_REGISTRATION.md` for frozen cuts. Paths are relative to `kalshi-scalper/` (`data/` is git-ignored, local to this box). Times are UTC. ## How to read the pool columns (rules I used, stated so they can be checked)

- **Floor.** 150 treated trades is the registered floor for any read. "Treated" is after the trigger, so the pool counts below are rows BEFORE a trigger. A trigger that keeps 5 to 10 percent of rows needs 1,500 to 3,000 rows for a floor of 150.
- **Last third.** The held-out last third leaves n/3 rows (or events). A test needs its treated rows in that third to reach 150 as well.
- **Three windows (PF).** "Y" means each of three calendar windows could hold at least 150 treated trades at a 5 to 10 percent trigger and each window spans at least 3 weeks of dates (the day-clustered z needs days, not rows). "N" otherwise. For the 15 minute book the three windows are the ledger's: W1 2026-08-06 to 09-04, W2 09-05 to 10-04, W3 10-05 to the end (W3 is frozen for many families, see key).
- **Clustering warning.** 18 of the 26 15 minute series close in the same windows and move together, so pooled rows are not independent; the honest sample size for a pooled test is dates (about 83 for the 15 minute book), not rows.
- Counts of "settled" mean `result in ('yes','no')` in the market table, nothing more.

## Gaps (what is missing for a backtest, ranked by how much it blocks)

1. **No long Kalshi history.** Kalshi's public API starts 2026-08-24 for settled 15 minute markets and 08-05 for the older candle fetches, so every 15 minute test has about 90 days (83 to 85 days on file) and a W3 that is frozen. Nothing before August exists for any 15 minute series.
2. **No window outside the frozen set can serve as the third.** The 15 minute book's W3 (10-05 on) is frozen by many registrations (at least EVENT1, PUB20, TODSEAS1, VOLSLOT1, MECHOI1), 10-28 on is GEN1's and OICOREX1's holdout (12,569 windows), and the validation windows 10-01 to 10-29 for BTC and gold are spent (F10). A new 15 minute family has W1 and W2 (71,611 windows) and the new days after 11-05 only; it must register its own cut first.
3. **Book depth exists only from 10-29** (`book15r6`, `book`, 9 days, 4 to 5 series at 15 minutes; Bitcoin hourly at 3 s). Candles have no sizes, so every queue, maker and depth backtest before 10-29 is impossible. R6 reaches the registered 14 days on about 11-12 and the planned end 11-13.
4. **Candle files lag the recorders.** `data/fresh.sqlite` ends 11-05; the alts files end 10-13; `fxusd`, `newh15`, `racelock` end 10-30; sports end 10-26 to 10-28; `props1` and `scout1` end 10-03; `weather.sqlite` ends 10-13; GEN rows end 10-27 to 10-29. Anything between those ends and 11-06 needs a refresh (the nightly `fresh_update` only covers `fresh`).
5. **Tape coverage is a sample for Bitcoin** (`gen9` 1,004 of 3,983 markets; ends 10-27) and zero for the 15 minute ETH, XRP, DOGE at 10 s before 10-29.
6. **Forex and metals have no minute reference** in the pre-cut history (Yahoo keeps about 7 days of 1 minute data, FXREF1, HRLYFX1 and COMDAY1 failed on that). The RECREF recorder fills it from 11-04.
7. **Perp history is only the exchange's own candles** with no sizes, no premium (the market object has no funding-rate field), only 23 to 31 days for 8 tickers (US 500, Tech 100, WTI, copper, aluminum), equities none before 2026-11-05T18:39Z, and the holdout is one shot per ticker. The hourly recorder (`perp-data:perps`) is sparse (about 6 samples a day) and 6 of its 17 days are frozen (F6).
8. **Forward recorders are short**: `wxens`, `raincal`, `sportspre`, `ref`, `daily`, `deribit` hold 3 to 5 days (19 event-days at best); the family collectors planned in `workers/COLLECTORS_2026-11-04.md` need 30 to 60 days; `daily` needs about late January 2027 for 25 stress days.
9. **Missing forward data nobody records**: no 15 minute book for SOL, BNB, NEAR, HYPE, ZEC or any metal (R6 holds BTC, ETH, DOGE, XRP, GOLD only); no NHL pre-puck-drop ladder (collector 1 in the collectors note, not built); no hourly ETH book (KXETHD only through `quotes/ladder.csv` at 3 s in the last 150 s).
10. **Housekeeping risks.** `data/` is git-ignored and local to this box (6.9 GB); `data/perpgen/clean/holdout/` and the raw 3 day windows contain the locked holdout; `stage/fresh.sqlite` is a stale copy; two sqlite files in `data/` are zero bytes; three journal files remain (`newlist1`, `props1`) from interrupted writes; the pooled-dedup order matters (first file wins), so a ticker present in two files takes the older file's candles.
