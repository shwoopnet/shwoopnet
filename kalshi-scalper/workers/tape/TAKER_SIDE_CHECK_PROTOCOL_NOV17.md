# taker_side check, written before any look (Desk A, Nov 17, 2026; the Head's order, steps 1 to 3)

Question: in the perps tape, does taker_side "bid" mean the taker BOUGHT and "ask" the taker SOLD (or the reverse)? Kalshi's docs only say "Side of the taker in this trade", enum bid or ask (step 1, no meaning given).

Data: FORWARD live data of 2026-11-17 UTC only (in no research window), branch perp-live-data: perplive/2026-11-17/HHMM/tape.csv.gz (every job file of that date) and the book files book.csv.gz and book_fast.csv.gz of the same folders (best 5 levels with sizes). Prices and quotes only: no result, no outcome, no candle. Script tape/side_check.py, which REFUSES unless HELDOUT_PULL_RUNS.md has a line with the mark "readable ONLY by Desk A's taker side check", "side_check.py", its sha256 and the sha256 of the sorted "<folder>/<file> <sha256>" list of the files read.

Rules (fixed now):
1. Trades: non-block rows of tape.csv.gz whose created_time is on 2026-11-17 UTC; same six fields (ticker, created_time, price, count, taker_side, is_block_trade) with DIFFERENT t_recv_ms are one trade seen twice (earliest t_recv_ms kept), the same t_recv_ms kept (as the tape builder). taker_side must be exactly bid or ask; any other string is counted and left out.
2. Quote: for each trade, the LAST book snapshot of the same ticker with t_send_ms <= the trade's created_time in ms AND within 10,000 ms before it (the book rows are the exchange state when asked; a trade is compared with the book BEFORE it). No such snapshot, or bid_px_1 >= ask_px_1 (crossed or empty), and the trade is counted "no quote" and left out.
3. Class: price >= ask_px_1 is AT_OR_ABOVE_ASK; price <= bid_px_1 is AT_OR_BELOW_BID; otherwise INSIDE. A trade is never in two classes (the book is uncrossed).
4. Report, for taker_side bid and for ask SEPARATELY, counts and shares of the three classes, overall and for the eight development tickers (KXBTCPERP, KXETHPERP, KXGOLDPERP, KXSILVERPERP, KXUS500PERP, KXUSTECH100PERP, KXHYPEPERP, KXBNBPERP) and "all other tickers", plus counts of no quote, other strings, block rows left out.
5. Decision threshold (fixed before looking): share >= 0.80 on the ALL-ticker line, with at least 1,000 trades on each side. Mapping A: bid trades AT_OR_ABOVE_ASK share >= 0.80 AND ask trades AT_OR_BELOW_BID share >= 0.80, then bid = taker bought (buy), ask = taker sold (sell). Mapping B: bid trades AT_OR_BELOW_BID share >= 0.80 AND ask trades AT_OR_ABOVE_ASK share >= 0.80, then bid = sell, ask = buy. Anything else (including one side clear and the other not, or fewer than 1,000 on a side) is NO MAPPING: the amendment keeps taker_side as "unknown side" and the Head decides. The same pattern must hold on at least 6 of the 8 development tickers that have 100 or more trades of each side (reported, not part of the all-ticker rule if fewer than 6 qualify, in which case NO MAPPING is not triggered by that alone but is reported).
6. No other analysis of the same files. The script prints its own output sha first; the numbers are read after.
