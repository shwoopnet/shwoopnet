# CFRTI1 protocol (V2, Nov 11, 2026, registered before any score)
Card: CFRTI1 | settlement-index replication | risk class: win rate about 10 to 30%, loss = the cheap price, group crypto 15 min | stage Idea
Uniqueness (self-assessed, the Verification Analyst scores): (1) signal = multi-venue (Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp) trade prices rebuilt into the settlement basis, not Kalshi's own data, but the closest existing idea is the spot-lead filter, so this may count as a duplicate; (2) entry 120 s before close on the cheap side only on a model edge, exit hold; (3) correlation not measurable on this history; (4) not on the declined list.
Mechanism: settlement is a 60 second average of a multi-venue index. At T-120 s, rebuild the index from the three venues' trades and ask whether the cheap side's quoted price is below the model chance.
Data: quote-data branch `quotes/<date>/<hhmm>/{quotes,spot,results}.csv.gz` (Oct 22 to Nov 11); BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, DOGE 15 minute markets.
Rule (fixed now, no tuning): at the last quote at or before close-120 s (within 3 s), index P0 = mean over venues with a trade in the last 10 s of that venue's last price. sigma = std of 10 s log changes of the venue-mean index over the previous 10 minutes, scaled by sqrt(8) (about 80 s of variance for a 60 s average starting 60 s ahead). Model P(YES) = Phi((ln(P0/strike))/sigma_scaled). Cheap side = the side with ask 0.03 to 0.30. Buy it when model chance minus ask >= 0.05 and the quote spread is <= 0.03. One contract, hold to settlement, fee 0.07 p (1-p) unrounded, cash PF first. Baseline = every cheap-side row in the same sample without the model edge.
Split: days sorted; first two thirds development, last third held out and read ONCE after the development read, only if development PF >= 1.3 and n >= 100.
Bar: development cash PF >= 1.3 over n >= 100 events, event-clustered z above the Bonferroni bar (N about 5,207 after this try), edge beats the baseline in the same sample. Prior 4%.
