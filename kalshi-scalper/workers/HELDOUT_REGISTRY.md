## What it cannot tell you (read this first)
- **STATE is conservative and mostly from file names.** READ (spent) rests, for most cards, only on a result file with the card's name existing: that proves a development read happened, not that a frozen third or held-out was opened. A card marked READ may still hold an unread held-out; check its row's note and the card itself before treating it as spent.
- **Column 'held-out rule' was picked from each registration by keyword** for the old research branch (not by a person): a few rows quote a loosely related line. Check the card before relying on them.
- **Dates that are not constants** are marked 'computed in <file>'.
- **FORWARD_CALENDAR.md is dated 2026-11-03 and stale.** Scheduled dates before Nov 13 show as FORWARD READ SCHEDULED with a note 'status unknown' (MAKERMID1 and MAKERTAIL1 2026-11-12 are examples); the calendar's own rulings drop USWEEP1's favourite watch items, GAS1-F, ABSORB-F and the Nov 12 L1 reads (CLOSED UNREAD).
