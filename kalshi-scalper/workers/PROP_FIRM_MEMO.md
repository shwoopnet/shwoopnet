# Prop firm memo (public docs only, Nov 9, 2026; no signup, no spend)
A prop firm does not create an edge. It sells a rule-bound simulated account for a monthly fee and pays a share of simulated profit if the rules are met; our tested rules so far (FUT1 crude/gas, FUT2 NQ/GC, 50 variants; Kalshi N 1.5M) have no edge after costs, and a rule with no edge pays fees. Verified below for TOPSTEP only (each fact read from the firm's own page on Nov 9, 2026). Other firms (Apex, MyFundedFutures, Take Profit Trader and similar) are NOT verified here and would need the same page-by-page check.

## Monte Carlo (scripts/prop_mc.py): what the rules do to a strategy with Sharpe 0.5, 1.0, 1.5
50K Combine ($49 monthly, $49 reset after each MLL breach, $149 activation) then a Standard XFA (payout at 5 winning days of $150+, 50% of balance up to $2,000, 90% split), repeated for up to 20 Combines. Daily P&L iid normal with a dollar daily vol (300 or 600) and a stated annual Sharpe; intraday breach approximated. Output per row: P(a first payout within 20 Combines), median fees paid to that payout, mean fees, mean (first payout minus fees), median months.
sd 300/day: Sharpe 0: P 0.98, fees median $837, mean net -$791, 9.5 months; Sharpe 0.5: 0.997, $639, -$481, 7.3 mo; Sharpe 1.0: 1.00, $492, -$260, 5.7 mo; Sharpe 1.5: 1.00, $394, -$117, 4.6 mo.
sd 600/day: Sharpe 0: P 0.93, $739, -$337, 3.9 mo; Sharpe 0.5: 0.97, $639, -$128, 3.4 mo; Sharpe 1.0: 0.99, $543, +$29, 2.8 mo; Sharpe 1.5: 1.00, $492, +$188, 2.5 mo.
Reading: even with ZERO edge a first payout is likely (the payout rule is low, 5 days of $150), and costs a few hundred dollars in fees; the first payout is small (mean about $400 to $700), so the net first-payout result is negative or about zero unless Sharpe is 1.0 or more at a higher daily vol. The model ignores fat tails, serial dependence, consistency and prohibited-strategy rules, and the real risk of breaching with size; real results will be worse. Conclusion: a prop firm is a vehicle for a strategy that already has an edge, not a source of one; with our current rules (none) the expected result is a loss equal to fees.

## Monte Carlo variant: fat tails (Student-t, 3 degrees of freedom, same sd; `python3 scripts/prop_mc.py fat`, Nov 9)
sd 600/day: Sharpe 0: P(first payout) 0.94, median fees $786, mean net -$429; Sharpe 0.5: 0.98, $639, -$175; Sharpe 1.0: 1.00, $492, +$52; Sharpe 1.5: 1.00, $443, +$187. sd 300/day: net -$912, -$492, -$234, -$73. Same picture as the normal case, slightly worse at low Sharpe (more breaches and resets): fat tails do not rescue a no-edge strategy; the first-payout net is about zero only at Sharpe 1.0 or more with a larger daily vol.
