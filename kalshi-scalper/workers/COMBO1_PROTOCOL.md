# COMBO1 PROTOCOL (registered Nov 8, 2026, before any outcome is joined)

Research only. No trading code, no key, no order, no fetching: local sqlite files only. Owner question via main (Nov 8): have paired or multi-leg orders across markets that close in the same window been tested? Prior record checked in README: BK1 (sibling catch-up, crypto), XM1, ARB1 to ARB5, LOCK2/LOCK2H, RACELOCK, CONF1/CONF2 (confluence as an L1 filter), PAIRS1 (perps), HS1/HS1b, GEN3 (adjacent strikes, hourly BTC). **Never tested: a pair of cheap-side legs in two different metal markets that close in the same 15 minute window, held to settlement, costed on both legs and scored as ONE trade.** BK1's sibling idea (crypto) died because the same makers quote siblings at the same instant; that is the prior against this one.

## Data and cuts
Series: KXGOLD15M, KXSILVER15M, KXCOPPER15M, KXPLATINUM15M, KXPALLADIUM15M (FX pairs have only 10 dev dates and are left out; they would be NOT_ENOUGH_DATA). Files (first file wins per ticker, as in DATA_INVENTORY A13): alts2, alts3, rep, fresh, l1b, book, five. Pair = two different series with a settled market closing at the same time (10 pairs; the order gold, silver, copper, platinum, palladium fixes which is leg A). Dev: closes before 2026-10-05 00:00Z (ts 1791158400; 4,091 windows with at least two metal markets on 57 dates), W1/W2/W3 = thirds of its dates. Held out: closes from 2026-10-05 up to but not including 2026-10-28 00:00Z (ts 1793145600; the F2 line is never loaded), read ONCE behind `scan.guard_check`. Only counts were looked at.

## Trade and features (fixed)
Look 840 to 180 s before the close (step 60), the same minute for both legs. Four side combos per pair row (A yes or no, B yes or no); a combo is tradable only if BOTH legs have a valid quote and BOTH asks are 3c to 30c (cheap side only; a NO ask is 1 minus the YES bid). One contract per leg, taker at each ask, fee `ceil(0.07 p (1-p) 100 - 1e-9)` cents on EACH leg, stress x1.2, both held to settlement; net = sum of the two legs, one trade. Features (from the YES quotes at the decision candle only): a_ask, b_ask (YES asks), a_bid, b_bid, a_spread, b_spread, a_d1, a_d3, b_d1, b_d3 (YES bid change over 1 and 3 minutes), a_rng5, b_rng5, askdiff (a_ask - b_ask), d3diff (a_d3 - b_d3); categorical pair id (0 to 9), hod4, dow. Direction slot = the side combo (yy, yn, ny, nn). Bar unchanged: PF 1.5 in each of W1, W2, W3, floors 40 / 150 / 3 dates, deflated by the one-sided Bonferroni at 0.05 / N. Trades of different pairs that share a market are not independent; the day-clustered z is the harness's.

## Runs and N
One scan: 3 x 10,000 distinct rules, seeds 81501 to 81503. Ledger before scoring. Survivors: freeze, audit, held-out once.

## Prediction (before anything is read)
Zero survivors (about 90%): a pair of cheap tickets pays two fees and two spreads, and the metals are quoted by the same makers. A survivor would also have to be shown not to be a single gold or silver edge in disguise. Any live version would need the direction lock (a gold leg collides with L1's gold positions) and the owner's rules (no hedging against L1, cheap side only). Zero closes the pair idea.
