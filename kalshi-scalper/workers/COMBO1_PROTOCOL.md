## Hosts and rate
Only api.elections.kalshi.com public GET. 1 request per 2 seconds. STOP on the first 429, write what was read, resume later, never work around. No ESPN, score sites, Polymarket, Yahoo, Open-Meteo. Weather ground truth is not needed (Kalshi settles the legs and the combo); NWS api.weather.gov is NOT used, so no terms file is needed.

## Step 1: census (no outcome used)
Per collection series (KXMVENBASINGLEGAME, KXMVENFLSINGLEGAME, KXMVENBAMULTIGAMEEXTENDED, KXMVENFLMULTIGAMEEXTENDED, KXMVESPORTSMULTIGAMEEXTENDED, KXMVEMENTIONSSINGLE, the weather MVE series if found (KXCITIESWEATHER collections), KXMVECROSSCATEGORY): GET /markets?series_ticker=S&status=settled|open, limit 1000, at most 40 pages per series and status. Count combos by status and by leg count (mve_selected_legs, 2 to 5 and 6+), first and last close_time, median volume_fp, and for OPEN combos median spread (ask minus bid, two sided only) and the share with a real ask (above 0 and below 1). The counted unit is a combo market. N counted: 1.

## Step 2: pricing test (development only until a survivor exists)
Universe: the collection with the largest settled count whose combos have 2 to 5 legs. Unit = one settled combo, 1 contract.
Entry price: GET candlesticks for the combo (period 60 min), the YES ask close of the last candle ending at or before T = close_time minus 2 hours (and not before open_time plus 1 hour; if no ask is quoted the combo is skipped and the skip count is reported by collection). Leg prices at the same T from each leg's own candlestick mid (two sided only; a combo with any leg unquoted is skipped for rule R2 only).
Fee: taker 0.07 p(1-p) per contract on the combo ask, unrounded for the PF figure, and ceiled to the cent per order (1 contract) as the stricter figure; the PF gate uses the rounded figure. The combo-specific Kalshi fee, any combos-shard fact and the third-party figures (about 10 to 26.5 percent vig, half taker maker fee, 3.1 percent uncorrelated markup) are UNVERIFIED unless shown by a market object or Kalshi docs.
Rules (2 tries, running N = 1 census + 2):
- R1 (cheap band): buy YES at the ask when 0.04 <= ask <= 0.30 and spread <= 0.05, any leg count 2 to 5, hold to settlement. Reported by leg count band (2, 3, 4 to 5) as descriptive splits, not extra tries.
- R2 (model edge): independence product P_ind of the legs' mids (NO legs use 1 minus mid). Same-game legs are positively correlated by construction (same game and team), so the joint lower bound is P_ind there; legs from different games or weather cities, or mentions of different speakers, are treated as uncorrelated and the lower bound is P_ind too; a same-game combo whose legs run in opposing directions (for example team win plus under) is treated as negatively correlated and its lower bound is the Frechet lower max(0, sum - (n-1)), so it can never be a candidate. Candidate when P_ind - ask - rounded fee >= 0.02 and 0.04 <= ask <= 0.85.
Native haircut (reported for every collection with data, no outcome needed): median of ask / P_ind and of last price / P_ind at T. A median above 1 means the market embeds a margin over independence; the card is then falsified cheaply for R2 because the lower bound is below the ask.
Splits: development = the earlier two thirds of settled combos by close_time; held-out = the last third, read ONCE and only for a rule that passes on development.
Gates (both must hold on the held-out read, and development must show them first): cash PF >= 1.5 (payout 1 minus ask minus rounded fee, win payout), n >= 100, z above the deflated bar (shared N about 1,100,000 plus this count, about 5.34; the small-count bar N 9,009 about 4.39; the bar applied is 5.34), both halves of development PF >= 1.2, no single game or event over 30 percent of net. Combos from one game are one cluster for z (cluster-robust, the game's event_ticker prefix).
Verdicts: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (with the dates when data would suffice), FALSIFIED, PASS-TO-SHADOW (owner decision, no live change, shadow log only). No promote verb.
Prior: PASS under 3 percent. Prediction: R1 loses (a cheap long-shot combo carries a vig); R2 has no candidates (the native haircut exceeds the leg mispricing, as in PARLAY1, NFLCOMBO1, ARB).
Sampling cost control: candlestick reads are about 1 + legs per combo, so development is read for at most 300 combos per run, taken by a fixed rule (every k-th settled combo by close_time order, k fixed after the census from the count, written into this file before the first candle read).

## Amendment 1 (Nov 11, 2026, before any entry-time price or per-combo outcome is read): owner caps for a FUTURE live probe, relayed via main (caps only, NOT permission for any live order)

## Amendment 2 (Nov 12, 2026, committed BEFORE any candlestick or entry-time price read; written after the historical census, before pricing)

What this means: a Kalshi-prices-only rule that buys a combo at a posted ask cannot be tested from candlesticks and probably cannot be traded from the public book at all. A different design is needed (request-for-quote fills are not observable here), so R1 to R3 are not run and not falsified. The sample of 36 could not pass n >= 100 anyway; reads for n >= 100 under the original design would have been about 300 to 500 combo reads (about 2 hours at 4 a minute), but the zero-ask finding says they would all be skipped. Reopen only if a source of two-sided combo quotes at a time (a recorder of the live combo book, 15 minute polling of open combos) shows real asks; the 37 stale open sports combos seen earlier had none.
