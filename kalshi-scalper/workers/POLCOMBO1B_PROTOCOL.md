## Rate and stop rule (all three Nov 11 protocols share ONE bucket)
Public GET on api.elections.kalshi.com only. The V2 share is 4 requests a minute TOTAL for POLCOMBO1B, CLINICAL2 and MENTPERSIST1 together: one process, one pacer, 15 seconds between requests, the three protocols run one after another and never in parallel. The first 429 stops the run, writes what was read and the position (cursor, step), and it is resumed by a later run; no retry loop, no second client, no workaround. Every read is logged (path, params, UTC time, status) in `workers/POLCOMBO1B_READLOG.json`. Recommended order across the three: CLINICAL2 census (20 reads), then POLCOMBO1B steps 1 to 4, then MENTPERSIST1.

## Step 1: find the collections (at most 5 reads)
GET /multivariate_event_collections (limit as the endpoint allows, follow the cursor, at most 5 pages). Keep every collection whose `associated_events` (or equivalent) list contains an event whose ticker or title matches the five politics events: Alaska Senate, Mississippi Senate, Kansas Governor, Alaska Governor, NC-03 House (match on the words "Alaska"/"Mississippi"/"Kansas"/"NC-03" together with "Senate"/"Governor"/"House" in the event title, or on the event ticker prefixes KXSENATE, KXGOV, KXHOUSE with those state codes). Record the five politics event tickers verbatim from the listing. If the listing does not carry event tickers or titles, the fallback is the POLCOMBO1 leg-prefix list (KXPRES, KXSEN, KXHOUSE, KXGOV, KXELECT, KXTRUMP, KXCONGRESS, KXBILL, KXSHUTDOWN, KXFED, KXLEAVE, KXSCOTUS, KXCABINET, KXPOLL, KXAPPROVAL) applied to the legs' event tickers in step 2, and the fact is reported. No collection found within 5 reads: verdict NOT_ENOUGH_DATA with the 5 pages read; the card is not otherwise touched. A POLITICS LEG is a leg whose event_ticker is one of the five recorded events.

## Step 2: combo census (at most 150 reads)
For each collection kept in step 1 (largest `size`/event count first): GET /markets?mve_collection_ticker=<collection>&status=open&limit=1000, follow the cursor, at most 60 pages per collection and 150 pages in total. No other filter, so the page order is the API order and the sample is not chosen by price. Legs come from `mve_selected_legs` (market_ticker, event_ticker, side), or, if absent, from custom_strike "Associated Markets" and "Associated Market Sides" as in PARLAY1. Report per collection: pages read, markets read, whether the cursor ended (COMPLETE) or the cap stopped it (PARTIAL, with the fraction read), the count with at least one politics leg, with 2 or more, and the histogram by leg count (2 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 to 30, over 30), the close_time range, the median volume_fp, the median spread, and the count passing each filter of step 3. Compare with Refinement's 394 and 302 as a description only (a COMPLETE read should reproduce them; a gap is reported, not explained away). The unit is a combo market. N counted: 1.

## Step 3: candidate selection (fixed now; uses no outcome)
A combo is a CANDIDATE when all hold: at least one politics leg; 2 to 30 legs (owner, Nov 11); the side to buy has a real ask between 0.04 and 0.90 (NO ask = 1 minus YES bid) and (ask minus bid on that side) <= 0.05; every leg's single market status open at the leg read. The owner's live band is 0.10 to 0.90 (docs/POLITICS_RULES.md section 2); a candidate with an ask under 0.10 is priced for description but cannot reach an owner popup. Both sides are examined: the side with the lower ask is the primary, the other is a second row only if its ask is also in band. From the candidates take a PRICING SAMPLE by a fixed rule: three strata by leg count (2 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 to 30), in each at most 8 combos with the smallest md5(combo ticker), at most 24 combos. If a stratum has fewer, all of it is taken and the shortfall is reported. If there are more than 24 candidates the rest are COUNTED, not priced.

## Step 4: pricing (at most 150 reads)
Procedure is COMBOPRICE1 section 2, unchanged. Reads: for each sampled combo re-read the combo market (1 read, timestamp T0), then its legs. FIRST test, once, whether GET /markets?tickers=a,b,c (comma separated, up to 100) returns the named markets; if yes, all legs of a combo not already cached are fetched with one read per 100 legs (so about 1 to 2 reads per combo), if the parameter is ignored (the response is not the named markets), the fallback is GET /markets/<ticker> one per leg, cached for the run. Leg quotes must be within 5 minutes of T0 or are re-read once; a leg with no two-sided quote or a spread over 0.10 makes the combo UNQUOTED (no estimate, no last price filled in). Staleness and the 24 hour rule are COMBOPRICE1 section 2 step 3. Reads for a combo: 1 + legs in the worst case; the cap is 150 reads. A combo not reached by the cap is UNPRICED and listed. Candlesticks are NOT read in this protocol.
Written link, rho and reason, fixed BEFORE any ask or quote is used. The script first writes `workers/POLCOMBO1B_LINKS.json` from the combo TICKERS and leg tickers only (no price), hashes it (sha256 stored in the file), and only then joins quotes. Rule table (applied by ticker, no discretion): (a) legs that are the same event or the same game: rho 0.5, reason "same event"; (b) two politics legs in the same state with the same party direction (for example Republican wins the Alaska Senate and the Alaska Governor): rho 0.3, reason "same state, same party direction"; (c) two politics legs in different states with the same party direction: rho 0.1, reason "same election night"; (d) a politics leg with a sports or other leg: rho 0, reason "unrelated"; (e) legs with opposing direction inside one event: no copula, only the Frechet lower bound; (f) a combo takes the LARGEST rho of any pair as its single written rho only if at least half of its leg pairs share that class, else the median pair rho (computed by the script, shown). rho is never fitted to a price. Where the rule gives rho 0 for the combo, no correlation edge may be claimed (COMBOPRICE1 section 3) and the independence product is the estimate.
Per priced combo report: legs and sides, leg mids, independence product, the written rho and reason, copula joint at the written rho, Frechet range, WIN CHANCE (independence and at the written rho), combo ask and bid, fee (standard taker formula, ceil to the cent; combo fee UNVERIFIED unless a market-object field or Kalshi's fee schedule shows one, the field names captured), haircut = ask over independence product, edge per dollar = (win chance minus ask minus fee) over (ask plus fee) at rho 0 AND at the written rho, break-even rho, LOCK-UP: expected settlement date (the market's `expected_expiration_time` if present, else the latest leg close_time), years to settlement, return per dollar-YEAR = edge divided by years, and the gate summary of COMBOPRICE1 section 7. The honesty table (win chance falls fast with legs) is printed for the combo's own legs. Every spec states MONEY IS HELD UNTIL SETTLEMENT (up to Nov 2027, about a year).
Native haircut: median of ask over independence product, by leg-count stratum, reported for the priced sample. A median above 1 at rho 0 means the market already embeds a margin; then a candidate needs a positive written rho to show any edge.

## Owner popup spec template (for a FUTURE spec; nothing here is a request to place anything)
Produced by `format_popup_spec` in `src/scalper/combo_price.py`. One page: exact legs (ticker, side, chance), side and price, win chance (independence, written rho, Frechet range), the written link, lock-up and expected settlement date, MONEY HELD UNTIL SETTLEMENT, the edge table by rho and per dollar-year, break-even rho, the caps ($2 per parlay, $40 total stop, up to 10 positions, kill: net under -$15 after 20 fills or 5 losses in a row; return: 14 day cool-off, shadow PF 1.3, owner approval), the four uniqueness checks of docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md 7d, and the declined-list line. The $2 and $40 are caps for a future spec only. A popup is produced for a combo only if it passes every item of COMBOPRICE1 section 7 including edge at least 0.10 per dollar at the written rho and per dollar-year at least 0.10 on the settlement basis, and the owner approves each live probe by its own popup. The paper-edge bar before any live probe (POLCOMBO1: 40 settled paper combos, both halves, no event over 30%) cannot be met by a live combo before 2027-11-03, so until then the only paper evidence is the simulation of step 5, labelled SIMULATION.

## Step 5: simulation from singles, politics singles (at most 361 reads)
COMBOPRICE1 section 6 rules apply. Universe: settled politics single markets, series found by GET /series?category=Politics (1 read, plus GET /series?category=Elections if that category exists, 1 read), restricted to series whose ticker begins with a prefix on the POLCOMBO1 list. Settled markets: GET /markets?series_ticker=S&status=settled&limit=1000 AND GET /historical/markets?series_ticker=S&limit=1000 merged by ticker (/markets alone returns only markets settled since 2026-09-12, so the historical tier is required), at most 40 reads in total, series taken in md5 order. A market with result yes or no is kept. Synthetic combos of 2 to 5 legs: legs are markets in different events closing on the same UTC day (the shared topic is the day); per day, at most 20 combos by smallest md5 of the sorted leg tickers; the written link per class follows the step 4 table by series (same state, same party direction, and so on) and is fixed from tickers before prices. Leg prices: the last two-sided daily candle ending at or before close minus 24 hours (period 1440), spread <= 0.10; one candlestick read per leg market, cached, at most 300 reads, legs taken in md5 order until the cap; a combo with an unquoted leg is skipped and the skip count reported. Output per leg count: number of synthetic combos and event clusters, the REALISED joint frequency, the independence product, the estimated rho (the rho at which `copula_joint` matches the realised frequency, with a Wilson interval), and, only against the HYPOTHETICAL ask set in advance as median native haircut of step 4 for that stratum times the independence product (if step 4 priced no combo in the stratum, haircut 1.10), the cash result of buying that synthetic combo (payout 1 if all legs yes, minus the hypothetical ask, minus the rounded fee). N counted: the number of joined families (leg-count strata, at most 4) added to the deflation.
Gates for the simulation (all required): at least 100 synthetic combos per leg count and no more than 30% from one UTC day (else NOT_ENOUGH_DATA for that leg count); development = the earlier two thirds of combos by close day, held-out = the last third, READ ONCE and only for a leg count that passes development; cash PF at least 1.5 on the held-out read with n at least 100 and development PF at least 1.5 too; no single day over 30% of net; cluster-robust z (cluster = the UTC day) above the deflated bar. A pass is "worth watching or asking Kalshi for a real combo", never a probe, and carries the label SIMULATION in every line.

## Deflation
Shared N about 1,100,000 (main family count) plus this card: 1 (census) + at most 24 (priced combos) + at most 4 (joined strata) = at most 29. The bar is z 5.34 (N about 1,100,000). The small-count bar at N 9,009 is z 4.39 and is reported beside it; the bar APPLIED to any verdict is 5.34. A rule that clears 4.39 but not 5.34 is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA or FALSIFIED, never a pass.

## Verdicts, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA dates, kill
- CLOSED by census: step 2 COMPLETE and zero candidates in step 3.
- NOT_ENOUGH_DATA: (a) no collection found (step 1); (b) step 2 PARTIAL (the cap stopped it): the unread fraction is stated and the census is repeated from the saved cursor; (c) fewer than 100 synthetic combos for a leg count: the date it would end is today plus (100 minus count) divided by the count of new same-day pairs per week measured over the settled window, written by the script into the result; (d) the live combos cannot give a paper edge before 2027-11-03 (all five events close that day): that date is the earliest a real settled-combo paper edge (40 combos) can exist. A 429 is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA for the unread steps with the resume position.
- FALSIFIED: the held-out simulation read fails a gate (the PF, the count or the z bar), or, in step 4, no priced combo passes COMBOPRICE1 section 7 with a positive edge at its written rho.
- PASS-TO-SHADOW (owner decision, no live change): only the simulation held-out read passing every gate AND at least one priced combo passing section 7. There is no promote verb.
- KILL (the card is retired): the native median haircut at rho 0 is above 1.05 in every stratum AND no priced combo has a written rho above 0 with edge at least 0.10 per dollar; or two successive sampled runs produce no candidate. RETURN only by a new registration with a new written link rule.
