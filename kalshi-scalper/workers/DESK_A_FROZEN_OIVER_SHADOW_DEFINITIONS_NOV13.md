## 0. What a place is (7g)
Each place is a VERSION of OICORE, not a new strategy: it takes no seat, spends one try each (N +1), and is logged AGAINST THE PARENT. The parent is OICORE exactly as in `DESK_A_OICORE_VERSIONS_NOV13.md` (Bitcoin 15 minute, cheaper side 0.08 to 0.50, open interest rise >= 86,300 inclusive over the candle ending close - 180 s minus the one ending close - 360 s, no slip, limit = the ask seen, held to settlement). A version trades a subset of the parent's signals, so every parent signal is logged once and each place records pass or skip with its reason. Shadow size is 20 contracts (the scorers' C); no money, no order, no Kalshi call from any desk.

| Place | Added condition (inherited constant, no tuning) |
|---|---|
| OIVER-SPREAD | YES ask minus YES bid <= 0.03, inclusive (1e-9 tolerance) |
| OIVER-CAP30 | the cheaper side's ask <= 0.30, inclusive; the 8c floor stays |
| OIVER-DEPTH | displayed size at the touch the order would meet >= 40 contracts (2 x 20), inclusive |

## 1. Decision poll and clock
- **Source:** R6 Bitcoin books, `book15r6/<date>/<HHMM>/book.csv.gz` on `quote-data` (never a candle, never a market list copy). R6 records Bitcoin to 2026-12-31 23:59Z (`scripts/record_15m_book.py` on main: `BTC_SERIES`, `END_UTC`); this is the ONE end date for these places. (v1 wrongly said 2026-11-20.)
- **Decision instant** D = close time minus 180 s EXACTLY, in EXCHANGE time. **Decision poll** = the last R6 row **of the window's ticker AND close_time** (R6 rows carry many tickers; both must match) with exchange time at or before D and at most 10 s before it; none: "no signal: no poll", counted.
- **Exchange-time offset (G2):** for a candidate row, the MEDIAN over the 120 most recent rows of the same run folder (any ticker, ordered by receive time, at or before that row, rows with a readable `server_date_header` only) of (the header time in seconds + 0.5 - t_recv_ms / 1000). **Minimum 30 rows with readable headers;** fewer (the start of an HHMM folder, or unreadable headers): "no signal: no offset", counted, and no earlier folder is borrowed. `clock.csv.gz` (t_local_ms, offset_ms) is NOT the offset source: it is the recorder's own hourly clock check, not Kalshi's Date header, and the exchange-time convention of the wheel sheet and of `oiver_depth.py` is the Date-header median.
- **Book fields at the decision poll (the ladder spelled out, G3):** YES bid = best `yes_bid_px_1`; YES ask = 1 minus best `no_bid_px_1`. A YES buy meets the NO bids: its ladder is the NO bid levels, each as an ask of 1 minus `no_bid_px_k`, sorted by ask. A NO buy meets the YES bids: its ladder is the YES bid levels, each as a NO ask of 1 minus `yes_bid_px_k`, sorted ascending by ask. The cheaper side and its ask (ties to YES). Touch size = the size at that ladder's best level (`no_bid_sz_1` for a YES buy, `yes_bid_sz_1` for a NO buy). A missing side, or a book not two-sided (rule 18: 0 < bid < ask < 1, 1e-9 slack), is "no signal: not two-sided", counted.
- **The parent's trigger** is the open interest rise, from the NIGHTLY open interest candle pull (Main's choice (b): Desk B's job, one batched candlesticks request per close). **Source and field:** the `open_interest` of the 1 minute candle ENDING at close - 180 s minus the `open_interest` of the candle ENDING at close - 360 s (`end_period_ts`), the same two candles as OICORE's parent and the history screens, so identical, not the bot logger's `oiRise`; the rise must be >= 86,300, inclusive. The bot's P3 skip rows are NOT a source. A window whose candle is missing or whose `open_interest` is null: "no signal: no candle", counted, no neighbour substituted. A market without populated open interest per the OIFIELD check: withdrawn by a dated line. Until Main's pull has produced a night of candles and a dated line says so, no place records a signal.

## 2. Fill check and price
- **Fill check (G1, G3):** the NEXT R6 row **of the same ticker and close_time** after the decision poll must have `t_recv_ms` at most **4 s** after the decision poll's `t_recv_ms` (a difference of two receive times, so the exchange-time offset cancels; no offset is needed for the gap). A later next row: no second look, a miss, counted, not scored. Both polls must offer 20 contracts at or under the limit (limit = the ask seen at the decision poll, plus 0) on the ladder above, over the recorded levels (up to 5); otherwise a miss, counted.
- **Fill price:** the worse of the two polls' size-weighted walked average asks for 20 contracts. Fee per order: fee_up_order(p, 20) = ceil(0.07 x 20 x p x (1 - p) x 100 - 1e-9) / 100. Cash per contract = win - price - fee / 20.
- OIVER-DEPTH's size condition uses the decision poll's touch size ONLY; the fill check applies to every place equally.

## 3. Result source and the read
- **Result (G4):** `book15r6/<date>/<HHMM>/coverage.csv.gz`, column `result` ("yes" or "no"), for the row with the window's ticker and close_time, across ALL coverage files that hold that ticker and close_time: the value that is yes or no is used; if two files give different values, STOP and report (no score). If none has a result at the block read, the ONLY later read is the kalshi-pull list job that Desk A builds at each block read and the Chief of Staff runs (`GET /markets/{ticker}`, the `result` field; no desk calls Kalshi itself).
- **Void (G5):** a ticker still without a yes or no result 7 days after its close is VOID: EXCLUDED from PF and from every score and counted (as the history screens do); it is never booked as a fee-only loss.
- **Block read (G6):** none before **2026-11-21 AND not until CFRTI-K's read has been made and its read marker is spent.** CFRTI-K's held-out CLOSES start 2026-11-14T00:00Z; its read is first allowed 2026-11-20 and only at n of 50 or more, so it may slip. These places log the same KXBTC15M windows (closes from Nov 14), so the first reading of those windows must be CFRTI-K's, never here (rule 17). The block rule is the wheel's (n >= 60, >= 7 US Central days, 10 wins and 10 losses, PF >= 1.3 as the place's own bar), and ADDITIONALLY the 7g comparison against the parent on the same windows: cash PF beats the parent's by 0.10, net per contract-week not lower (weeks over the parent's trades, same divisor for both), second half not worse (halves by FLOOR(n/2) in date order), 60 signals. Under 100 trades: "no evidence at n = x".
- **Scorers:** the history screens are `desk_a_verify/oiver_spread.py`, `oiver_cap30.py`, `oiver_depth.py` (development range closes 2026-09-12 to before 2026-11-09, read once, never tuned). They are not the forward scorers; the forward reader reuses their `decide()` rules on the R6 poll fields above.
