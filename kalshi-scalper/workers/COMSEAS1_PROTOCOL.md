# COMSEAS1 (registered Nov 9, 2026, before any month is scored): calendar-month seasonality in crude oil and natural gas futures
Research only; EIA daily futures settlements held locally (WTI 1985 to 2024-04, Henry Hub gas 1994 to 2024-04), roll-adjusted per FUT1 Amendment 2. Mechanism and counterparty: producers and utilities hedge on a calendar (gas: winter demand, crude: driving season and refinery runs), so a futures price may drift predictably into or through a month; the loser is the hedger or inventory holder who pays up or sells down on a schedule.
Test (fixed, no search): for each market and each calendar month, a position held through that month each year, long or short (24 market-month-direction pairs per market, 48 cells), P&L per year = sum of daily roll-adjusted dollar returns over the month minus one round trip (2 sides, fee 2.50 + 1 tick per side) and one extra side if the month contains a roll. Years in time order; the first two thirds of years are development, the last third (crude 2013 on, gas 2014 on) is held out and read ONCE for a shortlisted cell. Shortlist: mean net P&L > 0 and a t statistic across development years at least the Student-t critical value (Cornish-Fisher) for two-sided alpha 0.05 / N with N = 170 (futures ledger) + 48 = 218; at least 10 years. Prediction: zero shortlisted cells (about 92%); with 48 cells and about 25 years each, a |t| above 3.5 is not expected by chance, and EIA futures were not free of arbitrage over 25 years.
