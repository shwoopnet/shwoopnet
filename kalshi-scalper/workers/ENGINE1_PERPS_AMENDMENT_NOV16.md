The rates come only from the funding extract (the perp-data funding files, its own wall: the same tickers, prints from 2026-11-06T00:00Z on, an extract Forensics builds, an allow line from Auditor B). If a print is missing from the extract, or no funding extract is supplied, the trade is labelled "funding not booked" and is left out of every gate figure (counted, printed per rule). A Friday entry held over the weekend crosses three UTC prints (Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
