4. **Funding is booked (point 2):** a hold that crosses a daily 4:00 PM ET print (21:00 UTC under EST), strictly between the entry candle end and the exit candle end (an exit AT the print pays nothing), is charged or credited that print's rate as a fraction of notional: a positive rate is paid by longs and received by shorts, a negative rate the reverse. The rates come only from the funding extract (the perp-data funding files, its own wall: the same tickers, prints from 2026-11-06T00:00Z on, an extract Forensics builds, an allow line from Auditor B). If a print is missing from the extract, or no funding extract is supplied, the trade is labelled "funding not booked" and is left out of every gate figure (counted, printed per rule). A Friday entry held over the weekend crosses three UTC prints (Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
5. **BH denominator (point 3):** m = read 1's 504 + every new rule counted before results. Read 1 (SPY, QQQ, 252 rules each) is a first look at Nov 6 to 14; the SPY and QQQ rerun under this amendment (420 rules each, 840) is a SECOND look at the same days, disclosed as a rerun after a zero, and counted in m.
