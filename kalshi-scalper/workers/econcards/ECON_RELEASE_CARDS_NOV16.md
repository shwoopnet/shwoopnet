# Two release-rule cards, thresholds frozen before any data is in (Desk B, Nov 16, 2026; the Head's order of 15:20Z)
Both use the historical lists of ECONHIST_LISTS (landing pending) for the event calendar and then candles pulled for them through Main. No number below changes after the lists land. Nothing at or after a series' held-out date is touched (KXCPI and KXPCECORE: 2025-09-26T00:00:00Z, NOWCAST1; other series: only closes before 2026-09-10, the endpoint's end; the forward live list is a later, separate read). Fee: per order, rounded up (7% x p x (1 - p) per contract, 10 contracts, charged on every order; a held-to-settlement order has the entry fee only). Slip: +1c on every entry and exit (2c primary for any order sent in the last 2 minutes before a close or sold in them, per C11 and the Head's rule). "Not fill-checked: upper bound."

## DRIFTPRINT1: buy the side that has been drifting into the release, before the release
- **Mechanism:** a release ladder is a strip of bracket markets; the market's own price rises into a release if informed flow or hedgers lean one way. The card asks whether following that drift pays after costs. Not a version of ECONLAG (withdrawn, the Fed fast-market lead-lag) or ECONFADE (after the print); it acts BEFORE the print, from the market's own price path, no Fed market, no forecast.
- **Event:** one release of one series (the release time T is the close time of the settled event, as ECONLAG amendment 1 did for the calendar; ET clock check as in econlag.py `scheduled`).
- **Rule (frozen):** among the markets of the event whose mid at T-3h (hourly candle ending then) is between 0.20 and 0.80, compute rise = mid at T-30min minus mid at T-3h on 5-minute candles. Pick the market with the largest absolute rise if it is at least 0.05 (ties: lowest ticker); the side to buy is the side that rose (YES if mid rose, NO if it fell). Buy at the T-30min candle's ask plus 1c if that price is between 0.15 and 0.85, else no trade. One entry per event.
- **Exits (both registered):** HOLD to settlement (primary read); EARLY: sell at the bid of the 5-minute candle ending T+5min, minus 1c. Both are reported; the primary is HOLD.
- **Primary series:** KXJOBLESSCLAIMS only (weekly, up to about 100 events before 2026-09-10; the only series with enough events). KXPAYROLLS, KXUE, KXCPINDEX, KXUSPPIYOY, KXISMPMI, KXUSISMSERV, KXCONTCLAIMS are reported as description and are not tries. KXCPI and KXPCECORE, only before 2025-09-26, description.
- **Bar:** n at least 60 trades; cash PF 1.3 or better at 1c, PF 1.0 or better at 2c, both halves (first floor(n/2) by close time) PF 1.0 or better, no event date over 30% of net. Under 60 trades: "no evidence at n = x".
- **Prediction (written now):** n 60 to 90; PF near 1.0 at 1c; fails at 2c. Kill: a read that misses the bar closes the card; no variants.
- **Data needed (pull through Main):** for the primary series' events: 5-minute candles for each event's markets whose hourly mid at T-3h is contested, T-3h to T+10min. Request count to be sized from the landed lists (events x contested markets); if over 420 requests the pull is cut to the most recent events first, stated before the read.

## FADEPRINT1: fade the first move after the release
- **Mechanism:** after a release the ladder jumps; if the first move over-reacts and the price gives back part of it, the opposite side pays. ECONFADE (Desk A's count, Refinement row 87) ended NOT_ENOUGH_DATA on a few weeks of data (shock 0.15, retrace 0.04, CPI and payrolls only); this card is the SAME mechanism with a long history, so it is a re-test, not a new mechanism, and it keeps ECONFADE's thresholds.
- **Event:** as above. **Rule (frozen, ECONFADE's constants):** for each market with mid between 0.10 and 0.90 at T-5min, shock = mid at T+10min minus mid at T-5min; if abs(shock) is at least 0.15, the first move is the shock's side; at T+10min buy the OPPOSITE side at the 1-minute candle's ask plus 1c if its price is between 0.10 and 0.90. One entry per event: the market with the largest abs(shock) (ties by lowest ticker).
- **Exits (both registered):** HOLD to settlement (primary); EARLY: sell at the bid of the candle ending T+20min (a 10 minute retrace window) minus 1c.
- **Primary series:** KXJOBLESSCLAIMS; KXPAYROLLS, KXCPI (before 2025-09-26) and the rest as description.
- **Bar:** the same as DRIFTPRINT1 (n at least 60, PF 1.3 at 1c, 1.0 at 2c, both halves, top date 30%).
- **Prediction:** the 15c shock happens on a minority of weekly claims prints (the claims ladder moves little), so n is the risk; expected n under 60. If so: "no evidence at n = x", not a pass.
- **Data needed:** 1-minute candles T-5min to T+20min for the primary series' events (about one request per contested market per event).
