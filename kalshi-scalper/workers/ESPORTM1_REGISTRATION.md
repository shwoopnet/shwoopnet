## 4. Question, counterparty, prior
Is the underdog of an esports match priced below what a rating built only from earlier settled Kalshi matches implies? **Counterparty:** retail fans who sell the unfamous team and buy the famous one. **Honest alternative (expected):** market makers copy bookmaker odds and public rankings, which know far more than a Kalshi-only Elo; a model-at-least-3x-ask flag then marks the Elo's error (roster changes, a team on a streak the Elo has not yet seen, small samples of 8 matches) and the win rate equals the price or less, with the cheap-tail favourite-longshot bias on top. **Prior of a pass: 2%.**

## 5. Reference (fixed now, per series so a team name in one game never meets another game)
Elo table per series. Start 1500, scale 400, `K` one of {16, 24, 32, 48}, **fitted once on the first two thirds (by close time) of the qualified matches by the lowest log-loss of the walk-forward Elo probability against the settled outcome (no price is used in the fit), then frozen** and applied to all. No home-region or regional effect: the listings carry only a team-name string and no region field (registered deviation from the brief). A match updates the two teams together when its `close_time` (the moment the winner is declared) is at or before the look time. Only matches with two markets and a parsed ticker are rated. A team needs at least 8 prior settled matches in that series (both teams). P(team A wins) = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B - R_A)/400)).

## 6. Universe, look, trade (fixed now)
Qualified events (section 3) with start before the cut. Candles: batched 1 minute candlesticks, 8 markets per request, window start minus 6 hours to start. **Look:** the last 1 minute candle that ends at or before start minus 120 seconds and has a valid two-sided quote (0 < yes bid < yes ask < 1, closing values; the shared `candlefield` parser reads live and historical names). No such candle: no row, counted. The Elo at the look uses matches with `close_time` at or before the look time. Both sides priced from that quote (YES ask; NO ask = 1 - YES bid). **Cheap side:** the side whose ask is in [0.03, 0.30]; if neither, no row. **Treated trade:** Elo probability of that side >= 3 x its ask, ask at most 0.30 (hard limit); buy 10 contracts at the ask, held to settlement, max one per match, fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded UP to the cent per order (stress line fees x1.2). A quote is not a fill; stated. **Control:** every cheap-side row in the same universe, same fee. Cluster: the match (the event); also reported clustered by calendar day (US Central) for robustness. Windows: halves by start of the treated trades (median), and separately the last third of the qualified matches by close time (the test third; K was fitted on the first two thirds).
**Time box:** the candle fetch runs on the qualified events in md5-of-event-ticker order (an unbiased prefix), at most 1 to 2 threads, 3.3 seconds between requests, backoff on 429, at most 2.5 hours. The scored read uses the prefix actually fetched; if it is partial, treated n is projected to the full 4,141 and the verdict is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if the fetched prefix has under 150 treated trades (the sample is stated). No other prefix, universe or parameter is tried afterwards.

## 7. Numeric prediction (written before any result)

## 8. Kill rules

NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if under 150 treated trades in what was fetched (with months-to-go from the forward plan). A pass would only mean a forward test on the post-cut matches, never a trade.
