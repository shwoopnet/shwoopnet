## 3. The rule (fixed now)
**Mechanism (stated in advance).** Same as the goal ladder: retail underprices depth and tail players in an anytime-player ladder, so the sum of asks over the 1+ rungs is below the expected number of players who record the stat. Counterparty: market makers and retail pricing tail rungs off a headline rate. No outside model.
**Universe.** Per series, the 1+ and 2+ rungs with volume above 0 of eligible games (above), in **strict md5 order of the game part of the event ticker** (e.g. 26JUN14CARVGK, so the same game sits at the same place in both series), fresh games first, goal-sample games after all fresh ones. A game is used only when ALL its listed rungs have candles (each market is retried up to 8 times; a game with one market that still fails is dropped whole and the process moves on; a 404 on the historical endpoint twice drops the game) and it has at least 5 valid 1+ quotes.
**Look.** The last 1 minute candle of the market ending at or before ESPN game start minus 120 seconds with a valid two sided quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1, ask - bid <= 0.10) (`period_interval 1`, start-3600 to start). Cheap side = YES if ask in [0.03, 0.30]; else NO if 1 - bid in [0.03, 0.30]; the other side is never evaluated or printed.
**Pooled rule.** Buy the cheap side at its ask on every such rung, **C = 10 contracts, hard limit 0.30**, held to settlement, **max 2 per game chosen by sha1 of the ticker** (lowest hash first; no price used). Fee 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded UP to the cent per order, divided by C (stress x1.2). Taker at the ask. Results other than yes/no dropped.
**Under-round filter.** Per game, ladder sum = sum of YES asks at the same look over its valid 1+ rungs. **E = the mean number of those 1+ rungs that settled YES per game, computed on the FIT games only (first two thirds of the complete games in fetch order).** The standing instruction asks for E from ESPN box scores; the stored ESPN table has goals only, and the ladder is a partial list of players (a box score total would overstate E and make every game look under-round), so E is the listed players' realised count from the settled rungs, which is what NHLROUND1 also used (listed players who scored). Disclosed deviation. Under-round game = ladder sum below E. The filtered rule = the pooled rule restricted to under-round games (same bar; it is expected to be smaller). Over-round shown as information. E uses outcomes of FIT games only for one number per series.
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract after fee, game-clustered SE, z and lower 95% bound; also date-clustered (ET event date) SE; PF = gross wins over gross losses after fee; win rate against mean price; halves H1 and H2 = complete games split at the median start time; fees x1.2. Information only: uncapped all-cheap line, by strike (1+, 2+).

## 4. Pass bar (fixed now)
Per series, for the pooled rule and the filtered rule separately:
- **LEAD** only if n >= 150 trades AND net after fees above 0 in BOTH halves AND game-clustered lower 95% bound above 0 AND PF >= 1.5.
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** if under 150 trades.
- **FALSIFIED** otherwise (net not above 0 in both halves, or lower bound not above 0, or PF under 1.5, with at least 150 trades). A LEAD would not be a promotion: z bar 5.27, no promote verb.
Dependence note for any LEAD: it counts as at most partly independent evidence beside the goal hint.

## 5. Time box and procedure
At most **150 minutes** of candle fetching in total, ONE process in strict md5 order, sequential: assists up to 70 minutes, then points up to 80 minutes; each series stops when 150 candidate trades are reached (counted from quotes only, no result) or its box ends; no extension. Endpoint `/historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks`, one market per request, 0.6 s sleep, backoff on 429 (api._get), honest default User-Agent, cache `/home/user/.cache/nhlast1/nhlast1.sqlite` outside the repo.
