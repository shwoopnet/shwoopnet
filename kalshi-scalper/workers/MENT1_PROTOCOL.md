# MENT1 (registered Nov 10, 2026, 11:40 AM CT, before any mention market list, candle or outcome is read): calibration of "will X say word Y" markets, cheap side
Research only, public keyless Kalshi data, no order. Cheap side only (ask 3c to 30c). Not favourite-and-hold.
Universe, fixed now by structure and a hash, never by a price or result: Kalshi series whose ticker contains MENTION (457 listed Nov 10 in the public /series list). Take the 120 with the smallest md5(ticker). For each, list its settled markets from /historical/markets and /markets (status settled). Per series keep at most 12 markets by smallest md5(market ticker); the final sample is at most about 1,400 markets and is cut to the first 500 by md5 to cap the candle requests (about 500 requests).
Data: hourly candles for the 30 hours before each market's close_time. Decision quote = the last candle ending at least 1 hour before the close, both sides quoted, spread <= 0.05. Fee ceil(0.07*C*p*(1-p)*100)/C/100, C = 10.
Groups: EARN (series containing EARNINGSMENTION) and OTHER (all other MENTION series). Cells = group x side (YES, NO) x band (0.04-0.12, 0.12-0.20, 0.20-0.30) = 12. Ledger N about 5,172, z bar about 4.28. Event-clustered z (cluster = the market's event ticker). Development = first two thirds of markets by close time inside each group; last third read ONCE only for a cell that shows PF >= 1.5, n >= 100 markets in >= 30 events, mean >= 2c and z at or above the bar.
Counterparty: the retail buyer who overpays a topical or dramatic word and the one who underpays a routine word. Cash PF reported first for every cell with n >= 20.
Prediction before running: fails (prior 10%). Reasons: 500 markets cannot fill 12 cells to 100 each, most cells will be NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, and earlier calibration scans of sports, macro and weather found no cheap-side cell above fee.
