## Split and bar (all three)
Events sorted by close time; development = earlier two thirds of distinct close times, held out = last third, read ONCE and ONLY for a development survivor. Survivor = cash PF >= 1.5 AND n >= 100 rows AND event-clustered one-sided z above 5.34 (shared N about 1,100,000 + 32 tries; Bonferroni 0.05/N), and also reported against 4.39 (N 8,795, the lenient bar). Clusters = the hourly close. A test with fewer than 100 development rows is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, with the date at which 100 rows would exist at the observed rate. Count of tests: 32 (PIN1 4, ROUND1 16, AVG1 12).

## PIN1 (4 tests)
Census (2 tests, BTC and ETH): for every hourly close from 2026-06-01 to the last Binance day held, A = Binance spot 1 minute close of the bar ending at the close. M = 500 (BTC) or 50 (ETH). x = distance from A to the nearest multiple of M divided by M (uniform 0 to 0.5 expected under a smooth model). Statistic: share of events with x <= 0.05 against the expected 0.10, one-sided binomial z, development third-of-time split as above. Pricing step (2 tests, BTC and ETH) is run ONLY if the development census z >= 3.0 for that asset: source ladl1 look 360, strikes that are multiples of M, spot S the Binance close at the look, sigma_rem from the last 60 one-minute log returns times sqrt(6.33); when |ln(S/K)| <= sigma_rem the side NOT currently in the money is bought if its ask is in 0.30 to 0.88. Otherwise the pricing test is recorded NOT RUN (census failed) and still counted.

## ROUND1 (16 tests)
Source ladl1 union of shards (BTC and ETH hourly ladders, dedupe by ticker and look). Dimensions: series (BTC, ETH) x look (600, 360) x band (cheap, mid) x round class (strict: multiple of 1000 BTC or 100 ETH; loose: multiple of 500 BTC or 50 ETH). Rule: at the look quote, for markets whose strike (rounded to the integer) is in the class, buy the side whose ask is in the band (YES if both). Comparators, descriptive only and not tries: the same band at non-round strikes; realised win share against the lognormal model by |ln(S/K)|/(sigma sqrt(tau)) bin (sigma from the last 60 Binance 1 minute returns, tau = minutes left plus 1/3). A round class is only a candidate if it beats the non-round comparator in the same sample.

## AVG1 (12 tests)
Source quote-data quotes/<date>/<hhmm>/ladder.csv.gz and spot.csv.gz (BTC and ETH pooled). Index = mean over venues of the last trade within 10 s (CFRTI1 loader). Decision t = close - 60 + k, k in {0, 20, 40}, using the ladder quote last at or before t within 3 s. A_fixed = sum of index sampled each second from close-60 to t (k seconds); u = 60 - k; sigma_s = std of 10 s log changes of the index over the 10 minutes before t divided by sqrt(10), times the index. P(A > K) = Phi((A_fixed/60 + u S_t / 60 - K) / sd), sd = sigma_s sqrt(u(u+1)(2u+1)/6) / 60; k = 0 reduces to a plain 60 s mean. Buy the side with model chance minus ask >= edge, ask in the band, edge in {0.05, 0.10}, band in {cheap, mid}. 12 = 3 k x 2 band x 2 edge. Baseline = every in-band row at the same k.
