# CHEAPTAIL1 registration (DRAFT, Desk A, Nov 17, 2026): one strategy for forward counting

Status: DRAFT for the Verification Analyst's honesty check. Nothing is live. No order. Log only, under the owner's standing shadow order. Needs the VA's check before it is committed as final.

**What it is.** The four cheap-side, early-exit rules on HYPE and BNB that held in the held-out reads, counted as ONE strategy so their forward trades pool. Mechanism family: cheap-tail price entry with a timed early exit (a spread or momentum condition, no hold to settlement). No new rule and no new setting.

**What "M60" means as an exit.** Sell the bought side at the bid of the candle ending EXACTLY 60 seconds before the market's close, minus the haircut h (1c primary, 2c second, floor 1c). It is an exit 60 seconds before the close, not a 60 second hold. If that candle is missing or not two sided there is no sale and the trade is booked as not scored ("no mark"); if the entry itself came after that mark, no trade ("too late"). Cash is net of the fee at both ends.

**One position per market and window.** In any 15 minute window on a market, at most ONE of the rules above opens a (logged) position. The earliest look in time wins (HYPE: 840 s, then 600 s, then 420 s); a later sister rule on the same contract logs nothing for that window. Rules on different markets (HYPE and BNB) do not conflict. This is what makes the pooled count honest: no stacking of one idea on one contract.

**Pooled signal rate and the date of 100 forward trades.** From the held-out prints, the four rules fire 2.94 (rule 1), 3.26 (2), 3.19 (3) and 3.97 (4) signals a day, 13.4 a day if every one counted. The one-position rule removes the overlaps between the three HYPE rules, and I have NOT measured how many; the true pooled rate is below 13.4 and above the best single rule (4.0). Arithmetic, not a forecast: at 13.4 a day the 100th forward trade is about 8 days after all four are running; at 8 a day it is about 13 days; at 4 a day, 25 days. The first read is the date the 100th pooled forward trade settles. Start: rules 1 and 2 have run in V1 since Nov 16; rules 3 and 4 start at the runner merge of their list. Pooled counting starts the day all four run; the earlier forward signals of rules 1 and 2 stay in their own V1 record.
