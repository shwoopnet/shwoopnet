# CMD1 to CMD4 shared rules (commodity, metal and index perps versus their futures reference at one minute; written 2026-11-06 before any perp-versus-futures return, basis or outcome was computed)

Discovery only. No live code, no functions/, no index.html, no promote verb, no credentials. Strategy Research V2 worker. **Counterparty:** retail and thin-book participants on the Kalshi perp (spreads 0.5 to 8 bps) versus the CME futures book, which is deeper. The perp is a copy of the futures price made by a small number of makers; if a maker is slow, perp-minus-futures basis opens for a minute or two and a taker who sees the futures could trade it. **Honest alternative:** the makers re-quote within the same minute (the one minute grid is too coarse to see a lag), basis is just the cost of carry and noise, and the 12 bp a side placeholder fee swamps any residual.

## Timestamp convention (stated now, tested on known events below)
Kalshi candle `end_period_ts` T is the END of the minute and every close field is the quote at T. Yahoo `ts_unix` is the START of the bar (the first row of the file, 1793260800, is 04:00 ET, the pre-market open). **Futures price at time T = close of the Yahoo bar whose ts_unix is T - 60.** Perp price at T = mid of the bid_c and ask_c of the candle ending at T.
**Alignment check (CMD0, information, no try):** scheduled releases inside development: PCE Fri 2026-10-30 08:30 ET (12:30Z), ISM manufacturing Mon 11-02 10:00 ET (15:00Z, EST from Sunday 11-01 02:00, so 15:00Z), a second 10:00 ET release Tue 11-03 (JOLTS, 15:00Z). For each event and each of the six pairs, find the minute T in [event-5, event+10] minutes with the largest absolute one minute log change of futures (futures bar start T-60) and of perp mid; count the pairs where they are the same T. Shift s in {-1, 0, +1} minutes applied to the futures grid is chosen as the one with the most coincident minutes (ties go to 0); if s is not 0 it is stated in every result and a result is labelled "alignment unconfirmed" if fewer than 9 of 18 coincide at the chosen s. One shot, not tuned on any trade return.

## Cost
Taker both ways on the perp: long buys at ask_c(T) and sells at bid_c(T+H); short sells at bid_c(T) and buys at ask_c(T+H) (the recorded Kalshi 1 minute bid and ask candle close at the decision minute), plus fee **12 bp a side** (24 bp round trip, unverified placeholder), also reported at **5 and 24 bp a side**. Funding ignored (holds of 30 minutes or less; metals fund once a day at 10:00 ET, WTI 14:30 ET, indices 16:00 ET: a trade whose hold spans a funding time is flagged and kept, not dropped). No forward fill: a trade needs a valid perp quote and a futures bar at the entry minute, and a valid perp quote at the exit minute or within 3 minutes after it, else skipped and counted.

## Basis and the roll screen
x_T = ln(perp mid_T / futures_T). Deviation d_T = x_T minus the median of x over the previous 60 minutes of valid pairs in the same session (needs at least 30 of the 60; uses only T-60 to T-1). Roll and bad-tick screen: a minute is FLAGGED if the futures one minute |log change| is at least 30 bp and the perp mid change over the same minute is less than 20 percent of it. A trade is dropped if a flagged minute lies in [entry - 60 minutes, exit]. Flag counts are printed (they are the CMD3 events).
