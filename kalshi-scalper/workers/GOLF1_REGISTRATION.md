## 2. Reachability and census (listings, close times and ESPN names only; no price and no Kalshi result read)
ESPN `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/golf/pga/scoreboard?dates=YYYYMMDD` for an event's start date returns the whole field with, per player, per-round strokes and the 18 hole scores inside each round. Reachable, keyless, 1 request per second, cached outside the repo: 149 PGA Tour calendar events 2024 to 2026-10-03, 141 with a field (team events dropped). Settled Kalshi markets closing before the frozen cut 2026-10-03T16:00Z (live plus historical): KXPGATOUR 8,601 markets / 75 events (from 2025-02), KXPGATOP10 4,415 / 39, KXPGATOP20 4,415 / 39 (both from 2026-01), KXPGAR1LEAD 4,169 / 36, KXPGATOP5 4,415 / 39, KXPGAR2LEAD 3,933 / 33, KXPGAR3LEAD 3,814 / 33. Events matched to an ESPN tournament (an ESPN end date within -5 to +4 days of the earliest market close and at least 30 shared player names): **TOUR 64 of 75, TOP10 and TOP20 36 of 39 each.** Tournaments with at least one candidate market at a look (below): **TOUR 63, TOP10 35, TOP20 35, 66 distinct tournaments, 274 (series, tournament, look) groups, 2,128 + 3,369 + 322 candidate markets.** A discovery that shapes the design: **a player's market closes and settles NO when he is eliminated** (a missed cut closes the winner market on Friday night; the Masters winner market closes before the weekend for players cut), so only markets whose close time is after the look are tradable at that look; this reads a listing field, not a result.
Expected treated trades: not knowable without prices (a treated trade needs a model probability of at least 3 times the ask). Upper bound is 3 per group = 822; the realistic count is a small fraction of it and **the independent units are the 66 tournaments, the three series in one tournament and the two looks share the course, the weather and the same model error**, so 150 treated trades will be at most about 60 to 70 independent observations. **Power is low and stated up front.**
The last third is frozen: no tournament whose markets close on or after 2026-10-03T16:00Z is listed, fetched or joined. The ESPN events used for ratings are those that START before the cut.

## 3. Mechanism, counterparty, prior
Is the long tail of a golf leaderboard (players several strokes off the lead, priced 3c to 30c on the winner, top 10 or top 20 market, or the favourite whose NO side costs 3c to 30c) priced below what a scoring model implies after round 2 or round 3? **Counterparty:** retail buyers who anchor on names and the pre-tournament odds and sell players who are behind. **Honest alternative (expected):** the quoters price the leaderboard with a better model (current form, course fit, weather and tee-time wave, the cut line), so a results-only model's 3x gaps are its own error; plus the favourite-longshot bias (cheap contracts usually overpriced). **Prior of a pass: 2%.**

## 4. Model (fixed now; fitted ONLY on the first two thirds of tournaments)
Per player and round, deviation `x = strokes - field mean strokes that round` (complete 18 hole rounds only; cancels the course). Walk-forward rating `s/(n + k)` of the player's deviations over ESPN rounds in EARLIER tournaments only (event date strictly earlier), shrunk to the tour average (0). **Fit:** the tournaments that have a Kalshi match are ordered by ESPN date and the first two thirds (44 of 66) are the train set; `k` in {5, 10, 20, 40, 80} chosen by minimum walk-forward MSE of `x` over the train set, `sigma` = RMSE at that `k` (measured: k 10, sigma 2.83 strokes). Nothing else is fitted. **Probability at a look after round r in {2, 3}:** the players alive (a round r+1 entry in ESPN and all rounds up to r complete; cut players drop out). 4,000 simulations of the remaining 4-r rounds, each player's remaining total `round(rating x (4-r) + N(0, sigma^2 (4-r)))` added to the strokes so far, seed from md5 of event id and round. P(win) shares ties equally, P(top N) counts a player if fewer than N players are strictly better (ties included, as Kalshi's rule says). The model is a score model, not a market model; it reads no price.
**Look time:** event date (ESPN `date`, the Thursday) + r days + 10:00 UTC, before any US round r+1 tee time and after round r in the US (ESPN gives no venue; an event whose round runs into the morning or is delayed, and Hawaii, may leak a few holes; stated, not fixed). **Alive** uses ESPN's final data (a player who withdrew before teeing off round r+1 drops out: a small look-ahead on a handful of players; stated).

## 5. Universe, look, trade (fixed now)
Kalshi KXPGATOUR, KXPGATOP10, KXPGATOP20 markets with close time before the cut, matched as in section 2, player matched to ESPN by `norm` of `yes_sub_title` (accents, punctuation and Jr/Sr/II/III dropped); unmatched players are counted and dropped. **Candidate** (to limit requests; chosen by the model, not by price or result): the market is open after the look (close time later) and the model P(yes) is in [0.09, 0.91] (outside this no side can have p >= 3 x ask). **Look quote:** batched 1 minute candles (8 markets per request; per ticker for historical markets), the last candle that ends at or before the look time (and no more than 6 hours before) with a valid two-sided quote (0 < yes bid < yes ask < 1, closing values); YES ask, NO ask = 1 - YES bid. **Cheap side:** the side whose ask is in [0.03, 0.30]; none, no row. **Treated trade:** model probability of that side >= 3 x its ask, hard limit 0.30; at most **3 per (tournament, series, look), highest model-to-ask ratio first**; buy 10 contracts at the ask, held to settlement, fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded UP to the cent per order (stress line fees x1.2). A quote is not a fill; stated. **Control:** every cheap-side row in the candidate set (it is the same model-selected set, so the control isolates the 3x ratio rule, not the candidate filter), same fee. **Cluster = tournament** (the unit of dependence).
Kalshi fetch: 1 thread, 3.3 s pause (Kalshi returns 429 on about 40% of faster calls), backoff in `api._get`, cache `/tmp/g1/golf1/candles.json` outside the repo, resumable, tournaments in md5(event id) order. **Time box: 90 minutes of candle fetching on the md5 prefix** (one 60 minute extension allowed only if `project` is within 25% of the 150 trade bar). The fetched prefix is a random sample of tournaments; the projection to all 66 scales the treated count by tournaments.
**Gate before any result is joined:** `golf1 project` counts treated rows from prices and the model only (no Kalshi result is read) and projects to all tournaments. Projected treated trades under 150 or fewer than 40 tournaments with candidates => verdict NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, no scored read, forward plan below. The scored read (`golf1 run`) is refused unless project says RUNNABLE, and is refused a second time.

## 6. Numeric prediction (written before any price or result)
Treated win rate at or below the mean ask (the model's 3x gaps are its error), mean net about -3c to 0c per contract, PF about 0.6 to 1.0, treated minus control about 0, not significant. Verdict most likely FALSIFIED or NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (probability of NOT_ENOUGH_DATA about 55% on count alone). If anything beats the bar I suspect first the look time (a round already partly played), then ties.

## 7. Kill rules
FALSIFIED if the treated mean net after fees is not above 0 in BOTH halves (split at the median ESPN date of the treated trades) or PF is under 1.5. NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if fewer than 150 treated trades (projected, then actual) or fewer than 40 tournaments. NOT_YET_FALSIFIED only if mean > 0 in both halves, PF >= 1.5, mean >= 2c, tournament-cluster z >= 5.27 and the treated mean beats the control mean. A pass would only mean a forward test on the frozen tournaments, never a trade.

## 8. Forward plan (if NOT_ENOUGH_DATA)
The PGA Tour plays about 38 stroke-play events a year; the fall series runs to mid December. Kalshi lists TOUR, TOP10 and TOP20 for each (about 1 tournament a week, 2 looks, 3 series). At the treated rate observed by `project` per tournament, weeks to 150 treated trades on post-cut tournaments = 150 / (rate per tournament) weeks; the frozen third (from 2026-10-03) is untouched and is read once when the count is projected to reach 150. The count will in all likelihood be reached only in the 2027 season (an honest wait of many months); a pre-event recorder of Kalshi quotes at the two looks is the cheaper route and is NOT built here.
