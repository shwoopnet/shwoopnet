# LADCONV1 (registered Nov 9, 2026, before any pair is evaluated): riskless inconsistency across the hourly Bitcoin ladder
Research only; no order, no key. Data: `data/hist15/KXBTCD.sqlite` candles (14,270 strikes, 758,001 one-minute candles, Jun to Aug 2026), bid close and ask close per strike per minute.
Mechanism and counterparty: the chance of finishing above a higher strike cannot exceed the chance above a lower strike. If the YES bid of the HIGH strike exceeds the YES ask of the LOW strike at the same minute, selling YES high (buying NO high at 1 - bid) and buying YES low locks a payoff of at least 1 - bid_high... precisely: cost = ask_low + (1 - bid_high), payoff at least 1 (it pays 1 if the price is below the high strike or above the low strike, and 2 in between), so the locked profit per pair is bid_high - ask_low minus both taker fees. Counterparty: the slow maker who left a stale quote on one strike.
Test (fixed, no search): for every (event, minute) with two or more quoted strikes (bid > 0, ask < 1, spread at most 5c), every pair of strikes in the chosen set; locked profit per contract = bid_high - ask_low - fee_low - fee_high, fee = ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1-p) x 100)/10 cents on the price of each leg (low leg at its ask, high leg's NO at 1 - bid), C = 10. Report: pairs checked, pairs with locked profit above 0, above 1c, above 2c; distinct events and hours; the distribution of lengths of consecutive minutes a violation persists (how executable); and the same count if a one-minute delay is applied (quotes of the NEXT minute for both legs, the cheap execution test). No outcome is read: the profit is riskless by construction, so no holdout is needed; the figure is executable only if size at the touch exists, which candles do not show (size unknown: stated as the limit of the test). Count: 1 try. Prediction: violations of more than 1c after fees are essentially absent (under 0.1% of pairs) and vanish with a one-minute delay; if any survive they are thin-book quotes whose size cannot be known from this data.
