## Rules (exact, no tuning after this commit; one parameter set each)
All three are riskless sets: payoff at settlement is at least what is paid, so no outcome is needed to count them. Outcomes are only used afterwards as a check that the set paid at least $1 a set.
- **M1, nested pair (daily).** Within one event, markets whose YES sets are nested: for strike type `greater` or `greater_or_equal` (including the monthly touch-above series), floor K1 < K2 means YES(K2) is a subset of YES(K1); for `less` (touch-below monthly series), cap K1 < K2 means YES(K1) is a subset of YES(K2). Let S be the subset market and T the superset. Set = buy YES(T) at yes_ask(T) and buy NO(S) at 1 - yes_bid(S). Payout at least 1 (2 if S happens). Lock when yes_bid(S) - yes_ask(T) is positive. Both quotes two sided (bid at least 0.01, ask at most 0.99, ask above bid). All pairs in an event (not only adjacent), best pair per event-snapshot. Fee: 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order, C = 10, on both legs; also fees x1.2. **Net per set after fee must be at least +1c at C = 10** to count as a lock. One-touch caveat: monthly MAX or MIN sets are nested only if floor and cap strikes of the same event are on one reference path, true by construction.
- **B1, bracket partition (daily).** Events whose markets are one `less` tail, `between` buckets with contiguous floor and cap, one `greater` tail (checked from strikes, count of events failing contiguity reported). Buy every YES: lock when sum of asks plus fees leaves at least +1c a set (payout exactly 1). Buy every NO: lock when payout n - 1 exceeds sum of (1 - bid) plus fees by at least 1c. Every leg two sided.
- **M2, nested pair (book).** KXBTCD strikes, same event, K1 < K2. Using the best level only: yes_ask(K1) = 1 - best NO bid(K1), yes_bid(K2) = best YES bid(K2). Set: buy YES(K1) at that ask and NO(K2) at 1 - yes_bid(K2). Strikes in one cycle are read up to 1 s apart, so a "lock" can be time skew: a lock is **persistent** only if the same pair is a lock (at least +1c after fee at C = 10) in two consecutive cycles. Size of a set = min(ask size, bid size) from the level 1 sizes; sweep depth is not modelled beyond level 1. Report counts of cycles, pair-cycles, locks, persistent locks, median and max size, and the average dollars a persistent lock would have made with the size at level 1.

## Counterparty and information
Counterparty: a quoter (or two different quoters across adjacent strikes of a 7 to 300 strike ladder) who posts a higher bid on a strike that is a subset than the ask on the superset, or a bracket set whose asks do not sum to at least 100c. Information: none outside the book; the structure is arithmetic. **Honest alternative (expected):** Kalshi's matching engine and the market makers' own sums prevent a lock that survives fees; every earlier scan found zero.

## Kill criteria and verdicts
- Any rule with **zero** locks: FALSIFIED for the data read (M2 on a week of 3 second cycles is a real sample; M1 and B1 on 7 snapshots are reported as NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, "no locks in 7 snapshots", and the forward plan is the recorder as is, re-read once on 2026-12-05).
- A lock count above zero is a LEAD only if all of: net at least +1c a set at C = 10 and at fees x1.2, persistent (M2), level 1 size at least 10 sets, and on a market with volume or open interest above 0 (M1, B1). Anything else is an artifact and is said to be. A lock is never a LEAD from a quote alone: it needs a book snapshot showing size and one consecutive confirmation before an owner is asked about any capped probe. No promote verb; a riskless set also needs the settlement check (the set paid at least $1 on every settled event).
- The z bar (about 5.27) does not apply to an arithmetic lock (no sampling distribution), only to any directional claim, and none is made.
