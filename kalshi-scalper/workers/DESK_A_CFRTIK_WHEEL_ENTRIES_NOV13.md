# Five wheel admit entries: "CFRTI-K on <market>" (Idea Desk A, Nov 13, 2026; for the Head of R&D, due 06:00Z)
Each entry is the SAME rule as the probation seat and as section 1 of `DESK_A_FROZEN_WHEEL_DEFINITIONS_NOV13.md`, with no parameter changed for any market: look = close minus 120 s exactly; cheap side = YES if the YES ask is 0.05 to 0.30, else NO if the NO ask is 0.05 to 0.30; spread 0.03 or less; model edge 0.05 or more; index = floor_strike x (perp mark at D) / (perp mark at close minus 900 s); sd = the sample standard deviation of the log changes of the 11 one minute marks (D back to D minus 600 s; at least 6 changes) x sqrt(4/3); model YES = Phi(ln(index / floor_strike) / sd); hold to settlement; 10 contracts for the record; fee rounded up for the order. Only the market, the perp and the quote source differ. Forward only, from the date the Head lists each place, and only once the first nightly `kalshi1m` archive file carrying the perp ticker has landed on `perp-data` (until then every window is "no signal: no mark").

- **What is counted:** windows whose last NC15 poll at or before D (at most 10 s earlier) shows the cheap side at 0.05 to 0.30 with a spread of 0.03 or less, over the 1.18 days NC15 holds (`desk_a_held/nc15_cfrtik_counts.py`, book columns only). **The model edge test is NOT counted** (it needs the perp candles, which are not archived for these tickers yet): the signals per 21 days are therefore "unknown until the archive exists", and these counts are an upper bound. For scale only: in the Bitcoin development 42% of the cheap-and-tight windows passed the edge test (610 of 1,458); nothing says the same share holds on another market.
- **Quote and fill check (the slower log):** the quote is the NC15 book at the last poll at or before D; the fill check is the NEXT NC15 poll, at most 6 s after the signal poll, each showing 10 contracts at or under the limit (the signal poll's ask, touch plus 0, the probation seat's registered limit); otherwise "not fill-checked". Result source and void rule as in section 0 of the sheet. NC15 stop 2026-12-31; the perp archive stop 2027-02-26.
- **Perp mark:** (bid close + ask close) / 2 of the perp's 1 minute candle keyed by `end_period_ts` (the unit factor cancels in every ratio the rule uses); a side missing or 0 = the mark is missing; a window without mark 0, the opening mark and 6 log changes is "no signal", never interpolated.

## The four caveats, beside every entry
1. **Endpoint fields unverified** for the FUTURE ticker (`KXWTIFUTURE`) and for the non-Bitcoin crypto perps (`KXHYPEPERP`, `KXBNBPERP`); verified only for `KXBTCPERP`. One request each on the candle endpoint settles it; the archive's first file also settles it.
2. **The strike's source differs from the perp.** Each binary's strike is the average of its own reference (60 seconds before the open); the perp's reference is a different instrument. The rule applies the perp's RETURN since the open to the strike, so a constant basis cancels and a changing one does not.
3. **Trading hours, checked (public hourly perps list on `perp-data`, 96 snapshots over 23 days, 32 of them in the weekend window Friday 22:00Z to Sunday 22:00Z).** All five tickers were `active` with a two-sided quote at every snapshot, weekends included: the perps quote around the clock. The spreads do not stay tight: weekday median relative spread silver 1.6 bp, copper 3.0, WTI 2.3, HYPE 6.9, BNB 5.8; in the weekend window silver 2.4 bp, HYPE 6.7, BNB 5.5, but COPPER 130.7 bp (maximum 20,000 bp) and WTI 274.2 bp (maximum 488 bp). So on copper and WTI, the perp's quote is wide or stale on weekends, when the underlying futures are shut; the binary markets are also quiet then. The rule has no filter for a wide perp spread and none is added (no parameter changes); a window that trades on a stale weekend perp mark is possible.
4. **The Bitcoin read found no edge near the bar:** development n 610, PF 1.09, z 0.71 against 5.46 (unrounded fee); with the fee as charged PF 1.05, mean +0.61c a contract. The time-exit variant lost to hold. These five places are the same rule on other markets; nothing says the Bitcoin result carries over, and nothing says it does not.

## Rule for missing minute marks
A window without every mark the formula needs (mark 0, the opening mark, and at least 6 log changes between neighbouring marks of marks 0 to 10) is "no signal"; a missing mark is never interpolated, carried forward or replaced by `reference_price`.

## Addendum (Nov 13, 2026, the Head's ruling on the weekend finding; PRINTING ONLY, no definition changes)
No filter on the perp's spread (a filter would be a parameter). Instead every logged signal on these five places prints the perp's RELATIVE SPREAD at the decision mark (mark 0: (ask close - bid close) / mid of the candle ending at D), and the block report prints the block's results with and without the signals whose perp spread was over 50 bp, as information only. The block verdict uses all signals. Accepted rulings recorded here: the later result read is a small kalshi-pull list job built by Desk A at each block read and run by the Chief of Staff; the worse-of-two fill price is the rule; the `KXBTCPERP` archive is with the Chief of Staff (until it lands the Bitcoin place logs "no signal: no mark"); each entry counts from the first archive file carrying its ticker and Auditor B's "closed" on this sheet.
