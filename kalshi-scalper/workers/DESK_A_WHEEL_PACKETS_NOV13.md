## Packet 1: PERPPAIR (index-perp relative value). One packet; two markets; NOT buildable as a seat by Sunday (see "buildable")
- **id:** PERPPAIR.
- **Mechanism, one sentence:** two Kalshi perpetuals on the same underlying index (the S&P 500: `KXSPYPERP` against `KXUS500PERP`; the Nasdaq 100: `KXQQQPERP` against `KXUSTECH100PERP`) are quoted by separate makers, so when their price ratio drifts from its recent median by more than the full cost of crossing both books, the pair is bought rich-against-cheap and closed when the ratio returns.
- **Market or series (seats are per market):** two pair-markets: SPY/US500 and QQQ/UST100.
- **Entry:** side = long the cheap leg and short the rich leg (log ratio of mids below or above its rolling median); look = every whole minute on the 1 minute candles (bid close and ask close of each leg); signal = |ln(ratio) minus its median over the previous 240 minutes| at least 2 x (the two legs' relative spreads added, at that minute), i.e. the deviation must exceed the round-trip cost of crossing both books, so a full return to the median pays zero net before fees (the threshold is the cost, not a number looked up in results); at least 120 minutes of history before the first signal.
- **Exit (a rule (i) variant, with its mechanical reason):** a perpetual has no settlement, so an exit is part of the rule: close both legs when the deviation is back inside half the entry threshold, or after 60 minutes, whichever first (reason: the relation is an identity up to maker lag; the time stop bounds a break in the relation). Both legs taker, real spreads on both legs, both fees, funding paid or received while held: the round trip must clear the bar.
- **Log folder that already records the signal inputs at decision time, with its stop date:** `perp-data` branch, `kalshi1m/<date>.csv.gz` (Kalshi 1 minute candles with bid and ask close for the equity and index perps; `record-perps-candles.yml`, archives the previous finished UTC day, Monday to Saturday), stop 2027-02-26; also `funding/<date>.csv.gz` (funding history) and the 30 second open and close windows (`perps30/`, stop 2027-02-26). Inside 21 days: no (stops in 105 days).
- **Expected signals per 21 days, COUNTED from the log (no outcome, no P&L joined; spreads only, fees unknown so these are UPPER bounds):** from 6 days of candles (Nov 6 to Nov 11, 5,523 common minutes): SPY/US500 6 episodes (48 signal minutes), i.e. about 21 episodes per 21 days; QQQ/UST100 16 episodes (899 signal minutes), about 56 per 21 days. An episode starts after 30 minutes without a signal. Median combined relative spread: 0.03% (SPY/US500) and 0.025% (QQQ/UST100). The count is small and rests on 6 days, one weekend gap; the QQQ pair's 899 signal minutes in 16 episodes suggests a slowly moving ratio (a calibration drift), which the 240 minute median only partly follows.
- **Closest existing mechanism and why this differs:** lock and parity scans (ARB2 to ARB5) were riskless locks inside Kalshi binary events and found nothing; this is a statistical relation between two perpetual instruments, not a lock, with an exit by convergence or time. CFRTI-K takes a model value for one Kalshi contract from another instrument's price; PERPPAIR trades two instruments against each other. A reviewer could still call it the same family (cross-instrument value); I do not claim it is clearly outside.
- **Declined list check (docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md 7e):** no row for perp pairs, perps relative value, or index perps. Not market making (takers on both legs), not favourite-late, not taker flow, not PERPBIN1 (open interest) or CFRTI-K (the perp as a reference for a binary).
- **Buys at 88c or above?** No (no price in that sense; not a binary).
- **Buildable (honest):** there is no perpetual trading engine in the bot at all (no signing for the margin API, no position, margin or funding handling, no loss stop for a two-legged position); the margin API's fees, margin and funding terms are not checked by me. So it cannot be a wheel seat by Sunday; it is a shadow candidate only (a log of the signal and the would-be exit prices from the same candles, no money). Its cost line is unknown until the perp fee schedule is read.
- **Odds, stated:** under 5% that it clears cash PF 1.3 after fees; the spread alone is 3 bp per pair round trip, so the fee schedule decides everything.

## PERPPAIR closes unread on fees (Nov 13, 2026; the Head asked for Kalshi's own fee terms; read from Kalshi's public documentation and the search summary of it, not from a signed-in page)
- **Terms found:** Kalshi's fee schedule PDF (kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf, headed "July 2026 - 7.7.26 Update": confirm it is current) gives a perps TAKER fee from 12 bps (lowest volume tier) down to 2.6 bps (top tier) and a MAKER fee from 5.0 bps down to 0.6 bps, charged on the full exposure when a position is opened AND when it is closed. Funding: stock, index and metals perps once a day at 4 p.m. ET, crypto three times a day; a payment between traders, capped, rates under 0.01% set to zero. Margin: minimum 15.5% of notional for stock perps (about 6.45x).
- **The arithmetic:** a two-leg round trip has FOUR taker fees (open both legs, close both legs): 10.4 bp at the top tier, 48 bp at the lowest tier. The packet's cost threshold was only 2 x the combined spread, about 6 bp. Over 6 days of 1 minute candles the pair's deviation from its 240 minute median was: SPY/US500 median 0.6 bp, 90th percentile 1.6 bp, 99th 7.4 bp; QQQ/UST100 median 1.4 bp, 90th 6.5 bp, 99th 14.5 bp. So the fees alone (10.4 to 48 bp) exceed the typical deviation, and exceed the 99th percentile for SPY/US500 at every tier and for QQQ/UST100 at the lowest tier. With maker fees on both legs the four fees are 2.4 bp (best tier) to 20 bp (tier 0), but a resting entry has the adverse selection that failed five resting tests.
- **The few signals that survive fee-inclusive thresholds** (SPY/US500 4 episodes, QQQ/UST100 5 in 6 days at top-tier taker fees; 3 each at tier 0) come from the data's maximum deviation of about 310,000 bp, which is a bad quote or a unit break, not a trade. They are glitches and count for nothing.
- **Verdict: PERPPAIR is CLOSED ON FEES, UNREAD** (0 tries). Reopening needs a maker-fee tier at the top end AND a fill model for resting orders on both legs, neither of which exists.
