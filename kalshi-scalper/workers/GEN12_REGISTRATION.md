## Scope and rules (owner and charter)
Research only; no live or `functions/` change; no network call at all (local sqlite only). Traded series (15): silver, WTI, copper, natural gas, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, HYPE, BNB, NEAR, ZEC. Gold and Bitcoin are loaded as REFERENCES for partner features only and are never traded. Data: `data/alts2`, `alts3`, `alts4`, `fresh`, `rep`, `l1b`, `book`, `five` (.sqlite), deduped by ticker (first file wins), settled markets with candles only (73,846 settled markets of the 15 series; per-series counts in the cuts file). CHEAP SIDE ONLY: a row exists only where the bought side's ask is 0.04 to 0.30 and the spread is at most 0.03. No favourite-and-hold, no favourite-late (88c to 97c) variant exists in the grammar.

## Grammar (GEN2's, restricted)
One strategy = scope (all 15, one series, or one family: metals {silver, copper, palladium, platinum}, energy {WTI, natural gas}, forex {EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD}, small crypto {HYPE, BNB, NEAR, ZEC}) + side (yes, no, both) + an entry look window (left 180 to 840 s, 60 s steps, width 60 to 600 s; the earliest matching minute in a window is the entry, one trade per market) + 1 to 4 conditions (feature <= or > a pooled DEVELOPMENT quantile, quantile in 10..90 step 10) + one exit. Features (all signed to the bought side): ask, bid, spread, d1, d2, d3, d5 (bid change over 1, 2, 3, 5 minutes), rng5, vol, volz (volume over the series' own development median), doi (open interest change over 3 minutes), hour (UTC), dow, winidx (quarter hour of the hour), mkt_d2 (mean 2 minute mid move of the other series closing the same minute), part_d2, part_mid (partner: metals to gold, energy WTI and natural gas to each other, forex to EUR/USD (EUR/USD to GBP/USD), small crypto to Bitcoin), prev_won, prev_last (same series' previous window result and last mid). The crypto-spot features of GEN2 (z, r3, tb1, tb5) are not used (no spot for these series).
Exits (226): GEN2's 216 fixed combinations (take profit 3, 5, 8, 12, 20 cents or none; stop 5, 10, 20 cents or none; time exit 60, 120, 240 s before close; book exit none, spread >= 3 cents, market against the side), 9 trailing stops (5, 10, 15 cents x 3 time exits), and HOLD to settlement (cash 1 or 0). Exits fill at the recorded bid of the candle that triggers them (never at the threshold).
Fee: UNROUNDED 0.07 p (1-p) per contract on the entry and on every sale (none on settlement), cash basis, 1 contract. Zero-volume guard: a strategy's share of trades with a zero-volume entry or exit candle must be at most 20 percent.

## Search size and N
300,000 strategies are DRAWN (seeds 12000001 to 12000004, 75,000 each, python `random`, drawn from the grammar uniformly per field) and EVERY draw is scored on development, including those with few trades, and counted. No deduplication (more conservative). N for the Bonferroni bar = 800,000 (shared README ledger, rounded) + 300,000 = 1,100,000 +  one-sided z bar = inv_cdf(1 - 0.05 / 1,100,000), computed in code (about 5.33). The 7,731 bar (4.4) is also reported.

## Split
Per series, markets sorted by close time: the first two thirds by close time are DEVELOPMENT, the last third is HELD-OUT (cut times in `GEN12_cuts.json`). Held-out rows are built into a separate file that the search code does not load; it is read once, only for the frozen list.

## Gates
Stage 1, development (every strategy): cash PF >= 1.5, n >= 150 trades on >= 25 distinct days, zero-volume share <= 20 percent. Strategies passing stage 1 are the "dev passes". The frozen list = the dev passes whose development day-clustered z (`calibration.cluster_mean_z` over day buckets of per-trade cash P&L) is >= 3.0, capped at the best 500 by development z (a screen, not the deflation; the count of dev passes and of the frozen list are both reported). The list is written to `workers/GEN12_frozen.json` and committed BEFORE the held-out file is read.
Stage 2, held-out, read once: cash PF >= 1.5, n >= 50 on >= 10 days, zero-volume share <= 20 percent.
Stage 3, deflation: day-clustered z of the pooled development + held-out trades >= the z bar for N (about 5.33), and positive mean in both thirds.
SURVIVOR = stage 1, 2 and 3. Only a survivor gets the overlap read (daily cash P&L correlation with an L1-style stream: gold, bought side's ask 0.88 to 0.97 at 330 to 400 s before close, held, same fee; UTC day buckets; days both trade) and IMPLEMENT, KILL and RETURN rules. Anything weaker is not reported as a finding. Verdict words: FALSIFIED, NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA. Basis named next to every figure (cash, unrounded fee).

## Limits stated in advance
AUD/USD and USD/CAD have 15 days (10 in development), EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY 43 days, so forex-only strategies cannot reach 25 days in development. Candles are 1 minute closes: an exit "at the bid" is the bid at the end of a minute, optimistic about speed and about depth (no size check). Sizes are 1 contract; nothing here says a cheap-side fill is available at size.
