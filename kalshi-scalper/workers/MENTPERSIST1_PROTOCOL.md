# MENTPERSIST1 protocol (card 84, mention persistence; registered Nov 11, 2026, BEFORE any score, outcome join or new candle read for this test). A READY-TO-RUN test; it is NOT run now.
Research only. Nothing live. Source of the idea: BASERATES1 table B4 (workers/BASERATES1_TABLES.md): in Kalshi's own settled mention history the next call of the same word is YES 55.9% after a YES (n 7,257) against 32.4% after a NO (n 9,234), +23.5 points, in-sample, with the caveat already written there: it is mostly word propensity (a fixed topical word), and a market price may already know it. This protocol turns that into a price test and states the cost of running it.
Declined list (docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md 7e, origin/main, 11 rows, Nov 11): none is a mention market; MENTCOMBO1 (mention combos) and MENT1 cheap-side calibration are CLOSED rows in `RND_QUEUE.md`, and this is a different signal (the word's previous-call result, not the cheap-side price) in single markets, so it is not a renamed MENT1. The favourite-and-hold ban (Charter item 5) is respected by the 0.60 ceiling on the side bought. Uniqueness: signal is the word's prior-call outcome, a source no live strategy uses.

## Data and what is already held (counts only; no outcome used to design this)
data/ment1.sqlite: 22,188 settled word markets, 114 series, 1,124 calls, Feb 2025 to Nov 10, 2026 (tables mk, candle, done, ser). 500 markets were chosen for candles by MENT1 (md5 rule) and all 500 are fetched (the `done` table), 496 have candle rows, hourly candles for the 30 hours before the close. Word = ticker suffix, call = ticker minus the word (event ticker), as in BASERATES1; the call time ec = the median close_ts of the call's markets.
Counts computed from tickers and quotes only (no result column read), for budgeting, Nov 11: 16,644 of the 22,188 markets have at least one earlier call of the same series and word (998 calls); 276 of the 500 candle markets have one. At the decision look fixed below (T = close minus 6 hours), 157 of those 276 have a two-sided quote with spread <= 0.05 and 86 of them have a mid between 0.10 and 0.90. (At 1 hour before the close only 47 are in that mid range: the 235-market B5 table is dominated by quotes near 0.01 and 0.99, already resolved; that is why the look is moved to 6 hours. The look was chosen from this availability and the B5b description of price bins, not from any outcome. At T = 12 hours the in-band count was 89, so the look is not sensitive.)
Contamination, disclosed: MENT1 scored outcomes for the 500 held markets (cheap-side calibration), so they are DEVELOPMENT ONLY here. The held-out third is taken only from markets NOT in the 500 (never read for any result), chosen below.

## Rate
Public GET on api.elections.kalshi.com only (candlesticks), inside the shared V2 bucket of 4 requests a minute across POLCOMBO1B, CLINICAL2 and MENTPERSIST1 (one process, 15 seconds apart, sequential; this test runs after the other two). First 429 stops the run, writes the position, no retry loop. Read log `workers/MENTPERSIST1_READLOG.json`. No outside host. No list refresh: markets that closed after Nov 10 are not in the universe (an optional refresh is a dated amendment).

## The persistence-conditional base rate b (computed offline from the held table, no read)
For a target market m (series s, word w, call time ec(m), close c(m)) let PREV be the most recent earlier call of the same (s, w) whose close is at or before c(m) minus 6 hours, with its outcome o in {YES, NO}; a market with no PREV is skipped. Pooled conditional rates come only from PAIRS (earlier call, next call) of consecutive calls of the same (s, w) where the NEXT call closed at or before c(m) minus 6 hours (an expanding window: nothing from the future of m enters b). The pooled cell is (family, o) with family EARN (series containing EARNINGSMENTION or MENTIONEARN) or NONEARN: b_pool(family, o) = YES share of next calls in the cell. A cell needs at least 300 pairs; otherwise b_pool(o) over both families; otherwise the market is skipped. A market also needs at least 3 earlier calls of its word for rule R2.
- R1 (persistence): b = b_pool(family, o).
- R2 (persistence plus the word's own history): b = (k_w + 10 * b_pool(family, o)) / (n_w + 10), k_w YES count and n_w number of earlier calls of the word (closing at or before c(m) minus 6 hours).
Tries counted: 2.

## Decision quote and trade (fixed)
Quote: the last hourly candle ending at or before c(m) minus 6 hours, both bid and ask present, ask minus bid <= 0.05. No quote, no trade (the skip count is reported). YES side ask = ask, NO side ask = 1 minus bid. Trade (one contract, one market, hold to settlement, cash = payout 1 if the side wins minus ask minus fee, fee = ceil(0.07 p (1-p) to the cent), the rounded figure used for the gates; unrounded reported):
- buy YES when 0.10 <= ask <= 0.60 and ask <= b - 0.10;
- buy NO when 0.10 <= 1 - bid <= 0.60 and (1 - bid) <= (1 - b) - 0.10.
CHEAP SIDE (ask under 0.35) and MID SIDE (0.35 to 0.60) are reported as descriptive splits, not extra tries. The 0.10 margin is a margin over b minus the fee at the bands used, fixed now and not tuned.

## Splits and gates
Development = (a) the held 500 markets that qualify, plus (b) the earliest two thirds by call date of the NEW markets read (below). Held-out = the last third by call date of the NEW markets only, READ ONCE, and only for a rule that passed development. Gates, all required on the held-out read and shown first on development: cash PF >= 1.5; total trades >= 100 across development and held-out for that rule AND at least 30 in held-out in at least 15 calls; both halves of development PF >= 1.2; mean cash >= 3c per contract; no single call (event) over 30% of net and no single series over 50% of net; cluster-robust z (cluster = the call) above the deflated bar. Words inside one call share an outcome tendency (BASERATES1 B3), so a call is one cluster for z.
Deflation: shared N about 1,100,000 + 2 tries = about 1,100,002, bar z 5.34 (the small-count bar at N 9,009 is 4.39, reported beside it). The bar applied is 5.34.
Return per dollar: a mention market settles within hours to days of the quote, so lock-up is under 2 days and return per dollar-year is reported but is not the gate.

## Which reads, and how many, to reach n >= 100 trades
New candidates: markets with a PREV and in the series set, NOT among the 500, taken by smallest md5(ticker) after ordering the pool by md5 only (no price or result used), so the sample is spread over the full Feb 2025 to Nov 2026 range and the last third by call date lands in Aug to Nov 2026. Pool size: 16,644 minus 276 = 16,368 markets. One read each: GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks when the market's stored source is hist, else GET /series/{series}/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks, params start_ts = close_ts - 30 hours, end_ts = close_ts, period_interval = 60 (exactly the MENT1 call, so the cache `candle` and `done` tables are reused).
Yield, from the held counts: 86 in the 0.10 to 0.90 mid range out of 276 markets with a PREV = 31% (the 0.10 to 0.60 trading band will be a little lower); the signal must also fire (ask at least 0.10 under b); the fire rate is unknown and is assumed 30% for planning (the planning number only, not a rule). Trades per candle read is therefore about 0.31 x 0.30 = 0.09, so the held 500 give about 15 to 25 development trades and about 80 to 85 further candle reads per 8 to 9 trades.
Plan: read candles in blocks of 200 (50 minutes each). After each block count qualifying trades for each rule (counting trades by the rule, not their outcomes); stop at the first block where either rule has total >= 130 trades (the margin covers the loss of trades to the 30% cap) or at 1,600 reads. Expected: 1,000 to 1,300 new reads (250 to 325 minutes at 4 a minute, about 4 to 5.5 hours) to reach n >= 100; the hard cap 1,600 reads (400 minutes). If the fire rate is 15% the need doubles to about 2,400 reads and the run is cut at 1,600.
No outcome is read during the read blocks: the blocks fetch candles only, the trade counts use the pre-registered quote and b (b needs earlier outcomes from the held history, which is allowed: it is history before the target). Scoring (PF) runs once at the end, development before held-out.

## Verdicts, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA dates, kill
- NOT_ENOUGH_DATA: after 1,600 reads a rule has fewer than 100 trades or fewer than 30 held-out trades. Date it would end: read date plus (100 minus trades) divided by the monthly accrual, planning 790 new settled markets with a PREV a month in this series set (16,644 over 21 months) times the observed trades per read, and not earlier than the date the last third of the held-out accrues (about 30 held-out trades need about 330 new markets read, roughly 14 trade-free days of calendar). The script writes the number; this file commits to the formula. Reading more candles is the only way to reach n sooner, at about 11 minutes per trade.
- FALSIFIED: a rule fails a gate on development (PF under 1.0 with at least 60 trades stops reads for that rule and is final) or on the single held-out read.
- PASS-TO-SHADOW: owner decision only, no live change, shadow recording of the b-and-quote rule; there is no promote verb. A shadow also needs the four uniqueness checks of 7d and the correlation against live strategies under 0.3 (they would be measured at the first shadow read).
- KILL: development PF under 1.0 on 60 or more trades closes the card. RETURN: a new registration with a new base-rate rule (for example call-level, not word-level) or the call-level correlation rule.
Prior: a pass about 5%. Prediction written now: the price already carries the word's propensity (B5b shows quotes track the prior in the middle bins), so R1 and R2 trade near zero edge before the fee and lose to the fee at the 10c to 60c prices; R2 is more likely to find any edge than R1 because R1 is a pooled average over very different words.
