# ALTLAD1 registration: altcoin above/below ladders, CHEAP side only, against an OUTSIDE reference (OKX spot) and a fat-tailed (Student t) nowcast; looks at 30 and at 10 minutes before each close

Worker ALTLAD1, Strategy Research V2, 2026-11-04. Research only, discovery only, no promote verb, no live code. Written and pushed BEFORE any Kalshi candle, any Kalshi result or expiration value was fetched for this study, and before any OKX bar was compared with a Kalshi quote. N +4 (2 rules, the 30 minute look and the 10 minute look; series pooled inside a look). Owner rule (strict) applies: only the CHEAP side (ask 3c to 30c) is ever a candidate; the code has no favourite-side rule and prints no favourite-side result. This is a distance-to-strike nowcast on hourly/daily ladders, NOT a 15 minute candle trigger.

## The rule (exact, no tuning after this commit)
- **Looks:** D = close_time minus L, L = 30 minutes (rule A) and L = 10 minutes (rule B). Scored separately, never pooled.
- **Spot at the look:** S = OKX close of the 1 minute kline that ENDS at D.
- **Volatility:** sigma_min = sqrt(mean of squared 1 minute log returns of OKX closes over the 240 minutes ending at D), zero mean, needs at least 200 valid returns, else no model. Horizon H = L minutes minus 0.5 (centre of the final 60 second averaging window).
- **Tail model:** ln(S_T/S) ~ Student t with df fitted on the **first two thirds of the development dates only**, using OKX alone (no Kalshi quote or outcome): the standardized move z = ln(S_close / S_D) / (sigma_min sqrt(H)), with S_close the OKX close ending at the close, maximum likelihood of the variance-matched t over df in {3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12, 30}, one df for all series and both looks (fitted separately per look, two numbers). Scale = sigma_min sqrt(H) sqrt((df-2)/df), zero drift. P(YES at K) = 1 - F_df( ln(K/S) / scale ). The Gaussian value is printed in the calibration table as information only. Disclosure: the last third of the development dates is not in the df fit but W2 overlaps the fit's second half (OKX only, harmless to outcomes, stated).
- **Strikes considered:** `greater` strikes whose model P(YES) is between 0.03 and 0.97 at that look (OKX-only pre-filter that sets which candles are downloaded; candles cover close minus 2,400 s to close minus 540 s, both looks). A cheap side with model probability above 0.97 is not a candidate.
- **Quote and fill:** the Kalshi 1 minute candle in force at D + 60: latest candle ending at or before D + 60, not older than 900 s, two sided (0 < yes_bid < yes_ask < 1). Candidates: YES at the yes_ask a with 0.03 <= a <= 0.30 and P(YES) >= 3a; NO at b = 1 - yes_bid with 0.03 <= b <= 0.30 and (1 - P(YES)) >= 3b. **Hard limit 0.30.** Taker at that price, held to settlement, no stop. **At most 2 trades per event per look:** the two candidates with the largest model edge (p - price); ties by lower strike. Events are the cluster.
- **Costs:** C = 10, fee 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent per order; also fees x1.2 and +1c slippage.
- **Control (no model, same candles):** every strike-side with a two-sided quote at D+60 and an ask 0.03 to 0.30 among the pre-filtered strikes, no ratio rule, no cap, same hold, clustered by event. **Calibration (information, not a try):** every such strike-side bucketed by ratio p/a (under 1, 1 to 3, at least 3) and by model p: n, mean price, win rate, mean model p.
- Overlap with L1: none by construction (ask 3c to 30c, not 0.88 to 0.97; alts hourly, not 15 minute); the share of qualifying entries priced 0.88 to 0.97 is zero by the rule and is not computed.

## Counterparty and information
Counterparty: the quoters of the wings of hourly/daily alt ladders (hundreds of strikes per event, repriced from a spot feed by an automated maker with slower re-quoting on thin alts) and retail takers who sell the cheap side. Information: an outside spot (OKX) and a realised-volatility estimate with fat tails, which prices a far strike higher than a lognormal would. **Honest alternative (expected):** the quoters already price the wings with a smile wider than any single-vol model (VOLGAP1, IDXFUT1), the favourite-longshot loss makes every cheap bucket win less than its price (COMDAY1: 5 to 8 points under), and a model that is least wrong where the market is most right selects exactly the strikes where the model is mistaken.

## Kill criteria and verdicts
- Pooled treated trades in W1 + W2 (per rule) under 150: **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA**, with trades per day, days to go to 150 (treated trades per UTC day in development, same rate) and the dated forward freeze below. Informational lines still printed.
- **FALSIFIED** if the mean net per trade after fees is not above 0 in BOTH W1 and W2, or PF is under 1.5 in W1 or W2, or the mean at fees x1.2 is not above 0, or treated minus control mean per trade is not positive in both windows.
- Only if all hold AND the event-clustered z of the net per trade is at least 5.27 (N about 730,400): NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, which earns a W3 read once and a forward registration only. Anything weaker is a lead at best.
- `NOT_RUNNABLE` for a series with no settled markets or no OKX history. The windows, df grid, looks, band, ratio, cap of 2 and hard limit 0.30 do not change after this commit; no variant is run (another tail, a different vol window, range ladders, 1 hour look: a new counted try).
