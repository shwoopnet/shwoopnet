Research only. Nothing places an order. No live code, no functions/, no index.html, no L1, no promote verb. Verdict words: FALSIFIED, NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, NOT_RUNNABLE, DUPLICATE. **Owner rule (strict): cheap side only (ask 3c to 30c), taker, held to settlement. No favourite-side result is computed or printed anywhere in this test.** The pooled cheap-side baseline is about -5.5c to -6.2c per contract, so only an outside fact that lifts the win rate by 6c or more per contract matters.

## 3. Question, counterparty, prior
Is the cheap side of a cricket match priced below what a rating from earlier public ball-by-ball results, the playing XI and the toss implies? **Counterparty:** retail, in leagues that no sportsbook model or public rating covers well (county one-day cups, associate internationals, Major Clubs), who anchor on team names and sell the unfancied side. **Honest alternative (expected):** the quoters follow bookmaker lines that include pitch, form and injuries; a results-only Elo that says 3x the ask is mostly the Elo's error, and the win rate equals the price or less. **Prior of a pass 2%; of NOT_ENOUGH_DATA about 95%.**

## 4. Reference (fixed now)
Source: Cricsheet `all_json.zip`, matches with `match_type` T20 or IT20 (pool T20, used for KXT20MATCH) and ODI or ODM (pool 50-over, used for KXODIMATCH), both 2 teams, rated separately by gender. Ratings use ONLY Cricsheet matches whose date is strictly before the Kalshi event's Eastern start date (a double header the same day is not used). All 2005 to date history updates the ratings, matched to Kalshi or not.
Team Elo `R_T`, start 1500, scale 400, `K_T = 250 * kt / (n + 5)^0.4` (n the team's earlier matches in the pool). Player Elo `R_P` keyed by the Cricsheet registry id, start = the player's side's team rating at his first match, `K_P = 20 * kt / (m + 5)^0.4` (m the player's earlier matches). A side's strength `S = (1 - w) * R_T + w * mean(R_P over the XI)`, XI = the `players` list of that match file (the 11 named at the toss; impact substitutes are not in it); a player never seen before counts as the team rating. `p0 = 1 / (1 + 10^((S_B - S_A)/400))`. Toss: `logit p = logit p0 + b * (+1 if A won the toss, -1 if B won it, 0 if no toss recorded)`. **After a match:** team ratings move by `K_T * (result - p0)`, each player by `K_P * (result - p0_of_his_side)`; ties and no results leave ratings unchanged. **Fitted only on Cricsheet matches dated 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31 (before Kalshi's cricket series existed, 2026-02):** `kt` in {0.5, 1, 2} and `w` in {0, 0.5, 1}, choose the minimum log loss on the T20 pool; then `b` = the logistic regression coefficient of the toss sign on the residual logit (no intercept), clamped to [-0.15, 0.15]. The same `kt, w, b` are used for the 50-over pool without refit. Fixed now, never refit on a Kalshi outcome. **Ready:** both teams have at least 5 earlier matches in the pool.

## 5. Universe, look, trade (fixed now)
Kalshi events of the two series with close before the cut, exactly two settled markets, a start time in the ticker, matched to exactly one Cricsheet match (the candidate whose date is nearest the UTC date of the start; ties between two candidates: dropped and counted), both teams ready. One request of 1 minute candles for the event's first market by ticker, from start minus 24 hours to start (historical endpoint if the market closed before 2026-09-07, else live). **Look:** the last candle ending at or before start minus 30 minutes (the toss, about 30 minutes before the start) AND no more than 3 hours before the start, with a valid two sided quote (0 < yes bid < yes ask < 1, closing values); YES ask is the ask of the first market, NO ask = 1 - yes bid. A stale or missing quote: no trade, counted. **Cheap side:** a side with ask in [0.03, 0.30] (if both, the lower ask; if neither, no row). **Treated trade:** model probability of that side >= 3 x its ask (hard limit 0.30); 10 contracts at the ask, taker, held to settlement, fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded UP to the cent per order (stress: fees x1.2); one trade per event. The model probability of the second market is `1 - p` of the first. A quote is not a fill; stated. **Control:** every cheap-side row in the same rated universe, same fee. Event is the cluster.

## 7. Kill rules and halves
Halves D1 and D2: the treated trades split at the median Eastern start. **FALSIFIED** if the treated mean net after fees is not above 0 in BOTH halves or PF is under 1.5 (also checked at fees x1.2). **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** if fewer than 150 treated trades (this is the expected verdict; it becomes a forward project, not a result). **NOT_YET_FALSIFIED** only if mean > 0 in both halves, PF >= 1.5, mean >= 2c, event z >= 5.3 and treated beats control. Even a pass would only permit a forward test on the frozen third, never a trade. If treated is under 150 the table is still printed, labelled information, and the verdict is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA with the date the forward read becomes possible.
