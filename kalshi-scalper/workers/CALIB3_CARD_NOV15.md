## Unit, looks, sides, buckets
- **Unit and event:** one strike market. Its **event** = the ticker up to the strike part (the same hour close); markets of one event share an underlying and an outcome path, so the EVENT, not the market, is the cluster.
- **Looks:** the candle ending EXACTLY close - 1800 s (30 min), close - 600 s (10 min) and close - 120 s (2 min). Missing or not two-sided (0 < bid < ask < 1): no row, counted per look. Nothing carried over.
- **Both sides per look:** YES at its ask, NO at 1 - yes_bid. **Buckets:** the side's ask in 5c buckets, 20 of them, edge to the higher bucket, 1e-9 slack. A ladder strike far from the money sits near 0 or 1 for the whole hour, so a stratum by the strike's rank is NOT added (that needs the underlying's price, which the archive file does not hold); the near-the-money buckets (0.30 to 0.70) are printed apart from the tails.
- **Outcome:** the side wins if the market's result is that side; a result that is not yes or no is excluded and counted.
## Printed per look, per bucket, per third (1, 2, 1+2), for KXBTCD
n; wins; win rate w; mean ask a; gap g = w - a (cents); the mean per-contract fee at the ask (per-order rounded at 1 contract); the median spread; **the number of distinct EVENTS E in the bucket; the event-clustered z of the gap**: for each event e, s_e = the sum over the bucket's rows in that event of (won - ask); z = mean over events of s_e / (sample sd over events of s_e / sqrt(E)), None under 2 events; **thin-third rule: "no evidence" when a third has fewer than 60 events in the bucket**; and the binomial z, labelled NOT independent. Buckets inspected is printed: 1 series x 3 looks x 20 buckets x 2 sides = 120.
## What a candidate needs (fixed now)
A bucket (one look, one side's price range) is a CANDIDATE for a rule card only if ALL hold: (1) at least 300 observations AND at least 60 distinct events in EACH of thirds 1 and 2; (2) in each third the gap g is above the bucket's mean fee + 2c (g >= fee + 0.02, as CALIB1); (3) the same sign in both thirds and the event-clustered z of the gap at or above 2 in each third. The bucket's two neighbours are reported with it (CALIB1 Amendment 1). `expected_chance_candidates_null` is printed (CALIB1 Amendment 2) PER BUCKET AND THIRD FROM THE EVENT COUNT, not the market count: with the bucket's events E_t and its per-event mean ask a_e, sd_t = sqrt(sum over events of a_e (1 - a_e)) / E_t, P(pass third t) = 1 - Phi((fee_t + 0.02) / sd_t), P(both) = the product; summed over the buckets that meet the floors. It still UNDERSTATES the chance count (strikes inside an event are tied far more than assumed), which is why the card adds the event-clustered z and the event count.
## Caveats stated now
- A ladder event holds many strikes (tens), so n inflates quickly while the information does not: the 60-event floor and the event-clustered z are the real tests; a bucket that reaches 300 rows from five events is not evidence.
- Candle ask, not book (no haircut in the table; the 2c in (2) covers spread and slip); the hour structure (opening minutes, the last minutes) differs from the 15 minute markets, so CALIB1's results do not transfer.
- Selection: any rule from this table is selected on thirds 1 and 2; third 3 is its only clean test, and its first read (as in CALIB1 Amendment 2) must be registered before any other card reads it.
