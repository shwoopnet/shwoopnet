**Rule family (grid):** one entry per hourly event, at the 120 seconds before the close look (the last 150 s window holds no earlier look in the engine's 60 s grid), the side that is cheap (the lower priced side), price bands 1 to 12 (5c to 60c, cheap and mid bands only), filter none or spread (round trip), hold to settlement or exit at 60 s before the close; haircuts 1c and 2c, 10 contracts, per-order fee rounded up. 96 rules (the engine counts the cheap side as two sides, yes and no). **No favourite side and no band above 60c** (favourite-and-hold is declined; flagged, not included).
**How it differs from G3H1, CORR1 and LADL1 (from the repo notes; I have not seen their specs):** G3H1's ladder file kept strikes chosen from the YES mid 35 to 25 minutes before the close and entered 51 to 46 minutes before: the 50 minute clock with a universe look-ahead, VOID; CORR1 re-reads it on a file with strikes chosen before entry; LADL1 is queued. Mine enters in the last 150 SECONDS (the recorded window) on strikes the recorder chose from the mid at that moment (the recorder's own near-the-money rule: mid 3c to 97c, nearest 50c first, at most 40 a series), a different clock by a factor of 20, and Ethereum only. **A universe warning I state against myself:** the recorder's strike choice uses the mid in the same minute as the entry, so the universe is "strikes near the money at the look"; that is a selection at the entry time, not after it (no look-ahead), but strikes that were far from the money and then moved are not in the file. It is Ethereum, correlated with the held Bitcoin ladder: no Bitcoin outcome is touched.
**Prediction:** most cells PF under 1.0 at 1c (late hourly strikes near 50c pay the spread, and the mid band is the efficient part of the ladder); I expect no survivor. n per cell is about 60 to 130 entries over 19 days; under 100 reads "no evidence at n = x".
