# PERPLEAD1 card (Desk B, Nov 16, 2026): does the ladder-implied price lead Kalshi's own perp? LANE 2, DRAFT for the Head and Auditor B

**Question.** Kalshi lists hourly "above K" binaries (the ladder) and a perpetual on the same underlying. Near each hourly close the ladder's strike prices give an implied distribution of the price at the close. Does the MEDIAN of that distribution lead the perp's mid over the last minutes before the close, and does the gap (implied median minus perp mid) predict the perp's next move by more than the round trip fee?
**Mechanism (the counterparty).** The perp's mid is set by perp traders and the book; the ladder's strikes are set by binary traders who price the hourly settlement index. A gap means one venue's flow has not reached the other. Who loses: whoever on the lagging venue is quoting a stale price in the last minutes. This is a cross-venue lag test, not a favourite or a hold; the declined list (favourite-and-hold, GOLDHOLD, forecast against the market, NWS against price) does not name it. Closest existing: LADHOUR1 and ETHLATE1 (same ladders, but they trade the BINARY's own cheap side; PERPLEAD1 trades the PERP from the ladder's information), SPXSCALP and ESLEAD1 (index futures leading the ladders; there the lead goes from outside to the ladder, here from the ladder to the perp). Different entry, different instrument, different exit (a time exit on a perp, no settlement).

## Definitions (fixed before any read)
- **Ladder-implied median at a minute:** the one minute candle (the loader's last-row-of-each-clock-minute rule, as ETHLATE1) of each strike of the hourly event gives a YES mid = (bid + ask) / 2 for P(price at the close above K). The median is the K where P = 0.5, linearly interpolated between the two adjacent strikes that bracket 0.5 (one with mid at least 0.5, one below). **A minute with no bracketing pair is no signal** (counted, not filled). The recorder keeps only strikes with a mid from 3c to 97c, so most hours do bracket 0.5.
- **Perp mid:** (bid_close + ask_close) / 2 of the perp's one minute candle ending the same minute (end_period_ts); a missing or one-sided candle is no signal. **Unit check, before any gap is read:** the perp's price field carries a scale (the 2026-11-15 hourly list shows KXETHPERP at 1.766); the scale is the single power of ten (1, 10, ..., 10^4) for which implied median and perp mid agree within 5% on the first ladder day (2026-10-28), one number for the whole run, written in the first print. A day that then disagrees by more than 5% is printed as a count, not scored.
- **Gap G (bps)** = 10,000 x (implied median / perp mid - 1) at the candle ending 120 s before the hourly close.
- **Rule:** if G >= +theta, buy the perp at that minute's perp ASK close and exit at the perp BID close of the exit candle; if G <= -theta, the mirror (sell at the BID, buy back at the ASK). The registered direction is CONVERGENCE (the perp moves toward the implied median). The fade (the opposite direction) is also a registered rule (it is counted in m) so a negative result is not silently flipped.
- **Grid (20 rules per underlying):** theta in {2, 4, 6, 8, 10} bps x exit in {candle ending 60 s before the close, the candle ending at the close} x direction in {convergence, fade}. One entry per hourly event per rule (so events and trades are the same unit here).
- **Costs, two ladders:** taker 12 bps a side (24 bps round trip; the primary), maker 5 bps a side (10 bps round trip, "upper bound, not fill-checked"). The spread is already in the ASK and BID entry and exit. Funding is zero over under three minutes and is not counted (stated, not tested).
- **Latency (Auditor B, Nov 16):** the entry at the ask or bid close of the SAME minute whose candle made the gap is a zero-latency fill; it stays the registered rule and EVERY figure is labelled 'upper bound, zero latency'. A robustness column (information only: not a rule, not in m) prints the same entries filled at the NEXT minute's ask or bid, with the exit moved by the same minute.
- **Per trade net (bps)** = gross move in the trade's direction in bps of the entry price minus the round trip fee. **Profit factor** = sum of winning nets over the sum of losing nets, per rule.
- **Margin report (the Head's ask, as ruled Nov 16), every figure in price terms AND percent of margin:** with maximum leverage Lmax = 1 / 0.045 = 22.2x (assumed, from the 4.5% maintenance margin; Kalshi's own size-specific maxima are not public) the three leverages are 10%, 25% and 50% of Lmax = 2.2x, 5.6x and 11.1x, i.e. initial margin 45%, 18% and 9% of notional; return on margin = net bps / (10,000 x margin fraction) x 100; liquidation distance = margin fraction minus 4.5% (about 40.5%, 13.5% and 4.5% of notional, adverse move at which the position is liquidated). The Head's wording "10%, 25% and 50% of the size-specific max" is read here as the share of the size-specific maximum position used; the size-specific maximum itself and the maintenance margin are Kalshi's table, which is NOT in this repository, so the **liquidation distance** is printed as (fraction minus maintenance margin) x 10,000 bps with maintenance margin an argument that must be filled from Kalshi's own table before the print is read (the card does not guess it).
