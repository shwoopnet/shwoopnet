# FWD-AUDIT: readiness of every dated forward read (research only)

Written 2026-10-30, about 11:15 AM CT. Hygiene only: no result file was read or written, no score, mean, PF or z was computed or is quoted,
N unchanged, nothing live touched. Data on hand was measured with `git fetch origin quote-data perp-data`, `git ls-tree` and `git log` on those
branches (no checkout) plus each read's own `count` command (counts only). GitHub Actions run status was read through the GitHub MCP tools
(read only). Rates use the REAL recent rate, outages included. (One slip: a RECORD1 `look` command was run with a filter that printed its
information dict; it is not used or quoted anywhere here.)

## Verdict

The data feeds are accruing and every dated read has a module that imports and a date constant matching its registration. Three things need
attention: (1) **GAS1-F, ABSORB-F and FLIP1 have no autoscore trigger**, and RECORD1's registered read has none either (autoscore only logs its
count); for the first three that is the owner's instruction of 2026-10-28 (README: "NOT run unless the owner asks"), so it is recorded, not
fixed. (2) **QUOTE2B will not reach 300 usable closes by Nov 12** at the measured rate (about Nov 24 to 25). (3) **MKTTIME1-F interim will
land about Dec 1 to 3, not Nov 24 to 26.** The recorders ran with gaps (quotes and book about 50 to 65 percent uptime, R6 65 percent), so a
slow schedule is the real risk; RECFEED (now wired) makes it visible.

## Flags

1. **QUOTE2B is at risk of being unreadable on Nov 12.** At the measured 11.5 usable hourly closes a day it has about 165 by Nov 12 and reaches 300 about Nov 24 to 25. The trigger is fine; the date is late. Cause: `record-book` is a 4-hour job that starts 0 to 5 hours late, about 50 percent uptime.
2. **MKTTIME1-F interim date** is about Dec 1 to 3, not Nov 24 to 26 (gold's block holds about 3 entries a day, none on weekends). The formal read (15 losses) is later than "about December".
3. **No autoscore trigger:** GAS1-F, ABSORB-F, FLIP1 (owner's Oct 28 instruction, so left alone), and **RECORD1's registered read** (only its count is logged daily). RECORD1 reaches 300 on 10 dates about Nov 4 to 6; whoever owns the daily task must read the count crossing. Not wired here: adding a trigger to RECORD1 would be a new automated read and is the lead's choice.
4. **R6 had a 4.7 h hole** (Oct 30 08:02 to 12:43 UTC): no scheduled run fired. GitHub run history confirms runs 3 (23:54 to 04:02), 4 (05:37 to 08:02) and 5 (12:43 to 16:01) all succeeded; no failure, simply no run. Reads on R6 data (MAKER5, L1Q1, GF1, GF2) still have large margins (about 2,000 windows by Nov 6).
5. **Gold weekend halt** removes about 2 days of MM1 data (Fri 17:00 CT to Sun 18:00 CT): MM1 lands Nov 5 to 6, inside the registered Nov 5 to 10.
6. **The status line was broken before this audit.** `recfeed.py`, as committed, printed "never pushed" for every recorder: its `git log -- <folder>` was relative to `kalshi-scalper/`, not the repo root. Fixed with a `:(top)` pathspec and a test.
7. A dead AUTO loop is covered only for the OI core feed (OIFEED) and `fresh_update`, not for the recorder branches before today; RECFEED now covers those.
