## Data model I assume (please correct): lists = market objects per game {event/game id, ticker, player key, strike, open_time, close_time, occurrence_datetime, result}; candles = per ticker {end_ts: (yes_bid_close, yes_ask_close, volume_fp)} from the batch candlesticks; a candle is usable if 0 < bid < ask < 1; the NO quote is bid_no = 1 - yes_ask, ask_no = 1 - yes_bid.
## Readings (N1 to N13) where the card leaves a choice
N1 Clock: all rungs of a game must share ONE occurrence value O, with minute :00 or :30 and second 0, else the game is dropped and counted. O is the scheduled end.
N2 Decision time D = O - 120, 60 or 30 minutes. The decision candle is the candle ending exactly D; it must exist and be usable, else the rung has no decision (counted "no_decision"); a rung whose close_time <= D is "closed by D" and skipped (counted). The entry is at that candle: yes side pays the YES ask, no side pays 1 - the YES bid.
N3 Bands on the side's ask, half-open [lo, hi) for 30 to 40, 40 to 50, 50 to 60, 60 to 70 cents, compared after rounding to 6 decimals.
N4 Move filter (30 minutes): mv = mid(D) - mid(D - 30 min) of the YES quote, both candles usable, else the rung has no feature (excluded from the move options, counted); down = mv <= -0.05, up = mv >= +0.05, flat = otherwise (6 dp compare).
N5 Distance filter: per player, among that player's rungs usable at D, the line rung = the one whose YES mid is closest to 0.50 (ties: the higher strike); a rung is below if strike < line strike, at if equal, above if greater. Players with no usable rung at D give no feature.
N6 Spread = YES ask - YES bid at D: option '3c or less' means <= 0.03, '6c or less' means <= 0.06 (6 dp compare); both are options of their own (overlapping).
N7 Volume change: V1 = sum of volume over candles ending in (D - 10 min, D], V0 = the same over (D - 20 min, D - 10 min]; a missing minute counts zero; rising = V1 > V0, not rising = V1 <= V0.
N8 Rung age = D - open_time: under 6 hours or 6 hours or more (>= 21600 s).
N9 13 filter options = none + 3 move + 3 distance + 2 spread + 2 volume + 2 age; rules = 3 D x 4 bands x 2 sides x 2 exits x 13 = 624 (an assertion in the build).
N10 One entry per rule per rung; the rule's trades are all rungs of all kept games that satisfy the band and the filter at D. Cash per trade = the engine's per-order form on 10 contracts (engine1_core_second.score_entry: entry cap 0.99, sale floor 0.01, per-order rounded fee, void keeps the entry fee), haircut h = 0.01 (primary) and 0.02.
N11 Exits: H = settlement. M10 = sell at the bid of the candle ending exactly O - 600 (the scheduled end stands in for the close time in the mark); no two-sided candle there, or the rung closed first, = 'no mark': HELD to settlement. Printed both ways: M10 with the held fallback and M10 on rungs with a mark only.
N12 Gate (after the 48 trade filter = 3 a day x 16 days): n >= 100, games >= 40, trade days >= 10 (UTC date of D), PF at h 0.01 >= 1.3 with both halves >= 1.0 (halves = the first floor(n/2) trades by (D, ticker) and the rest), PF at 0.02 >= 1.0, and Benjamini-Hochberg 5% over m (624 + the carried tally argument). Unit = the game: p from the one-sided z of the mean of game-level sums of cash at h 0.01 (z = mean / (sd / sqrt(G)), sd with n - 1).
N13 Dollars a week at 5 contracts = the rule's total cash at h 0.01 scaled from 10 to 5 contracts (cash is linear in n except the per-order rounded fee, so it is recomputed with n = 5), divided by the development days / 7.
