# NDQ15M pull: the Nasdaq-100 15 minute series (KXNDQ15M), development data for an index-15-minute engine family (Testing Desk, Nov 16, 2026; the Head's order of 15:30Z: "write a pull job (lists plus candles, Nov 5 to before Dec 1, development only, at most 470 requests a file); I approve the budget")

Purpose: KXNDQ15M (the Nasdaq-100 15 minute market) is not held anywhere (no candles, no list); it is the second index series of an index-15-minute ENGINE family beside KXINX15M (whose Nov 5 to 13 candles are held from the SPXOI1 jobs, under an allow line Auditor B is drafting). Read-only public data, development only: closes on 2026-11-05 up to the day the job runs and always before the held-out cut 2026-12-01T00:00:00Z (the KXINX15M cut of the registry: SPXFV1 and SPXOI1 HELDOUT_FIRST 2026-12-01). Nothing is read from the pulled files until a reader is allowed (Auditor B's allow line, Main's acceptance); only the list's own count and ticker format are looked at by the Testing Desk to build the candle job.
Step 1, this file: NDQ15M_LISTS.json: one paged read of settled KXNDQ15M markets with closes from 2026-11-05T00:00Z to 2026-11-16T00:00Z (the market list only, no candle, no price field is used; the answer carries tickers, close times, results and last prices, which stay in the pull branch). At most 3 requests. Step 2 (after the list lands, with the real tickers, because the Nov 13 INX job showed the suffix of a ticker cannot be guessed from the close time): NDQ15M_CANDLES.json, one minute candles (period_interval 1) of each listed market from 30 minutes before its close to 120 seconds before its close, every price field and open interest, built from the list, in batches that keep the file under 470 requests (the Head's cap); a thin window (about 9 trading days to Nov 14, a few more to Nov 16), so the expected result is "no evidence at n = x" and the family is a forward card that keeps reading until the Dec 1 cut (later list and candle jobs add days, each its own job file under the same cap).
The Testing Desk reads no KXNDQ15M datum until the allow line exists. The job file names owner MAIN because the runner's owner set (V2, REFINEMENT, DESK_A, DESK_B, MAIN) has no Testing Desk entry; the registration is this file. Tries: counted by the family spec that reads the data (each family a try per market, and the SPXSCALP and ESLEAD1 reads of KXINX15M prices on Nov 5 to 13 are disclosed: they used different rules, ES leads and scalps).
