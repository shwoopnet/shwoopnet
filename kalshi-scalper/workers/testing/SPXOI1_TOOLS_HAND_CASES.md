## Tool 1: the SPXOI1 shadow
- Exchange time of a poll (sheet section 0, lane (u)): offset = median over the 120 most recent rows with a readable Date header, in the run's order after a STABLE sort by receive time, up to and including the poll's own row, of (header seconds + 0.5 - t_recv_ms / 1000); exchange time = t_recv_ms / 1000 + offset. A row with an unreadable header takes the median of the earlier readable ones and adds none; a row with no readable receive time is dropped; a row with no offset at all has no exchange time (no signal). The close time is the row's close_time column read as exchange time.
- Poll shape out: {t (exchange seconds), yes_bids, no_bids, close, recv} as scorekit.r6_book plus close and recv; levels (price, size) best first, empty cells skipped.
- Rule (Card 2, the Head): oi_rise = open interest at the candle ending close - 180 s minus the one ending close - 360 s (both required). Fires only if oi_rise >= T (1e-9). Signal poll = the FIRST poll with exchange time in [close - 180, close - 120] (1e-9) at which the cheaper side's ask (lower ask, tie to YES; YES ask = 1 - best NO bid, NO ask = 1 - best YES bid) is in [0.08, 0.50]. Limit = ask + limit_add (the Head says "limit at the ask": limit_add = 0.0; Desk A's addendum says ask + 0.01 as OICORE's span: I flag the difference; limit_add is a required argument, no default). One signal per market (sheet (s)).
- Shadow dict values for liveshadow.compare: ("trade", side, ask, {"limit", "t"}) | ("no_trade", reason) | ("no_data", reason); reasons: "oi rise under T" (no_trade), "cheaper side outside 0.08 to 0.50" (no_trade), "no poll in window" (no_data), "no open interest" (no_data).
- Fill check (NC15, sheet section 0): second poll = the NEXT poll of the same ticker after the signal poll, at most 6 s after it (1e-9); later or none: NOT FILL-CHECKED (not counted). Both polls must offer 10 contracts at or under the limit (sizes of levels with ask <= limit + 1e-9 sum to 10 or more) else MISS (counted, not scored). Walked price = size-weighted average ask of the first 10 contracts of the ladder sorted by ask; fill price = the HIGHER of the two walked prices.
- Fee and cash: fee = ceil(0.07 x 10 x p x (1 - p) x 100 - 1e-9) / 100 with p the fill price; cash = 10 x (win - p) - fee; a void is -fee; cents = floor(100 x cash + 1e-6) (lane (g)).
- Hand numbers: fill p = .20: fee ceil(11.2 - 1e-9) = 12c; win = 10 x .8 - .12 = $7.88 = +788c; loss = -2.00 - .12 = -212c; void = -12c. (g) example: a win of $6.754 is 675c; a loss of $6.754 is -676c.
- Invented book cases (test): a quiet book with NO bids [(.80, 6), (.79, 6)] gives YES ask levels .20 (6) and .21 (6): the first 10 walk = (6 x .20 + 4 x .21) / 10 = .204; a limit .20 offers only 6 at or under: MISS; limit .21 offers 12: ok. oi_rise exactly T passes; T - 1 does not.

## Tool 2: the lane block reader (parameters default to the registered values; tests lower them to trace by hand)
- Counted signal: decision time (exchange) at or after the start time (l); fill-checked; fillable (not a miss); not skipped under (i) (a limit of 88c or above is skipped unless the rule is registered in the favourites class). Skips, misses and not-fill-checked are counted by reason and are NOT in any block, do not break "in a row", and do not enter the count, the days, the 21 days or the money (m).
- Order (p): decision time, then ticker ascending, then side (yes before no).
- Class (i): from the registered band as limits in whole cents: all at or under 50c: the rarer outcome is WINS (10 needed); wholly above 50c: LOSSES; crossing 50c: both. 88c or above: favourites class only (block at least 150 signals, losses 10).
- Block (a): grows in order along the unbroken run of signals with FINAL results (r); it ends at the first signal at which all hold together: n >= 60 (150 for favourites), at least 7 different US Central days (d), the rarer outcome count >= 10 (c, a void is in n and its fee in L but is neither a win nor a loss for that count, n). Complete only if that signal's decision time is at most the first signal's time + 21 x 24 h (b, q). EXPIRED (k, q): by the clock, once now passes first + 21 x 24 h with no completion AND every counted signal up to that time has a final result; otherwise "waiting for results, expiry not yet decided". 
- Read (g, e): gross wins W and gross losses L in integer cents (signals' cents; voids in L); PASS the block if 10 W >= 13 L AND W >= L in each half (first floor(n/2) signals, then the rest); L = 0 and W > 0 passes; W = 0 fails. No day-concentration test (h removed). A failed block: the next block starts at the first counted signal after its last (no rolling re-read). An expired block: the next starts at the first counted signal whose decision time is after the expiry time (t).
- Confirmation (j): the next 30 counted signals after the cleared block's last signal, all final, completed within 21 x 24 h of that last signal's time, else EXPIRED; passes if W >= L (L = 0 and W > 0 passes, W = 0 fails). Then QUALIFIED (and no further block is read, t). A failed or expired confirmation: its signals never count toward any block; the next block starts at the first counted signal after it (after the expiry time if expired).
- Day (d): the US Central calendar day (America/Chicago) of the signal poll's exchange time.
