- **Blocks (changed from the card by Desk B before data):** base closes before 2026-10-27T00:00Z; price block 2026-10-27 to before 2026-11-13T00:00Z; held-out from 2026-11-13T00:00Z, never listed or requested. The price block carries NO result field (asserted in the test).
- **Instant:** start = the Tie market's `occurrence_datetime` minus 180 minutes; guard: the first-period market (`first_period_series`) closed 20 to 120 minutes after the start, else "start not derivable" (counted); T = start minus 30; price = the candle ending at T, stepping back a minute at a time to T minus 30 for the first two-sided minute, else "not two-sided"; nothing filled in.
- **Cells (6):** NHL_P1, P2, P3, NHL_REG (one market a game, ticker ends `-1`, YES = level after regulation), NFL_Q (base rate per quarter series), SOC_1H (per series). Events with fewer than three settled markets (one for NHL_REG) are dropped and counted. A series that returns no market is counted as "no such series".
- **Reported per cell, counts first:** base events, base rate and standard error per key, price-block games with a derivable start, two-sided games and share, median spread, quartiles of (base rate minus Tie ask), share of two-sided games where the ask is 10c or more under the base rate and the spread is 4c or less. Minimums 150 base events and 40 two-sided games else "no evidence at n = x". Verdict exactly as registered: closed "efficient, move on" with the best cell's median gap unless a cell has a median gap of 8c or more with median spread 4c or less; closed "nothing to buy" if under half are two-sided in every cell. Every output carries "candle freshness measured on Bitcoin 15 minute only, not on sports".
- **Assumption to confirm on the first lists answer:** a game is the suffix after the series prefix of the event ticker, the same in the Tie series and the first-period series (needed for the guard). If it is not, the guard cannot run and games drop as "start not derivable".
