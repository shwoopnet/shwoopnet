# S3 combo house side, build 2: hand case (Testing Desk, Nov 15, 2026). Written BEFORE any code.
Source: S3_COMBO_HOUSE_SIDE_REGISTRATION.md on claude/idea-desk-b, last change 8f10fc49, file sha256 700ca935e8286075 (the registration, the clarifying lines of Nov 13 and Nov 15, and the dated answers of Nov 15 15:00Z) ONLY. Not Desk B's scorer. Paper only: reads two kinds of gzip CSV, sends nothing, writes nothing. INVENTED data in every test.

## What I build
`s3_second.py <mode> <asof ISO> <root>`; modes `dev`, `held`, `monitor`. `<root>` holds `combo/DATE/HHMM/quotes.csv.gz` and `results.csv.gz`. Files are read in sorted path order. Aggregates only; no per-fill line, no ticker is printed. `dev` and `held` refuse an as-of before 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z and 2026-12-12T00:00:00Z. The clock is never read.
Windows by receive instant `t_recv_ms` (epoch ms), start inclusive, end exclusive: dev 2026-11-13T00:00:00Z to 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z; held 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z to 2026-12-10T00:00:00Z (entries to Dec 9). MY READING: the walk that decides blocking ("one per ticker and per event, ever") starts at the dev start in BOTH modes, so a held read is blocked by dev fills; only fills whose receive instant is in the read's window are scored.

## Entry (per snapshot = one quotes file; columns by NAME)
A row qualifies if: `no_ask`, `yes_bid_size`, `volume`, `legs`, `close_time` readable (any unreadable or missing: out); `no_ask` below 1.0; price paid p = no_ask + 0.01 in [0.60 - 1e-9, 0.90 + 1e-9]; `yes_bid_size` at least 3; `volume` above 0; `legs` an integer 2 to 30 inclusive; close_time (ISO, Z) minus receive instant between 3600 s and 1,209,600 s, both inclusive, NO slack (compared in whole milliseconds); `t_recv_ms` inside the window. Candidates sorted by ticker (plain string order; equal tickers keep file order, the first row is used). Walk them: skip a ticker already filled (any earlier snapshot or earlier in this one); skip a combo whose non-empty `event_ticker` already has a fill (any earlier snapshot or earlier in this one); stop after 10 fills in the snapshot. A combo cut only by the cap may enter at a later snapshot. Fills are ordered by (receive time, ticker) = entry order.
## Result, cost, net
cost = p + 0.07 p (1 - p), unrounded; net per contract = 1 - cost if the result is `no`, -cost if `yes`. Results: the first row of a ticker in sorted file order wins (any later row is ignored, a conflict does not stop); value read as `yes` or `no` with case and spaces ignored; any other value is "other result" (counted, not settled). A fill is DUE when close_time + 48 h is at or before the as-of; settled = due and yes or no; due with no row anywhere = unresolved. Paper net at $2 per fill = net x 2 / cost.
## Kill and bar
KILL 1: running sum of paper net$ over settled fills in entry order; killed as of the first settled fill where it is below -40. KILL 2: needs 100 settled; the first 100 settled in entry order, PF = sum of positive nets over the absolute sum of negative nets (infinite if no loss); killed if PF is under 1.2 (strict). BAR (over ALL settled fills of the window, at least 100): sum net / sum cost at least 0.10 with no slack; first floor(n/2) fills by entry order and the rest each with sum net / sum cost above 0; at least 10 distinct close dates (UTC date of close_time); the largest date's net / total net at most 0.30 with total net above 0 (MY READING: net = net per contract); bootstrap: `random.Random(20261112)`, close dates sorted ascending are the clusters, one draw = `rng.choice(dates)` once per date, fills of the chosen dates concatenated (a date twice counts twice), net per dollar = sum net / sum cost on them, 2000 draws, sorted ascending, the value at index 100 above 0.
## Labels (decision list)
Unresolved share = unresolved / due; over 10 percent is L1. (a) n settled under 100: "no evidence at n = x". (b) else L1: "NOT_ENOUGH_DATA" and every later line is a description (kill lines print "would read KILLED (description only)", the closing line "NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, description only"). (c) else kill 2 fired: "KILLED (cash profit factor under 1.2 on the first 100)". (d) else the bar: all five conditions hold: "paper edge shown (unproven)" in dev (held: "bar met on held-out"), otherwise "bar not met". KILL 1 is a separate line printed FIRST whenever it fires (even under 100): "KILL 1: KILLED as of settled fill k" (or the description form under L1); the labels above still print after it. MY READING, to be confirmed: kill 1 firing does not remove the label of the first failing step among (a) to (d).
## No-weather line and series counts
Leg series = the text before the first hyphen of each leg ticker in `leg_tickers` (one cell, `TICKER:side` joined by `|`, may be empty or end in `|...`: only well-formed entries are legs). A fill is "weather" if ANY leg series starts with KXHIGH, KXLOW, KXTEMP, KXRAIN, KXSNOW, KXHURR, KXTORN or KXWIND. A combo's legs come from any snapshot that stored them (the first non-empty); a combo with no stored legs ever is "leg unknown": in the all-combos line only. Every figure is printed twice: all fills, and fills with legs known and no weather leg (that line says "would read" in place of a verdict, with the tag "information only; the registered verdict is the all-combos line"). Also printed: the count of every leg series seen over the paper fills (each leg of each fill once).
## MONITOR
Development window only: paper fills, due, settled, unresolved, wins among the settled, cash PF so far and net per dollar so far, headed "MONITOR, not the registered verdict; the Nov 26 read is the verdict". No label, no kill line, no bootstrap.

## Hand cases (whole numbers worked by hand; the tests use invented gz files)
1. Price: no_ask 0.59 pays 0.60: in; 0.5899 (p 0.5999): out; 0.89 (p 0.90): in; 0.8901 (p 0.9001): out; 0.9 pays 0.91 out; no_ask 1.0 and a blank no_ask: out.
2. Other gates: yes_bid_size 2 out, 3 in; volume 0 out; legs 1 out, 2 in, 30 in, 31 out; close 3599 s after receive out, 3600 in, 1,209,600 in, 1,209,601 out; unreadable close_time out; a receive instant 1 ms before the window start out, at the start in, at the end out.
3. Cap and blocking: 12 qualifying combos with distinct events in one snapshot: the 10 lowest tickers fill; the other two, still qualifying in the next snapshot, fill there (cut only by the cap). Two tickers of one event in one snapshot: only the lower fills; in the next snapshot the other is blocked; a ticker filled once never fills again; an empty event ticker never blocks.
4. Cost and net: p = 0.80: cost 0.8112, a `no` result nets +0.1888, a `yes` result nets -0.8112; paper net at $2 is 2 x 0.1888 / 0.8112 = 0.46548... for the win and -2 for the loss (net/cost = -1).
5. Due and unresolved: close 2026-11-14T00:00:00Z, due at 2026-11-16T00:00:00Z: as-of one second earlier: not due; at that instant: due. A due fill with no results row: unresolved; with a row `maybe`: other result (counted, not settled, not unresolved).
6. Results precedence: the same ticker in two files with `yes` first and `no` later: `yes` wins (the first in sorted path order); the second row is ignored without a stop.
7. Kill 1: nets -2, -2, ... (each loss at $2 is -2): 21 losses give -42 as of settled fill 21 (the sum at 20 is -40, not below -40; killed at 21).
8. Kill 2: first 100 settled with 40 wins of 0.1888 per contract at p = 0.80 and 60 losses: PF = 40 x 0.1888 / (60 x 0.8112) = 7.552 / 48.672 = 0.15516; under 1.2: killed. With 100 wins and no loss PF is infinite: not killed.
9. Bar arithmetic: n = 100 fills at p 0.80, 90 wins, 10 losses: net = 90 x 0.1888 - 10 x 0.8112 = 16.992 - 8.112 = 8.880; cost = 100 x 0.8112 = 81.12; net per dollar 0.109467: above 0.10. Same with 89 wins: net = 16.8032 - 8.9232 = 7.88; /81.12 = 0.09714: below.
10. Labels: n = 99: "no evidence at n = 99"; n = 100 with 20 due-unresolved of 150 due (13.3 percent): NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, kill lines in description form; the same at 15 of 150 (10.0 percent exactly) is NOT over 10 percent.
11. Bootstrap: with the seed fixed, the same input prints the same 5th percentile twice; changing the order of the file rows does not change it (clusters are sorted dates); the independent test recomputes it with `random.Random(20261112)` in the test itself.
12. Weather: legs `KXHIGHNY-26NOV15-T70:yes|KXNBAGAME-X-Y:no` is weather; `KXNBAGAME-A-B:yes|KXMLBGAME-C-D:no` is not; an empty `leg_tickers` in every snapshot: leg unknown (all-combos line only); a combo whose legs appear in a later snapshot takes them from there. The series counts print `KXHIGHNY 1, KXNBAGAME 2, ...` over the fills.
13. Window and refusals: `dev` with an as-of before 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z refuses; `held` before 2026-12-12T00:00:00Z refuses; `monitor` has no such rule and prints only the monitor lines.
14. Printed output contains no ticker string and no per-fill line (the test greps for the invented tickers).

## ADDENDUM 1 Nov 15 (Desk B's dated line, registration commit 8b989a5c, file sha256 8929386db794f213): incomplete leg lists. Written BEFORE the code change.
A `leg_tickers` cell ending in `...` (after trimming) is an INCOMPLETE leg list (the recorder cuts at 600 characters at a whole leg and ends the cell with `|...`). `...` is never a leg series. A combo is "legs incomplete" if no snapshot stored a complete list for it and some snapshot stored an incomplete one. A complete list from ANY snapshot replaces an incomplete one (the first complete list in sorted file order is used); with no complete list the first incomplete list is used. A combo with an incomplete list is in the ALL-COMBOS line only and never in the NO-WEATHER line; a weather leg shown in a cut list still makes it a weather combo (so it is excluded from the no-weather line either way). The legs line prints a "legs incomplete" count (the fills whose legs stay incomplete), beside the "no stored legs" count. The leg series counts include the legs shown in an incomplete list.
Hand cases (invented): (a) cell `KXNBAGAME-A-B:yes|KXMLBGAME-C-D:no|...` alone: incomplete, legs [KXNBAGAME-A-B, KXMLBGAME-C-D], not weather: in the all-combos line, NOT in the no-weather line, counted as legs incomplete 1. (b) the same ticker in a later snapshot with the complete cell `KXNBAGAME-A-B:yes|KXMLBGAME-C-D:no|KXNFLGAME-E-F:yes`: complete replaces incomplete: three legs, not weather, in the no-weather line, legs incomplete 0. (c) `KXHIGHNY-X:yes|...` alone: weather combo (incomplete or not), never in the no-weather line. (d) a complete list in the FIRST snapshot and an incomplete one later: the complete one is kept. (e) `|...` alone or an empty cell: no legs stored (leg unknown), not "incomplete".
