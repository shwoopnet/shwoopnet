## What the code enforces (Head's and the registration's conditions)

- **State only from the sibling market's own close time:** map 1's `close_time` is when map 1 ended; the only result ever used is the MAP-1 market's (who is one map up). A match record holds no match result and no map 2 or 3 result (asserted; and a test flips every other result and gets an identical output).
- **Sample:** the 1,500 most recently closed best-of-three matches (a total-maps market at 2.5 and none higher, map markets 1 and 2 present) with match closes from 2026-09-12 to before 2026-10-23T00:00Z, all titles together, never extended. Held-out (from 2026-10-23T00:00Z) never listed or requested.
- **Start:** the key's date and four-digit time read as America/New_York (daylight time handled); guard map 1 ended 15 to 150 minutes after S, else "start not derivable"; guard map 2 closes at least 15 minutes after map 1, else dropped; each counted.
- **Prices:** minute-end candles only, two-sided only. Pre-match: candle ending at S minus 10, step back to S minus 40. Look: the candle ending at the first whole minute at or after map-1 close + 120 s, step forward to close + 5 minutes until both match contracts are two-sided. Nothing filled in.
- **Interpretation I made (Desk B can overrule before data):** the "needed contracts" at the pre-match instant are the match contract of the team that won map 1 plus BOTH map-1 contracts, all two-sided in the same minute, so the placebo and the model use the same minute.
- **Model and placebo:** p from the pre-match mid of the map-1 winner's match contract, q by bisection from p = q x q x (3 - 2q), V = 1 - (1 - q)^2; gap = value minus that team's own ask at the look; placebo at the pre-match instant: q against the ask of that team's map-1 contract and 1 - q against the other's; cheaper side = larger gap.
- **Reported per title and pooled, counts first:** sampled, filled pre-match, filled at the look, median minutes from map-1 close to the look, median spread and round trip (spread plus two fees of 0.07 x mid x (1 - mid)) of the match contracts at the look, gap median, quartiles, 90th percentile, share at 8c or more with spread 4c or less split by team up and team down, and the same for the placebo. Line on every output: "candle freshness measured on Bitcoin 15 minute only, not on esports".
- **Kill per title (as registered):** under 150 filled at the look "no evidence at n = x"; closed on cost if the median round trip is above 5c; "efficient, move on" with the 90th percentile gap unless the share is at least 10 percent AND at least twice the placebo's; the card is closed if every title is closed or has no evidence.
