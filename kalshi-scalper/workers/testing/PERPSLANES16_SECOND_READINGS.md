R1 Signal candle e = entry candle: the entry fills at e's own quotes (ask x (1 + h) long, bid x (1 - h) short), as POOL1 trend rules; exit checks start at e + 60 s; time stop at e + 60 T.
R2 "mid at e - 60 L minutes" read as e - L minutes (L in 30, 60): R_i = ln(mid_e / mid_(e - 60 L seconds)).
R3 RS_i = R_i minus the mean of R_j over the OTHER kept names of the basket; every other kept name needs a usable candle (0 < bid < ask) at e and at e - L min, else no signal. Basket = kept names only (a name needs at least 5 trading days and 2 prints as in POOL1's pool filter); a basket with under 3 kept names is dropped and printed.
R4 u_i = POOL1 sigma at e (the 60 one-minute log mid returns ending at e, at least 45 valid, POPULATION sd) x sqrt(30); signal when |RS| > c u strictly; no sigma = no signal (counted no_vol).
R5 FOLLOW side = sign of RS (long if RS > 0), REVERT the opposite; RS = 0 no signal.
R6 Signal window: candle ends 10:31 to 15:30 ET inclusive, on session days only, DST-aware ET clocks; the exit may run past 15:30 (no session cap beyond the data: no candle at the time-stop end = no_mark, as POOL1).
R7 Every rule (signal x exit) is run on its own, one position per name, POOL1 cooldown: a signal with e < the name's last exit end is blocked; sorted by (e, name).
R8 Pairs: unordered pair (a, b) with a < b by ticker, s_t = ln(mid_a) - ln(mid_b) on candles where both are usable; baseline = the candle ends e - 60 W, ... , e - 60 (W wall-clock minutes before e, e itself excluded), at least 0.8 W of the W points present, population sd of s over them, sd > 0; z = (s_e - mean) / sd; fire when |z| >= z_in. "Across the previous day": the baseline is wall-clock minutes, so at 10:31 a W = 240 baseline reaches back to 06:31 the same day; if the perps trade overnight those candles count, if not the 0.8 W rule blocks the signal. (Tell me if you mean the last W available points instead.)
R9 z > 0 (a rich): short a, long b; z < 0: long a, short b. Both legs fill at e: long leg ask x (1 + h), short leg bid x (1 - h).
R10 u_s = POPULATION sd of one-minute spread changes (s_t - s_(t-1)) over the 60 candle ends e - 3540 ... e (both usable on both ends, at least 45 valid) x sqrt(30). Targets and stops are checked on the mid spread s_t on the candles after e: for a short-a/long-b trade target if s_t <= s_e - kt u_s, stop if s_t >= s_e + ks u_s (mirror for the other direction), stop and target on the same candle: stop first; time exit at e + 60 T on the candle ending then, both legs at the quote on that candle (long leg bid x (1 - h), short leg ask x (1 + h)); the same candle exit prices on target and stop candles. Both legs must have a usable candle on every checked candle (a candle with either leg unusable is skipped).
R11 Pair net: gross = g_long + g_short (each on its own leg notional 1), cost = 4 x 12 bp, funding = booked sum of both legs, then the REPORTED net = (g_long + g_short - 0.0048 - f_a - f_b) / 2, i.e. per unit of combined notional (24 bp of cost). PF, halves, shares and bps all use this reported net. A pair trade is booked only if BOTH legs' funding is booked (else counted funding_not_booked and out of the gate as in POOL1).
R12 One position per name also for pairs: a pair signal is skipped (counted blocked) if EITHER leg's name has a position at e (last exit end > e); a taken pair occupies both names to its exit end.
R13 Maker entry (single name): limit = signal candle e's own-side touch (long: bid close of e; short: ask close of e); fills at the first candle f in e + 60 ... e + 60 N whose ask close <= limit - tick (long) or bid close >= limit + tick (short), usable candle required, fill price = limit, fee 5 bp, NO haircut on the entry; the position then starts at f: entry end = f for funding, u from the signal candle e, target and stop distances from the fill price (limit), time stop at f + 60 T, checks from f + 60; exit taker with h and fee 12 bp (ME). Unfilled = skipped and counted, with the TT trade of the same signal printed in the unfilled mean. Cooldown per A1.4: a signal is skipped when the name's last exit end > e; an unfilled or waiting order occupies nothing.
R14 MM (single name only): same fill, target resting at fill x (1 + kt u) (long; short fill x (1 - kt u)), filled when a later candle's bid close >= target + tick (short: ask close <= target - tick), exit price = the target, fee 5 bp, no haircut; the stop (bid <= fill x (1 - ks u), short ask >= fill x (1 + ks u)) is checked first on each candle and exits taker at that candle's bid or ask with h; there is NO taker target exit in MM; the time stop is taker with h.
R15 Pairs ME2 and ME5: both legs rest at their own touch at e (long leg at its bid close, short leg at its ask close) each with its own tick; each leg fills on its own through-by-1-tick rule in candles e + 60 ... e + 60 N; BOTH must fill within N or the pair is skipped (counted, with its TT net printed); the position starts at the LATER fill candle f; entry prices are the limits (no haircut, 5 bp per leg), u_s from the signal candle, s_f (mid spread at the candle f) is the reference for target and stop in R10 (instead of s_e), time stop at f + 60 T, exits taker with h and 12 bp per leg; funding booked from f.
R16 Tick per name = the smallest positive difference between distinct values in the sorted set of ALL bid closes and ask closes of that name in the loaded window (census tick used if Desk A supplies one: please send the table or say none); printed.
R17 TT is the gate; the pass flag MAKER_ONLY = the rule meets the gate in a maker column and not in TT; MAKER_ONLY rules are not in the pass list or the BH set.
R18 Gate = POOL1's: TT trades booked, trades per trading day >= 3 (trading_days POOL1's definition), PF >= 1.3 at h 0.0002 and PF >= 1.0 at h 0.0005, each also in both halves (floor(n/2) by (e, name)), no ET day above 30 percent of net, net at h 0.0005 > 0; the PF of h 0.0005 halves 1.0. R20 Margin at x = 10, 25, 50 percent of the name's page max: L = x x pagemax; percent of margin = 100 x net per unit notional x L (pair: 100 x the sum over legs of the leg's net per leg notional x its L, where the leg net is its gross minus its own fees minus its own funding); liquidation distance = 1 / L - maintenance (0.0447 gold, 0.045 others), worst (smallest) and mean over the rule's trades (pair: the smaller of its two legs' distances); a name with 1 / L at or below maintenance is listed as no buffer; a name with no page max is REFUSED (run stops).
