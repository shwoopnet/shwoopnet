## The change (and nothing else)
- fit(): the coarse-to-fine grid stays as the starting point; then a Levenberg-Marquardt / Gauss-Newton polish on the same objective (the sum of squared differences of the three winner legs and every total strike, winner mids not renormalised, rates kept at or above 0.02), numeric central-difference Jacobian, until the step is under 1e-13 or 80 iterations; it never accepts a step that raises the sum of squares. The returned rmse is sqrt(SSE / (3 + number of totals)) as before.
- Printed figures unrounded: gap quartiles and p90, medians of spread, round trip and one-way cost, shares, the residual median: raw floats (no round()). The registered fee rounding (cents) is NOT touched.

## ADDENDUM 3 Nov 14 (the Head's 06:24Z message: the History Miner's full per-game file spec; supersedes addendum 2 item 4's layout). Written BEFORE the code change.
The ONE real run writes `socderiv_pergame.csv` (CSV, header row, unrounded floats at full repr precision): one row per game per derivative leg, columns in this order: game_key, fit_iters, cap_hit, rate_home, rate_away, sse, leg_ticker, leg_type, strike, model_value, market_mid, gap, fires. Sorted by game_key then leg_ticker (a tie on both: league, then leg_type). No result field of any kind. Development windows only.
My readings (the Miner's text decides if it differs): game_key = the game key after the series prefix; rate_home = the fitted rate of team1 (the lower team code, l1), rate_away = team2 (l2); fit_iters, cap_hit and sse are those of the fit used at that leg's instant (sse is the unrounded sum of squares over the winner mids and the total strikes; rmse is dropped); leg_type is `btts` or `teamtotal`; strike is the team-total strike (1.5 and so on) and empty for btts; model_value is the leg's model value; market_mid is (bid + ask) / 2 of that leg; gap and fires as before (fires is `True` or `False`). The placebo rows are NOT in the file (the spec lists legs; the placebo is a refit with a strike left out, not a leg). Floats are written with repr; an empty strike is an empty field.
