## The change (and nothing else)
- fit(): the coarse-to-fine grid stays as the starting point; then a Levenberg-Marquardt / Gauss-Newton polish on the same objective (the sum of squared differences of the three winner legs and every total strike, winner mids not renormalised, rates kept at or above 0.02), numeric central-difference Jacobian, until the step is under 1e-13 or 80 iterations; it never accepts a step that raises the sum of squares. The returned rmse is sqrt(SSE / (3 + number of totals)) as before.
- Printed figures unrounded: gap quartiles and p90, medians of spread, round trip and one-way cost, shares, the residual median: raw floats (no round()). The registered fee rounding (cents) is NOT touched.
