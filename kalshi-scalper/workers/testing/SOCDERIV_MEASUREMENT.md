# SOCDERIV measurement: reader, job builders and scorer (Testing Desk build note, rewritten Nov 12, 2026 to Desk B's registration)

Authority: `kalshi-scalper/workers/socderiv/SOCDERIV_REGISTRATION.md` and `SOCDERIV_SERIES.json` at commit 9b37bd6 on `claude/idea-desk-b` (the registration wins over the card); the leagues file is copied byte for byte to `socderiv_series_frozen.json` and hash-checked in `socderiv.py` (`01b7febc...`). **Built and tested on synthetic data only: no Kalshi request, no data opened, no job sent to Main, no try, NO RESULT READ AT ALL.** Runs SECOND, after TIE3.

- **Jobs:** `kalshi_pull_jobs/SOCDERIV_LISTS.json`: **67 requests** (16 leagues x 4 series = 64 live lists, 3 fee-field reads), about 80 to 131 pages, job cap 100, passes `--dry-run`. `build-candles`: every leg of the sampled games (three winner markets, every total strike, both-teams-to-score, readable team totals; about 16 contracts a game), the 61 minutes ending at kickoff minus 30, packed by kickoff time (at most a 3 hour span, 100 tickers, 9,000 minutes x tickers). **My count is above Desk B's 160**: because one request has one time window, games that kick off on different days cannot share a request, so I expect about 200 to 300 for up to 960 games, in two runs. Exact once the lists are reported; Desk B can lower it only by shrinking the sample.
- **Sample:** the 60 most recently settled games of each league with closes from 2026-09-12 to before 2026-11-13T00:00Z, never extended. Held-out never listed or requested.
- **Kickoff:** the GAME (winner) market's `occurrence_datetime` minus 180 minutes (sibling series carry different times, so only the GAME market is used); guard: it closed 100 to 135 minutes after the kickoff, else "kickoff not derivable".
- **Instant:** from kickoff minus 30 step back a minute at a time to kickoff minus 90; the FIRST minute at which the three winner markets, at least three total-goals strikes and the target contract are all two-sided at the candle close; every leg read at THAT minute; otherwise "not filled" and counted.
- **Model:** independent Poisson rates fitted by least squares to the winner mids and the two-sided total-strike mids; both-teams-to-score and team-total values follow; team 1 is the lower team code of the winner tickers; a team-total market is used only where `floor_strike` is given and its team is readable from the ticker's last segment, else counted unreadable.
- **Placebo:** refit with the total strike nearest 50c left out, report the same gap statistics for that strike.
- **Reported per group (A big leagues, B smaller leagues and cups) and target type (both-teams-to-score, team total), counts first:** games, kickoff derivable, filled and share, median spread and round trip (spread plus two fees of 0.07 x mid x (1 - mid)), gap quartiles and 90th percentile, share at 8c or more with spread 4c or less, the same four numbers for the placebo, and the fit residual. Verdicts exactly as registered: under 150 filled "no evidence at n = x"; closed on cost if the median round trip is above 5c; "not tradable" if under half of the derivable-kickoff games are filled; "efficient, move on" with the 90th percentile gap unless the share is at least 10 percent AND at least twice the placebo's. Every output carries "candle freshness measured on Bitcoin 15 minute only, not on sports".
- **Tested:** the model recovers known rates; the 60 game cap; the kickoff guard; efficient, surviving, noisy-placebo, cost and thin cases; no result field kept anywhere.
