# QR1 JUMPFADE600: a quick first read, registered BEFORE any data was read (Testing Desk, Nov 15, 2026; the Head's owner order of 21:20Z: new quick first reads on data we hold, mechanisms not yet tried, lighter gate)
Mechanism (not favourite-late, not open interest, not lead-lag): a one-minute jump in a 15 minute market's own mid, 10 minutes before the close, is the book moving on thin liquidity; the next ten minutes pull it back toward where it was (a quote gap, not information). Rule: buy the side OPPOSITE to the jump at its ask and hold to settlement. The idea is that a thin book overreacts; the bet is that settlement pays the pre-jump side more often than the jumped price says. One rule, no parameter grid.
## The rule, fixed now
Data: the Forensics OIREL-X extract (claude/forensics-desk 9d6933f7 `OIREL-X_extract.sqlite.xz`, xz sha256 7146c816902939897640a0728cbd72654b39cc9059219d4dd789dc06681fe93e, decompressed sqlite sha256 d9a24e7a37bc81e8296afd9d666b08c63cfa3fb953f4577c0f796875a668cfcf): 18 fifteen minute series, closes Aug 6 to Oct 2 (development only; nothing from Oct 3 on is read; no held-out window). Tables market(ticker, series, open_ts, close_ts, result, strike, src) and candle(ticker, end_ts, bid_c, ask_c, ...). Candles ending after a market's close are dropped; candles with a null bid or ask are dropped at load; a result other than yes or no is excluded and counted.
For each market: D = the candle ending exactly 600 s before the close, P = the candle ending exactly 660 s before the close (either missing: skip, counted). Both must be two-sided (0 < bid < ask < 1) else skip (bad quote); D's spread (ask - bid) must be 0.04 or less (1e-9 slack) else skip (spread). mid = (bid + ask) / 2; jump = mid(D) - mid(P); the rule FIRES if |jump| >= 0.10 (1e-9 slack: so a jump of 0.0999999999999999 fires; the words, not the float). jump > 0: buy NO at round(1 - bid_D, 4); jump < 0: buy YES at ask_D. The entry price must be 0.10 to 0.60 inclusive (1e-9 slack), else skip (band). Hold to settlement, 10 contracts.
Haircut (rnd-queue C1 to C11): price = min(round(ask + h, 6), 0.99), h = 0, 0.01, 0.02; fee = ceil(0.07 x 10 x price x (1 - price) x 100 - 1e-9) / 100 per order; cash = 10 x (win - price) - fee. PF = sum of positive cash over the absolute sum of negative cash (infinite with no loss, 0 with no win and a loss, n/a with neither). Halves floor(n/2) by close time (ties by ticker). Entry candle is 600 s before the close, not in the last 2 minutes: 1c primary (C8); the C11 series (silver, copper, WTI, natural gas, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, HYPE, BNB, INX) take 2c primary. The extract holds BNB, HYPE, silver, copper, WTI, natural gas and the three FX pairs of those; the others are standard series (XRP, Dogecoin, ETH, SOL, NEAR, ZEC, gold, platinum, palladium).
PASS (the paper bar, per group; a read PASSES if either flag is true): `pass_standard` on the standard group = n >= 100 and cash PF at 1c >= 1.3 and both 1c halves >= 1.0 and PF at 2c >= 1.0; `pass_c11` on the C11 group = n >= 100 and cash PF at 2c >= 1.3 and both 2c halves >= 1.0. The pooled set is printed for information. Printed for each set: n, wins, net and PF at 0, 1c, 2c, the halves, the first two thirds and last third of the distinct close days (information only), per-series rows (information only), and every count. Tries counted: 1 rule (read as the two groups: 2 flags, no other variant). Label: "upper bound, not fill-checked; fee mode per-order rounded". Sanity: Auditor B (one build under the lighter gate); result by message only.
Not allowed afterwards: changing the 0.10 jump, the 600 s look, the price band, the 0.04 spread limit or the series list after seeing a figure; that would be a new try, registered before its read.
