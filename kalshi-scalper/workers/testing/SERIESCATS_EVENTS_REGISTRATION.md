Purpose: count events per day per series and per category (from each event's own strike or close date) so the top 3 categories that clear 3 independent events a day get engine family specs. Metadata only: event ticker, series, category, strike or close date. No market, no price, no result; nothing read for performance. Weather is Desk B's (the 14 hourly weather series pass to them).
## Hand case (written before the pull; the builder script was written in the same step and its output was then checked against these figures)
- The SERIESCATS lists (1,599 unique series): Mentions 465, Companies 193, Science and Technology 393 = 1,051 series; the 2 daily Science series (KXTOKENUSED, KXTOP3AID) are inside the 393.
- The runner's series pattern is [A-Z0-9]{2,32}: KXNEWOUTBREAK-P and KXNEWOUTBREAK-PHEIC contain a hyphen and are refused, so they are left out (named here, 2 of 1,051). Requests: 1,051 - 2 = 1,049, one page each (limit 200), cap 1,049 (the hard cap is 1,200). At 1 a second: 1,049 s, about 17.5 minutes, under the 40 minute run limit.
- A series with more than 200 events answers with a cursor: it is listed apart from the manifest and asked in a follow-up job (no silent truncation).
- The probe job (6 requests, 3 series: KXTOKENUSED, KX60MINMENTION, AAPLCEOCHANGE, each with and without status=settled) shows whether the default list includes settled events. If the default omits settled events, the main job is changed to status=settled plus a second pass for open, and re-validated; its cap then rises to at most 2,098 and is split in two runs (1,200 each at most).
- Window: the events list has no date filter; the whole series list is kept and the reader keeps events with a strike or close date in the last 60 days (to Nov 16) plus open ones. Nothing else is read.
- Output of the reader (counts only): per series events per day over its active days, and per category the number of series with a median of 3 or more independent events a day. "Independent" = distinct event tickers with distinct strike or close times; the two daily Science series give 1 a day each and cannot clear 3 alone.
