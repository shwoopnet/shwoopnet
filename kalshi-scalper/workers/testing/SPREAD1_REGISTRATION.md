# SPREAD1: spread widening ahead of a move, NC15 markets. Registered Nov 16, 2026, BEFORE any read of a spread or result (Testing Desk)

Mechanism (not favourite-late, open interest, lead-lag, jump-fade, previous-window result, restart gap or volume spike): market makers widen the quote when they expect an informed move and want protection. If the quote widens while the mid is already moving, the move is more likely to be real information than noise and should continue to the close. Prediction: when the entry candle's spread is at least twice the market's recent spread and the mid has moved, the side the mid moved toward wins more often than its ask implies. One variant, tried 1. A baseline row (the same side and band rule, no widening condition) is printed beside it; the spike result passes only if that baseline itself does not clear PF 1.5 at 1c or 2c (Auditor B's rule from VOLSPIKE1; the shape overlaps the closed favourite-late family because the band takes in favourites).

Data: the OIREL-X extract already held; the ten non-crypto series (KXGOLD15M, KXSILVER15M, KXCOPPER15M, KXPLATINUM15M, KXPALLADIUM15M, KXWTI15M, KXNATGAS15M, KXEURUSD15M, KXGBPUSD15M, KXUSDJPY15M). The reader refuses any selected market with close_ts >= 1791043200. Candles ending after the close are dropped first. Development data already read for OIREL-X, no held-out inside.

CANDLE-COUNT GEOMETRY CHECK (written before freezing, structure only: counts of candles, no spread, price or result looked at). A 15 minute market opens at close - 900 and its first candle ends at open + 60, so the candles ending at or before close - 660 are the four ending at close - 840, -780, -720 and -660. In the extract, of 21,384 selected markets 21,314 have exactly 4 two-sided candles ending at or before close - 660 (bid > 0, ask < 1, ask > bid), 24 have 3, 18 have 2, 15 have 1 and 13 have 0. So the prior set can hold at most 4 values and the median rule here needs at least 3 two-sided prior candles (P included); the lesson of VOLSPIKE1 (a 5-candle requirement that could never be met) is applied: no requirement above 4 appears anywhere in this rule. With 3 or 4 values the median is noisy; that is accepted and stated.

Rule (per market, close C): E = the candle ending exactly 600 s before C; P = the candle ending exactly 660 s before C. Both must be two-sided. Prior spreads = ask - bid of the market's two-sided candles ending at or before C - 660 (P included); at least 3 of them, else skip. Widening: spread(E) >= max(0.03, 2 x median(prior spreads)), compared on 6 decimals. Move: d = mid(E) - mid(P), mid = (bid + ask) / 2 on 6 decimals; d >= 0.01 buys YES, d <= -0.01 buys NO, else skip. The side's ask (YES ask, or 1 - YES bid for NO) must be 0.35 to 0.80 inclusive. Hold to settlement, 10 contracts; void: price refunded, entry fee kept.

Haircut and cash: C1 to C11 (price = ask + h capped at 0.99, h in {0, 0.01, 0.02}, per-order rounded fee, cash = 10 x (win - price) - fee). 2c primary for the NC15 set; gold, platinum and palladium print both bars as flags. Label "upper bound, not fill-checked".

Pass bar: n >= 80 trades; 1c and 2c PF >= 1.5; both halves (floor(n/2) by close time, ticker) >= 1.0 at 2c; day-clustered z >= 2.0 at 2c; and the baseline PF below 1.5 at 1c and 2c. Printed: n, the baseline n and PFs at 1c and 2c, the difference. Result line: development data already read for OIREL-X, no held-out inside, a pass is a lead for the forward shadow; favourite-late family caveat; the baseline of the VOLSPIKE1 read (a different rule, a different band) had 2c PF 0.84 and was seen before this was registered; no spread or widening figure has been looked at.
