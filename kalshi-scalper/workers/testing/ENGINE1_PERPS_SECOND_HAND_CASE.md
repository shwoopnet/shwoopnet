## Definitions (my reading; every choice marked CHOICE)
- Candle = (end_ts, bid, ask). Two-sided: 0 < bid < ask. mid = (bid + ask) / 2. An entry candle e and every candle used by a feature or an exit must be two-sided; one-sided or missing = missing. Sides: long fills at ask x (1 + h), short at bid x (1 - h); h relative: primary 0.0002 and 0.0005, stress 0.001 and 0.002. Exit fills: long exits at bid x (1 - h), short at ask x (1 + h).
- Returns: long exit/fill - 1; short (fill - exit)/fill; net = gross - round trip fee (taker 0.0024, maker 0.0010) - funding (below).
- Features (candles ending at or before e only): mom_k = ln(mid(e) / mid(e - 60k)) for k in 1, 3, 5, 15 (the candle ending exactly e - 60k); up > 0, down < 0, 0 matches neither; spread_bps = (ask - bid)/mid x 1e4, classes low <= c1, mid <= c2, else high, where c1 and c2 are the ceil(n/3)-th and ceil(2n/3)-th sorted spreads of the SEARCH entries (two-sided candles) of that ticker (given as inputs). Time of day by e's UTC seconds of the day: pre [09:00, 14:30), open [14:30, 15:30), mid [15:30, 20:00), close [20:00, 21:00), post everything else (21:00 is post). orb: only when e ends at 15:01 UTC or later; window = two-sided candles ending 14:31 to 15:00 UTC inclusive on e's UTC day; orb_up if mid(e) > the highest window mid, orb_down if < the lowest; empty window = missing. sess: window = the same UTC day's two-sided candles ending from 14:31 UTC up to e - 60 inclusive; sess_high if mid(e) > the highest, sess_low if < the lowest; empty window = missing. Missing feature = no entry (counted), never "does not match".
- Exits: T3, T10, T15, T30, T60, T240: the candle ending exactly e + 60 m minutes, else no mark. TS15, TS30: target and stop k = 15 or 30 bp from the entry FILL (after the haircut): long target bid >= fill x (1 + k/1e4), stop bid <= fill x (1 - k/1e4); short target ask <= fill x (1 - k/1e4), stop ask >= fill x (1 + k/1e4), tested on the RAW bid or ask of two-sided candles ending after e up to e + 1800 s in order, first trigger wins; the exit then fills at the haircut quote of that candle; with no trigger the exit is the T30 mark (no mark if missing). TC: the candle ending 21:00 UTC the same UTC day (an entry ending at or after 21:00 is "no mark"). TO: the candle ending 14:31 UTC on the first Monday to Friday UTC day strictly after the entry's UTC day.
- Cooldown per rule = its hold, applied to TAKEN entries only (CHOICE: an entry skipped for no mark does not start a cooldown): a new entry's candle end must be >= the previous taken entry's end + 1800 s for T3, T10, T15, T30, TS15, TS30; + 3600 for T60; + 14400 for T240; >= the previous exit time for TC (21:00) and TO (the next open).
- Boundary clip: with a boundary B (the first VALIDATE candle end), a SEARCH entry (end < B) whose exit time >= B is dropped and counted. Funding: a hold crossing a 21:00 UTC print strictly between the entry candle end and the exit candle end pays or receives that print's rate as a fraction of notional: positive rate paid by longs and received by shorts (net -= rate for a long, += rate for a short); a missing print for a crossed 21:00 = "funding not booked", the trade is excluded from the gate figures and counted; a Friday hold over the weekend crosses three prints (Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
- Statistics: PF = sum of positive nets / sum of negative nets (inf with no loss and some win, nan with neither); halves floor(n/2) by entry time; day-clustered z by UTC entry day (sum per day, mean / (sample sd / sqrt days)).

## Addendum 2 (Nov 16, 2026, pass 3: DST-aware ET clocks and funding from the extract's own print timestamps; Desk A's amendments 3 and 4, Auditor B's ruling; written before the code change)
**ET clocks.** US Eastern: UTC-4 from 2026-03-08T07:00Z to 2026-11-01T06:00Z, UTC-5 otherwise; the ET clock of a candle is its end_ts plus the offset in force at that instant. time of day (ET): pre [04:00, 09:30), open [09:30, 10:30), mid [10:30, 15:00), close [15:00, 16:00), post everything else. orb: only when e ends at 10:01 ET or later (before: False), window = two-sided candles ending 09:31 to 10:00 ET inclusive on e's ET day, empty after 10:01 = missing; sess: window from 09:31 ET of e's ET day to e - 60 inclusive; TC = the candle ending 16:00 ET on e's ET day (e at or after it: no mark); TO = the candle ending 09:31 ET on the next ET weekday strictly after e's ET day. The ET day's own offset is read at that day's midday (a day that changes offset at 02:00 is a stated edge, no trading data on it for stock perps). For dates from 2026-11-01T06:00Z these equal the earlier UTC constants (pinned by the old tests).
Hand values (EDT, Tuesday 2026-10-13, candle i ends at 13:50Z + i min, i.e. 09:50 ET + i; the KXHAND prices): orb_up true at i11 (14:01Z = 10:01 ET) and i12, false at i10 (14:00Z = 10:00 ET); sess_high false at i10, true at i11 to i13, false at i14; sess_low false; tod: 13:29:59Z pre, 13:30:00Z open, 14:29:59Z open, 14:30:00Z mid, 18:59:59Z mid, 19:00:00Z close, 19:59:59Z close, 20:00:00Z post, 07:59:59Z post, 08:00:00Z pre. TC from i0 exits at the candle ending 20:00Z (16:00 EDT); TO from Tuesday exits Wednesday 13:31Z (09:31 EDT). The DST step: an entry on Friday 2026-10-30 (EDT) with TO exits on Monday 2026-11-02 at 14:31Z (09:31 EST); an entry on Saturday 2026-10-31 and on Sunday 2026-11-01 before and after 06:00Z likewise exit Monday 14:31Z; TC on Friday 2026-10-30 exits at 20:00Z, on Monday 2026-11-02 at 21:00Z.
**Funding from the print timestamps.** funding = {print_ts: rate} of one ticker, whatever the hour. A position (entry candle end e, exit candle end xt) pays or receives each print p with e < p < xt (an exit AT the print pays nothing): a long pays the rate (net -= rate), a short receives it. A position is "funding not booked" when it overlaps a stretch with no print: e < the first print, or xt > the last print + 26 h, or two neighbouring prints a and b more than 26 h apart with e < b and xt > a, or no prints at all. Hand values (prints Mon 2026-10-12 20:00Z +0.0001, Tue 10-13 20:00Z -0.0002, Wed 10-14 20:00Z +0.0003, then Fri 10-16 20:00Z +0.0001): LONG TO entered Tue 13:50Z exits Wed 13:31Z crossing Tuesday's print only: funding -0.0002 (the long receives it), booked; a position entered Mon 10-12 19:00Z and exited 19:30Z: e < the first print: not booked; one entered Tue 10-13 21:00Z, exited Wed 13:31Z: crosses no print, booked with funding 0 (covered); one entered Wed 20:30Z and exited Thu 10-15 20:30Z: the neighbouring prints Wed 20:00Z and Fri 20:00Z are 48 h apart (over 26 h) and e < Fri, xt > Wed: not booked; one exiting after Fri 10-16 20:00Z + 26 h = Sat 22:00Z: not booked; a short LONG-opposite of the first booked position gets +0.0002 less... precisely: SHORT TO entered Tue 13:50Z: funding -(-0.0002) = +0.0002 paid in the sign convention net -= fund so net = gross - fee - 0.0002 for the short.

## Addendum 3 (Nov 16, 2026, Auditor B's 16b ruling on the counter order, written before the code change)
Per candle, in this order: (1) two-sided, else skipped silently; (2) COOLDOWN (a candle inside the previous taken entry's hold: skipped silently, nothing counted); (3) the feature: None counts "missing", false is silent; (4) the boundary clip, counted only for an entry whose signal is true; (5) no mark, counted only for a true signal that was not clipped. My first build tested the feature before the cooldown, so a missing feature inside a cooldown was counted; that is replaced. Hand case: series with candles at t0 - 60, t0, t0 + 60, t0 + 120, t0 + 180 and t0 + 300 (the candle at t0 + 240 absent), rising mids, filter mom1_up, LONG T3: the candle t0 - 60 has no candle 60 s before it: missing (1); t0 has mom1 up and a T3 mark at t0 + 180: taken; t0 + 60, + 120, + 180 are inside the 1800 s cooldown (silent); t0 + 300 has no candle at t0 + 240, so its feature is missing but it is inside the cooldown: silent. missing = 1, trades = 1 (the feature-first order would give 2).
