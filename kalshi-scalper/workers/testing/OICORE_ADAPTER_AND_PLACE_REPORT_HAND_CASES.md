## 1. oicore_shadow.py: the OICORE mechanism as a shadow adapter for ANY NC15 or R6 market
spxoi1.py stays as audited. The new module takes the series, the band and T as REQUIRED arguments (no defaults for any of them, nor for limit_add or the log type):
- oicore_shadow(polls_by_ticker, cs, series, band_lo, band_hi, T, limit_add, log) -> {ticker: ('trade', side, ask, {limit, t}) | ('no_trade', reason) | ('no_data', reason)} for the tickers whose series part (the text before the first '-') equals `series`; other tickers are ignored. band_lo and band_hi are the cheaper side's ASK in dollars, inclusive with 1e-9; log is 'NC15' (second look: the next poll at most 6 s after the signal poll) or 'R6' (the first poll after, at most 4 s after); any other value raises.
- The mechanism is OICORE's (Card 2 and the Head's rulings): oi_rise = open interest at the candle ending close - 180 s minus the one ending close - 360 s (both needed), fires at oi_rise >= T (1e-9); the signal poll is the FIRST poll in [close - 180, close - 120] (1e-9) whose cheaper side (lower ask, tie YES, both book sides needed) has its ask in [band_lo, band_hi]; limit = ask + limit_add; one signal per market. Reason strings as in spxoi1 except the band text, which names the band given ('cheaper side outside 0.10 to 0.40').
- fill_check(polls, i, side, limit, log) as spxoi1's with the second-look span from `log`.
- Two invented markets: A = series KXAAA15M on 'NC15', band 0.08 to 0.50, T = 500; B = series KXBBB15M on 'R6', band 0.10 to 0.40, T = 1,000. Close C for both.
  - A1 (KXAAA15M-1): oi 1000 -> 1600 (rise 600 >= 500); a poll at C - 175 with NO bids [(.80, 20)] and YES bids [(.15, 20)]: YES ask .20 in band: TRADE YES, ask .20, limit .20 (limit_add 0).
  - A2 (-2): the same book but the NO bid at .45 (YES ask .55) and YES bid .40 (NO ask .60): cheaper side .55 above .50: no_trade 'cheaper side outside 0.08 to 0.50'.
  - A3 (-3): rise 499: no_trade 'oi rise under T'.
  - B1 (KXBBB15M-1): oi rise 1,000 exactly: trade at ask .12 (band .10 to .40): TRADE YES at .12.
  - B2 (-2): ask .45 (inside A's band, outside B's): no_trade 'cheaper side outside 0.10 to 0.40' (shows the band is B's own).
  - B3 (-3): rise 999: no_trade 'oi rise under T'.
  - A series ticker given to a B call (and the reverse) is ignored.
  - Fill checks: signal poll at C - 175, the next poll at C - 170 (5 s): NC15: filled; R6: the next poll is 5 s > 4 s: not fill-checked; a next poll at C - 171 (4 s) fills on both; at C - 169 (6 s): NC15 filled (exactly 6), R6 not fill-checked.

## 2. place_report.py: how many fill-checked signals each registered wheel place would log per trading day (counts and dates only)
- Input: place specs, R6-shape polls by ticker and the candles' open interest where a place needs it. No outcome column, no result, no price is read or kept: the output holds days, counts and rates only. Folders go through scorekit.guard (HELDOUT_ and listed runs are refused). Each place has `heldout_first` (a UTC timestamp or None) and a start time; a window whose close is on or after heldout_first is NOT read (counted as held-out windows not read), one whose signal poll is before the start time is not counted (l).
- Place kinds built (the two whose signal needs book columns and open interest only): 'depth' = the NC15 depth cell (frozen sheet section 2: D = close - 600; signal poll = the last poll at or before D and at most 10 s before it; imb5 = (sum of the five YES bid sizes - sum of the five NO bid sizes) / their total; in the cell if |imb5| >= 0.50; imb5 > 0 buys YES, < 0 buys NO; the implied side's ask in [0.10, 0.90]; limit = ask + 0.01; fill check = the next poll within 6 s with 10 contracts offered at or under the limit on both polls) and 'oicore' (the OICORE mechanism of tool 1). A signal with a limit of 88c or above is skipped under lane (i) (not counted, not a miss). CFRTI-K needs the perpetual's marks (not in the archive yet): NOT built, named in the output as 'not built'.
- Counted signal = fill-checked and fillable (status filled) and not skipped. Per place and per US Central day (d) of the signal poll's exchange time: windows examined, signals counted; misses and 'not fill-checked' as counts; days = the Central days on which the place's series has any poll at or after the start time; rate = total counted / days with data; days to a 60-signal block = max(7, ceil(60 / rate)) (the lane's 7 day minimum), None at rate 0.
- Hand case (invented): series KXAAA15M, depth place, start time = before everything, heldout_first = day 3 00:00Z. Day 1 (Central) windows W1..W3: W1 imb5 +0.60 (YES bids sizes 20,10 and NO bids 5): YES ask .30: signal, next poll 5 s later offering 10 at or under .31: COUNTED; W2 imb5 +0.40: not in the cell; W3 imb5 -0.60, NO ask .95: outside the band: no signal. Day 2: W4 imb5 -0.70 NO ask .25, next poll 8 s later: not fill-checked; W5 imb5 +0.80, YES ask .20, the next poll offers only 4: miss; W6 imb5 +0.55, YES ask .88: limit .89 >= 88c: skipped (i); W7 imb5 +0.90, YES ask .40, filled: COUNTED. Day 3: W8 (close on day 3 00:00Z or later): held-out: not read. Expected: day 1: examined 3, counted 1; day 2: examined 4, counted 1, not fill-checked 1, miss 1, skipped 1; held-out windows not read 1; days with data 2; total counted 2; rate 1.0 a day; days to a block = max(7, 60) = 60.
