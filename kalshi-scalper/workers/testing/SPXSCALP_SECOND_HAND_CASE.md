## The rule as I read the card and Amendment 1
- Windows: KXINX15M settled markets; development = closes before 2026-12-01T00:00:00Z (a constant; a window on or after it is NOT touched and counted). Window start O = close - 900 s. ES close at minute end t = the bar with ts_event t - 60 s (Databento start stamp); a missing bar is missing, never carried; an instrument_id change between the start bar and a look bar drops that look (counted).
- Looks: the candles ending at O + 60 m for m = 1 to 5 (end at or before close - 600 s), in time order. At a look: move = ES(t) - ES(O), threshold 0.0005 x ES(O) (1e-9): |move| >= threshold; up = YES candidate, down = NO candidate, re-evaluated at each look. Candidate ask = YES ask close (YES) or 1 - YES bid close (NO); a missing side = no price = no entry at that look. The FIRST look where the move AND the band 0.20 to 0.25 (variant band 0.20 to 0.30) hold (inclusive, 1e-9, on the ask SEEN) is the one entry; one entry per window per band. No ES start bar: the window is dropped (counted).
- Entry fill = ask + s with s = 0.01 and 0.02, both printed; 1c is primary (every look and the time exit are earlier than the last 2 minutes). 1 contract; per-order fees rounded up (scorekit.fee_rounded), charged on each order at its own price.
- HEADLINE exit: the first candle after the entry candle, through the minute ending close - 300 s, whose bid close >= 2 x the entry fill price (1e-9): exit at that bid - s; else the time exit at the candle ending close - 300 s at bid close - s; no candle or no bid at the time exit (when no target hit): the entry is DROPPED and counted, never filled by assumption. Net = (exit - entry fill) - fee(entry) - fee(exit).
- VARIANT exit (half and 80c): modelled as a 2-contract position (my reading: 'half' needs two units; the card says size 1 for the read): one ENTRY order of 2 contracts (fee on 2 contracts, rounded up), one exit order of 1 contract at the 2x target (as above) and one exit order of 1 contract at the first candle whose bid close >= 0.80 (through close - 300 s) at bid - s; a half with no hit goes at the time exit (an order of 1 contract each); a needed missing time-exit candle drops the entry. Net = sum over the two contracts. PF is scale-free.
- HOLD to settlement: entry fill, payout 1 if the side wins; a void refunds the price and keeps the fee (net = -entry fee); a result other than yes, no or void on an entered window stops the run naming it. Windows dropped for the exit arms (no time-exit candle) still count here.
- Halves: the first floor(n / 2) entries in window-close order and the rest, per arm. A rule COUNTS only if at 1c the PF is >= 1.3 with BOTH halves >= 1.0 and at 2c the PF is >= 1.0 (unrounded, 1e-9). Label: FALSIFIED if fewer than 100 entries, or a half PF under 1.0 at 1c, or the 2c PF under 1.0; else 'counts' if it counts; else 'no evidence'. Every line says 'upper bound, not fill-checked'.
- C1, first: the share of look candles with both sides and the spread distribution (median, p90 in cents).
