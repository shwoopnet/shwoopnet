# PERPSLANE4 exact definitions (Testing Desk, Nov 16, 2026): the text a blind second build is written from. With specs/PERPSLANE4.spec.json and LANE4_HAND_CASE.md. If a sentence leaves a choice, ask before building.
**Inputs.** candles[name] = {end_ts: (bid, ask)} (the 1 minute candle closes, end_ts = the end of the minute); usable = 0 < bid < ask; mid = (bid + ask) / 2. funding[name] = {ts: rate}. Underlyings for A2 only: u[name][ET date] = (official open, previous trading day's official close). Names = the pool (at least 5 ET dates with a candle and 2 prints) of the stock perps plus KXGOLDPERP, KXUS500PERP, KXUSTECH100PERP. Nothing at or before the data start and nothing with an entry or exit end at or after 2026-11-17T00:00:00Z (1794873600) is used: such trades are dropped and counted ('boundary').
**Clock.** et_end(L, hh, mm) = the UTC instant of hh:mm ET on ET date L, the offset read at that ET day's midday (EDT = UTC - 4 from 2026-03-08T07:00:00Z to 2026-11-01T06:00:00Z, EST = UTC - 5 otherwise). A weekday is Monday to Friday (ET date); next_weekday(L) skips Saturday and Sunday.
**Fills and returns.** Entry long at ask x (1 + h), short at bid x (1 - h) of the entry candle; leave long at bid x (1 - h), short at ask x (1 + h) of the exit candle; gross = exit / fill - 1 (long), (fill - exit) / fill (short); h = 0.0002 and 0.0005 primary (0.001 and 0.002 stress, counted apart). Both candles must be usable, else 'no mark' (counted, no trade).
**Funding (booked).** A position (entry end e, exit end x, long or short) is BOOKED with funding f = the sum of the rates of the prints with e < ts < x, a long pays it and a short receives it (the sign is the page's: a positive rate is paid by longs) and f is subtracted from the net of a long and added for a short; it is NOT booked (excluded from every gate, counted per name) if e is before the name's first print, or x is after the last print plus 26 h, or two neighbouring prints a < b with b - a more than 26 h satisfy e < b and x > a, or the name has no prints. net_fee = gross - 2 x fee - f, fee = 0.0012 and 0.0005 a side (both shown, neither the headline).
**One position at a time.** Per (rule, name): an entry at e is taken only if e is at least the previous taken trade's exit end (e == exit end allowed); check order: signal condition, cooldown, marks, funding, boundary.
**Rule A (4).** For every ET weekday L with a usable candle ending at 16:00 ET (entry) and a usable candle ending at 09:31 ET of next_weekday(L) (exit): class weekend if L is a Friday, else weeknight; side long or short. 4 rules (class x side).
**Rule A2 (24).** On an ET weekday L: u_open and u_prev from the underlying; P_0925 = mid of the candle ending 09:25 ET of L; P_prev1600 = mid of the candle ending 16:00 ET of the latest earlier ET weekday whose 16:00 candle key is present (it must be usable, else skip); the entry candle E = the candle ending 09:31 ET (usable); g = (u_open / u_prev - 1) - (P_0925 / P_prev1600 - 1); half = ((ask - bid) / 2) / mid of E; the trigger: round(|g|, 9) > round(2 x half, 9) AND round(|g| x 10000, 9) >= thr, thr = 40 or 100; direction: g > 0 is 'up'; follow = long on up and short on down, fade = the opposite; class Monday (L is a Monday) or Tuesday to Friday; exits T30 (the candle ending exactly 30 minutes after E), T60 (60 minutes), TC (the candle ending 16:00 ET of L). 2 classes x 2 thresholds x 2 modes x 3 exits = 24 rules.
**Rule B1 (36).** On an ET weekday L: O = the candle ending 09:31 ET, E_N = the candle ending N minutes later (N = 5, 15, 30), both usable; m = mid(E_N) / mid(O) - 1; fires if round(|m| x 10000, 9) >= thr (20 or 40); up if m > 0; follow = long on up, short on down, fade the opposite; entry at E_N; exits T30, T60 (minutes after E_N) or TC (the candle ending 16:00 ET). 3 x 2 x 2 x 3 = 36 rules.
**Rule B2 (8).** On an ET weekday L: m = mid(candle ending 15:30 ET) / mid(candle ending 15:00 ET) - 1 (both usable); thr 20 or 40 bp (>=, 9 dp); follow or fade as in B1; entry at 15:30; exit the candle ending 16:01 ET of L or the candle ending 09:31 ET of next_weekday(L) (TO). 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 rules.
**Rule B3 (2).** Entry at the candle ending 15:30 ET of every ET weekday, exit at the candle ending 16:01 ET, long or short.
**Rule C (27).** For every print ts T of a name (the extract's own timestamps): entry candle end = T - x minutes (x = 60, 30, 15), exit candle end = T + y minutes (y = 15, 30, 60); sides long, short, or RECEIVE: the last rate = the rate of the latest print with ts strictly before the entry candle end (none = no trade); receive = short if that rate > 0, long if < 0, no trade if exactly 0. One trade per (name, T, x, y, side rule). Day = the ET date of the entry end. 3 x 3 x 3 = 27 rules.
**Pool and gate (pooled over names, per rule, per fee).** Trades sorted by (entry end, name); n = booked trades at h = 0.0002; trade days = distinct ET dates of the booked trades; trading days = distinct ET dates (before the boundary) on which at least one pool name has a usable candle ending 09:31 ET; per day = n / trading days. Gate: n >= 100 AND trade days >= 10 AND per day >= 3 AND PF(h 0.0002) >= 1.3 overall and in each half (halves = the first floor(n/2) trades of THAT haircut's own booked trade set and the rest) >= 1.0 AND PF(h 0.0005) >= 1.0 overall (and the same halves test at 0.0005). PF with no losses = infinity (passes); no trades fails. The day clustered z of the 0.0002 net: sum per ET date, mean over dates, sd with n - 1 over sqrt(dates); p = the upper normal tail 0.5 erfc(z / sqrt 2); BH at 5% over the rules with a defined p at k x 0.05 / m_total, m_total = m_carried + 101 (m_carried an argument). Printed per rule and per fee: n, trade days, per day, PF at both haircuts, halves, day z, p, gate, counts of skips by reason and funding-unbooked per name.
**Margin terms (per survivor, messages only).** L = f x page max of the name, f = 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 (page max from Forensics' census); liquidation distance approx 1 / L - maintenance (4.5% assumed; gold 4.47% measured, 17.4x), labelled 'maintenance assumed'; weekly percent of the account = net per trade x trades a day x 5 x L.
