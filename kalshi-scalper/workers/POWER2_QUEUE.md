## Notes
1. **Zero-read cards worth keeping, in order of value.** MMSIM1 (and with it MMHOUR1 and MMMAP1) reads on Nov 24 and costs nothing. Its PF bar (1.3) sits at or above the shrunk estimate (1.14), so the probable outcome is a fail; power peaks near 0.46 and FALLS with n if the true PF is under 1.3 (0.02 at 1,600 events), so waiting longer does not help. The verdict is reachable; a pass is not likely. CFRTI1-BTC is the other reachable one (Nov 22 to 24), but the bar (PF >= 1.3, n 50, one try) is soft: a rule with no edge passes 24% of the time. A pass there is a prompt to re-test, not a result.
2. **MMSIM1 arithmetic.** From MMPOWER1: PF 1.28 at the bound, mean +3.71c per event, sd 41.7c, so gross loss per event is 13.25c and se(PF) at n 300 is 0.18; this reproduces MMPOWER1's own P(PF >= 1.5) of 0.11 (D) to 0.17 (I). Shrunk true mean 1.86c gives PF 1.14. The daily correlation clause needs 32 days (MMPOWER1), so the full bar is read Nov 29, the PF clause Nov 24.
3. **CFRTI1 simulation.** Binomial, n 50, price 16c (backed out of win 33% and mean +15.6c with a 1c fee). The 50% shrink also matches the best-of-5 selection shrink (z 2.11 vs zmax(5) about 1.16 gives k 0.45).
4. **NC15DEPTH1 has no development estimate and no cell definition**, so the 3c (and 1.5c shrunk) effect is an assumption, not a finding. Under it the card is a pooled, forward-only job that needs 60 to 150 days of recording for a z 4.39 verdict. Pre-register ONE pooled cell before any outcome is read; per-series 2x2 cells cannot be powered (about 100 windows each in a week, z 0.33).
5. **MENTPERSIST1.** Assumed true gross edge 5c (half the rule's 10c margin), 2c fee, ask 30c: net 3c per contract, PF 1.14, sd 0.48. z 5.34 needs about 7,200 trades; at 0.09 trades per read that is 80,000 reads. Even the registered cap (1,600 reads, 64 hours) reaches about 150 trades, z 0.8. The only reachable verdict is a development kill (PF under 1.0 on 60 trades, about 645 reads, 26 hours of the whole budget). Cheaper: score the 48 R1 and 20 R2 trades already by the rule on the held 500 offline (zero reads) and stop fetching. The 5 reads already logged are not enough for anything.
6. **AVG1 and STALE1 are frozen by their own pinned cuts** (Amendment 1 in each). Development cannot grow, and new events fall in the held-out set, which is read only for a development survivor. AVG1's outcome is also a proxy (the same index the model uses), so a high PF there is partly circular. Reopening needs a new registration with a rolling cut AND real settled results for the hourly ladder.
7. **EARLYCLOSE1.** The only positive spot is one calibration bin (0.20 to 0.25, win 33% vs 22.5%, n 63, z about 2, post hoc); at half the effect it needs 1,219 markets.
8. **TIECOND1 arm B** is not registered anywhere in this repo; I read it as the tie rate in break, weekend and holiday windows (existence counts in the TIECOND1 result). Stage 1's best lower bound is 1.96% against the 6% kill, so a pass needs the true rate to be about 3 times anything seen.
9. **Generic cost of the z bar** (sd 0.40 to 0.50 per contract): 1c edge needs 31k to 48k trades (z 4.39), 3c needs 3.4k to 5.4k, 5c needs 1.2k to 1.9k (`power2_calc.py`, top block). Any queued Idea card built on a 1 to 3c edge is out of reach inside 90 days unless it pools over 10 or more series.
