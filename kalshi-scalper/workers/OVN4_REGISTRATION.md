# OVN4: quoted spread by hour, who pays it overnight (brief test d; 2026-11-06)
Shared rules: `workers/OVN_COMMON.md`. Written before any spread was grouped by hour. (Recorded spreads were glanced at only to see that US500 is about 1 bp in cash hours; no hourly grouping was done.)

**Mechanism / counterparty.** With the cash market shut, market makers quote wider (no hedge in the cash market), so a taker who trades the perp overnight pays the extra; the maker earns it.
**Data.** Hourly candles of the universe perps (bid.close and ask.close, both present and ask>bid) from the first candle to before the cut (end_ts = 2026-11-01T00:00Z), plus recorder rows. rel spread = (ask-bid)/mid in bps. Buckets by ET: CASH (09:30 to 16:00 Mon to Fri), AFTER (16:00 to 20:00), NIGHT (20:00 to 09:30 Mon to Fri), WEEKEND (Fri 20:00 to Mon 09:30 candles from Saturday and Sunday).
**Numeric prediction.** Median rel spread NIGHT / CASH between 1.5 and 4, WEEKEND / CASH between 2 and 6, in bps about 1 to 3 bps cash, 3 to 8 at night, all small next to the 24 bps fee. **Cost conclusion registered:** the spread premium overnight is under 10 bps, so a spread-driven strategy is not possible; the round-trip fee is the binding cost.
**Kill rules.** FALSIFIED if NIGHT/CASH median ratio is under 1.3 (no overnight widening); NOT_ENOUGH_DATA if CASH or NIGHT has under 100 candles per perp or under 10 distinct days; LEAD if ratio at least 2 in both halves (D1 before 2026-10-27 using USTECH100/US500 candles; if USTECH100 has no D1 candles the half uses US500 only) and the NIGHT minus CASH extra spread exceeds 10 bps (it is not expected to). Not a P/L test: no z, no deflation use beyond the N count.
**Enough data:** about 2 weeks of candles already exist for US500 (from 2026-10-06); single stocks need quoted candles since 2026-11-05: 100 night candles each = about 10 nights, about 2026-11-20.
