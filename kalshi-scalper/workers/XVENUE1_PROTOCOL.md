# XVENUE1 (registered Nov 9, 2026, before any price is compared): Kalshi against Polymarket on identical public events
Research only; no account, no order, no key. Free public data: Kalshi `/markets` and Polymarket Gamma/CLOB read endpoints (reachable from the sandbox: Gamma 200, CLOB 200 on Nov 9).
Mechanism and counterparty: the same event (for example "Bitcoin above X on date D", a Fed decision, an election-style contract) is quoted on two venues by different makers and retail crowds; a persistent gap larger than both venues' fees and spreads is a locked or near-locked trade; the loser is the slower venue's retail and makers.
Step 1 (feasibility, no outcome): list currently open Kalshi markets of the series BTC/ETH price-above, Fed decision, CPI, and open Polymarket markets on the same underlying, date and strike; match pairs by exact event text, date and strike (a pair needs the same resolution source; ambiguous pairs are dropped and counted). Report: pairs found, per pair both venues' bid and ask, the gap (cheapest YES on one venue vs 1 minus the cheapest NO... precisely a locked profit = 1 - ask_YES_A - ask_NO_B - fees), and how many pairs show a locked profit after both fees (Kalshi C = 10 fee, Polymarket taker fee as published). 1 try. Step 2 only if step 1 shows pairs: a forward snapshot over several days (every 5 minutes) before any claim. Prediction: matched pairs are few; locked profits after fees essentially absent (about 95%); resolution-source differences (index vs exchange price) are the usual trap. Count: 1.
