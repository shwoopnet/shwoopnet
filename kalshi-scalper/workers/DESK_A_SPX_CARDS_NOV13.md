## Card 1: SPXFV1 (the unique candidate: futures-implied fair value)
- **Mechanism and counterparty (named):** Kalshi's resting quotes in the last minutes lag the S&P 500 futures move since the window start. The index is a deterministic function of the same stocks that ES and MES futures price continuously, so the futures return since the window open is a free, public-in-principle fair-value input for "index at close is at or above the open candle". Counterparty: a quoter or taker who has not repriced against the futures move (stale quotes against a futures move), buying the cheap side while the futures say it is worth more.
- **Reference (permitted source only):** ES or MES 1 minute bars from the owner's licensed Databento feed, a named-list pull inside the owner's cap and personal-use terms-risk ruling of Nov 11 (no all-symbols request). Nothing Databento-derived is committed; Yahoo SPY and any other source are barred. Gate 0: the pull exists for the window times with whole minutes, including the minute that ends at the decision time.
- **Rule (a port of CFRTI-K, `CFRTI_K_REGISTRATION.md` section 12, constants inherited and not tuned):** at the decision candle (end time = close - 120 s), model chance of YES = Phi( ln(F_now / F_open) / sd ), F_now the futures mark at that minute, F_open the futures mark at the window's opening minute (the return since the open is applied to the strike, so the futures-to-index basis cancels, exactly as in CFRTI-K), sd = sample standard deviation of the last 10 one-minute futures log changes (at least 6 present) times sqrt(4/3). Buy 1 contract of the cheaper side at its ask when the model chance exceeds the ask by at least 5 cents, the YES spread is at most 3 cents, and the cheap side's price is inside the inherited band 0.05 to 0.30. Held to settlement; fee as Kalshi's quadratic schedule, unrounded for scoring, then up-rounded per order for the stress line. Any other band, look time or edge threshold is a NEW try, registered first.
- **Toolkit (standing rule (i)), each a separately counted try only if used:** a resting limit one tick inside the ask, a profit target at the bid reaching a stated level, a stop, a time exit at 60 s before the close. The registered line is the plain taker, hold to settlement.
- **Numeric prediction (written before any data):** the model's cheap side wins at least 8 points more often than its price implies in the traded set, giving a mean net of at least +2c a contract after the fee, and cash PF 1.5 or better in each of three equal date thirds of the held-out, with n at least 150. I have no development figure for this market. Honest prior: low. The same idea on Bitcoin (CFRTI-K) had n 601 and cash PF 1.07 to 1.09 on development, "not falsified, bar not cleared", so a pass here needs a different result.
- **Kill criteria:** gate 0 fails (no futures minute bars aligned to the windows); fewer than 150 traded windows by the read date (NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, and a second such read closes the source); cash PF under 1.0 pooled at n of 100 or more; or the fired-trade win rate within 3 points of the traded price's implied rate at n of 150 or more (no edge over the market).
- **Held-out:** HELDOUT_FIRST = 2026-12-01T00:00:00Z as a date constant (the Head's proposal): windows closing on or after it are read once, in one sitting; development is everything before it (about 17 weekdays of recording, roughly 425 windows, so development is small: "no evidence at n = x" under 100 traded, and nothing is tuned on it). The Dec 1 constant lives in the scorer and is frozen with its hash before any read.
- **Declined-list check (docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md 7e, all rows read):** lock and parity scans: not a lock; ES and the index differ by a basis, the model is a probability not a bound, and the trade can lose. Taker order-flow signals: not flow. Weather forecast against market: no forecast model, an exchange-priced reference. Cheap-side price, regime and grammar rules without a new data source (the Head's row 190): this card IS the new data source clause (an external futures return), and sweeps nothing. Favourite-and-hold (Charter ban): it buys the CHEAP side (5c to 30c) and holds, not a favourite; no exception is needed, and I state this for the Release Manager. Two-sided making, gold or XRP variants, GOLDHOLD: none apply.
- **Uniqueness (7d):** closest mechanisms: CFRTI-K (mine, Bitcoin perp index, development "not falsified"); OICORE (cheap-side open interest); EVENT830 (calendar). Different from all: the signal is an external continuously traded reference on an equity index, a different asset class and venue; correlation with any live strategy expected near zero (different market, different hours). Mechanism family: information lag on an external reference (new for live; CFRTI-K is the only relative and is not live).
- **Classification card (7f):** family = external-reference lag, cheap side, hold to settlement; risk class = small fixed contracts, loss bounded by the price paid; stage = Idea; proven bar = cash PF 1.3 or better over 100 fills in 2 of 3 windows; kills as above; owner popup required for any live use (the stage ladder has no auto-promotion).

## Dated lines, Auditor B's findings D1 to D8 (Nov 13, 2026, Desk A, on the Head's 16:43Z order; the body above is not rewritten; where a line here differs from the body, THIS governs)
- **D1, the sd factor (a STATED change from CFRTI-K).** KXINX15M settles on one candle close, not a 60 s average, so CFRTI-K's sqrt(4/3) is NOT kept. The horizon is the time from the end of the F_now bar (close - 120 s) to the settling second, which is the prior second to the close: **H = 119 s**. The 10 one-minute log changes give sd_1m; the registered sd for the model is **sd = sd_1m x sqrt(119 / 60)** (about 1.408 x sd_1m; the Head's sqrt(2) is the 120 s rounding of this and is not used). Model chance = Phi( ln(F_now / F_open) / sd ). Any other horizon or factor is a new try.
- **D2, uniqueness and the prior.** Nearest analogue: **GOLDFV1** (gold 15 minute against COMEX futures, all four tries FALSIFIED: "Kalshi gold already prices the latest minute").
