# CLINICAL2 protocol (registered Nov 11, 2026, BEFORE the Kalshi reads that start at 22:15Z; no Kalshi price, volume or outcome for the clinical family has been read)
Research only. Nothing live. Replaces the 14 day feasibility of CLINICAL1 (which found 0 of about 45 held markets settled within 14 days of open) with the owner's longer hold: up to 90 days to settlement, 1 contract per market, at most 3 open at once, inside the $200 politics and parlay lifetime budget (docs/POLITICS_RULES.md section 5), return measured per dollar-YEAR. Those are caps for a FUTURE spec; research places nothing. Declined list (docs/STRATEGY_LIFECYCLE.md 7e, origin/main, 11 rows, Nov 11): none is a clinical or health market; the favourite-and-hold ban (Charter item 5) applies to any live version with the favourite side above 0.70, so the cheap and mid bands below are 0.10 to 0.60 only. Uniqueness: signal is Kalshi's own price history against a base rate, a source no live strategy uses.
Inputs already held, no new host: CLINICAL1_CARDS.md, CLINICAL1_TERMS.md, PHASEBASE1_TABLES.md (Drugs@FDA aggregates: counts only, no denominator, no goal dates, no complete response letters, so NO approval rate and NO timing against a goal date can be computed from it). No outside host is read by this protocol; ClinicalTrials.gov stays AMBIGUOUS and unread; openFDA is not used.

## Rate and stop rule
Public GET on api.elections.kalshi.com only, inside the shared V2 bucket of 4 requests a minute across POLCOMBO1B, CLINICAL2 and MENTPERSIST1 (one process, 15 seconds apart, run one after another; recommended order: this census first, it is 20 reads). First 429 stops the run, writes what was read and the position, no retry loop, no workaround. Read log in `workers/CLINICAL2_READLOG.json`.

## Step 1: series list and census (at most 20 reads in total)
1. GET /series?category=Health and GET /series?category=Science%20and%20Technology (2 reads; if a category name returns an error that is reported and the read is not repeated with variants beyond one). Keep a series if its ticker or title contains FDA, DRUG, APPROV, PDUFA, TRIAL, VACCINE, CDC, EBOLA, MEASLES, H5N1, BIRDFLU, PANDEMIC, OUTBREAK, MEDIC, CANCER, HEALTH (case-insensitive), or the series is in the fixed list KXFDANOM, KXFDAAPPROVE, KXFDAANNOUNCE, KXNEWDRUGAPPNTLA, KXNEWDRUGAPPCMPS, KXNEWDRUGAPPBEAM, KXEBOLACOUNTRY, KXMEASLES, KXH5N1COUNT (and any other KXNEWDRUGAPP* the list reveals). AI-in-health, Nobel, and healthcare-cost policy series (KXAIHEALTH, KXNOBELMED, KXHEALTHCOST, KXHHSYOUTHCARE) are excluded by name: not clinical-data questions (CLINICAL1).
2. Per kept series in the fixed order above (listed series after the fixed list, by ticker), two reads: GET /markets?series_ticker=S&limit=1000 (open and settled since 2026-09-12; /markets alone does not return older settled markets) and GET /historical/markets?series_ticker=S&limit=1000 (settled before the historical cutoff), merged by market ticker, the live copy winning on a duplicate. Follow a cursor only if the budget allows. The fixed list is 9 series = 18 reads; with the 2 listing reads that is exactly 20, the CAP. Series revealed by the listing beyond the fixed nine are COUNTED and queued for a second run (named in the result), not read now; they extend the census only after this file gets a dated amendment.
3. Per series record (no price, no outcome used beyond the result field to count): markets listed, settled, open, with result yes and no counts (counts only, for the base-rate description in step 3), first and last open_time and close_time, median days from open to close, median volume_fp, and ONE verbatim `rules_primary` and `rules_secondary` of a market of each distinct strike type. The unit is a market. N counted: 1.

## Step 2: classify each series from its rule text (before any price)
By fixed keyword rule on `rules_primary`, written into `workers/CLINICAL2_CLASSES.json` before step 4 and hashed: 
- TYPE A, approval by a date: text contains approv* and a close/expiry date (FDAAPPROVE, NEWDRUGAPP*). The comparison for PDUFAGOAL1 is defined as: the market asks "approved on or before date D" for a named product; the goal date is not in Drugs@FDA, so the only goal-date info is the market's own close date, and PDUFAGOAL1 is run ONLY as price versus the series' own earlier settled YES rate (rule A below); no literature approval rate (the 70 to 85 percent in CLINICAL1_CARDS is a literature figure, unverified) is used as a trading base.
- TYPE M, a monthly or period count of novel approvals: text contains novel/new drug approvals and a count threshold. This is the only type PHASEBASE1 informs: Poisson with mean 4.12 per month (2020 to 2025 distribution; 2023 to 2025 4.28 as a sensitivity), dispersion near 1. If no such series exists, rule B is NOT RUN and reported as no market.
- TYPE C, case counts (Ebola, measles, H5N1): compared with CDC/WHO data in CLINICAL1 card HEALTHCOUNT1 (terms not reviewed), NOT run here beyond the census and the calibration rule C.
- TYPE N, a nominee or an announcement (KXFDANOM, KXFDAANNOUNCE): not a data question; census and calibration rule C only.
- Anything else: reported as UNCLASSIFIED, census only.
PHASEBASE1 therefore enters one comparison only (TYPE M); the tables hold counts only and say nothing about a named product. Stated plainly in every result line.

## Step 3: the 90 day price history (candles, at most 200 reads)
Universe for the price step: markets of the nine fixed series that are settled, with a result yes or no, and for which the series is TYPE A, M, C or N. Entry look = lead L before the close: L = 30 days (primary and only traded lead), with 14, 60 and 90 days reported as descriptive availability only (not tried as rules). One read per market: candlesticks for the 90 days before the close, period 1440 (daily), for a historical-tier market GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks, for a live-tier market GET /series/{series}/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks, with start_ts = close_time minus 90 days and end_ts = close_time. If the number of such markets exceeds 200, take 200 by smallest md5(ticker) and say so. The decision quote at lead L is the last daily candle ending at or before close minus L, with both a bid and an ask, spread <= 0.10 (daily markets here are thin); a market that was not open at close minus L, or has no two-sided quote, is SKIPPED and the skips counted per series and lead. No price is looked at before the rules below are fixed.
Count to report (the plausibility count for n): markets per lead with a usable quote, in total and per series, per event (event_ticker), and the listed rate of new settled markets per month over the last 12 months (from close_time).

## Step 4: pre-registered rules (the first cheapest test, only if n can reach 100)
Gate before any rule is scored: the number of distinct EVENTS with a usable quote at L = 30 days, in the pooled TYPE A, M, C, N markets, is at least 100. If it is below 100 the rules are NOT scored (no outcome read), the verdict is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, and the result prints the count, the monthly rate r and the date it would end: today plus (100 minus count) divided by r months, using r = the median monthly count of new settled usable-quote events over the last 12 months (if r is under 1 per month the date is stated as "beyond 2028" ). With about 45 held settled markets in about 12 series the expected outcome is exactly this.
If the gate passes (unit = one market, 1 contract, hold to settlement, fee = ceil(0.07 p (1-p) to the cent), the rounded figure used for the gate):
- Rule A (own history): buy the side whose ask at lead L is between 0.10 and 0.60 when that ask is at most (the series' YES rate over its EARLIER settled markets, or 1 minus it for NO) minus 0.10, requiring at least 8 earlier settled markets in the series. TYPE A only.
- Rule B (count base rate), only if a TYPE M series exists: the probability of the threshold from a Poisson with mean 4.12 per month versus the quote; buy the side whose ask (band 0.10 to 0.60) is at most the Poisson probability of that side minus 0.10.
- Rule C (calibration, all types): buy the cheap side (ask 0.10 to 0.35, either side) at lead L; report the cell and its price bins; descriptive plus a rule.
N added: 3 rules + 1 census = 4; shared N about 1,100,000 + 4; z bar 5.34 applied (the small-count bar 4.39 at N 9,009 reported beside it).
Gates, all required: cash PF >= 1.5 with n >= 100 markets in >= 50 events and no single event over 30% of net; DEVELOPMENT = the earlier two thirds of markets by close date, HELD-OUT = the last third, read ONCE and only for a rule that passes development with PF >= 1.5 and mean >= 3c; both halves of development PF >= 1.2; cluster-robust z (cluster = event_ticker) above 5.34 on the held-out; return per dollar-year = (net cash per dollar staked) divided by (L in years = 30 / 365.25), reported always, and the 0.10 per dollar bar is not met by annualising a thin edge. Also reported: max simultaneous open positions on the date axis (the owner's cap is 3; a rule needing more than 3 open at once cannot be run live and is flagged).
Verdicts: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (with count and date), FALSIFIED, PASS-TO-SHADOW (owner decision, no live change). No promote verb. Kill: the card is closed if the gate count is under 100 AND r is under 2 per month (it would need over 4 years), or if a scored rule has development PF under 1.0 with n >= 60 (stop, no held-out read). RETURN: a new registration with a new source or a new series family, or a count of 100 events reached.
Prior: a pass under 2%. Prediction written now: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (about 45 held settled markets; one market per event; 12 series; irregular).

## Read budget at 4 a minute
Step 1: exactly 20 reads (5 minutes). Step 3: at most 200 candle reads (50 minutes); run only if the census shows at least 30 settled TYPE A/M/C/N markets, otherwise the candle step is cut to the count found (typically 45 to 80 reads, 11 to 20 minutes). Total at most 220 reads = 55 minutes, typical about 70 reads = 18 minutes.
