**RESIL1: TEST, with one design change.** Taker rule: after a sweep, follow it only if the swept side is NOT refilled; hold to settlement; 1 contract, no resting order. Data: R6 books (current, 2 s) and the R6 tape (exchange time on every trade), both current sources. Events: five 15 minute series from Oct 29 to Nov 13 (Bitcoin and Ethereum to Nov 20), thousands of windows; the count is taken after registration. **Design change, registered with it:** the card's refill field was a displayed SIZE restored at the swept price, and Forensics and the Verification Analyst find that book sizes change every 2 seconds at every level and are not depth truth. So the registered field is PRICE-based (does the touch price return to its pre-sweep level within 6 seconds), with the sweep taken from the tape (one taker side at two or more prices in one whole second, as DEPTH1's f7). The family flag stands: order flow already has FLOW1 and TAPEFLOW1 FALSIFIED; RESIL1 FALSIFIED would be the third and close the family, and a verdict that closes a family is still a verdict. Registration: `RESIL1_REGISTRATION.md`.
