# VOLFAIR1 registration (Desk A, Nov 16, 2026). Written before any data is read and before any code. Hand case first.

**Family card:** VOLFAIR1 | model fair value (a volatility model from the underlying's own recent 1 minute returns; any side whose price is far from the model) + hold to settlement | one contract at a time, hourly Bitcoin ladder | stage Idea.

**Question:** 15 minutes before an hourly Bitcoin close, is an hourly "above K" strike priced away from the probability a plain lognormal model gives from spot, the strike and the last hour's realized volatility, by more than fee plus haircut plus a margin?

**Data, and how the strike file is built (condition b).** KXBTCD books from the QUOTE2B book recorder (`quote-data`, `book/`, the 7 strikes nearest the money chosen by the recorder at about 55 minutes before the close, so BEFORE any entry here), read at the 15 minute mark only; spot from the recorder's public Kraken 1 minute closes (scratch copy `kraken_minute.csv`, Oct 22 onward), only candles ENDING at or before the entry minute. No strike is added or dropped using any quote after the entry minute, no price filter is applied to build the file, and the list of strikes for a close is fixed by the recorder row set at entry time. A close with a missing spot minute in its window, or fewer than the 7 strikes, is skipped and counted. Day-ahead holds: HOLDS H02 and H03 refuse KXBTCD closes from 2026-11-03T16:00Z (H03 from 2026-11-07T15:00Z, MMSIM1 owns it), so the DEVELOPMENT window is closes from 2026-10-29 to before 2026-11-03T16:00Z only (about 5 days, about 120 closes); this is thin and the result will probably be NOT_ENOUGH_DATA. A read after the holds open is a separate, later step with its own line.

**Definitions (frozen).**
- Entry minute t = close minus 15 minutes (the candle ending exactly then). Spot S = last Kraken close ending at or before t.
- Realized volatility per minute s = standard deviation of the last W 1 minute log returns of spot ending at t (population standard deviation, zero mean). W in {30, 60, 120}.
- tau = 15 minutes. P_yes = Phi( ln(S/K) / (s * sqrt(15)) ) with Phi the normal CDF (no drift, no jump term, no smile).
- Side bought: YES if P_yes - ask_yes - fee(ask_yes) >= M, NO if (1 - P_yes) - ask_no - fee(ask_no) >= M, where ask_no = 1 - bid_yes and fee(p) = ceil(round(0.07 * p * (1-p) * 100, 9)) / 100 per contract at 10 contracts rounded as the research does (per-order rounded). M in {0.03, 0.05, 0.08}. At most one entry per close (the largest net edge).
- **Price band, to stay unique:** the ask paid must be 0.10 to 0.90 inclusive (so it is neither the favourite-late 0.88 to 0.97 band's core nor G3H1's cheap tail at 0.10 or less; 0.88 to 0.90 overlap is accepted and counted).
- Entry at the ask of the chosen side with a 2c haircut (1c shown for information); held to settlement; two-sided quote required; cost as everywhere (per-order rounded fee). Result from the settled market.
- Tries: 3 windows x 3 margins = **9**, all counted in the deflation (N tried so far is carried from the ENGINE tally plus these 9, none dropped after seeing a result).

**Prediction (numeric).** At least one of the 9 cells has 2c PF 1.3 or more with n at least 100 on development (needs about 100 entries, which about 120 closes will probably NOT yield: most closes show no model edge above M). **Kill:** the best cell's 2c PF under 1.0, or n under 100 in every cell (then NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, not a pass; no second look).

**Uniqueness (7e and 7d).** Compared with G3H1 and G3H2 (cheap side, ask 10c or less, the side's bid rising over 2 minutes, 51 to 46 minutes before the close, exit +3c; falsified by CORR1 on the look-ahead free file), LADL1 (L1's favourite rule at 10 and 6 minutes on the hourly ladders; falsified), CORR1, GEN3, E4 and GEN3-F1: none of them uses an outside price and a model. VOLFAIR1's signal is the distance between the strike and spot in volatility units (an external information source: spot), its entry is both sides across the whole 10c to 90c range 15 minutes out, its exit is settlement. It is not a price band, not a momentum rule and not an open interest rule. Closest family risk: the declined favourite-and-hold ban (check 5) applies only to the favourite side at the top of the band; this rule buys the model's side whatever the side, so it is not a favourite rule, but a pass would still need the Head's popup before any live use. Correlation with live strategies is NOT checked here (condition 7d check 3): to be computed on the development entries against gold L1 results before any shadow slot.

## Hand case (invented numbers, no data)
Spot S = 100,000; W = 60 returns with per-minute standard deviation s = 0.0002; tau = 15 minutes so s sqrt(15) = 0.00077460. Strike K = 100,050: ln(S/K) = -0.00049988; d = -0.64533; P_yes = Phi(-0.64533) = 0.25939 (Phi(-0.65) = 0.25785, density 0.3230 at 0.65, so +0.00467 x 0.3230 = +0.00151, giving 0.25936; tolerance 1e-3). YES ask 0.18: fee(0.18) with the 10 contract per-order rule = ceil(round(0.07 x 10 x 0.18 x 0.82 x 100, 9)) / 10 / 100 per contract = ceil(10.332)/10/100 = 0.0110 per contract. Net edge = 0.2594 - 0.18 - 0.011 = 0.0684 >= M = 0.05: BUY YES (the 2c haircut then pays 0.20). A second strike K = 99,900 in the same close: ln(S/K) = +0.0010005; d = +1.2917; P_yes = 0.9017; YES ask 0.93 is outside the band and NO ask 0.08 is outside it too: no entry.
