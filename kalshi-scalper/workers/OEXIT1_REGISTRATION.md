# OEXIT1 registration: does selling half at 3 times the entry price change OICORE's payoff and streak risk (2026-11-03, written and pushed BEFORE any outcome was computed)

## Disclosure: what was read before this commit
README sections SLOTEX, GEN6, REF_GEN6_OICORE; `OICORE_SHAPE_INVENTORY.md` (e); `gen6.py`, `oicorex1.py` (strat, pack, window_list), `candlefield.py`, `streaks.py`, `XOI1_REGISTRATION.md`. One structural look at the candle FILES, no result, bid-after-entry or PnL computed: the candles of one Bitcoin window (`data/l1b.sqlite`) and the date range of the files (`fresh.sqlite` runs to 2026-11-03 11:15 UTC). **That look changes the question and is stated here plainly:** OICORE looks at the candle ending 180 s before the close and fills at the NEXT candle's ask (ending 120 s). The only candles after the fill are the one ending 60 s before the close and the one ending AT the close (its bid is already a settled quote, about 0.99+ for the winning side). There is no "mid window" after the fill: the position lives for about two minutes. So a 3x test can first show at the candle ending 60 s, and the sale (next candle) is at the bid close of the candle ending at the close.

## The two rules (C = 10 contracts)
- **(a) WITHOUT:** the OICORE entry exactly (`strat(86299.56000000001)` through `oicorex1.window_list` + `gen6.window_rows`/`gen6.trades`: doi = oi(-180) - oi(-360) > 86,299.56, YES checked first, ask 5c to 50c at the -180 candle, fill at the next candle's (-120) ask, one entry per window), held to settlement. Net = win payout (1 per contract) minus price paid minus entry fee.
- **(b) WITH:** same entry. At each candle AFTER the fill candle (ending -60, then 0), if its BID close >= 3.0 x the entry price paid, the first such candle K "showed 3x"; the order to sell HALF the contracts (C/2 = 5) is placed at the bid close of the candle AFTER K (no look-ahead: the quote that showed it is not the price sold at). If K is the last candle (ending 0) there is no later candle and no sale. If the later candle has no bid (bid_c <= 0) or is missing, no sale. Sell once only; the other half is held to settlement. **Fee:** 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent on EACH leg: the entry on all 10 contracts at the price paid, the sale on 5 contracts at the sale price (settlement carries no fee, as in gen6). Net dollars = 5 x (sale - entry) + 5 x (settle - entry) - entry fee - sale fee, where settle = 1 for a win and 0 for a loss.
- **(c) Variants counted as separate tries:** the same with 2.0x and 4.0x. N tried +3 (2x, 3x, 4x); running count about 728,730 (z bar 5.27, 0.05/N). 3.0x is the headline.
- Live list (`workers/OICORE_LIVE_ORDERS.csv`, 24 orders, 18 filled): window found from the close time (UTC) on `KXBTC15M`; the price path from the candles; the SAME rule at the price actually PAID, with the filled count (half = floor(filled / 2), the sale fee on that half). The filled orders' recorded PnL is shown beside, for reference only. "None" orders are excluded. n = 18 is far too small to decide anything.

## Data and windows
Local candle sqlite files (`l1b, book, fresh, five`), settled Bitcoin 15 minute windows with a usable open interest pair where the OICORE rule fires, close times 2026-08-06 to the end of the data. Dates with at least one fired trade are sorted and split into **three equal thirds by number of dates**: the first two thirds are the HISTORY, split into two halves (first half of the history dates = H1, second = H2); the **last third is the holdout, read once** after this commit and reported separately. No tuning on any of it.

## NUMERIC PREDICTION (written before scoring)
Because only two candles follow the fill, I expect the exit to be almost a no-op. A winner that shows 3x at -60 is already near 0.99 and is sold at about 0.999 at the close candle, which equals the settlement value minus a one cent fee on the sale (rounded up). So WITH loses about 1 cent per triggered win versus WITHOUT and rarely converts a loss: it needs the bid at -60 to be 3x entry and the market to flip to a loss within one minute. **PF with minus without: -0.03 to 0.00 at 3x (central -0.01); at 2x the same, slightly more triggers (-0.04 to 0.00); at 4x -0.02 to 0.00. Win rate: unchanged to the point (a trade wins if net > 0 after fees; at most 1 in 200 trades changes class). Longest losing run: unchanged (central), 15% chance it is one shorter and 3% chance longer.** Streak risk is therefore not changed by this exit. What is different from SLOTEX (where hold beat a 60 second exit): SLOTEX sold the whole position at the bid at 60 s after a signal-time entry, giving away the final minute of the move to settlement; here half is kept to settlement and the sale only fires after the price already tripled, so it differs from hold only by fees and the rare late reversal. The idea in the question (cutting the streak by locking profits that fade mid-window) cannot be tested on this data because the position has no mid window.

## DECISION RULE (fixed now, report only)
**SUPPORTS** only if profit factor WITH is higher than WITHOUT in EACH of the two history halves (H1 and H2) AND the longest losing run WITH is shorter than WITHOUT (full history sequence, ordered by close time). Otherwise **DOES NOT SUPPORT**. Same test for 2x and 4x, reported as separate tries. The holdout is reported separately and cannot rescue or sink the verdict. Streak numbers from `scalper.streaks`: empirical longest losing run and the chance of a 10-loss run in 100 trades at the observed win rate, with and without. Any change after this commit is disclosed in the result.
