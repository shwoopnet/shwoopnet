## Counterparty and mechanism
The market makers and retail takers quoting an alt's 15 minute market from its own flow, while Bitcoin's leveraged positioning (new open interest in the Bitcoin 15 minute market) has already jumped. The cheaper side's buyer wins when Bitcoin's move drags the alt across its strike before the quote is repriced. Honest limits: (1) the Bitcoin core's own counterparty is still unnamed (MECH1 did not support "late favourite takers"); (2) LAG15 found these alts' quotes already track spot at one minute resolution (model Brier 0.0764 vs quote mid 0.0287); (3) open interest in a Kalshi Bitcoin market is Kalshi contracts, not perpetual futures positioning, so "leveraged positioning" is the hypothesis, not a measurement. Even a pass would name no counterparty, which keeps it a capped probe.

## Universe (fixed on liquidity and cost only, before any outcome)
- Series: KXHYPE15M, KXNEAR15M, KXZEC15M, KXBNB15M, KXXRP15M. All five have a median quoted spread of 0.007 to 0.010 at the 180 s candle (`OICOREX1_INVENTORY.md`), so the cost rule passes for all of them. **Pooled as one alt group.**
- **Minimum quoted-volume rule (chosen now):** a window is eligible only if the alt traded at least **10 contracts in the 5 one minute candles ending at the look** (candles ending 180, 240, 300, 360, 420 s before the close), a two sided quote exists at the look (0 < bid < ask < 1) and a quote exists at the fill candle. This uses only quotes and volume known by the look (the fill candle only decides whether a fill exists).
- NOT traded (hard non-overlap): **Bitcoin** (OICORE trades it), **Ethereum, Dogecoin, gold** (L1 buys the favourite side there at 88c to 97c; a cheap-side trade in the same market and window would hedge L1). Also absent: SOL (history ends Oct 22), silver, WTI and others (not "Bitcoin leads" crypto; OICOREX1 covered them).
- XRP: live L1 pauses it (PAUSED_SERIES), but its position could still be open if un-paused; in this research it is included as asked and the non-overlap is reported by flag: a cheap-side XRP trade would be the opposite side of an L1 favourite if L1 resumed XRP. Reported separately in the per-alt table so it can be dropped without a rerun.

## The trigger (exactly OIREL, no threshold chosen by me)
On the BITCOIN 15 minute market of the same window: level = open interest at the candle ending 360 s before the close; doi = open interest at the candle ending 180 s before the close minus level; **fire when doi / level > 0.26764675317954373** (`REF_GEN6_OIREL.json` `rthr`, `src/scalper/oirel.py RTHR`; the BTC discovery quantile that matches the core's count of windows above 86,300; the definition is clear in `oirel.py`). Missing candle, missing oi or oi of 0 at either candle: the window is not eligible (no trade, not a "no jump"). Difference from OIREL, stated: OIREL also requires a Bitcoin side priced 5c to 50c at the signal candle because it trades Bitcoin; this trigger does NOT (we do not trade Bitcoin), so it fires slightly more often than OIREL.

## The trade
For each alt in the universe in a window where the trigger fired: at the candle ending **180 s before the close** (the look), the cheaper side = YES if its ask is 0.05 to 0.50, else NO if its ask (1 - yes bid) is 0.05 to 0.50, else no trade (the same YES first order as OIREL). It is simply the side priced below 50c at the look. **Entry price = that side's ask on the next recorded candle (ending 120 s before the close)**, the same convention as OICORE/OIREL (the signal candle's own close is the quote at the look; the first fill available after it is one minute later; no earlier price is used, so no look-ahead; this is the "first recorded ask after the look minute" and a fill one minute later is the sensitivity that cut the Bitcoin core's discovery PF from 2.28 to 0.94, so cost of lateness is real and stated). C = 10 contracts, taker at the ask, held to settlement (no sell fee). **Fee: 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order** (`gen6.fee`). One order per alt per window; up to five orders in one window (correlated, handled by day-clustering and reported).
- Direction report (asked): for each trade, the sign of Bitcoin's move in the signal window is the change in Bitcoin's YES mid from the candle ending 360 s to the candle ending 180 s before the close (up = positive). The cheaper side AGREES if it is YES and Bitcoin moved up, or NO and Bitcoin moved down; flat = no change. Reported: share agree / disagree / flat, win rate and PF of each (information, not a rule; the trade does not condition on it).
- OICORE overlap report (asked): share of XOI1 trade windows (distinct close times) in which OICORE's own Bitcoin rule also fires (fixed 86,299.56 doi, Bitcoin cheap side 5c to 50c, via `oicorex1.stage_score`, parity-checked against GEN6's 222 / 2.28 and 333 / 1.85), plus the Pearson correlation of daily net dollars of the two research rules (XOI1 pooled, OICORE Bitcoin) across the dates of the sample, on all dates (zero when a rule did not trade) and on dates both traded. **They share a trigger, so they are correlated by construction; I expect a visibly positive correlation, not independence.**

## Windows, thirds, holdout
Eligible dates (a date with at least one eligible alt window, i.e. Bitcoin oi pair, alt side and the volume rule, regardless of trigger or outcome) are sorted and split into **three equal thirds by number of dates**: 89 dates, W1 2026-08-06 to 2026-09-03, W2 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-02, W3 2026-10-03 to 2026-11-02 (counts printed by `counts`). **W3 is the frozen holdout, untouched until the rule is frozen (this file), and is scored only if W1 and W2 both pass.** No second look; no variant is run. Caveat: the OIREL threshold was fitted on Bitcoin discovery (Aug 6 to Sep 20), which overlaps W1 and part of W2 for the TRIGGER, not for the alts' outcomes; stated, not tuned.

## NUMERIC PREDICTION (written before scoring)
Pooled over all trades of the alt group: **win rate 21% to 27% (central 24%)**, average fill 22c to 30c, **payoff ratio (average win in cents / average loss) 2.6 to 3.6 (central 3.0)**, **profit factor 0.80 to 1.15 pooled (central 0.95)**, in each of the three windows 0.6 to 1.4, mean per contract -2.5c to +1.0c. Trades: about 3,000 to 4,700 over the history. Probability that PF is 1.5 or better in each of W1 and W2: **3%**. Probability pooled z reaches 5.27: under 0.5%. Probability the cheaper side agrees with Bitcoin's move in more than 50% of trades: 55% (it is partly mechanical: a cheap NO is likelier when the whole market has moved down). The prior is low because the Bitcoin core did not transfer to any of 8 markets on their own open interest and LAG15 found the quotes tracking spot; the one reason for a different answer is that the trigger is Bitcoin's information rather than the alt's own.

## KILL RULES (fixed now, no tuning)
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA**: fewer than 150 trades pooled in the windows scored.
- **FALSIFIED**: PF (C = 10, after fees) under 1.5 in W1 or W2, or (if read) in W3; or PF at fees x1.2 at or under 1.0 in any scored window.
- **NOT_YET_FALSIFIED**: PF at least 1.5 in each of W1, W2 and W3 and x1.2 PF above 1.0. History alone can earn no more. **A pooled day-clustered z below the deflated bar 5.27 (N about 728,704) is reported as NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, never a pass.** No named counterparty, so no promotion path exists in any case.
- Headline is Try A (relative trigger, alt group pooled). **Try B (information only, no verdict):** the same trade with Bitcoin's fixed OICORE trigger doi > 86,299.56 (so OICORE fires in all its windows by construction). Per-alt tables are reports of Try A, not tries.

## STREAK REPORT (computed after scoring with `src/scalper/streaks.py` at the OBSERVED pooled win rate)
Empirical longest losing run in the realised sequence (ordered by close time then ticker); the 95th and 99th percentile of the longest losing run in 100 trades and the chance of at least one 10-loss run in 100 trades (exact, independence assumed; real losses cluster across the five alts in one window, so these are a floor).
