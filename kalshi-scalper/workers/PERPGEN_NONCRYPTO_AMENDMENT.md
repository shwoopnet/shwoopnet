# PERPGEN NONCRYPTO slice: disclosures and interpretations (written 2026-11-06 BEFORE any rule of this slice was scored)

No bar, grammar item or cut of `PERPGEN_PROTOCOL.md` is changed. This file records one miscount in the protocol and the places where the protocol left a choice open, so nobody has to guess later. Code: `src/scalper/perpgen_noncrypto.py` (slice NONCRYPTO: KXCOPPERPERP, KXGOLDPERP, KXSILVERPERP, KXUS500PERP, KXUSTECH100PERP, KXWTIFUTURE).

## Defect found: the feature list has 34 numeric features, the text says 33
Counted from the names listed in the protocol: returns 5 (r5 r15 r60 r240 r1440), range and volatility 5 (vol60 vol240 volratio rng60 rng240), spread 2 (sp spr), flow 5 (vlr15 vlr60 doi60 doi240 tvm), funding 3, distance 5 (dhi240 dlo240 dhi1440 dlo1440 pir1440), cross-asset 7 leads plus 2 relative strengths = 9. Total **34**, not 33; "330 numeric atoms" should read 340 (34 x 2 operators x 5 levels), and with the 19 categorical atoms the grammar has **359 atoms**, not 349. This slice uses every listed feature (nothing is dropped, nothing is added). N is unaffected (the ledger counts rules, not atoms). Other generator workers may have resolved this differently; their ledgers are their own.

## Interpretations (protocol silent or ambiguous; each is fixed here before any score)
- **Atom sampling:** uniform over the 359 atoms, rejecting an atom whose feature is already in the rule. Direction and horizon uniform. Atom count 1, 2, 3 with probabilities 0.2, 0.4, 0.4. Canonical string: atoms sorted by feature name, then `|long|` or `|short|`, then the horizon in minutes. Dedupe is against earlier NONCRYPTO ledgers only (a (rule, group) pair counts once per group).
- **Windows:** a window of w minutes ends at the decision bar t and holds the w bars (t-w, t]; "at least 90 percent present" means at least 0.9 w usable quotes (for returns both end bars must also be usable; for volume and open-interest windows, present bars; for 1,440 minute windows the same 90 percent rule applies, so the 1,440 minute features are often missing on thin markets such as US 500 and Tech 100 and the atom is then false).
- **Zero volume:** a bar the exchange served with no trade counts as zero notional; vlr is missing if either sum is zero. tvm uses the last non-missing trade close among the 5 bars ending at t.
- **Funding features:** the last row with `funding_time <= t`. mtf (minutes to next funding) for these markets is computed causally as (last settlement + 24 hours) minus t, in buckets under 30, 30 to 120, 120 to 240, 240 and over; missing if no settlement yet or the computed time is already past. (It is off by one hour on the daylight-clock change day; accepted.)
- **hod4, dow, wkend:** from the decision time t in UTC; dow Monday 0.
- **Cuts:** `np.quantile` (linear) of the ticker's own DISCOVERY decision-time feature values (rows where the feature is present); fewer than 20 values: no cut, the atom is false for that ticker. Stored in `workers/PERPGEN_NONCRYPTO_QCUTS.json` with sha256.
- **Cross-asset leads** read the leader's dev rows (its discovery or validation region), per the protocol. Pairs not evaluable stay missing (BTC and ETH leads exist for gold and silver only on 2026-09-10 to 09-14; none for the other four).
- **Position blocking:** a signal at decision time t is ignored while the previous trade of that ticker is open (t below the previous exit bar's time, or the nominal exit time if the exit was void). A trade skipped for a missing or defective ENTRY quote does not occupy the ticker. A trade dropped because its exit lies outside the stage's region is dropped and counted.
- **Defect rule** is applied to the entry fill quote (spread over 10 times the ticker's discovery median or over 100 bps): the exit is never screened, because removing wide exits would remove the worst fills and flatter the result.
- **Halves** ("split at the median trade date"): sort trades by entry time, take the date of the middle trade, first half = trades on or before that date, second half = later dates; either half empty fails.
- **Date concentration** (25 percent): the largest single UTC entry date's summed net over the total net.
- **Day-clustered z** uses the per-date mean of per-trade nets.
- **PF with no loser** is capped at 99.
- **Quick rejection:** a rule with fewer than 200 discovery signal rows cannot make 200 trades, so it is recorded as failing the trade-count bar without running the sequencing loop (same verdict, faster; it is still in the ledger and in N).
- **Frozen id** of a run is `NC<nn>`; discovery reads only discovery rows of the six tickers (`load_stage`), validation rows are read only through `load_stage(..., "validation", frozen_id)` after `workers/PERPGEN_FROZEN_NC<nn>.json` is committed and pushed.

## Date starvation, stated up front (counts from the cleaned data, no return computed)
Discovery decision rows (quarter hours with a usable quote): copper 680, gold 1,732, silver 1,674, US 500 690, Tech 100 412, WTI 398 (5,586 in total, about 34 distinct UTC dates pooled). The 200 trade bar needs a rule to fire on at least 200 of those rows, and a 24 hour hold allows at most one trade a day per ticker (about 72 trades over the whole discovery set), an 8 hour hold about 216: **the 24 hour horizon can never reach the 200 trade bar and the 8 hour horizon barely can.** Those rules are still drawn (the grammar is not changed) and still counted in N; they are reported as date-starved, not as survivors.
