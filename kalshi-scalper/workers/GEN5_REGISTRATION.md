# GEN5 registration: a 1 minute strategy generator with OUTSIDE reference prices, gold and Bitcoin 15 minute markets (Strategy Research V2, 2026-10-29; written and pushed before any outside bar is compared with any Kalshi quote or outcome)

**Ledger check.** GEN1 (12,056 generated, 5,000 distinct, none passed discovery) already had, for Bitcoin, Deribit spot distance to strike in sigma units and a 3 minute spot return; GEN2 added series, market-wide moves, reference prices, trailing and scale exits. GOLDFV1 (one fair-value rule on GC=F, 4 tries) and XMAC1 (macro 2 sigma shocks, 4 tries) are single rules. NEW here: outside-asset returns over 1, 3, 5 and 15 minutes, distance from the window's open in units of the asset's own volatility, realised volatility and its 15/60 ratio, the model edge (normal fair value minus the side's ask), and for gold the dollar index and 10 year yield moves, all as conditions inside GEN1's grammar and exits. Every GEN5 strategy must use at least one outside feature, so none coincides with a GEN1 or GEN2 strategy. Not a rewording of a tested rule, but heavily overlapping in spirit with GOLDFV1 and XMAC1, which found the outside price and the Kalshi quote move in the SAME minute; so the grammar is built for states and regimes (volatility, trend over 15 minutes, distance from open), not lags.

**Disclosed before registering.** Only market counts and close ranges of the Kalshi files were read (`l1b`, `book`, `fresh`, `five`: Bitcoin 2026-08-06 to 10-29 20:45 UTC, gold the same), plus the shape of `data/gc1m.json` and two test rows of each outside feed. No price change, quote path or outcome was looked at.

**Counterparty.** Kalshi gold and Bitcoin quoters who price the 15 minute contract from the asset's current distance to the strike but under-react to the STATE of the outside market: a high or low volatility regime, a 15 minute trend, or a dollar and yield move against gold; a taker who conditions on those states is paid by them. Honest alternative: they quote from the same feeds and price these states correctly (GOLDFV1, XMAC1, IV1), and any pass is luck at this N.

**Data (public, keyless).** Gold: Yahoo 1 minute GC=F (COMEX gold future), DX-Y.NYB (dollar index), ^TNX (10 year yield), refreshed today, Sep 29 to Oct 29. Bitcoin: Coinbase BTC-USD 1 minute candles (`/products/BTC-USD/candles?granularity=60`, paginated back to 2026-08-06). **ES=F is NOT used for Bitcoin**: Yahoo 1 minute history only reaches about Sep 30, so it would be missing from all of Bitcoin's discovery (Aug 6 to Sep 30); dropped now, before any result. Kalshi 1 minute candles (closing bid and ask, volume) from `l1b`, `book`, `fresh`, `five` (a ticker in two files taken once).

**Look-ahead rule.** Yahoo and Coinbase stamp a bar with its START. At a decision at the end of Kalshi candle t (the candle covering t-60 to t), only outside bars with start at or before t - 120 are read (they ENDED at or before t - 60, before the signal candle began). Per asset a minute price series is forward-filled at most 5 minutes; a feature needing a missing price is missing, and a condition on a missing feature is false (no trade). Roll and bad-print guard: if any 1 minute change in the 60 minutes used exceeds 0.5% (gold, DXY), 0.05 points (TNX) or 3% (Bitcoin), that asset's features are missing.

**Features (signed toward the side: multiplied by +1 for YES, -1 for NO, where YES = price up from the window's open).** Let s = t - 120 and P(x) the outside price at minute x. o_r1, o_r3, o_r5, o_r15 = ln P(s) - ln P(s - 60k) for k = 1, 3, 5, 15. o_vol = standard deviation of the 60 one minute log changes ending at s (at least 45 real bars), unsigned. o_volr = the same over 15 minutes divided by o_vol, unsigned. o_dz = (ln P(s) - ln P(O - 60)) / (o_vol x sqrt(minutes from s to the close)), O = window open (the bar starting O - 60 ends at the open, the minute the strike covers; the basis cancels). o_fv = Phi(o_dz) minus the side's ask. Gold only: dx_r5, dx_r15 (DXY log returns), tnx_r5, tnx_r15 (TNX level changes). Kalshi: ask, spread, d1 and d3 (side bid change over 1 and 3 minutes), rng5, hour of day. Total 15 outside or Kalshi features for gold, 11 for Bitcoin.

**Grammar (GEN1's, per market, gold and Bitcoin separately).** ENTRY: a seconds-left range [L_hi, L_lo] (840 to 180 on a 60 s grid, width 60 to 600 s), side YES, NO or both, a conjunction of 1 to 4 conditions (feature <= or > its discovery decile 10th to 90th), at least one condition on an o_, dx_ or tnx_ feature. First matching minute in a window enters; at most one entry per window. Ask between 3c and 85c (GEN1's). EXIT: GEN1's 216 exits (take profit +3, +5, +8, +12, +20c or none, x stop -5, -10, -20c or none, x time exit at 60, 120, 240 s left, x book none, spread at least 3c, YES mid crossing 50c against), all before settlement, at the closing bid.

**Fills.** PRIMARY (gates): the NEXT candle's closing ask (the candle ending t + 60; skipped if it has no quote), exits scanned from the candle after it. Also reported: the signal candle's closing ask (ending t). Zero-volume: a strategy with more than 20% of entries or exits on a zero-volume candle is rejected; share reported.

**Fees.** 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up to the cent per order, entry and exit; gates at C = 10, C = 1 reported; fees x1.2 stress.

**Dates, fixed now (by close time, UTC).** Gold: DISCOVERY 2026-10-01 to 10-18, VALIDATION 10-19 to 10-29 (outside data only from Sep 30). Bitcoin: DISCOVERY 2026-08-06 to 09-30, VALIDATION 10-01 to 10-29. FORWARD: every window closing after 2026-10-30 00:00 UTC, automatic, through the candidate file. Honest note: gold's discovery days are the days GOLDFV1 and XMAC1 scored (their later third was left unread); Bitcoin's validation days are GEN1's and GEN2's validation days (they mined them; Oct 28 and 29 were GEN1's holdout, with nothing frozen). Only FORWARD is untouched by anyone.

**Generation, dedupe.** Random draws until 5,000 DISTINCT per market (GEN1's dedupe: Jaccard of discovery trade sets above 0.5 with exits within one step = same; at least 30 discovery trades to count), at most 300,000 draws per market. Every draw counts in N.

**Screen (fixed now).** DISCOVERY: PF at least 1.5 at C = 10, next-candle fill, Bitcoin at least 100 trades on 10 days, gold at least 60 trades on 8 days, zero-volume share at most 20%. VALIDATION (read once, frozen survivors only, sha256 of the survivor file committed first): PF at least 1.3 at C = 10, next-candle fill, at least 40 trades, stressed-fee PF above 1.0, zero-volume share at most 20%. Day-clustered z against the Bonferroni bar for N (about 439,000 + GEN5's draws, z about 5.2) is reported; it is NOT a GEN5 gate because the forward read decides. A validation pass earns only a FORWARD candidate (`workers/candidates/`, evaluator `scalper.gen5:evaluate`), whose bar is the project's: PF at least 1.5 after fees at C = 10 on at least 40 forward trades and 8 days, positive at fees x1.2, deflated over N. Verdicts only FALSIFIED, NOT_YET_FALSIFIED, NOT_ENOUGH_DATA.

**Prediction.** Bitcoin: zero pass discovery (GEN1 with spot features topped out at 1.25). Gold: a handful to a few dozen pass the looser discovery screen by luck (60 trades), zero to 3 pass validation at PF 1.3, none would survive deflation. Most likely: NO_WINNER, FALSIFIED.

**Count.** Every generated draw on both markets, reported exactly in the result. Running count before GEN5 about 439,000.
