# RAINFACT1 Amendment 1 (2026-11-05, written after the first score run, disclosed)

The first run (`score`, 222 triggers, 2 trades, 2 wins, 212 "no quote" because the book was bid 0.99 / ask 1.00) showed one triggered market-day that resolved NO: `KXRAIN-26SEP30-DTW`. Cause, a market-day window bug and not a source mismatch: the market's listed `close_time` is 2026-10-01 14:00Z (an early close, not the scheduled midnight), so the registered window [close_time - 24 h, close_time) held rain from 2026-10-01 05:53Z that belongs to the Oct 1 market day. The registration meant the scheduled midnight-to-midnight local standard-time day. Amendment: for a market whose `close_time` hour differs from the same city's close hour on the previous dated market (an early or irregular close), the market day is taken from the ticker date: [scheduled close of date D - 24 h, scheduled close) with the scheduled close = the same city's close time on the previous dated market + 24 h; where the city has no previous dated market (DTW has exactly one KXRAIN market in the window), the scheduled close of the Eastern reference city NYC on the same ticker date. Only `KXRAIN-26SEP30-DTW` is affected among triggered days (the other irregular closes, MIL, COL, PROV, LEX, are outside the 35 mapped cities). No threshold, delay, price limit, kill rule or verdict line is changed. I had seen the aggregate first-run counts (2 trades, 10 entry quotes, 1 NO) when writing this; the amendment is for a defect of definition, and the verdict lines in the registration are unchanged and stated BEFORE the rerun. Both runs are reported.
