- Exits (6): T3, T10, T15, T30: exit at the candle ending exactly e + 60 m minutes, at bid x (1 - h) for a long and ask x (1 + h) for a short, the candle two-sided, else "no mark" (skipped, counted). A triggered exit fills at bid x (1 - h) for a long, ask x (1 + h) for a short.
- Return per trade: long (exit / fill - 1), short (fill - exit) / fill minus 2 x 0.0012 (the 12 bps a leg fee). PF = sum of positive nets / sum of negative nets in size; net sum; halves floor(n/2) by entry time; day-clustered z by UTC entry day.
- Cooldown: per ticker and rule, an entry is taken only if at least 1800 s after that rule's previous entry.

Gate: SEARCH (the engine1_split dates: SPY and QQQ through 2026-11-10, NVDA and AAPL through 2026-11-09), n >= 100 entries, PF at 1 bp >= 1.3 and at 2 bp >= 1.0; VALIDATE the same bar on survivors plus BH at 5% over m = 816 on the one-sided day-clustered p of the 1 bp mean net.
