## Frozen definitions (the perps block of ENGINE1, kept apart from the binaries; fee 12 bps a leg ASSUMED and never confirmed)
- Data: the audited loader's perps output, four stock tickers, 1 minute candles (end_ts, bid, ask, last, volume, oi), prices in the perp's own units, Nov 6 onward; no result, no close.
- An entry is any candle that is two-sided (0 < bid < ask) with end e; side long or short. Long fills at ask x (1 + h), short at bid x (1 - h), h = 0.0001 (1 bp) and 0.0002 (2 bp), a stand-in for 1c and 2c.
- mid = (bid + ask) / 2; mom_k = ln(mid(e) / mid(e - 60 k)) for k = 1, 3, 5, 15, taking the candle ending exactly e - 60 k (missing = no entry for that filter, counted); classes up (> 0) and down (< 0); exactly 0 matches neither. spread_bps = (ask - bid) / mid x 10000; classes low, mid, high from tercile cut points of the SEARCH entries of that ticker (c1 = the ceil(n/3)-th, c2 = the ceil(2n/3)-th sorted value; low <= c1, mid <= c2). Time of day by UTC seconds of day (EST holds from Nov 6): pre 09:00 to 14:30, open 14:30 to 15:30, mid 15:30 to 20:00, close 20:00 to 21:00, post otherwise (the candle's end time).
- Filter options (17): none; mom1, mom3, mom5, mom15 each up or down (8); spread low, mid, high (3); time-of-day pre, open, mid, close, post (5).
- Exits (6): T3, T10, T15, T30: exit at the candle ending exactly e + 60 m minutes, at bid x (1 - h) for a long and ask x (1 + h) for a short, the candle two-sided, else "no mark" (skipped, counted). TS15 and TS30: target and stop at 15 or 30 bp from the entry FILL price (after the haircut): long target when a later candle's bid >= fill x (1 + k / 10000), stop when bid <= fill x (1 - k / 10000); short target when ask <= fill x (1 - k / 10000), stop when ask >= fill x (1 + k / 10000); candles after e and up to e + 1800 s, two-sided only, in order, the first trigger wins; with no trigger the exit is the T30 mark (no mark if missing). A triggered exit fills at bid x (1 - h) for a long, ask x (1 + h) for a short.
- Return per trade: long (exit / fill - 1), short (fill - exit) / fill minus 2 x 0.0012 (the 12 bps a leg fee). PF = sum of positive nets / sum of negative nets in size; net sum; halves floor(n/2) by entry time; day-clustered z by UTC entry day.
- Cooldown: per ticker and rule, an entry is taken only if at least 1800 s after that rule's previous entry.
- Rules per ticker: 17 filters x 2 sides x 6 exits = 204; tried = 204 x 4 tickers = 816 (all counted). Gate: SEARCH (the engine1_split dates: SPY and QQQ through 2026-11-10, NVDA and AAPL through 2026-11-09), n >= 100 entries, PF at 1 bp >= 1.3 and at 2 bp >= 1.0; VALIDATE the same bar on survivors plus BH at 5% over m = 816 on the one-sided day-clustered p of the 1 bp mean net. Information for the forward shadow only; label: upper bound, not fill-checked, fee ASSUMED, funding not booked.
