## Universe (chosen on COST, never on returns)
Non-crypto, `status` active in the sample, median relative spread (ask minus bid over mid) in development at or under 0.45%: gold 0.005%, US 500 0.010%, US Tech 100 0.011%, silver 0.019%, WTI 0.030%, copper 0.045%. Excluded on cost: platinum 0.069%, palladium 0.078%, aluminum 0.079%. Crypto excluded (FUND1 and PERPFLOW1 territory).

## Premium, costs, funding proxy (fixed)
premium p = mid / reference_price - 1 (mid, not the `price` field, which can be stale). Fills: a buy at the recorded ask, a sell at the recorded bid, at entry and at exit (so the spread is paid in full). Fee per side: **0.05% (verdict)** and **0.12% (stress, PERP1's Kalshi placeholder)**; the real Kalshi perp fee is not recorded. **Funding is a PROXY, not recorded:** over a hold of h seconds, F = sign(pbar) x min(max(|pbar| - deadband, 0), cap) x h / 28800, pbar the mean of the entry and exit premium (assumes the rate is per 8 hours and paid in proportion to time; Kalshi's interval and premium formula are not in the public data). Longs pay F when F > 0.
Hold: exit at the first later sample 7 to 12 hours after entry; no later sample in that window = no trade.

## Halves, clustering, N
Halves D1 and D2 as above; z is the mean of per-DAY mean returns over its standard error across days (day clustering). **N: +3 for the three tests (one rule each, direction fixed in advance); running about 730,455 before this, 730,458 after; the deflated z bar stays about 5.27.**

## Common verdict rules
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** if development has fewer than 100 trades or fewer than 20 trading days (the cluster count the z needs). Mean, D1, D2 are then printed as information only and NO FALSIFIED is declared. With 11 dates this is the expected outcome.
- **FALSIFIED**: at least 100 trades and 20 days, and any of: verdict-fee mean at or under 0, either half at or under 0, stress-fee mean at or under 0.
- **LEAD**: at least 100 trades and 20 days, verdict mean above 0 in both halves and in the whole, stress mean above 0, day z at least 5.27. Only then is the held-out third read once; a mean at or under 0 there is FALSIFIED. A LEAD is not a strategy; it is a forward claim.
- **Date it becomes enough:** at the recorder's rate (about 4.6 samples a day, 43 usable entry samples in 11 dates, about 4 a day) 20 trading days and 100 trades in a development set needs about 20 dates before the cut plus a fresh held-out third of about 10, so a re-registration with a new cut is possible on **about 2026-11-20** (30 recorded dates from 2026-10-21); the 30 second recorder around the open and close would add samples but is not on main yet.

## FUNDP1: premium sign predicts the next hours, fade it
**Mechanism and counterparty.** Leveraged traders who pay funding when the perp trades above its reference. When the perp is rich (p over +0.10%), a short earns funding and the premium should close toward the reference; when it is cheap (p under -0.10%), a long. Counterparty: the leveraged longs paying the premium. **Honest alternative:** the premium on these markets is mostly a stale or wide-spread artefact of the reference (index and oil references update outside the perp's hours), so it carries no information about the next move and the fee is the loss.
**Rule (no tuning).** At each development sample, for each universe name with a quote and a reference: if |p| > 0.0010 and |p| below the funding cap, SHORT when p > 0, LONG when p < 0, one unit, hold to the exit sample. Return per trade = side x (exit fill / entry fill - 1) + funding received (proxy) - 2 x fee. Names with |p| inside the deadband or at the cap are no trade; their count is a census (edge case (c): premium near the cap or inside the 0.002% deadband), printed, not scored.
**Numeric prediction.** About 180 to 230 entries but only 11 days; mean net per trade **-8 bp** at the 0.05% fee (range -25 to +10), win rate about 45%, the price leg a coin flip around zero and the 0.10% round-trip fee the main loss, partly offset by about +9 bp of funding proxy. Edge cases: about 2 over-cap premiums and 7 inside the deadband in development. Expected verdict NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (days under 20); if it were scorable, FALSIFIED (about 85%). Prior of a lead: about 5%.
**Kill criteria.** The common rules above, plus FALSIFIED if funding proxy is turned off and the mean is not above 0 (reported).

## CARRY1: cross-sectional carry, long the lowest premium, short the highest
**Mechanism and counterparty.** Same payer: the most crowded long (highest premium) pays funding, the least crowded (lowest premium) is paid or pays least. Dollar neutral per sample removes the common market move. **Differs from FUND1** (single venue crypto spot-versus-perp) and from FUNDP1 (outright direction).
**Rule.** At each development sample where at least 4 of the 6 universe names have a valid premium: rank by p, LONG the lowest and SHORT the highest, equal notional ($1 each leg), only if lowest < highest. Hold to the same exit sample. Return = long leg + short leg price returns at the fills + funding received on both legs (proxy) - 4 x fee. Cross-asset premium ranking mixes metals, indices and oil (a weakness: reference staleness differs), stated, not tuned.
**Numeric prediction.** About 40 trades; mean net **-15 bp** per trade at 0.05% (four fee sides = 0.20% plus two spreads against about +10 bp of funding spread), range -40 to +10; win rate about 40%. Expected verdict NOT_ENOUGH_DATA; if scorable, FALSIFIED (about 90%). Prior of a lead: about 4%.
**Kill criteria.** Common rules.

## BASIS1: funding paid versus basis convergence (is the carry itself bigger than the cost?)
**Mechanism and counterparty.** The funding proxy for a fade entry (|p| over 0.10%) is income only if the premium persists, and the premium closing is the basis gain; this separates the two from price drift. Claim: **mean funding proxy per entry is larger than the mean round-trip cost (2 x 0.05% fee + entry and exit spread)**, i.e. the carry alone pays for the trade. Also reports mean and median convergence = 1 - |p exit| / |p entry|.
**Numeric prediction.** Mean funding proxy about **+9 bp** (range +4 to +16) against mean round-trip cost about **+11 bp** (10 bp fee + 1 to 3 bp spread): funding is a bit under the cost; median convergence about 60% (range 30% to 90%).
**Kill criteria.** Per-entry difference (funding minus cost) used for the common rules: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA under 100 entries or 20 days; FALSIFIED if at least 100 entries, 20 days and the mean difference at or under 0 overall or in either half; LEAD only with a positive mean in both halves and day z at least 5.27, then the held-out dates read once. Nothing here trades: it is a statement about the carry alone, and price drift is not hedged.
