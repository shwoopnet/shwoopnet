## Rounding edge and depth (the Head's request, Auditor B's PR 945 read)
The logger rounds the YES ask UP and the NO price from the floored bid to whole cents; the research rule uses the raw quote. At XRPFAV10's LOWER edge 0.88 a raw favourite ask of 0.875 (anything above 0.87 and under 0.88) rounds UP to 0.88 and enters the band in the logger but not in the recompute; at the upper edge 0.92 nothing changes (rounding up cannot move a raw ask of 0.92 or less above 0.92; a raw 0.9201 rounds to 0.93 and is out in both). The favourite choice (YES price >= NO price) is made on the rounded prices, so a tie in whole cents goes to YES. Depth at the touch and `wouldFill` come from the live quote at the look.
