# Free, keyless history sources (tested from the sandbox, 2026-11-07)

Owner mandate (Nov 7): analysts are backtest-first, so wherever our own history is short we look for longer free history. Discovery only: no trading code, no key, one small request per source, nothing archived. "Answered" means the status code from THIS sandbox today. Row sizes are bytes of the one test response, not of the archive. Terms notes are my reading of the public pages, not legal advice; check them before redistributing anything.

## Headline

1. Kalshi's own history is SHORT. Earliest events I could find (bisected with `/events/{ticker}`, plus or minus a week, assuming no gaps): BTC and ETH 15 minute 2025-12-11, gold 15 minute 2026-07-30, BTC/ETH hourly ladders and BTC hourly range 2024-10-24, S&P hourly above/below 2025-02-15, gold daily 2026-03-22, NYC daily high temperature 2024-10-24 (2024-10-24 is shared by four families, so it may be the floor of the new KX ticker names, not the true start). Legacy un-prefixed series go further back (see (a)).
2. The long history for the SAME underlyings is free and answers from here: Binance Vision spot minute klines from 2017-08-17, Coinbase minute from 2017, Bitstamp minute from 2012, Kraken tick trades from 2013, Dukascopy tick data for FX and gold from at least 2005, Yahoo daily back to 1993 (SPY) and 2003 (EURUSD), OKX swap minute from 2019-12.
3. Blocked from here: Binance main API (451), Bybit (403, country block), Stooq (proxy tunnel closed), FRED CSV (proxy tunnel closed). Recorded, not worked around.

## Kalshi rate-limit note

Historical and market endpoints returned 429 in bursts even at 1.5 s spacing; 2.5 to 3 s spacing with one 6 s retry got through. `api.elections.kalshi.com` was used for every call. Cursors on `/historical/markets` run newest first, so finding the oldest record by paging costs about 2 MB per 1,000 markets; I did not page whole series.

## Ranking of the five fixes (years of history gained divided by ingest cost)

Score = years of NEW history for a family we already trade or research, over hours of polite fetching. Rough, order of magnitude only.

1. Binance Vision bulk zips for BTC and ETH spot minute klines plus perp funding. About 8 years of minute history for the crypto 15 minute rules (currently about 11 months of Kalshi markets, 69 days live). One year is about 1 hour of 365 small downloads and about 40 MB. Roughly 8 years for 8 hours, so 1 year per hour, with the 15 minute bar derived locally. Caveat: it tests the UNDERLYING path, not Kalshi's price, so it can test a signal such as smooth climb or rng5 but not the fill price.
2. Dukascopy XAUUSD and EURUSD hourly tick files (from 2005). Gold is the only ladder market and has a Kalshi history of 3 months (KXGOLD15M since 2026-07-30). About 20 years of gold ticks for about 3 hours a year of fetching and about 180 MB a year, so aggregate to minute bars on ingest. 20 years for about 60 hours, so 0.3 years per hour, but the value per year is the highest because gold is where the live money is. Needs the 503s understood first.
3. Kalshi `/historical/markets` plus `/historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks` for each 15 minute series. Adds 11 months (BTC, ETH) and 3 months (gold) of REAL quotes and fills that nothing else has, at about 1 hour a series per 1,200 markets. It is the only source that can say what the contract cost, so every external backtest needs it as the calibration step. 1 year for about 8 hours, so 0.12 years per hour.
4. Weather: IEM ASOS plus NOAA GHCN daily for the Kalshi city stations. 25 to 100 years of the actual settlement station data for the families Kalshi has had since Oct 2024 (about 2 years of Kalshi history), for under 2 hours of fetching and a few MB per city. Highest ratio of all (25 years per 2 hours), but it only answers a weather question; Kalshi's 2 years of weather prices still limit what a strategy can be tested against.
5. Yahoo daily (SPY 1993, EURUSD 2003, VIX, GC=F, CL=F) with Treasury yield CSV and EIA WTI for the S&P, rates, FX and oil daily and hourly families. 20 to 30 years of daily history for under 1 hour of fetching, but daily bars are too coarse for 15 minute or hourly families, so the use is regime and drift context, not entry rules. Counted last for that reason even though the ratio is high.

Not ranked, because they gain nothing: Hyperliquid funding (3.5 years, but Kalshi's own perp funding history is only 5 months and the venues differ), Kraken tick trades (large, order flow only), Open-Meteo (rate limited today), Stooq and FRED (blocked here).

## Gaps and caveats

- Earliest-event dates for Kalshi come from guessed event tickers and assume no gaps and the same naming in the past. A true earlier start under a different ticker (the legacy un-prefixed series, e.g. `INX-22APR28`, `WTI-22SEP09`) would have been missed. The 2024-10-24 floor shared by four families is suspicious.
- KXGDP, KXAUDUSD15M and the NYC high temperature oldest event were not finished: GDP got a 429, AUDUSD 15 minute's ticker pattern failed at 2026-09-02 (a different hour or naming), NYC weather paging stopped at 400 events.
- No file was saved to the repo besides this one. Test responses are in a scratch folder only.
