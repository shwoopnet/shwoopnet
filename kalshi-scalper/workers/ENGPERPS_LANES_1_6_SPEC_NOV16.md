## LANE 1: relative strength and pairs inside baskets (one pooled pass, the POOL1 engine)

**Baskets (version 2, Nov 16 after Auditor B's row 1: STOCK baskets only; the metals and crypto baskets are dropped from this spec because their perps live in the ENGINE1 perps extracts, a different file, loader, window and cut, not in POOL1's 94 name wall; a later spec may add them with their own extract, loader, window and allow line and the identical date window):**
- semis: NVDA, MU, AMD, AVGO, AMAT;
- mega-cap tech: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL (KXGOOGLPERP), AMZN, META;
- energy: XOM, CVX, COP.
All 13 names are in the POOL1 wall (Auditor B checked). A basket needs at least 3 names with at least 5 trading days of candles, and every name at least 5 trading days; the names that fail are printed and dropped. A name sits in one basket only, so no trade repeats across baskets. Each basket is evaluated on its own and pooled by trade; a trade carries its basket id. Index and ETF perps are not in any basket. **Data and window:** POOL1's loader (a13fd79b, allow line merged), the same extract and the same window, Nov 5 18:39Z to before Nov 17 00:00Z. **Development label on every result:** "development; the same seven days as POOL1 and the ENGINE1 perps passes".

**Conventions** are the POOL1 spec's (v3.5): ET clocks, mid and usable-candle rules, fill at ask or bid with haircut h of 0.0002 (gate) and 0.0005, taker 12 bp a side, funding booked from print timestamps (a position pays or receives a print iff entry end < print < exit end, everything before the first print counts as not booked), one position per name, cooldown, `END_EPOCH` 2026-11-17T00:00Z, loader behind a wall, allow line, Auditor B's CLEAN and a second build.

**Signals (two families, 144 new rules):**
1. **Relative strength against the basket (8 signals, each x the 12 POOL1 exits = 96 rules).** For a name i in a basket B at each candle end e from 10:31 to 15:30 ET: R_i = ln(mid_e / mid at e - 60 L minutes) for L in {30, 60}; RS_i = R_i minus the mean of R_j over the OTHER names in B (all must have a usable candle at both times, else no signal). The signal fires when |RS_i| exceeds c x u_i (c in {1, 2}; u_i = sigma_i x sqrt(30) from the name's own 60 one minute returns, as POOL1). Modes: FOLLOW (go with the sign of RS) and REVERT (the opposite). 2 lookbacks x 2 c x 2 modes = 8 signals.
2. **Pairs z-score inside the basket (4 signals, each x the 12 exits = 48 rules).** For every pair (a, b) in B (all pairs, no per-pair tuning), spread s_t = ln(mid_a) - ln(mid_b) on candles where both are usable. z at e = (s_e - mean of s over the W minutes before e) / sd of s over the same W minutes (population sd, at least 0.8 W points, sd above 0 or no signal), W in {120, 240}, entered when |z| >= z_in, z_in in {2, 3}: short the rich leg and long the cheap leg, 1 unit of notional each (dollar neutral, no beta fit). Pair legs fill at ask and bid with h like any trade. The exit uses the SPREAD's own volatility: u_s = sd of one minute spread returns x sqrt(30) over the last 60 minutes, target and stop k_t x u_s and k_s x u_s in {1, 2} in spread terms with the time stops 15, 30, 60 minutes (the same 12 exits as POOL1). 2 windows x 2 entry levels = 4 signals.
**Both legs' fees and funding are booked:** a pair trade has 4 taker fees (12 bp x 4 = 48 bp on the notional of one leg, 24 bp on the pair's combined notional) and each leg's funding is booked by its own side and prints, summed; the gate and the display use net on the sum of both legs' notional (so a pair with a 2 bp edge on its spread is read against 24 bp of cost, not 12). A pair trade counts as ONE trade in n.

**Pool, gate and counts:** as POOL1: pooled trades per trading day at least 3; PF 1.3 at taker + h 0.0002 and PF 1.0 at taker + h 0.0005, each also in both halves; no single ET day above 30% of net; net positive at 0.0005. Each rule's n counts trades with a mark. p from the day-clustered one-sided z where the day count allows (with 7 days it is a rough guide only; BH is run and printed but cannot be read as evidence).
**m, counted in full:** the Head's single carried number for every print: today 6,290,660 (it already contains the perps passes, POOL1's 218 and everything else), from Nov 17 7,492,484, plus anything registered after, plus these **144 new rules** (96 + 48), which count as new tries even though the data repeats. A print on Nov 16 reads m = 6,290,804; from Nov 17, 7,492,628. These are pooled rules (one rule applied to every basket and name; the baskets are NOT tested separately, so a basket-by-basket split is display only; a single basket picked from a split afterwards would be a new try and is counted as one by me). BH over that m, printed first.

## LANE 6: maker execution in the perps engine

**Fill model (the only place a maker price is used):** a maker order rests at the entry's own-side touch: a long rests at that candle's bid, a short at its ask (post-only, no crossing). It fills only if, within N minutes, the market trades THROUGH the limit by at least 1 tick: for a long, a later candle's ask close at or below limit minus 1 tick (the ask has come down through the resting bid); for a short, a later candle's bid close at or above limit plus 1 tick. N in {2, 5} candles (printed both). The fill price is the limit (no improvement), fee 5 bp a side (maker, a placeholder like taker 12 bp until the owner's fee screenshot arrives; Auditor B to confirm then). **Tick size is read from the census per name; if the census has none, the smallest observed price step of that name's bid and ask is used and printed.** With 1 minute closes only (no highs or lows) a through-trade inside a minute is invisible, so the model MISSES some real fills: fill rates are a lower bound, and the unfilled count is printed with what those signals would have earned at taker (the prior lesson: the missed ones are often the winners).
**What can rest:** a MAKER ENTRY (the entry above, then the rule's own exit logic from the fill time) and a MAKER TARGET EXIT (the target resting at the target price, filling on the same through-by-1-tick rule; the stop and the time stop always leave taker). Three columns for every rule: taker/taker (the gate), maker entry with taker exit, maker entry and maker target. Fee counted per leg actually used.
**A maker-only pass is flagged, not counted:** a rule that passes the gate only in a maker column is printed with the flag MAKER_ONLY and is NOT added to the pass list or to the BH survivors; the count of tries stays the same (the maker columns are not new rules, they are one rule read three ways, but all three are printed so the selection is visible). The maker columns apply to ENGINE1's perps rules, POOL1's 218, and LANE 1's 144.
**Interest credit (labelled a credit, never in the gate):** the perps balance earns 3.5% APY, up to 7% on funds backing open positions (the Head's figures; the owner's terms are for Auditor B to confirm). The credit per trade = margin x rate x hold time / 365 days, margin = notional / leverage at the size shown, rate 3.5% and 7% printed as two bounds. It is shown as its own line beside the net and never added to PF or to the gate.

## How every result is shown
Per rule and per column: n, win rate, PF at both haircuts, net per trade in basis points of price (price terms), and percent of margin on the margin at **10%, 25% and 50% of the size-specific max leverage** (the page maximum per name from the census TSV; a pair's margin is the sum of both legs'), with the liquidation distance = 1 / leverage minus maintenance (the Testing Desk's rule, Head Nov 16): maintenance is gold's measured figure of about 4.47% of notional (at 17.4x, from the owner's ticket) and an ASSUMED 4.5% for every other name, labelled "maintenance assumed"; at high leverage the buffer is tiny (gold at 17.4x has about 1.3%, not 5.75%), and a size whose 1 / leverage is at or below maintenance has no buffer and is printed as such. Page maxima are read from Forensics' census TSV (column "page max"), not from the POOL1 estimates. The first-week and one-regime warning is printed on every table.

## Amendment A1 (Nov 16, 2026, written with the code, before any run; for Auditor B's read)
1. **Maker scope in this build.** Maker columns are built for LANE 1's 144 rules only. Single-name rules (relative strength, 96) have TT, ME and MM at N = 2 and N = 5 candles (TT, ME2, ME5, MM2, MM5). **Pairs have TT, ME2 and ME5 only:** both legs rest, both must fill within N candles, the position starts at the later fill and an unfilled pair is skipped (printed with what its TT trade would have earned); a maker target is not modelled for a pair. POOL1's 218 and the ENGINE1 perps rules are NOT given maker columns here (they need event adapters; a later amendment, not claimed now); the carry rules (entry at an exact clock time) would be excluded from it.
2. **Maker legs take no haircut** (a resting limit pays no slippage; the haircut h applies to the taker legs only), fee 5 bp a side on maker legs, 12 bp on taker legs. A maker entry fills at the limit (the entry candle's bid for a long, ask for a short) at the first later candle end within N whose ask close (long) or bid close (short) trades through by at least 1 tick; the census has NO tick column, so the tick is always the smallest positive step between the name's distinct bid or ask prices in the extract (printed per name).
3. **After a maker fill** the rule's own exit logic runs from the fill candle: u from the signal candle (unchanged), target and stop distances from the FILL price, time stop = the fill candle end + 60 T. A maker target (MM) fills when a later close trades through the target by 1 tick (long: bid >= target + tick; short: ask <= target - tick) and exits at the target price; the stop is checked first on every candle and always leaves taker at the bid (long) or ask (short) with haircut.
4. **Cooldown:** the position occupies the name from the signal candle to its exit end (the same as POOL1); a resting order does not block the name while it waits (a name already in a position at the signal candle is skipped before any order rests).
5. **The three baskets and the day count:** `trading_days` is POOL1's (distinct ET dates with a 09:31 candle for at least one kept name before 2026-11-17T00:00Z). Window for both signals: candle ends 10:31 to 15:30 ET; pairs may take their z baseline back across the previous day (the log ratio is continuous), the other conditions as above.
5b. **Entry timing label:** every entry, taker or maker, is priced at the signal candle's OWN quote (the candle ending at e), not the next candle (POOL1's convention); a maker order rests from that quote and can fill only at a LATER candle end. Skips (no mark, unfilled, cooldown, no vol, unbooked funding) are counted and printed per column.
6. **Margin view:** each result at 10%, 25% and 50% of the name's census page max, % of margin = net per unit notional / (1 / (fraction x page max)) summed over legs for a pair; liquidation distance = 1 / (fraction x page max) minus maintenance (4.47% gold, 4.5% elsewhere, assumed), worst and mean over the rule's trades, names with no buffer listed. A name with no page max is REFUSED (removed from the run and printed, no default). The page-max table is pinned by file sha256 (printed in the report) once the Head names the authoritative table for these 13 names (PERPS_PAGE_MAX_20261116.tsv, commit a1beb3f6, sha8ccbb2c6, says 4.9 for all 13; the Head's PERPSCALP2 table says NVDA 4.5, MU 3.3, META 4.5; Auditor B flagged it); a literal pinned table goes in here then.
7. **Interest credit:** % of margin per trade = 3.5% or 7% x mean hold / 365 days, shown beside the net, never in the gate or the PF.
8. **Counts:** 8 relative strength signals + 4 pair signals = 12 signals x 12 exits = 144 rules; m = the carried number the caller passes (6,290,660 on Nov 16) + 144, BH on the TT column's day-clustered p of the rules that pass the gate (the gate is read on TT; a maker-column pass without a TT pass is flagged MAKER_ONLY and not counted).
Tests: `engine1_perps_lanes_test.py` (maker fill, maker target, stop, TT and ME hand cases, funding, pair TT and the legs-must-fill rule, margin and liquidation including gold's 1.28% and the no-buffer case, interest, relative strength and pair events, eligibility, a whole-report smoke run). ## Amendment A2 (Nov 16, 2026, rulings on the Testing Desk's readings R1 to R24 for the blind second build; code unchanged except the pair ordering)
- R2: "mid at e - 60 L minutes" means the candle ending e - 60 L SECONDS, that is L minutes before e (L in 30, 60). R3: the lookback candle must also fall on the same ET date at or after 09:31 ET; the kept-name rule is POOL1's loader (at least 2 prints) plus at least 5 trading days of candles.
- R8: the pair baseline is the W wall-clock minutes before e (candle ends e - 60 W .. e - 60), e excluded, at least 0.8 W present, so a baseline that reaches before the open counts only the candles that exist; no "last W available points". R9: pair (a, c) is formed with a < c by ticker, events sorted by (e, a, c).
- **R10 (pair exits):** target and stop are checked on the EXECUTABLE position return, not the mid spread: R_t = (long leg's leave quote x (1 - h) / its fill - 1) + (short leg's fill - leave quote x (1 + h)) / its fill) on each later candle where both legs are usable, from the fill prices (for TT the ask x (1 + h) and bid x (1 - h) at e; for ME the limits); stop R_t <= -ks u_s checked before target R_t >= kt u_s; the exit is the same candle's leave quotes; the time stop at t0 + 60 T on the candle ending then. u_s is the population sd of one-minute changes of the log mid ratio over the 60 minutes ending e (at least 45) x sqrt(30), from the SIGNAL candle. There is no mid-spread reference s_e or s_f.
- R11: the reported pair net = (R_exit + funding of both legs - 4 fees) / 2 (24 bp on the combined notional for taker); booked only if both legs' funding is booked. R12: a pair is skipped (cooldown) if either name is in a position at e; a taken pair occupies both names to its exit end. R13, R14, R15: as the Testing Desk read them, with the pair ME start = the later fill candle, fill prices = the limits and no s_f.
- **R20 (margin):** for a pair, % of margin = 100 x (sum of the two legs' net, each in units of one leg's notional, i.e. R_exit + funding of both legs - 4 fees) / (the sum of the two legs' margins 1 / (f L_i)); for a single name, 100 x net x f L. Liquidation distance = 1 / (f L) - maintenance (0.0447 gold, 0.045 others), for a pair the smaller of the two legs; "no buffer" when not positive. R21 as read.
- R16: the census has no tick column: the tick is the smallest positive step between distinct bid or ask closes of that name in the loaded window, printed per name. R22 labels are free; the same counts are printed. R19: BH over m = carried + 144 on the TT p of the rules that pass the gate in TT.
