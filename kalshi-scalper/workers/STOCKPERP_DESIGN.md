# STOCKPERP design pass: single stock perpetuals against the cash stock (Strategy Research V2 worker, 2026-11-06, US Central times unless marked ET or UTC)

Owner request (Nov 6, relayed): put V2 on the single stock perps as a focused design pass, not a generator. Discovery only; nothing live. Registrations: `STOCKPERP_REGISTRATION.md` (six tests, written and committed before any outcome). Earnings-date mechanics are left to EARNPERP1 to EARNPERP3; PERPEQ1 to PERPEQ4 are referenced, not repeated.

## 1. What is different about a stock perp (facts measured today, all descriptive, no test outcome)
- **It is a different price scale.** AAPL perp 6.87 against about 270 in cash (`contract_size` 0.02, `underlying_multiplier` 1.0). Returns need no scale; gaps against the cash print need one, estimated from a quiet span of the same pair.
- **Hours.** `schedule` shows the stock perps closed from Friday 22:00Z (17:00 ET) to Sunday 23:00Z (18:00 ET); quotes exist through the night. Quote minutes by ET hour: 17:00 to 18:00 has 177 minutes in 3 days, so there is a daily shut of about an hour at 17:00 ET (cash closes 16:00, so the cash after-hours session runs about an hour against a live perp). Holidays from `/margin/markets/schedules` (per ticker, 23 entries): Thanksgiving 11-26 closed (closure begins 18:00 ET on 11-25), 11-27 early close 13:00 ET, 12-24 early close, 12-25 closed. `halt_sources` is empty on all 88 markets today: the field exists, so a halt is machine readable on the live list but not in the candles.
- **Spread is a function of the clock, much more than the name.** Median spread of the 58 stock and ETF books, in bp, by ET hour of the 1 minute candles (2026-11-04 to 11-06): 00 to 07 ET 12 to 17 (overnight), 08 9.4, 09 10.8 (the 09:30 open), **10 6.2, 11 5.6, 12 4.8, 13 4.6, 14 4.1, 15 3.8**, **16 15.9 (cash shut, funding stamp at 16:00 ET)**, 18 to 23 ET 9 to 14. Midday cash hours cost a quarter of the night. Median touch is $1,000 to $10,000 notional for most single names. Everything below is a one to two contract test.
- **Funding is once a day, not every 8 hours.** `funding_rates/historical`: one stamp at 21:00Z (16:00 ET in standard time; 20:00Z in daylight time, until 2027-03-14), stocks from 2026-11-03 (only 4 stamps so far, 124 rates). 78% of rates are exactly 0 (deadband 0.2 bp a day, cap 200 bp a day on every stock), 12% positive, 10% negative; mean +0.03 bp a day, extremes +5.5 and -6.7. A funding payment is therefore 0 to 7 bp a day against a round trip of 24 bp in fees: funding is tiny, which is the opposite of crypto where it was the research object (PSET).
- **Funding against borrow cost (unhedged).** A cash short pays a borrow fee (general cost, about 0.3 bp a day for easy names, many times that for hard ones; not measured here and not quoted from any data source I read) and the cash stock pays no funding. The perp's funding at 0 to 5 bp a day is the same order. There is no cash leg on Kalshi, so a funding harvest is a price bet with 100 to 200 bp of daily volatility for a carry of at most 5 bp: not a strategy by itself (SPFUND1 reads only the stamp, CARRY0 tabulates it).
- **The perp follows the cash tape within the minute.** CASHW (index perps, 0.991 same minute correlation) and CMD1 to CMD4 found no 1 minute lag. The stock version is unmeasured: SPLAG1 prints the stock lead lag table (information) beside its rule.
- **First look at `perps30/2026-11-06/close.csv.gz`** (first file, a manual run, 15:46 to 17:00 ET, 20:46:25Z to 21:59:55Z). Schema = the hourly recorder's columns (ts_utc, ticker, asset_class, status, bid, ask, bid_size, ask_size, price, reference_price, settlement_mark, liquidation_mark, open_interest, volume_24h, notional_oi_usd, funding_cap, funding_deadband). 13,024 rows, exactly 148 sample times on a 30 second grid (no gap), 88 tickers (58 equity, 19 crypto, 6 metals, 2 index, 1 energy; the 20th crypto and US 2000 are inactive). In that window (it contains the 16:00 cash close and the funding stamp, the wide hour) the median spread of the 86 live books is 11.0 bp and 63 of 86 are above 5 bp; the cheapest are BTC 0.4, ETH 0.45, gold 0.74, US 500 0.92, Tech 100 1.2, QQQ 1.4, SPY 1.8, copper 1.6, silver 1.9, NVDA 2.9, AAPL 4.0 bp. Median touch (smaller side) AAPL $2,000, NVDA $3,000, QQQ $2,900, SPY $2,800, US 500 $100,000, Tech 100 $210,000, PLTR as low as $6 at times. Staleness against the 1 minute candles: for the 3,606 comparable samples taken in the last 10 s of a minute, |mid of the 30 s row over mid of that minute's candle close - 1| has median 0.00 bp, p90 2.7 bp, max 54 bp, so the candle close IS the last 30 s quote; the candles lose nothing about the quote itself, only sizes, the reference and the marks. The bid and ask are unchanged between consecutive 30 s samples in 36% of steps (median over the 58 names, range 0 to 66%), the cheapest names re-quote most. 16% of minutes have no usable candle (no two sided quote), so a missing leg is common and is counted, never filled. Nothing is scored on this file.

## 2. Who is forced to trade (the counterparties, one per test)
| Mechanism | Forced or price-insensitive side | Test | Data now | Readable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perp quote slow to the cash tape in cash hours | perp maker with a $1,000 to $10,000 touch against a deep cash book | SPLAG1 | candles + cash1m, 1 pilot day | 2026-12-18 (30 dates) |
| Opening and closing auctions (MOO, MOC orders) | auction participants who must trade at the print | SPAUCT1 | same | 2026-12-18 |
| Liquidation and stop flow on a 5 times levered book | liquidated holders | SPLIQ1 | same | trade bar about 2027-01-20 |
| Daily funding settlement | longs paying a positive rate | SPFUND1 | candles + funding history | rate at least 2 bp is rare: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA into 2027 |
| Sunday reopening after the weekend shut | weekend news takers in a book that was shut | SPW1 | first weekend 11-08 | 30 weekends: 2027-06-06 |
| Halt resumption auction | perp takers during a cash halt | SPHALT1 | cash gaps + candles | event count first |
Earnings: not here (EARNPERP). Dividends: whether the perp is adjusted at the ex-dividend date is NOT visible in any field I read (no dividend field on the market object); the cash close drops by the dividend on the ex-date and a perp that is not adjusted would show a gap against the cash. That is a question for the exchange or the fee page, not a test, and it is a reason every overnight test here carries a note about ex-dates (the scorer does not exclude them; the effect is under 1% for the 19 names).

**Considered and not registered (reason):** index additions and quarterly rebalance days (the forced flow is real, but the sign needs the exchange imbalance feed, which has no keyless source here, so a rule would be a guess; 2026-12-18 is the next quad witching); ETF against its constituents (QQQ against six names is 14 crossings and six fees); sector pairs and SPY or QQQ against the index perps (PERPEQ2, PERPEQ4); overnight gap fade (PERPEQ1); premium to reference (PERPEQ3); cross-venue basis with the cash stock (not tradable: no cash leg); a mass generator (declined by the owner, and PERPGEN non-crypto said NOT_ENOUGH_DATA).

## 3. The registered tests, one line each (full text in the registration)
SPLAG1: cash moves 15 bp or more in 3 minutes and the perp has caught up less than 10 bp of it: trade the gap for 3 minutes. SPAUCT1: perp against the cash print at 09:31 and 16:00 ET, 5 minute convergence. SPLIQ1: a 15 bp perp-only 1 minute jump with the cash flat, fade for 5 minutes. SPFUND1: previous funding at least 2 bp, buy after the stamp for 30 minutes. SPW1: Sunday 18:10 ET gap of 25 bp or more against Friday's last quote, fade to Monday 09:25 ET. SPHALT1: 5 or more missing cash minutes, trade the perp toward the cash at the resumption for 5 minutes.
All: 19 cost-chosen names (spread at most 5 bp), 3 times median spread screen, fee 12 bp a side (placeholder), control = reverse direction, pilot date 2026-11-05, development 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-30 read 2026-12-01, held out from 2026-12-01 read once on or after 2026-12-30 after a LEAD. N +14, running about 1,230,535, bar about 5.3 (the bar is out of reach in the development window by trade count or clusters for every test, and the registrations say so with dates).

## 4. Forward recorder: PROPOSAL for the owner (nothing built, nothing edited)
What the tests need that is not stored yet, in order of value:
1. **Nightly archive of the 1 minute candles and the funding history** (keyless GETs on `/margin/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks?period_interval=1` for the 19 universe names plus the 2 index perps, and `/margin/funding_rates/historical?ticker=` for the same). All six tests read candles, SPFUND1 reads funding, and the exchange's retention of 1 minute history is not guaranteed (it is the one dependency every test here shares). Cadence: once a night about 02:00 UTC and once on Friday after the 22:00Z close; fields: ticker, end_ts, bid_c, ask_c (the sentinel screened), the funding rows verbatim; 21 tickers x 2 windows of 22 h plus 21 funding GETs at 1.2 s spacing is about 65 GETs, **about 2 minutes of runner time a night, about 45 to 50 minutes a month**; output about 150 KB a night gzipped, to the `perp-data` branch only. The PERPEQ recorder request item 1 (61 tickers, 5 minutes a night) already covers this: ask the owner to approve that one and add the funding GETs.
2. **Sunday reopening book (SPW1).** Sunday 17:55 to 18:35 ET (the reopening at 23:00Z; Mondays after a holiday as well) every 10 seconds, the 19 names, the hourly recorder's columns (sizes, reference, marks). 240 samples a weekend in one 45 minute job started by one weekly cron at 22:40Z Sunday: **about 45 Actions minutes a week, about 200 a month**; about 40 KB a week gzipped. Purpose: sizes and spread at the reopen (the candles show the quote, not whether $1,000 was there).
3. **Add `halt_sources` and `schedule.is_open` to the existing recorder row** (a one line schema change in `record_perps.py` and `record_perps_fast.py`, no extra GET: both come from the same list call). Purpose: SPHALT1 currently infers halts from Yahoo gaps, the exchange field is the real signal. Cost 0 minutes. Run it on the cash-hours 5 minute list snapshots of PERPEQ request item 2 (390 minutes of runner time a trading day, about 8,200 a month, which is free on a public repository and above the 2,000 free minutes of a private one: the owner should confirm the repository is public before approving item 2).
4. **Keep the existing 30 second open and close windows and extend `STOP_DATE`** past 2026-12-07 to 2027-02-26 (PERPEQ request item 4): the sizes at the open and at the 16:00 funding and cash close are the only place SPAUCT1 and SPFUND1 can ask whether the touch exists at the print. Cost: the existing 80 minutes a window, two windows a day, about 3,400 minutes a month.
5. **Cash 1 minute archive: already running** (`cash1m/<date>.csv.gz`, 71 symbols including all 19 names). Confirm it also runs on half days (2026-11-27, 12-24) and does not stop at 16:00 ET; SPAUCT1 uses the 16:00 print and the after-hours bars.
Total for items 1 to 3: about 250 runner minutes a month on top of what runs, all public GETs, no key, no order, all to `perp-data`, nothing read by any trading code. Items 4 and the 5 minute snapshots are the large ones and are optional for these six tests.
Which tests need which: SPLAG1, SPAUCT1, SPLIQ1, SPHALT1 need only item 1 and the cash archive (SPHALT1 gains from 3); SPFUND1 needs item 1 for funding; SPW1 needs item 1, and item 2 only to know the size.

## 5. Honest summary
Single stock perps are a thin copy of a deep market with a spread of 4 to 6 bp at midday and about 15 bp at night, a fee placeholder of 12 bp a side, and funding that is zero most days. A trade here needs a reaction of more than 24 bp plus the spread within minutes of a forced flow, and every cash comparison so far (indices, metals) found the perp already there. The design therefore predicts NOT_ENOUGH_DATA or FALSIFIED for all six; the value of the pass is that each is a stated test with a counterparty, a date it becomes readable, and a recorder that costs under 300 runner minutes a month to keep it readable.
