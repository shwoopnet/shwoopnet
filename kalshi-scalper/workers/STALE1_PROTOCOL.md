# STALE1 protocol (V2, Nov 11, 2026, registered before any score)
Card: STALE1 | first quote after a closed gap (weekend, daily break) on metals, oil, gas, forex 15 minute series; cheap side or mid, time exit or hold | risk class: cheap side win rate 10 to 30%, loss = the price paid, 1 contract | stage Idea. Declined list (7e) checked: not favourite-late (88c to 97c is NOT traded), not gold (excluded, live), not a declined market, no Polymarket, ESPN, NHL, MLB, NBA.com. Uniqueness: different entry (a calendar event: first market after a closed gap), different exit (time or hold), not a filter on L1.
Data (read only, no API calls): settled markets with 1 minute candles (bid, ask, volume) in data/alts2, alts3, alts4, fresh, rep sqlite, deduped by ticker. Series: KXSILVER15M, KXWTI15M, KXCOPPER15M, KXNATGAS15M, KXPLATINUM15M, KXPALLADIUM15M, KXEURUSD15M, KXGBPUSD15M, KXUSDJPY15M, KXAUDUSD15M, KXUSDCAD15M. Gold excluded.
Gap event: a market whose open_ts minus the previous market's close_ts (same series) is at least 1800 s. Class W (weekend, gap >= 86400 s) or D (daily break, 1800 to 86399 s). Only the FIRST market after the gap (k = 1) is traded. Known limit: a hole in the stored data looks like a gap; reported, not repaired.
Entry: signal read from the candle ending at open_ts + LOOK (LOOK 180 s or 300 s); fill at the ask_o of the NEXT candle (one minute later, so no lookahead), taker. Spread = ask_c - bid_c of the signal candle, must be two sided and <= 0.06. Side: YES if its ask is in the band, else NO (NO ask = 1 - yes bid); band CHEAP 0.04 to 0.30 or MID 0.30 to 0.88; never above 0.88.
Exit: HOLD to settlement, or TIME: sell at the bid_c of the candle ending 5 minutes after the fill (fee on the sale too; if no candle or bid <= 0, the sale is 0). Fee 0.07 p (1 - p) unrounded on entry and every sale, cash basis, 1 contract.
Features (slices of the same entries): ALL; STALE (number of zero-volume candles from open to the signal candle >= 1) and FRESH (0); TIGHT (spread <= 0.02) and WIDE (> 0.02).
Cells: class {W, D} x LOOK {180, 300} x band {CHEAP, MID} x exit {HOLD, TIME} x slice {ALL, STALE, FRESH, TIGHT, WIDE} = 80 cells. Control (descriptive, not a cell): the same rule on non-gap markets of the same series.
Split: per series by close time, first two thirds development, last third held out and read ONCE, only for survivors. Survivor: development cash PF >= 1.5 AND n >= 100 AND day-clustered z above the bar. Bars: shared N about 1,100,000 + 80 cells, one-sided z about 5.34; and N 8,795, z about 4.39. Both reported.
Verdicts: SURVIVOR (passes development, then the held-out read); FALSIFIED (n >= 100 and PF < 1.0 pooled for the whole family); NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (no cell reaches n = 100), with the date the best cell would reach n = 100 at its observed rate.
Numeric prediction: the market is not stale at the first quote (listed quotes are two sided); every cell cash PF under 1.0, best cell PF about 0.9 to 1.2 with n under 60; prior of a survivor 2%.
Script: src/scalper/stale1.py. Research only; no live code.
