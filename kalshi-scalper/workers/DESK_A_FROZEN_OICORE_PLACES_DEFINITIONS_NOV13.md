## 1. Decision instant and the open interest rise
- **Decision instant** D = close time minus 180 s EXACTLY, in exchange time. The close time is the market's `close_time` from the coverage file.
- **Open interest rise** = the `open_interest` of the 1 minute candle ENDING at close - 180 s, minus the `open_interest` of the candle ENDING at close - 360 s, both from the nightly pull's batched candlesticks answer for that ticker (the same two candles as OICORE's own rule, so 3 minutes apart). A candle's end is its `end_period_ts`. The rise must be >= T, inclusive (1e-9 tolerance).
- **T per market:** the 90th percentile (nearest rank) of that market's own window rises (this definition, one rise per window, the same two candles) over its previous 20 TRADING DAYS of windows, from the same pull; it uses no price, quote or result. A trading day = a US Central calendar day with at least one window whose two candles exist. **Seating (the Head, Nov 13, 21:23Z):** the first 20 trading days of T come from Desk B's OICAND backfill (20 weekdays to Nov 13 02:35Z, one rise per close), a parameter from past open interest only (no price, quote or outcome). A place is SEATED once OICAND has landed and passed the structure check, NOT 20 days from now; after that T rolls forward by the same rule from the nightly pull. Before OICAND lands, no signal is recorded and none is back-filled. T is frozen for a day at its 00:00 US Central value (the 20 days before that day), so a day's signals use one T. T of 0 or fewer than 5 contracts: NOT seated, reported (gate 0).
- **The pull is the only source of the rise.** The book (R6, NC15) carries no open interest and plays no part in the rise.

## 2. Quote, side and fill check (from the book, replayed after the pull)
- **Book source and clock:** R6 (`book15r6/`, 2 s polls) for XRP and ETH; NC15 (`nc15/` on `nc-data`, 5 s polls) for the other eleven. Exchange time and the 120 row rolling median offset exactly as in section 0 of the wheel sheet.
- **Decision poll** = the last poll with exchange time at or before D and at most 10 s before it (R6 or NC15 alike); none: "no signal: no poll", counted. The book must be two-sided (rule 18: 0 < YES bid, YES ask < 1, bid < ask); else "not two-sided", counted.
- **Side and price:** the cheaper side's ask at the decision poll (ties to YES), from the shared rule: 0.08 to 0.50 inclusive; outside: no signal.
- **Order and fill check:** 1 contract in shadow, limit = the ask seen (plus 0). The NEXT poll after the decision poll must be at most **4 s** after it on R6 or at most **6 s** on NC15; a later next poll: no second look, a miss, counted, not scored. Both polls must offer 1 contract at or under the limit; fill price = the worse of the two polls' asks (the wheel ruling). Fee per order = fee_up_order(p, 1) = ceil(0.07 x p x (1 - p) x 100 - 1e-9) / 100. Cash = win - price - fee.

## 3. Missing items (never carried forward, never interpolated, counted by reason)
- **A missing candle:** if the candle ending at close - 180 s OR the one ending at close - 360 s is absent from the pull's answer, or its `open_interest` is null or blank: "no signal: no candle", counted, scored as nothing. No neighbouring candle is substituted, no earlier or later one is used. The same missing-candle windows are left OUT of T's sample (a window with a missing candle has no rise).
- **A truncated or failed pull** for a night: that night's closes are "no signal: no pull", counted; they are not re-pulled except by the same job on a later night if Main chooses, and a late answer does not count for a decision instant that was already past its pull time (the pull is a record, and a row with `body_kind` other than a normal candlesticks body is not usable).
- **Result:** the coverage file's `result` column ("yes" or "no") at the block read; if missing, the ONLY later read is the kalshi-pull list job that Desk A builds at each block read and the Chief of Staff runs (`GET /markets/{ticker}`, the `result` field). A ticker with no yes or no result 7 days after its close is VOID, booked and counted, never scored.

## 7. Version 3 additions (Auditor B's findings of 21:24Z; these override any earlier wording in this file)
- **F1, the field:** the candle's open interest is read from `open_interest_fp` (a string, converted to a number) FIRST, and from `open_interest` only if `open_interest_fp` is absent, as the scorers do. A value that is null, blank, not a finite number (nan, inf) or a bool is UNREADABLE, and an unreadable value is a missing candle (section 3). Everywhere above "open_interest" means this rule.
- **G1:** the decision poll and the next poll are rows of the window's ticker AND close_time.
- **G2, the offset, quoted here (not by reference):** each recorded row has `server_date_header` and `t_recv_ms`. For a candidate poll, the offset is the MEDIAN over the 120 most recent rows of the same run folder (any ticker, ordered by receive time, at or before that poll, rows with a readable header only) of (the header time in seconds + 0.5 - t_recv_ms / 1000); exchange time = t_recv_ms / 1000 + the offset. **Minimum 30 rows with readable headers; fewer: "no signal: no offset", counted, no earlier folder borrowed.** An edit to the wheel sheet does not change this sheet.
- **G3, the next-poll limit's clock:** the gap is `t_recv_ms` of the next row minus `t_recv_ms` of the decision poll (receive time, so the offset cancels): at most 4 s on R6, at most 6 s on NC15.
- **G4, the pull deadline:** the nightly pull that holds a close's candles must have completed by 12:00 UTC on the UTC day AFTER the UTC day of that close; otherwise "no signal: no pull", counted, and that window's rise is also left out of T's sample. (Every pull is after the close, so the pull is a record, not a live input; the deadline only decides which windows count.)
- **G5, T per series, and T's day:** T is per SERIES (the ticker's series, one T for all its windows). T for a US Central day uses only pulls that had landed before 00:00 US Central of that day, over the previous 20 trading days whose pulls had landed by then; if fewer than 20 such days exist, or the previous trading day's pull has not landed, the day's windows are "no signal: no T", counted. OICAND's backfill counts as landed pulls once it has passed the structure check.
- **G6, VOID:** a void (no yes or no result 7 days after the close) is EXCLUDED from PF and every score and counted, never a fee-only loss.
- **G7, coverage path:** R6: `book15r6/<date>/<HHMM>/coverage.csv.gz` on `quote-data`; NC15: `nc15/<date>/<HHMM>/coverage.csv.gz` on `nc-data` (confirmed present; its columns are close_time, series, ticker, polls, ok_polls, n429, batch_fallback, max_gap_ms, first_recv_ms, last_recv_ms, book_rows, tape_pages, tape_trades, dupes_dropped, tape_sum_count, volume_fp, result, note). `result` is read across all files holding that ticker and close_time; two files that disagree: STOP and report.
- **G8, ETH:** R6 records Ethereum to 2026-12-31 23:59Z (`ETH_END_UTC`, `scripts/record_15m_book.py` on main; the Auditor's "2026-11-20" is the older date), so OICORE-ETH has a book to the end of December and is seated once OICAND has landed and passed its structure check (section 1), not 20 days from now.
