- The rules as published: a snapshot of each market's book once a second; reference price = first price level walking down from the best bid where cumulative size reaches target/5; orders at or better than the reference price get multiplier 1, below it multiplier = discount^(ticks below); an order scores only if it "helps reach the target size" on its side; score = size x multiplier, normalised within the side; snapshot score = share(yes) + share(no) (max 2 for everyone together); a snapshot counts only if BOTH sides reach the target; reward = your score share x period reward x (counted snapshots / all snapshots); rounded down to the cent; a program payout under $1 is not paid; paid after the program ends; US members only, SSN on file above IRS thresholds; Kalshi may end or change the program at any time.

## This is a market-making lane
Any fill is inventory held to settlement. It is a different P&L stream (a payment for resting size), not a price edge, and not a 15 minute series (no L1 overlap on the 15 minute crypto and commodity series; programs on a series L1 trades would be excluded). Discovery only, no live code, no promote verb.

## Model (all of it fixed here)
Account $800; resting capital capped at $400 across all programs; $100 per market lane.
Sampling (public, keyless, 1 thread, backoff on 429): a seeded (random.Random(20261103)) stratified sample of up to 240 markets whose program is active and whose market status is `active`, stratified on daily pool in 6 bands; for each, `GET /markets/{ticker}/orderbook` and `GET /markets/{ticker}`; up to 3 passes at least 45 minutes apart (what a sandbox session allows; days of sampling are NOT possible here, so time-of-day and weekend variation are NOT covered and the result says so). A market with no book and no last price is `no_price` and not scored.
For each market and sample, per side (YES bids, NO bids), T = target:
1. Depth D = total resting size on the side; cumulative from the best bid; reference level r = first level with cumulative >= T/5. Our lane posts q = min(100, $100/(p_yes+p_no)) contracts on EACH side at the reference level of that side (behind the queue there). If a side has less than T/5 depth, our price is the best bid there, else last_price minus 3c for YES, (1 - last_price) minus 3c for NO.
2. Competing score on a side, Q = sum of size x multiplier over levels up to the level where cumulative reaches T, counting the crossing level in FULL (pessimistic) or clipped so the cumulative stops at T (optimistic). Our share s = q / (Q + q) if our level has cumulative-above < T, else 0.
3. The snapshot counts if D_yes + q >= T and D_no + q >= T; f = share of the market's samples that count (0 to 1, from the sampled passes).
4. Reward per day = pool_day x f x (s_yes + s_no)/2, set to 0 if s x (pool over the whole period) x f < $1.00 on both sides combined (the $1 payout floor).
5. Fills per side per day: volume_24h_fp / 2 x s (central), x 2s capped at q (pessimistic cost), x 0.5s (optimistic cost). Adverse-selection cost per fill: price <= 15c: pessimistic 27% of the price, central 12.5%, optimistic -2% (a gain); price > 15c: UNMEASURED assumption, pessimistic 3.0c, central 1.5c, optimistic 0. Capital cost 5% a year on resting capital.
6. Net per day per $100 resting = reward - fills x cost - capital cost. Three cases: reward-pessimistic (Q crossing level full, cost pessimistic, fills pessimistic) is the verdict case; central; optimistic.
7. Gap lane (information, not the verdict): for markets with f < 0.5, q_gap = T - D on each short side at the prices in item 1; capital = q_gap x (p_yes+p_no); reported only if capital <= $400.

## Kill rules and verdict words (fixed)
- NOT_ENOUGH_DATA: the program list is not publicly readable, or fewer than 100 markets could be scored (book and market read, not `no_price`).
- NOT_VIABLE: under the verdict case, fewer than 10 scored market-lanes (and fewer than 5% of scored lanes) have net per day per $100 resting above $0.10; OR the median scored lane's central reward per $100 per day is below its pessimistic adverse-selection cost; OR the $1 payout floor and the US/SSN/eligibility rules exclude a $100 lane in more than 90% of scored markets.
- VIABLE_SMALL_PROBE: at least 10 lanes and at least 5% of scored lanes positive (net > $0.10 per day per $100) in the verdict case in at least 2 of the passes. The probe spec then is: the best 3 lanes, $100 each, hard prices, kill after the first 20 fills with negative net or after 5 days with total reward under $2; worst day = the full loss of the resting capital of any one market (stated in $).
A positive result here is NOT a trading result: no reward has been paid to this account, the pessimistic Q and fills are modelled, and the book is sampled a few times. The result states what a probe would have to measure (paid reward vs the model) before any size.
