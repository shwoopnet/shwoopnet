## Families (fixed, counted before running; futures ledger N now 160 + 20-30 more here)
(1) Time-series trend: sign of the trailing return over 60/120/250 days per root, vol-targeted. (2) Carry/roll: sign and size of the C1-C2 spread (backwardation long) per root, vol-targeted. (3) Cross-sectional momentum: rank the 22 roots on the 120-day return, long the top third and short the bottom third, vol-targeted. Portfolio of equal risk per root (inverse 60-day vol), 10% annual vol target, rebalanced daily at the settle with next-day returns, contract identity roll as in FUT1 (zero return on a roll day, one extra side). Costs per contract per side: commission and exchange fee 2.50 plus 1 tick slippage (tick values from contract specs, to be fixed in the amendment before scoring) with a x2 stress. Windows by thirds of dates; the last third held out and read once; bar: net Sharpe >= 0.5 and PF >= 1.5 on daily P&L in W1 and W2 each, month-block bootstrap p < 0.05/N. Prediction: zero survivors on the PF 1.5 bar (about 80%); a diversified trend/carry portfolio may reach Sharpe 0.3 to 0.6 net but not PF 1.5 in each window.
