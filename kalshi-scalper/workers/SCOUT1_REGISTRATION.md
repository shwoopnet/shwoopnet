## 3. MLBKS1: the idea, the rule, the look, the probability rule, the fit rule, the trade
**Idea.** Kalshi lists, for each announced starting pitcher, rungs "N+ strikeouts" (YES if strikeouts >= N, N from 2 to about 10). The far rungs cost 3c to 30c. A pitcher's strikeouts are a count with more spread than a Poisson and a mean that moves with the pitcher's recent starts and the team he faces. If a game-log negative binomial says a cheap rung is at least three times likelier than its ask, buy it and hold it.
**Who is on the other side.** Retail buyers of a famous pitcher's low rungs and market makers who set far rungs with a thin, round-number tail; honest prior that the quotes already carry this model plus lineups, rest days and the pitch count plan, which the model cannot see (stated as a blind spot).
**Universe.** Series KXMLBKS, markets with `floor_strike`, settled `yes`/`no`, event date (US Eastern, from the ticker) and expected expiration before 2026-10-03T16:00Z. Regular season (ESPN season type 2) and postseason (type 3) games that are FINAL and start before the cut. **The frozen third (games starting at or after 2026-10-03T16:00Z) is never fetched or joined**; every query is bounded by the cut.
**Reference data.** ESPN `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/baseball/mlb/scoreboard?dates=YYYYMMDD` for 2026-03-25 to 2026-10-02 and `summary?event=ID`: for each pitcher the line (IP, K, starter flag), for each team the batting total K. 1 request a second, one thread, own User-Agent, cache outside the repo (`data/scout1.sqlite` is git-ignored like the others).
**Matching.** Market name = `yes_sub_title` before the colon, normalised (lower case, accents folded, punctuation and Jr/Sr/II/III removed); ticker date (US Eastern); ESPN game where a pitcher of that normalised name appears in the pitching block within one day of the ticker date; the closest date wins. Unmatched markets are counted and dropped, the match rate is printed.
**Split.** Matched games sorted by start time: **FIT = first two thirds**, **TEST = last third**, TEST halves **T1** (older) and **T2** (newer) by game count. Nothing in TEST is used to fit anything.
**Model (fixed now; the grid is the only freedom and is fitted on FIT).** For pitcher p in game g (start time t): prior = p's strikeouts in his earlier STARTS in the 2026 ESPN data before t (oldest first), at least 3 required, else the market is skipped. m10 = mean of the last up to 10; n10 = how many. `mu0 = (n10 x m10 + k x m_lg) / (n10 + k)` with m_lg the FIT mean of starters' strikeouts. Opponent factor: the opposing team's batting strikeouts per game over its last up to 20 games before t, shrunk `(sum + 10 x lgK) / (n + 10)` with lgK the FIT league mean; `f = (oppK / lgK)^gamma`. `mu = mu0 x f`. X ~ NegativeBinomial(mean mu, variance mu + mu^2 / r). `P(YES) = P(X > floor_strike) = nb_sf(floor_strike, mu, r)`. **Fit:** (k, r, gamma) from k in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}, r in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64}, gamma in {0, 0.5, 1}, maximising the FIT log likelihood of the actual strikeouts of every FIT start (the pitcher's start line, one count per start, at least 3 priors). Chosen values are printed.
**Look.** The last 1 minute candle of the market ending at or before **ESPN game start minus 300 seconds** with a valid two sided quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1 and ask - bid <= 0.10). The cheap side is YES if its ask is in [0.03, 0.30]; else NO if 1 - bid is in [0.03, 0.30]; else no candidate. The other side is never evaluated or printed.
**Sample.** All matched TEST rungs of a game (a game has about 14 rungs, under the cap of 40 by sha1 of the ticker that is kept for safety).
**Treated trade.** Buy the cheap side at its ask, **C = 10 contracts, hard limit 0.30, held to settlement**, when **model probability of that side >= 3 x ask**. At most **2 per game** (highest ratio, ties by sha1 of the ticker). **Control:** every cheap side of the same sample, same look, no model condition, also capped at 2 per game by sha1. Both reported.
**Fee.** 7% x C x p x (1 - p) rounded up to the cent, divided by C; stress x1.2. Taker at the ask. Markets whose result is neither yes nor no (voided pitcher scratches) are dropped and counted.
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract after fee (cents) over treated trades, with the standard error and z clustered by game; PF = gross wins over gross losses after fee; win rate against mean price paid; treated minus control; T1 and T2 separately; fees x1.2. Information only (not a result): the model's calibration on all TEST rungs (Brier of the model vs the quote mid on the rungs that have a quote), the match rate, the gap p_side - ask.

## 5. Kill rules
- **FALSIFIED** if, with at least 150 treated trades on at least 25 games, the pooled treated mean net after fees is not above 0 in BOTH T1 and T2, or PF is under 1.5.
- **NOT_ENOUGH_DATA** if under 150 treated trades or under 25 games with a treated trade: report n, the months to go at the observed rate, and the forward plan below.
- A pass also needs the game-clustered z of the treated mean at the deflated bar (about 5.27), positive at fees x1.2, and a treated mean above the control mean in both halves. Only then may the frozen third be opened (it is nearly all October postseason, so it is thin: about 40 games).
- If the match rate is under 80% or the ESPN fetch covers under 80% of regular season games, the verdict is NOT_RUNNABLE and row #2 is taken as an amendment (registered before any of its outcomes).
- A failed fetch is retried but not replaced by a different rule. Nothing in the model, the look, the band or the ratio is edited after the first TEST candle is read.
