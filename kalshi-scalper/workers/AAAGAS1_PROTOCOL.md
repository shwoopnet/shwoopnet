# AAAGAS1 (registered Nov 10, 2026, 10:00 AM CT, before any gas strike candle or outcome is read): daily AAA national gas ladder vs a persistence model
Research only, public keyless Kalshi data, no order. Cheap side only (YES ask 3c to 30c). Not favourite-and-hold.
Series: KXAAAGASD (national daily "AAA price above K", strikes every 0.005 dollars; ticker date D, closes 04:59Z on D). Data: Kalshi /historical and /markets settled lists (results), hourly candles for chosen strikes.
Free information source built from the markets themselves: each settled day's ladder brackets the AAA value v_D between the highest YES-settled strike and the lowest NO-settled strike (width 0.005; v_D = midpoint). No outside data is used.
Model, fixed now: at decision time (the candle ending closest to 12 h before the close, ties earlier), the latest known value is v_{D-1}. Two variants: M0 persistence (mean = v_{D-1}); M1 persistence plus momentum (mean = v_{D-1} + 0.5*(v_{D-1} - v_{D-2})). Standard deviation = the sample sd of the previous 20 daily changes v_d - v_{d-1} (needs 20 earlier days, so the first 20 days are warm-up and unscored), floored at 0.003. Model P(v_D > K) = 1 - Phi((K - mean)/sd).
Universe (chosen without any outcome of day D): for each day D, strikes K with v_{D-1} - 0.020 <= K <= v_{D-1} + 0.060 on the 0.005 grid (17 strikes). 
Entry: buy YES at the decision candle's ask when 0.03 <= ask <= 0.30, spread <= 0.05, and model P exceeds ask + fee + 0.02 (fee = ceil(0.07*C*p*(1-p)*100)/C/100, C = 10). Hold to settlement, one contract. The NO side is the favourite side and is not tested.
Test: cash PF first, mean cents per contract after fee, event-clustered z (cluster = day); development = first two thirds of scored days by date, last third held out and read ONCE only if development shows PF >= 1.5 with n >= 100 trades on at least 30 days and z at or above the bar. Cells 2 (M0, M1); ledger N about 5,156 + 2 = 5,158, z bar about 4.27.
Prediction before running: fails (prior 8%). Reason: the ladder is 0.005 wide and priced by makers who see the same v_{D-1}; the cheap strikes that clear the 2c edge will mostly be the ones where the model and the market agree the move is large.
