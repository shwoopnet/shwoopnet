## Data and conventions (all tests)

Copy in `workers/stockperp/data/candles_1m.csv.gz` (fetched 2026-11-06 22:21Z; the pilot date and later dates are the only ones the scorer loads).
- Cash: `origin/perp-data:cash1m/<date>.csv.gz` (Yahoo 1 minute bars incl. pre and post). cash(T) = close of the bar that STARTS at T-60 (convention tested in CASHW_COMMON: 0.991 same-minute correlation for the index perps). Perp price level is a scaled number (AAPL perp 6.87 against about 270 cash), so a perp-to-cash gap is always measured against a scale: ln(cash / perp mid) median over a reference span (SPAUCT1, SPHALT1 say which); returns need no scale.
- Universe, chosen on COST only and frozen: the 19 single names and ETFs of PERPEQ's U (median spread at most 5 bps in `PERPEQ_COSTMAP.csv`): XOM QQQ AAPL SPY SPCX GOOGL MU NFLX NVDA TSLA KO MSFT COST AMZN VZ CVX PG PLTR META (the two index perps left out). A quote whose spread exceeds 3 times its cost-map median is skipped (counted).
- Cost: buy at the close ask, sell at the close bid, plus 12 bp a side (placeholder, NOT confirmed against Kalshi's fee page), also 14.4 and 24; break-even fee per side reported. Funding is ignored for holds under 2 hours except SPW1 (a weekend, no 21:00Z stamp lies between Sunday 18:10 and Monday 09:25 ET except Monday 21:00Z, after the exit, so none is crossed).
- Control for every test: the same entries with the direction reversed.
- **Cut.** Pilot date: trading date 2026-11-05 only (the first full cash day with live stock quotes); it is read now, for information, with no verdict. Development: trading dates 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-30 (Thanksgiving 11-26 closed, 11-27 half day), read ONCE on **2026-12-01** (the scorer refuses `dev` before that date and never loads a date on or after 2026-12-01). ## SPLAG1: the stock perp lags the cash tape (price-insensitive: cash market makers and index arbitrageurs move the cash first)
Mechanism and counterparty: in cash hours the cash book is far deeper than a perp book with a $1,000 to $10,000 touch; if the perp maker is slow, a cash move of 3 minutes is only partly in the perp, and a taker who sees the cash can trade the remainder. The slow side is the perp maker quoting from a thin book. Honest alternative (and the CASHW and CMD results for index perps and metals): the maker re-quotes inside the minute, so there is no gap to trade, and 24 bp round trip swamps any residual.
Rule: every minute T from 09:36 to 15:54 ET for each name: s_c = ln(cash(T) / cash(T-3m)), r_p = ln(mid(T) / mid(T-3m)), both in bp, gap = s_c - r_p. Trigger: |s_c| at least 15 bp, gap same sign as s_c, |gap| at least max(10 bp, 3 times the quote's spread in bp). Trade the perp in the sign of the gap at T, exit at T+3 minutes. One trade per name per 15 minutes. Control: reverse.
Prediction: triggers 19 names x 6 hours x 60 x P about 0.003 = about 20 a day (range 5 to 60), gross at mid +1 bp (range -3 to +6), after crossing about -4, net at 12 bp a side about -28, PF under 0.3, break-even fee under 2 bp. Lead lag correlation (information): peak at shift 0 above 0.85 for the liquid names, nothing at other shifts above 0.1. NOT_ENOUGH_DATA at 2026-12-01 only if under 100 trades or 30 dates: with about 20 a day the trade bar is met within a week and the cluster bar (30 dates) on 2026-12-18; at 2026-12-01 about 17 dates exist so the verdict is NOT_ENOUGH_DATA (clusters), figures kept; a rerun on the same frozen rule at 30 dates is allowed, the held-out start moves only by a new registration. Probabilities: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA 85%, FALSIFIED 14%, LEAD 1%.

## SPAUCT1: convergence to the cash auction prints (price-insensitive flow: market-on-open and market-on-close orders)
Mechanism and counterparty: the 09:30 opening auction and the 16:00 closing auction absorb orders that must trade at that print whatever it is; the print can sit away from the perp's last continuous quote, and the perp then converges. The loser is the perp taker or maker still quoting the pre-auction price. Honest alternative: the perp already reflects the auction within a minute and the spread (10 bp median in the first minutes after 09:30, 16 bp after 16:00 in the descriptive table) is the whole gap.
Rule: slot open at T = 09:31 ET, slot close at T = 16:00 ET. d = ln(cash(T) / perp mid(T)) - scale, in bp, scale = median of ln(cash / mid) on a 5 minute grid over 10:00 to 15:30 ET of the previous cash day (open slot; 2026-11-05 has no previous perp day, so the slot is NOT_RUNNABLE on the pilot date, counted) or of the same day (close slot; a past span). Trigger: |d| at least max(15 bp, 3 times the quote spread). Trade the perp toward the cash (d > 0: buy at the ask) at T, exit at T+5 minutes. Control: reverse. Pooled verdict over both slots; each slot is also read separately (information).
Prediction: triggers about 19 x 2 x 0.25 = about 9 a day (range 2 to 20, the close slot in a 16 bp spread, hence the screen); gross at mid +2 bp (range -5 to +10), net at 12 bp a side about -30, PF under 0.3. NOT_ENOUGH_DATA on clusters at 2026-12-01 (about 17 dates), probabilities NOT_ENOUGH_DATA 85%, FALSIFIED 14%, LEAD 1%.

## SPLIQ1: a perp-only jump with the cash flat (forced flow: liquidations and stop orders on a levered book)
Mechanism and counterparty: a perp position is levered (about 5 times at the margin, `leverage_estimate`), so a thin-book move triggers liquidations and stops that are forced and price-insensitive; if the cash tape did not move, nothing fundamental did, and the perp should come back. The loser is the liquidated holder. Honest alternative: a perp-only move is information the Yahoo cash tape has not printed yet (a 1 minute bar is a coarse reading) and it continues.
Rule: every minute T from 09:40 to 15:50 ET: r1 = ln(mid(T) / mid(T-1m)), c1 = ln(cash(T) / cash(T-1m)), in bp. Trigger: |r1| at least max(15 bp, 5 times the spread) and |c1| at most 0.25 |r1|. Trade against the jump at T, exit at T+5 minutes. One trade per name per 30 minutes. Control: continuation.
Prediction: triggers about 2 a day (range 0 to 8), gross at mid +4 bp (range -10 to +15), net about -28 bp, PF under 0.4. Verdicts: NOT_ENOUGH_DATA at 2026-12-01 (about 30 to 40 trades by then) 92%, FALSIFIED 7%, LEAD 1%. Date the 100 trade bar is met at 2 a day: about 2027-01-20.

## SPFUND1: the settlement of the daily funding (forced payers: longs when the rate is positive)
Mechanism and counterparty: funding is paid once a day at 21:00Z (16:00 ET in standard time; 4 funding stamps are available, 124 rates, 78% of them zero, mean +0.03 bp a day, maximum 5.5, cap 200 bp, deadband 0.2 bp). A holder who does not want to pay a positive rate closes before the stamp and may re-open after it; if that flow is forced it leaves a dip into the stamp and a rebound after. Honest alternative: the rate (0 to 5 bp) is far below the spread and the 16:00 hour spread (16 bp median) swallows it; the 16:00 effect is also the cash close, which confounds it with SPAUCT1 (said now, not hidden).
Rule: for each name and each funding stamp whose PREVIOUS stamp rate (known) was at least +2 bp: buy at the ask of the candle ending 1 minute after the stamp, sell at the bid 30 minutes later. Control: sell then buy back (short). Names with rate under 2 bp or no previous stamp: no trade.
Prediction: stamps with a previous rate at least 2 bp: about 7 of 58 per day, about 1.5 among the 19; trades about 25 in development (range 5 to 50), gross at mid +1 bp, net about -30 bp, PF under 0.2. NOT_ENOUGH_DATA 97%. Information that matters: CARRY0, the mean funding per name per day against a cash borrow cost of about 0.3 bp a day (a 1% annual fee), unhedged: no cash leg exists on Kalshi, so a funding harvest is a price bet carrying 100 to 200 bp of daily volatility for 0 to 5 bp of carry.

## SPW1: the Sunday reopening of the weekend gap (forced flow: weekend news takers in a book that has been shut since Friday 17:00 ET)
Mechanism and counterparty: the perp is the only market that trades the stock from Sunday 18:00 ET to Monday 04:00 ET pre-market; the first minutes after the reopening are a thin book repricing a whole weekend. Faded into Monday 09:25 ET (before the cash open, so no cash price is needed). The loser is the Sunday evening taker. Honest alternative: the weekend gap is real news and the Monday cash open confirms it (PERPEQ1 reads Monday's 08:55 to 10:00 only).
Rule: for each name and each Monday date: Te = candle ending 18:10 ET Sunday, Tf = the last quote at or before 16:55 ET Friday, g = ln(mid(Te) / mid(Tf)). Trigger: |g| at least max(25 bp, 5 times the spread at Te). Trade against the gap at Te, exit at the candle ending 09:25 ET Monday. Control: continuation.
Prediction: the first weekend (2026-11-08) is in the development window: 19 names x 0.5 = about 9 trades per weekend, about 130 by 2026-12-01 only if all 4 weekends gave 9 (they will give about 36), so NOT_ENOUGH_DATA on clusters (4 weekends against 30) 99%; gross at mid +3 bp (range -30 to +30), net about -35 bp. The 30 weekend cluster bar is met on 2027-06-06.

## SPHALT1: the cash print gap and its resumption (forced flow: the halt resumption auction)
Mechanism and counterparty: when a single stock is halted in the cash market the perp keeps trading, so the perp prices the news alone; the resumption auction then sets a deep price. The loser is the perp taker during the halt. Detection (no halt feed here): in cash hours 09:30 to 16:00 ET, 5 or more consecutive missing Yahoo 1 minute bars in a name that printed at least 90% of the day's minutes. Honest alternative: Yahoo gaps are mostly data holes, not halts, and halts are rare.
Rule: T = the minute of the first bar after the gap plus 60 s; d = ln(cash(T) / perp mid(T)) - scale (scale: same-day 10:00 to 15:00 ET median); trigger |d| at least 20 bp; trade the perp toward the cash at T, exit T+5 minutes. Control: reverse.
Prediction: gap events 0 to 3 a month in the 19 names; trades under 5 in development; NOT_ENOUGH_DATA 99%. Date 100 trades: not within 2027 at this rate (a count of events is the output).
