# NONCRYPTO1 protocol (V2, Nov 11, 2026, registered before any score). Owner order via main: non-crypto 15 minute series from historical settled data, no waiting for the recorder.
Card: NONCRYPTO1 | feature sweep, cheap side, non-crypto 15 min | risk class win rate 10 to 30%, loss = the cheap price | stage Idea. Uniqueness: different markets (metals, oil, gas, forex, small crypto-adjacent), different field set than L1; the entry is cheap side (owner rule), not favourite-late; correlation not measurable on history; not declined.
Data held (settled with candles): silver, WTI, copper, natural gas, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NEAR, ZEC, HYPE, BNB (alts2, alts3, alts4, fresh, rep sqlite; deduped by ticker). Gold is the live market and excluded from the test (used only as a reference for F9). No Polymarket, ESPN, NHL, MLB or NBA.com data.
Rules: exactly SWEEP1's (workers/SWEEP1_PROTOCOL.md): cheap side ask 0.04 to 0.30, spread <= 0.03, 10 and 6 minutes before close, HOLD and TP(+0.15) exits, fee 0.07 p (1-p) unrounded, F1 to F8 and F10 bins unchanged. F9 changes: side agrees with gold's 3 minute yes-mid direction for silver, platinum, palladium, copper; with GBP/USD for EUR/USD and the reverse; other series no F9.
Added: per-series cheap-side calibration (hold, 10 minute entry, all bands) as 15 extra tests, descriptive of where any longshot bias differs.
Tests: about 112 + 15 = 127. Shared N: about 800,000 (README ledger, z bar about 5.0 to 5.3) and about 7,731 (V2 + Refinement, z about 4.5); both bars reported.
Split: per series by close time, first 2/3 development, last 1/3 held out and read once, only for cells that pass development: cash PF >= 1.3, n >= 100, event z above the bar. A short list of at most 3 follows only from survivors; if none, one line.
