# INPLAYJUMP clarifying line: tennis "in play" is a property of the MATCH minute (Nov 13, 2026; Desk A on the Head of R&D's ruling of 00:59Z)
Made after the pilot's counts and the second build's comparison, before any development candle of the full pull was read; no threshold, band, window or rule number changed.
The registered sentence is "A minute t is in play if at least 3 of the 20 one-minute steps ending at t - 1 moved the mid 2c or more." It names no contract. Ruling (Head): MATCH-WIDE. At the jump test, a tennis minute t is in play if EITHER contract of the match has at least 3 such steps (the same reading `first_in_play` and the placebo already used); a candidate jumper no longer needs its own contract to pass the alive rule. The clock families (e-soccer, table tennis, e-basketball) do NOT use the alive rule at all (in play = at or after S + 1 minute), so they are unchanged by construction.
