## 4. Rules (fixed now)
**Look.** Per market (1+ rung, strike 0.5, of a STARTER, open at least 35 minutes before kickoff): the last 1 minute candle ending at least **1,800 s before kickoff** with a valid two sided quote (0 <= bid < ask <= 1, ask - bid <= 0.10), candle window from the market open time to kickoff - 1,800 s. None: not available (counted). Lineups are assumed public at least 45 minutes before kickoff (release time not archived; stated as an assumption, and a quote at 30 minutes could have moved on the news, favouring the market).
**Cheap side only.** YES with ask in [0.03, 0.30], taker at the ask, C = 10, fee 0.07 x p x (1-p) rounded up per 10, divided by C (x1.2 stress), held to settlement; win from the Kalshi market `result`, read only after this file is pushed. NO side never evaluated or printed.
**Arm U (unconditional, +2).** Every cheap YES rung of a starter (any outfield or goalkeeper position, all counted; by-position table is information), at most 2 per match by lowest sha1 of the ticker (no price used). Fetch is only for starters: no non-starter market is fetched, so "starter" against "not starter" is NOT tested; U says only what cheap starters do.
**Arm M (model, +2).** Players A or M only. FIT = the first two thirds of the 104 matches by kickoff (ties by ESPN id); TEST = the last third. ESPN FIT-match scoring plays (own goals excluded) and roster sub clocks give per player goals g and minutes m (starter 90 unless subbed out, a sub 90 minus the clock he came on; if no clock is present 90 for a starter and 25 for a sub). Rate per 90 = (g + K x r_pos) / (m/90 + K) with K = 3 and r_pos the pooled FIT goals per 90 of that position group; E_min = pooled FIT mean minutes of starters of that group; **P(goal | starter) = 1 - exp(-rate x E_min / 90)**. TREATED when P >= 3 x ask (ask in 3c to 30c), max 2 per match by the 2 largest P/ask, held. No tuning of K, 3x or the cap. Scored on the TEST third only (the model never sees TEST). **Control for M:** every cheap YES starter rung (A or M, uncapped) at the same look in the TEST matches; the model arm must beat it.
**Descriptive, no trade:** win rate against mean ask by ask bucket (3-10c, 10-20c, 20-30c); gap model minus ask; ladder sum per match = sum of the fetched starter 1+ asks against the number of those starters that scored (Kalshi results); by league; by position group.
**Statistic.** Mean net per contract after fee, SE and z clustered by match AND by ET event date (the larger is used), lower 95% bound = mean - 1.96 x match-clustered SE; PF after fee; halves D1, D2 = trades split at the median kickoff date; fees x1.2. Scored ONCE.
