# REGIME1 (registered Nov 9, 2026, before any regime cut is scored): does gold L1 (and OICORE) work only in some market states?
Research only; the live per-order report (read-only function), kept in the scratchpad, never committed; only aggregates are written. Cash basis (fill + fee, exits at the exit price and fee), profit factor first.
Classifiers (observable at order time, no lookahead, thresholds FROZEN now from the FIRST 100 gold L1 rows, Oct 7 to Oct 21, which are then excluded from the test): R1 spread tight (seenAsk - seenBid <= 0.001, the first-100 median) vs wide; R2 clock: US day 07:00 to 16:59 Eastern vs other hours; R3 weekday vs weekend (Eastern date); R4 momentum: |rise3| >= 0.05 "moving" vs < 0.05 "still" (rise3 = the bought side's mid change over the previous 3 minutes; orders without it are skipped for R4); R5 depth: askSize <= 1,500 (the first-100 median) vs above. An event-day regime (CPI, jobs, FOMC) is NOT tested: no verified calendar in hand.
Test set: gold L1 settled orders 101 to 307 (Oct 21 to Nov 9), split by time: development = rows 101 to 204, held out = rows 205 to 307 (read ONCE, only for a cut that passes development). Cuts: 5 regimes x 2 states = 10 cuts on gold; OICORE (77 orders in total) is listed but cannot reach 50 per state (NOT_ENOUGH_DATA, not run). N = 4,920 (single-leg ledger) + 10 = 4,930. PASS in development (a regime-specific candidate): cash PF >= 1.5, n >= 50 inside the state, net >= +$5 after fees; then held out: PF >= 1.3 and net > 0 with n >= 30. Anything else: noise. Lead time reported separately: for each regime the share of consecutive gold orders (next window) that stay in the same state (persistence) and whether the state is known before the window opens (R2, R3 calendar: yes, days ahead; R1, R4, R5 from the quote at order time: seconds, so they switch the order in or out, not the strategy for the day).
Prediction: noise: with 10 cuts and about 50 orders a state, a PF of 1.5 appears by chance in 1 or 2 cuts and does not hold in the held-out half (about 90%).
