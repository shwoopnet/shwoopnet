# REGIME1 protocol (V2, Nov 11, 2026, registered before any score). Regime and time-of-day tags on the cheap side.
Card: REGIME1 | regime tags change the market, not one feature | cheap side only, win rate 10 to 30%, loss = the cheap price | stage Idea. Research only, no live change, no Kalshi call, no Polymarket/ESPN/NHL/MLB/NBA.com/Rotten Tomatoes. The favourite side is NOT computed under any regime (owner rule).
Data held: the 15 NONCRYPTO1 series (silver, WTI, copper, natural gas, platinum, palladium, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NEAR, ZEC, HYPE, BNB) plus BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE = 19 series. Gold is loaded for nothing and is excluded. Loaders and dedupe by ticker as in `noncrypto1.py`; BTC and ETH from `data/hist15`.
Entry and exits (as SWEEP1): cheap side ask 0.04 to 0.30, spread <= 0.03, one contract, at the candle 10 min (570 to 640 s) or 6 min (330 to 400 s) before close. Exits: HOLD, TP (sell at entry + 0.15 on any later candle high, optimistic). Fee unrounded 0.07 p(1-p), on the sale too when sold early. Basis: CASH (dollars per contract after fee), PF = gross wins / gross losses of the cash results.
Groups: CRYP (BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE), ALT (HYPE, BNB, NEAR, ZEC), MET (silver, platinum, palladium, copper), ENE (WTI, natural gas), FX (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD). Every regime bin is scored for POOLED (all 19) and for each of the 5 groups (6 scopes); R1 and R2 also per series (19 more scopes, 25 in all).
Regimes (bins fixed now, all from information known at the entry look):
 R1 volatility: v = mean absolute 1 minute change of the YES mid over all candles of the series' previous 16 windows (the 4 hours before this window; needs at least 8 of the 16 windows present). Terciles per series with cut points from that series' DEVELOPMENT markets only; bins low / mid / high. (The settled results of the same 4 hours enter through R4, not here.)
 R2 session, from UTC close time of the window: weekend = Friday 22:00 UTC to Sunday 22:00 UTC (metals, FX, oil, gas close then; applied to all series for one rule); otherwise Asia 00:00 to 08:00, Europe 08:00 to 13:30, US cash 13:30 to 20:00, late 20:00 to 24:00 UTC. 5 bins.
 R3 event day, coarse, no scraping: JOBS = the first Friday of the UTC month; CPI = weekdays dated the 10th to the 14th (a coarse stand-in for the mid-month release); OTHER. FOMC dates cannot be derived from the calendar and are NOT used (stated gap). 3 bins.
 R4 streak: the series' previous k windows (contiguous 15 minute closes) all settled the same direction, k = 2, 3, 4 (at least k, so the bins nest), crossed with the bought side being WITH the streak or AGAINST it. 6 bins.
 R5 spread: wide = spread 0.02 or 0.03 at the look; tight = 0.01. 2 bins.
Cells: R1 3 x 25 = 75; R2 5 x 25 = 125; R3 3 x 6 = 18; R4 6 x 6 = 36; R5 2 x 6 = 12; total 266 cells x 2 leads x 2 exits = 1,064 tests (cells with no rows are counted and reported as empty).
Shared N: 7,731 (V2 + Refinement ledger) + 1,064 = 8,795, Bonferroni z bar = inv_cdf(1 - 0.05/8,795) about 4.4; and 800,780 + 1,064 = 801,844 (README ledger), z bar about 5.0 to 5.3. Both reported; z is event clustered (one event per market per lead).
Split: per series closes sorted, first two thirds development, last third held out, read ONCE and only for cells that survive development.
Survive = development cash PF >= 1.5 AND n >= 100 AND event z above the bar. Reported: tests run, cells with n >= 100, how many pass PF >= 1.5 and n >= 100, how many pass each z bar, best three cells, survivors with held-out numbers. Script `src/scalper/regime1.py`. Nothing here can change a live rule; a survivor would go to the uniqueness test and the declined list first.
