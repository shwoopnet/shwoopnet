# EARLYCLOSE1 protocol (V2, Nov 11, 2026, registered before any score)
Card: EARLYCLOSE1 | markets that can resolve before their stated close: calibration of price vs outcome and time to resolution | hold to settlement | risk class: cheap side win rate 10 to 30%, 1 contract | stage Idea. Declined list (7e) checked: not favourite-late (band stops at 0.88), no Polymarket, ESPN, NHL, MLB, NBA.com data (data/espn1.sqlite and data/nhlg1.sqlite are NOT used).
FINDING BEFORE SCORING (structure only, no outcome read): the `can_close_early` flag in the USWEEP2 crawl and 60 day census (scratchpad us2/census.jsonl, 371,450 markets) is TRUE on every listed market, so it cannot name an early-close market. What can: a market whose close_time is not on a whole minute and is before its expected_expiration_time was closed when its event was decided (264,569 of 371,450, 99% Sports, then Mentions 1,752). That is known only AFTER the fact, so it is a descriptor, never a trade filter. The tradable ex ante class is the SERIES: ECS = series whose census markets are early closed in >= 80% of cases (computed from close timestamps only, no outcome); CONTROL = series under 10%.
Descriptive output (not cells): distribution of expected_expiration minus close_time for early closed markets, by category.
Data: census (close, expected expiration, result, early descriptor) joined to stored Kalshi candles (bid, ask) in data/sports.sqlite, sports1.sqlite, flb1.sqlite, theta1.sqlite, newlist1.sqlite (table cd). These candle files were fetched by earlier studies, so the sample is not random; stated as a limit. No API calls.
Entry: the candle nearest to expected_expiration minus H (H 6 h or 2 h; candle spacing is mostly 1 hour, tolerance 1800 s), only if that candle is before close_time minus 600 s (market still open, known at the time); two sided, spread <= 0.05; fill at the ask, taker; side by band CHEAP (ask 0.04 to 0.30, YES or NO) or MID (0.30 to 0.88); hold to settlement; fee 0.07 p (1 - p) unrounded, cash, 1 contract.
Cells: group {ECS, CONTROL} x H {6, 2} x band {CHEAP, MID} = 8, plus a calibration table for ECS (mid bins 0.05 wide up to 0.50 of the cheaper side, 10 bins, counted) = 18 cells. Bars: shared N about 1,100,000 + 18, z about 5.34; N 8,795, z about 4.39.
Split: per series by close time, first two thirds development, last third held out and read ONCE, only for survivors (cash PF >= 1.5 AND n >= 100 AND day-clustered z above the bar). Verdicts: SURVIVOR, FALSIFIED (n >= 100 and PF < 1.0), NOT_ENOUGH_DATA with the date when more candles could exist.
Numeric prediction: calibration holds within 2 points on the cheap side (longshot bias costs about the fee); no cell passes; prior of a survivor 2%.
Script: src/scalper/earlyclose1.py. Research only.
