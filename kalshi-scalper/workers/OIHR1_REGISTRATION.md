## Mechanism and counterparty
Makers on a ladder of about 16 strikes quote each strike from a model of spot and rarely reprice a far strike when new positions arrive. New open interest in the last 10 minutes means someone is building a position with information or a view about where the hour will close. The counterparty is the maker who sold the cheap tail at a stale price. Prior for this working: low (OICORE's own mechanism is unproven, `workers/MECHOI1_RESULT.md`; every transfer of it to another market failed). The spot-direction version (Try A) is the one mechanism with a stated direction; the cheaper-side version (Try B) is the literal OICORE copy.

## Definitions (one definition, no tuning)
- **Universe (by liquidity, fixed before outcomes):** KXBTCD, every hourly event in `data/conf2_lad.sqlite` with a stated result, the local candles giving the 55 minutes before each close (about 16 near-the-money strikes per event). KXETHD: local candles are 2 offsets only (`ladders.sqlite`) and `ladl1_KXETHD_*` hold only quotes at 600 s and 360 s, so no 30 minute open interest history exists: **not runnable, no fetch made** (a fetch is about 20,000 market candle calls; not worth it before BTC says anything). KXGOLDH: candles start 900 s before the close, so no 30 minute history: not runnable. KXETHD and KXGOLDH are stated as untestable here.
- **Look:** the one minute candle ending 1200 s (20 minutes) before the close. Back: the candle ending 1800 s before the close. (25 minutes is NOT also tried.)
- **Statistic S:** for the strikes that have an `oi` value at BOTH candles, S = (sum of oi at the look) / (sum of oi at the back) - 1. An event with fewer than 6 such strikes, or a zero back sum, is not eligible.
- **Trigger:** S is strictly above the 90th percentile (nearest rank, `ceil(0.9 n)`-th smallest) of S over the eligible events that closed in the 24 hours before this event's look time. Fewer than 12 events in that trailing set: the event is not eligible (no trigger, no control). Strictly relative and trailing, so nothing drifts.
- **Control:** every eligible event that is NOT a trigger (S at or under its own trailing p90), traded by the identical rule.
- **Centre and spot lean:** the centre is the strike at which the YES mid (average of YES bid and YES ask at the look candle, both present, ask above bid) crosses 0.5, interpolated linearly between the two bracketing strikes (it needs a crossing). The lean is up if the centre at the look is above the centre at the back candle, down if below, no trade if equal or the back centre cannot be formed. (No spot feed is local for the whole period; the ladder's own centre is the stated proxy for "where spot moved", tracking spot through the digital prices.)
- **Try A (lean side):** if up, the candidates are YES on strikes above the centre whose YES ask at the look is from 0.03 to 0.30; if down, NO on strikes below the centre whose NO ask (1 minus the YES bid) at the look is from 0.03 to 0.30. Take the candidate with the highest ask (nearest the money). One trade per event.
- **Try B (cheaper side):** for each strike take the side with the lower ask at the look (YES first on a tie); candidates are those whose cheaper-side ask is from 0.03 to 0.30; take the candidate with the highest such ask (nearest the money). One trade per event. Counted as a second try.
- **Fill:** at that side's ask at the NEXT candle (ending 1140 s before the close). Hard limit 0.30: if that ask is above 0.30, the quote is missing or one sided (bid 0 with ask 0.97 style), no trade. Held to settlement. Fee 7% x C x p x (1-p) rounded up per order, C = 10 (`gen6.fee`); x1.2 stress reported.
- **Windows (by event time):** the eligible events sorted by close time are cut into thirds by count: W1 first, W2 second, W3 last third **frozen and unread** (read only if W1 and W2 both pass the kill rules, which will not happen at these counts). Halves for the trigger-minus-control test: W1 and W2.
