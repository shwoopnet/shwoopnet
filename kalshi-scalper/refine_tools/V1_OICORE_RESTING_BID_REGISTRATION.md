# V1: OICORE resting bid on the cheap Bitcoin side (a 7g VERSION of OICORE). REGISTERED Nov 13, 2026 BEFORE ANY SCORING. Nothing live changes. No live figure in this file.
Ordered by the Head of R&D after the owner's "okay go" on the limit-entry versions (via the EA). Source of the idea: Forensics' measurement on recorded R6 books that a resting buy at the bid on the cheap Bitcoin side filled almost always over 349 windows WITHOUT OICORE's trigger (not yet checked by the Verification Analyst); that is a lead from non-trigger windows, selected after seeing it, so it proves nothing for the windows that fire OICORE.

## Parent (replayed, not changed)
OICORE: Bitcoin KXBTC15M window; trigger = open interest rise OI(candle ending close-180 s) minus OI(candle ending close-360 s) of 86,300 or more (public candles; the model trigger windows, as in OICORE_TRIGGER_WINDOW_TICKERS.csv); the CHEAPER side (the side with the lower own ask) at t0; band test: that side's ask at t0 is 0.05 to 0.50; entry TAKER at that ask at t0 (immediate); hold to settlement; 1 contract per signal in this replay. The parent is replayed on the SAME trigger windows from the SAME book row, so the two are compared window for window.

## The version (the only change: how it enters)
- t0 = the first recorded R6 book row (KXBTC15M, top 5 each side, about every 2 s) received at or after close minus 178 s (the OI candle ending close minus 180 s is known by then).
- Same trigger, same cheaper side, same band test on the ask at t0 as the parent (so the windows are identical).
- LIMIT PRICE RULE: rest a BUY of that side at its OWN best bid at t0 (no improvement of the bid, no price chase). If that side has no bid, or the bid is 0, no order is placed (counted, not scored).
- REST TIME: from t0 until close minus 120 s. CANCEL at the first recorded book row received at or after close minus 120 s. No taker fallback, no re-price.
- FILL RULE (decided now, against the recorded book and tape, the through-price rule): the order fills only if, after t0 and before the cancel, (a) a public trade in that market prints STRICTLY through our price (buying YES at P: a taker sold YES at a yes_price below P; buying NO at cost Q: a taker bought YES at a yes_price above 1 minus Q), or (b) a later recorded book row shows that side's ask at or below our price. Touching our price without passing it does NOT fill. Queue position is ignored (strictly through needs none). Fill price = our limit price. A partial fill is not modelled (1 contract).
- EXIT: hold to settlement (no exit, no stop, no target).
- FEES: parent at the taker schedule 0.07*p*(1-p) unrounded with +0c and +1c slip; the version at the maker schedule, primary 25 percent of the taker schedule (MAKERREPLAY1's convention), with 0 percent and the taker schedule shown as sensitivities.

## Measures and the 7g bar (decided now)
Per block: trigger windows with a valid t0 row; orders placed; fill rate; VERSION PF per contract over its FILLED set; PARENT PF per contract over ALL the same trigger windows (taker at the ask at t0, +0c and +1c); PARENT PF on the windows where the version did NOT fill (adverse selection: the winners it missed); net per contract-week (7 day blocks from the block start) of the version (sum over its fills) against the parent (sum over all trigger windows); each over chronological halves of the trigger windows.
7g BAR, all four: (1) version PF beats parent PF by 0.10 or more at +1c; (2) version net per contract-week is not lower than the parent's; (3) the second half is not worse than the first (version minus parent in the second half is not below the first half's); (4) at least 60 filled signals. The bar applies to the DEVELOPMENT block first; the FORWARD block must also meet it for the version to be proposed to the owner. FALSIFIED on development if (2) fails or the missed windows' parent PF is above the filled windows' PF by 0.10 or more.

## Deflation, prior, honest limits
Tries added to the shared ledger: 1 (one limit rule, one rest time, one cancel time; fee variants are sensitivities, not tries); the shared strict ledger is about 2,113,000 (DEFLATION_LEDGER.md on rnd-queue); the 7g bar is a version bar, not the research bar z 5.46. Selection bias stated: the idea came from a measurement on non-trigger windows. PRIOR (stated before any data): the version fills most of the time, saves part of a cent to about a cent a contract against the ask, and is likely to miss the winners that run away (the lesson of MAKERREPLAY1, which failed on adverse selection for gold): P(passes the 7g bar on development) about 20 percent, P(also forward) about 10 percent. The version is a recorded-book replay; a live resting order would also need new order code and a risk review, so a pass only permits that proposal to the owner. Overlap: none with another card's held-out (a replay of a live parent, nothing new is held out).
