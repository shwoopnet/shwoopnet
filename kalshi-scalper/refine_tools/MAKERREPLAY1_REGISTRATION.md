# W-MAKERREPLAY1 registration (2026-11-10, written BEFORE any outcome was scored)
Data: R6 quote-data book15r6/ Oct 29 to Nov 10 (13 days), gold KXGOLD15M only. Book every ~2 s (best 5 levels), full trade tape (tape sum matches volume), result from coverage.csv. Windows with no result are dropped (counted).
Signal: last book poll received at or before close-360 s and within 10 s of it. Favourite = side with the higher mid. Signal if the favourite's ASK (1 - best opposite bid) is in [0.88, 0.97]. One signal per market.
Taker entry (comparison): buy favourite at ask, plus 0c and plus 1c slip, hold to settlement, 1 contract.
Maker rule: order time t0 = that poll's receive time. Rest a buy of the favourite at P = min(bid+0.01, ask-0.01) (joins the bid when the spread is 1c) for 60 s. No market fallback.
Fill (strictly through, a LATER observation only): (a) a tape trade after t0 and by t0+60 s that is strictly through P (favourite YES: taker sold YES at yes_price < P; favourite NO: taker bought YES at yes_price > 1-P), or (b) a later poll within 60 s where the favourite's ask <= P. Queue position ignored (strictly-through needs none). Fill price P.
Fees, unrounded, per contract: taker 0.07*p*(1-p); maker at 25 percent and 0 percent of that. Cash PF = sum of gains / sum of losses, 1 contract, hold to settlement (no 25c exit).
Reported: n signals, fill rate; filled-set PF at maker price (25 percent maker fee, primary; 0 percent and taker-fee variants); missed-set PF entered at taker ask +0c (primary) and +1c; taker PF on all signals (+0c primary, +1c); filled-set PF if those same filled signals had been taken at the taker ask (selection check).
PASS bar (fixed): fill rate >= 70 percent AND filled PF (25 percent maker) >= taker PF (+0c, all signals) + 0.1 AND missed-set PF (taker ask +0c) <= filled-set PF. n < 60 signals: DATA_LIMITED, read date = Nov 14 (full R6 to Nov 13) or Nov 24 for n >= 60 at the second-half check. Shadow only, no order, no trading code. N tried: 1 rule (one cell), basis rules +0c/+1c as in REFINE_RETRACTIONS.
