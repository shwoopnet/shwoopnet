Per-series markets pulled from BOTH GET /markets (settled since the 2026-09-12 cutoff, plus open) and GET /historical/markets (older), merged by ticker, for the top 350 series by lifetime volume (97.6% of volume): 28,064 markets, 7,150 settled (yes/no), 1,441 settled events, 319 series with a settled market. Per-series columns are in census.csv.

OPEN NOW, CLOSING IN THE NEXT 14 DAYS: none found. Among the 350 pulled series every open market closes 2027-01-01 or later (19,244 of them on 2027-11, the midterm-2028 style long dated books). A scan of GET /markets?status=open with a 14 day close window hit the 40 page cap (40,000 markets, mostly sports parlays) with 0 Elections series, so series outside the top 350 (2.4% of volume) were not checked. No scheduled election night is open inside the rule window now.
Caveats: (1) close_time on a settled market is the actual close, so early closers look short; (2) spread = yes ask minus yes bid on open markets only, blank when none; (3) candle check is a sample and uses closing quotes of daily candles; (4) volume is lifetime contracts; (5) series outside the top 350 not pulled.
VERDICT: the sample size for a calendar test is the number of election nights, not markets. 152 nights exist in total, but about 42 had a short open-to-close, and about 93 of 155 sampled nights show a tradable 10c to 90c price in the 1 to 14 day window, so a defensible estimate of usable independent nights is roughly 60 to 90, concentrated in 2025 to 2026 primaries and the Nov 2026 midterm. Markets per night are heavily correlated (a single night has hundreds), thousands of markets add almost nothing. Thin for a 5.2 z bar; fine only as a registered forward test.
