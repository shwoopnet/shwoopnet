## How to read the columns

- **Family** uses the labels the files themselves use. The backlog has no numbered family list, only these words: information source (an outside number or forecast), relative value (another market), calibration (price bucket versus win rate), timing or settlement-source timing, overreaction (a jump and its retrace), microstructure or spot-lead, open interest flow (OICORE's trigger), mention markets, other venue. Where a card gives a "family N" number (cards 74 to 93) it is kept.
- **Uniqueness vs gold L1.** Gold L1 itself is the favourite-late family (88c to 97c side, last minutes, 25c exit). UNIQUE = different entry reasoning, different management and a different information source from gold. SAME AS OICORE = a filter or threshold on the cheap-side open interest rule that is already live as the second slot; it differs from gold but is not a third mechanism. EXCLUDED = favourite-late or favourite-and-hold, rejected by the owner hard rule (Nov 11).
- **Reads required** counts Kalshi requests that would still have to be made. "0" means the data is already held or arrives from a recorder that is already running (public data, no key). A request count is quoted only where a file states one.
- **Earliest decidable** is from POWER_TABLE_NOV12.md (Bonferroni bar z 4.55, N about 9,459, per-trade sd 35c, a 10c edge; "weak" = the plain 90 percent interval at 10c) counted from Nov 12. Where the power table says UNKNOWN, this table says UNKNOWN. A date in the 2027 column is "if a log starts now", not a schedule.
- "Weeks" are floors: correlated trades shrink the effective n.

## Is there any candidate that could replace gold by Nov 16?

No. Nothing in the files can replace gold L1 by Monday Nov 16, and the reasons are structural, not a matter of effort. The shared bar (Bonferroni z 4.55 over about 9,459 tries) needs about 254 independent trades at a 10c edge, and no candidate has that, nor any chance of it in four days (0.6 weeks): the fastest, W-DERIBIT1 at its theoretical ceiling of 168 hours a week, needs 1.5 weeks, and its real coverage gives 76 usable hours today against a bar of 80, with the registered prior at 3 to 5 percent, the result leaning FAIL, and Deribit's own terms (FORBIDDEN for commercial use) blocking any live use even on a pass. The second, W-SPOTLEAD1, has n 58 and PF 1.58 at +0c but missed its own n 80 and both-halves bar, its recorder stops Nov 13 so it cannot reach n 80 without an extension to about Nov 18 to 20, and it trades the same BTC and ETH 15 minute markets as OICORE, so even a pass would sit beside OICORE rather than replace a gold edge; its prior is 5 to 8 percent. The third, W-SPREAD-CELL1 (sports SPREAD cheap YES), is the only one that could return a verdict within days of a go (274 requests, about 11 hours at the 25 reads an hour budget, expected n about 320 against a bar of 100), but it is a lead chosen as the best of about 58 cells (lower 90 percent bound -3.6c, z 1.59), has not been started, and a one-time pooled sample is not a recurring rate that could carry a strategy. Everything else in the table is FAIL, FALSIFIED, UNDERPOWERED, BLOCKED on terms, or untestable on Kalshi alone (rows over 26 weeks at the Bonferroni bar), and the favourite-late family is excluded by the owner rule. The honest memo line for Nov 16 is therefore: gold L1 has no replacement and no pass-ready sibling; the near-term action is to keep the three leads above running or ask for their go, and to say that the next honest checkpoints are about 2026-12-01 (W-DERIBIT1, OICORE filters on ETH data, WXDAYEND weak bar if its logger starts) and the Nov 18 to 20 extension question for W-SPOTLEAD1.

## What this table does not say

It says nothing about whether any idea has an edge, only where each one stands in the files and how long a fair test takes if the assumptions in POWER_TABLE_NOV12.md hold. The 35c per-trade sd is a default; a real edge below 5c needs four times the 5c figure. Families are the labels as written in the files; a reviewer may merge W-SPOTLEAD1 into the OICORE family if its trades overlap OICORE's windows, which no file measures.
