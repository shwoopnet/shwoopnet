## Card 84 stage 2 specifics fixed Nov 11 18:40Z, BEFORE any word result or price is read for scoring
Call = one settled event. Word list per call = markets of the event (GET /markets?event_ticker=E, or /historical/markets for events before 2026-09-11), key = custom_strike.Word lowercased, result yes/no. Call time anchor A = max(close_time) over the event's markets. Events with no yes/no results are dropped. History for a word at call k = results of that SAME word in the 2 immediately preceding settled calls of the same series (calls ordered by A); the word must exist in both. Calls with fewer than 2 prior calls are history only.
Rule: YES-YES and ask(YES) <= 0.70: buy YES at ask. NO-NO and ask(YES)>0.30 i.e. NO ask = 1 - yes_bid <= 0.70: buy NO at 1 - yes_bid (cash at the ask of the NO side). At most 3 words per call: keep the 3 with the cheapest cost (ties by ticker). Fee 0.07*p*(1-p) per contract, slips +0c/+1c, hold to result.
Thirds: by A across all scored calls, equal numbers of calls; middle two... (train = first third, validate = second third, held-out = last third never scored). Cluster = call.
