## Data (Kalshi public API only, no outside feed)
- Series list: GET /series?category=Climate and Weather | Financials (series_all.json). Universe = series with frequency daily, weekly, hourly, monthly (quarterly for Financials). Excluded for cost: one_off, annual, custom (and fifteen_min, which is V2's). Index = ticker containing INX, NASDAQ, NDQ, RUT, DJI, FTSE, N225, NKY, HSI, SHCOMP, IBOV, NIFTY, SX5E, KS200, DAX, CAC; other Financials = "finance".
- Settled markets: GET /markets?series_ticker=T&status=settled (paginated), pulled in priority order (KXHIGH, KXLOW, KXRAIN, KXINX, KXNASDAQ, KXUSD, KXEURUSD, KXTEMP, KXAVGT, KXRUT, KXDJI, ...) until time runs out; series with under 50 settled markets are kept but a series is not counted toward n if its markets are too few only through the cell n rule. If not all series finish by the freeze, the cells say so (DATA_LIMITED) and the list of unpulled series is written to frozen.json.
- Quotes: GET /markets/candlesticks (batch, up to 10000 candles requested across tickers): hourly candles (period 60) over [close-7d (or open), close] grouped by identical close time, and 1-minute candles over the final 70 minutes before close for markets that had an hourly quote in the final 6 hours with yes ask under 0.995 and yes bid over 0.005. Quote = candle close of yes_ask and yes_bid at end_period_ts. YES cost = ask, NO cost = 1 - bid. A candle with missing ask or bid, ask <= bid, or cost >= 0.995 is skipped.
- The recorder (RECDAILY), raincal, wxens and older caches are NOT used in the main table (one consistent source, one quote definition); they may be used later only as a labelled cross-check.

## Cells (fixed by the registration)
category (weather, finance, indices) x price bucket of the side bought, by its cost: [0.05,0.15), [0.15,0.35), [0.35,0.65), [0.65,0.88), [0.88,0.97] x time to close (tte = close_time - end_period_ts): >24h, (6h,24h], [1h,6h], [15m,60m) x side YES, NO. Excluded: bucket 0.88-0.97 with tte under 6h (favourite late). 108 cells in my scope.
## Observation rule (no result is looked at)
Per market and cell: the earliest candle whose tte falls in the bucket and whose cost for the side falls in the price bucket. Per EVENT and cell: among that event's markets, the one with the earliest such candle; ties broken by the YES mid closest to 0.50, then ticker. One observation per event per cell. Hourly candles supply the three long buckets, the 1-minute candles the 15-60 minute bucket.
## Basis
Cash at the ask. Win if result equals the side. Net per contract = win(1/0) - c' - 0.07*c'*(1-c') with c' = cost + slip, slip 0c and 1c, unrounded fee, hold to settlement. PF = sum of positive nets over sum of negative nets at the stated slip. Mean in cents per contract. z = mean/(sd/sqrt n).
## Splits and statistics
Chronological thirds by close time of the observation's market over the whole scope (tertile cut points of all settled markets in the universe): TRAIN, VALIDATE, HELD-OUT (held-out is not scored by anything I run). Bootstrap: 5000 draws, seed 1, resampling event observations, 90 percent interval of mean cents. Extra (not in the registration, stated before results): a day-clustered bootstrap (resample close dates) reported for every shortlisted cell, because all cities of one weather day move together.
Deflation: about 7,731 + 222 = about 7,950 shared ledger, Bonferroni z about 4.5; naive z 2 and the README bar 5.0-5.3 also stated. Expected noise at naive z 2 is about 5 percent of cells.
## Shortlist rule (registration)
Train and validation both n >= 60 events, cash PF >= 1.3 at +1c in both, validation 90 percent interval lower bound of mean cents > 0. At most 2 cells (best validation lower bound). Cells under n 60 in either split are DATA_LIMITED. heldout.py reads the held-out third once; I do not run it.

## AMENDMENT A (Nov 11, before any scoring): historical endpoint
GET /markets only holds markets settled since the cutoff 2026-09-12 (GET /historical/cutoff). Older settled markets come from GET /historical/markets (lists/H_<series>.json). Historical candles are single-market calls (GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks, hourly), so one call per market; to afford it, for HISTORICAL events only ONE bracket per event is quoted: the one whose previous_price_dollars (fallback last_price, both pre-settlement fields of the market object, not the result) is closest to 0.50, ties by ticker; newest events first, capped at 1,500 candle calls per stage. The 15-60 minute cell has no historical data (1-minute historical candles not pulled) and is live-only (post 2026-09-12). Live events keep all brackets with the nearest-the-money tie rule above. Counts of events/markets before and after adding history are reported in the final reply.
AMENDMENT B (before scoring): thirds are cut at the tertiles of close time of markets that HAVE candle data (the scored universe), fixed at freeze and stored in frozen.json.
AMENDMENT C: the historical list pull proved slow (about 0.5 series a minute at the shared rate). Historical lists were taken for the 161 weather series first in priority order, then indices and finance only; remaining weather series have live-only data (post 2026-09-12). Stated before scoring.
AMENDMENT D (before scoring): the live universe is large (179,892 index, 89,931 weather, 47,885 finance settled markets since 2026-09-12; about 3,200 hourly-candle calls at 10,000 candles a call). Live candles are pulled newest close first, weather and finance and indices together, one pass of hourly then 1-minute; scoring and the freeze use whatever has been pulled by the freeze time, and every cell states n. Historical (pre 2026-09-12) data: weather lists for 161 series only, and historical candles only for those; finance and indices history is pulled only if time remains (otherwise live-only and the final report says so).
