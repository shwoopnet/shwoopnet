## Card TEMPNOW1: locked temperature brackets
- Rule: on a daily high-temperature event, when the running high from the index is already above a bracket's top strike (so that bracket can no longer settle YES), buy NO on that bracket if its NO ask is under 0.97 and over 0.50; one contract; hold to settlement (the outcome is mechanical, no exit or stop needed). Mirror for a daily low: running low already below a bracket's bottom strike.
- Mechanism and counterparty: information lag, not a view. The counterparty is a holder or quoter still pricing a bracket that has become impossible (stale resting orders, retail that does not watch the index). Information source: Kalshi's own index feed plus the market quotes.
- UNIQUENESS: different from every live strategy (daily weather, not 15 minute series). RISK TO FLAG: it is a favourite-and-hold at the bought side's high price, which the project bans without an owner exception; the owner would have to give one.
- Numeric prediction (stated before data): in every case where the index says a bracket is dead, the bracket settles NO at least 99 percent of the time; the NO ask is at or under 0.97 in at least 30 percent of those cases for at least 5 minutes; cash PF cannot be 1.5 on a price of 0.95 with one loss in 100, so the test is the settlement error rate and the available size, not PF.
- Kill and falsifier: any dead bracket that settles YES (the index and the settlement source differ) twice in the first 100 cases; or fewer than 50 cases with an ask under 0.97 in two weeks; or the feed timestamp lags the quote by more than 5 minutes in more than 10 percent of cases.
- Needs: the index history with timestamps and the settled brackets (public), the quote paths of dead brackets (hourly or one minute candles), 20 city-days minimum. Exit and limit rule (i): none (hold); a resting NO buy would be scored only with book and tape.

## Card SCOREJUMP1: relayed score changes
- Rule: when a relayed score change makes one side of a game or spread market a clear favourite, buy that side at the ask within the first seconds if the quote has not moved; exit as a taker at the bid after 60 seconds or hold to settlement (two variants are two tries, registered before data; a stop fills at the first bid after it is crossed).
- Mechanism and counterparty: the quote lags the relayed score for a few seconds; the counterparty is a slow quoter or retail resting order. Information source: the score feed and the market quote path.
- UNIQUENESS: overlaps the in-play jump work already queued by other desks (INPLAYJUMP clock files); to be checked against its registration before anything is written; if it is the same mechanism this card is a duplicate and should be dropped.
- Numeric prediction: the median move of the mid in the 30 seconds after a relayed score change is at least 2c in the direction of the score, and the fillable first-ask share is at least 50 percent; cash PF 1.5 after fee at +1c slip in each of three windows by close date.
- Kill: median move under the fee plus 1c; or fill rate under 40 percent at +1c; or the feed arrives after the quote has moved (lag under zero) in more than half of the events.
- Needs: score events with timestamps and a one second quote path around them (not hourly candles, so a recorder), which the project does not have today; development data cannot exist before the feed is recorded.
