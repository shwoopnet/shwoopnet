DEFLATION: N tried before this sweep about 900 (REFINE_STATUS); plus 6,480 cells = about 7,380. Bonferroni two-sided 0.05 gives z about 4.5; reported alongside the naive z. Every cell is scored, none dropped.
SPLITS (chronological, by window close date, fixed now): TRAIN first third, VALIDATE second third, HELD-OUT last third (read ONCE, after one cell family is frozen on train and validation); the Jun to Jul Bitcoin file is an extra independent check for Bitcoin cells.
BASIS: cash from the ask at the look (taker, +0c matches live, +1c harsher), unrounded fee 0.07*p*(1-p), hold to settlement, one contract per window per cell, per-day clustered bootstrap for intervals.
SHORTLIST RULE: a cell advances only if train and validation both have n at least 60 and cash PF at least 1.3 at +1c with the day-clustered 90 percent interval of mean cents per contract above 0 in validation; at most 3 advance (the best by validation lower bound); the held-out is then read once. A held-out PF at least 1.2 with n at least 40 and mean above 0 makes it a SHADOW CANDIDATE only. Anything not clearing the Bonferroni z is reported as exploratory, never a pass.
FAIL READS reported as plainly as passes: expected outcome under pure noise is about 0 to 1 cell clearing naive z 2 per 40 cells, so about 160 cells will look good by chance; the deflation is the point.
