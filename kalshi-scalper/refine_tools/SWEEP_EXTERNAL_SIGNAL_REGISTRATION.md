SHORTLIST RULE: a cell advances only if train and validation both have n at least 60 and cash PF at least 1.3 at +1c with the day-clustered 90 percent interval of mean cents per contract above 0 in validation; at most 3 advance (the best by validation lower bound); the held-out is then read once.
