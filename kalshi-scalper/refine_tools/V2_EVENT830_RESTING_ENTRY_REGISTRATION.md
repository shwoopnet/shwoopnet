## The version (the only change: how it enters)
- t0 = the first NC15 book row received at or after close minus 180 s. The same spread test (10c or less) and the same cheaper-side band test (5c to 50c, on the ask at t0) as the parent, so the market-windows are identical.
- LIMIT PRICE RULE: rest a BUY of the cheaper side at its OWN best bid at t0 (no improvement, no chase); no bid or a bid of 0: no order (counted, not scored).
- REST TIME: until close minus 120 s; CANCEL at the first NC15 row at or after that time; no taker fallback, no re-price.
- FILL RULE: identical to V1's (a trade strictly through our price on the tape after t0 and before the cancel, or a later book row with that side's ask at or below our price; queue ignored; fill price = our limit).
- EXIT: hold to settlement. FEES: parent taker schedule 0.07*p*(1-p) unrounded at +0c and +1c; the version at 25 percent of the taker schedule (primary), 0 percent and taker schedule as sensitivities.

One read of each: development after the scorer is frozen and audited (rules 16, 16b), forward on or after 2026-12-24.
