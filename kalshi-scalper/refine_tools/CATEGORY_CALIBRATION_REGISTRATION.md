# W-CAT-CAL1: non-crypto category calibration sweep (owner "find something", Nov 11). REGISTERED BEFORE ANY RUN. Nothing live changes.
Split with V2: V2 takes HISTORICAL settled-market data for the 11 non-crypto 15 minute series. Refinement takes the other high-volume non-crypto categories already held: economics, weather, finance, indices. FOUR CHECKS. ENTRY: a bucket of (category, price of the side bought, time to close) where the realized win rate differs from the price by more than the fee, found on Kalshi's own prices and settlements only (no outside feed). MANAGEMENT: none. EXIT: hold to settlement. CLOSEST EXISTING: gold L1 (favourite 0.88 to 0.97 late in a 15 minute window); DIFFERS: different markets (daily, weekly, monthly, hourly), time to close hours or days, mechanism = category calibration bias (longshot or favourite) in markets with retail flow. TO AVOID A FAVOURITE-LATE VARIANT, the cells with the bought side priced at 0.88 or more and under 6 hours to close are EXCLUDED. Declined list respected: no Polymarket, ESPN, NHL, MLB, NBA.com or paid feed. DATA HELD: (a) V2's RECDAILY recorder on quote-data (daily/: quotes of daily, weekly and monthly markets every 30 minutes since Nov 3, with results; watchlist kalshi-scalper/workers/RECDAILY_WATCHLIST.json on claude/epic-ptolemy-0amjd9), (b) RAINCAL and wxens quotes with results (weather), (c) my pulls: KXINXU hourly candles (w_row59), KXHIGH daily-high candles for NYC, LAX, Denver, Dallas (w_row49), KXGAS and KXAAAGASW, KXFED, KXJOBLESS and the economic series candle caches in the scratchpad, (d) Kalshi public settled markets, candlesticks and trades (keyless, 3 s spacing, 429 backoff, resumable). GRID (fixed now, 160 cells): category (economics, weather, finance, indices) x price bucket of the side bought (0.05 to 0.15, 0.15 to 0.35, 0.35 to 0.65, 0.65 to 0.87, 0.88 to 0.97) x time to close (more than 24 h, 6 to 24 h, 1 to 6 h, 15 to 60 min) x side (YES, NO), minus the excluded cells above (the 0.88 to 0.97 bucket with under 6 h is removed, leaving about 148). One contract per market per cell, one observation per market per cell (the first quote in the bucket). BASIS: cash at the ask, +0c and +1c, unrounded fee 0.07*p*(1-p), hold to settlement, N counted, per-event clustered bootstrap (an event's brackets are not independent: one observation per event per cell, the nearest bracket to the money if several). SPLITS (chronological by close date): TRAIN first third, VALIDATE second third, HELD-OUT last third read ONCE. DEFLATION: about 7,731 (shared ledger with V2, see the sweep registration) plus about 148 = about 7,880; Bonferroni z about 4.5; the README bar (5.0 to 5.3) also stated. SHORTLIST RULE: a cell advances only if train and validation both have n at least 60 events and cash PF at least 1.3 at +1c with the event-clustered 90 percent interval of mean cents per contract above 0 in validation; at most 1 or 2 cells advance (best by validation lower bound); the held-out is read once; held-out PF at least 1.2 with n at least 40 and mean above 0 makes it a SHADOW CANDIDATE only. If nothing survives: one plain line. The expected noise count at naive z 2 is about 7 of 148 cells: reported as exploratory, never a pass. EXPECTED SIZE: the recorder holds 8 days (about 400 daily or weekly markets), history candles cover about 10 weeks for weather, indices, gas; many cells will have n under 60: those are reported as DATA_LIMITED with the date they reach 60 at the current pace. RUN: workers allowed from 2026-11-13 16:00Z (usage reset); the pull scripts are resumable and run in the background. First numbers within 12 hours of usage availability.
